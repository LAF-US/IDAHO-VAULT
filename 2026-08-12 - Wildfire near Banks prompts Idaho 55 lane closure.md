*UPDATE: This story was updated on Aug. 12 at 1:48 p.m. to include the latest containment* information *released by the Idaho Department of Lands*

The Milepost 81 Fire near Banks is expected to be contained by noon Thursday, according to the Idaho Department of Lands.

Crews responded Wednesday to the new wildfire start reported near Idaho 55 about 1.5 miles northeast of Banks. Aerial suppression efforts have “largely halted” the fire from spreading further north and east, [an IDL social media post said](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1DH4LCq8Y8/).

Western States - Lookout creative - 4:1

The Milepost 81 Fire was first reported at about 3 a.m. on Aug. 12 and has since grown to about 100 acres.

![](https://valleylookout.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/08/valleylookout.com-wildfire-near-banks-reduces-idaho-55-to-one-lane-screenshot-2026-08-12-at-10.59.16-am.png)

The Milepost 81 Fire is burning between Idaho 55 and the Banks-Lowman Highway. Map: Via WatchDuty.org

Idaho 55 is reduced to one lane with pilot cars guiding alternating traffic while firefighters work to suppress the fire. [Videos posted to social media](https://www.facebook.com/reel/906985575815157) by passing motorists show flames clearly within view of the highway.

“ITD is coordinating with Boise County and the Bureau of Land Management to help maintain safe travel through the area while fire crews and equipment are operating near and on the highway,” said Anna McLean, a spokesperson for the Idaho Transportation Department.

![Valley Lookout house - generic](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/82689d5d-b8b7-4c7f-9d4c-b24100ffd632.gif)

Valley Lookout house - generic

Four fire engine crews are currently on the scene, with more resources and aircraft ordered. An exact number of personnel on the fire was not immediately available on Wednesday morning.

“As this is a newly started fire with rapid growth, lots of resources are called in within this initial time period so the number is quickly changing and growing,” said Jonathan Luhnow, an IDL spokesperson.

IDL is advising rafters, kayakers, and other recreational users to avoid the Payette River near Banks to allow free access for two helicopters to fill water buckets. Drone should also not be flown in the area to avoid disrupting firefighting operations.

The cause of the fire has not yet been determined and remains under investigation, Luhnow said.
