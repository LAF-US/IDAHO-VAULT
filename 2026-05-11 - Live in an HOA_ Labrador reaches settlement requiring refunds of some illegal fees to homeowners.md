Idaho’s Attorney General says that homeowners across the state were charged illegal HOA transfer fees, and that the issue stretches wider than just a handful of neighborhoods.

Last week, Attorney General Raúl Labrador announced settlements with two homeowners’ associations and one HOA management company after complaints that homeowners were charged transfer fees that were never disclosed in HOA governing documents.

The management company, Park Pointe Management Services of Ada County, contracts with approximately 70 HOAs across Idaho, according to the attorney general’s office.

Under the [Idaho Homeowners Association Act](https://boisedev.com/news/2022/12/30/icymi-live-in-an-hoa-the-idaho-legislature-quietly-created-a-new-hoa-act-heres-whats-included/), [Idaho Code 55-3205](https://28b3837c.click.convertkit-mail4.com/n4u7pwogqwfvhxp6pk4u6h647vpwrtlhol280/dpheh0henp3ww2fm/aHR0cHM6Ly9sZWdpc2xhdHVyZS5pZGFoby5nb3Yvd3AtY29udGVudC91cGxvYWRzL3N0YXR1dGVzcnVsZXMvaWRzdGF0L1RpdGxlNTUvVDU1Q0gzMi5wZGY_dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1ib2lzZWRldi1lbWFpbCZ1dG1fbWVkaXVtPWVtYWlsJnV0bV9jYW1wYWlnbj1ib2lzZWRldi1hbQ==), HOAs can only charge transfer fees if those fees are explicitly disclosed in the neighborhood’s governing documents, known as CC&Rs – Conditions, Covenants, and Restrictions. Idaho law does not give HOA management companies independent authority to charge transfer fees.

“Idaho families work hard to buy a home, and they deserve to know every cost before they sign,” said Attorney General Labrador. “Hidden fees collected without legal authority violate Idaho law, and we will hold HOAs and the companies that manage them accountable.”

The settlements, known as Assurances of Voluntary Compliance, were signed by: Pristine Springs Homeowners Association of Ada County, Armstrong Park Homeowners Association of Kootenai County, and Park Pointe Management Services of Ada County.

According to the Attorney General’s Office, Armstrong Park Homeowners Association must refund $195 to homeowners who were improperly charged transfer fees within 90 days. Park Pointe Management Services must identify homeowners who were improperly charged transfer fees and issue refunds within 90 days.

The Attorney General’s Office says it will continue monitoring the entities’ fee practices under the agreements.
