## League of Women Voters of Idaho to co-host voter registration drive at LUSH

![Residents take part in the voting process for the primary election on May 21, 2024, at Hillcrest High School in Idaho Falls](https://idahocapitalsun.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Voting-014-2048x1400.jpg)

Residents take part in the voting process for the primary election on May 21, 2024, at Hillcrest High School in Idaho Falls. (Photo by Pat Sutphin for the Idaho Capital Sun)

The League of Women Voters of Idaho and LUSH Fresh Handmade Cosmetics are teaming up to co-host a voter registration drive on Saturday in Meridian.

The nonpartisan voter registration event will be held from noon to 2 p.m. at the LUSH location at the Village at Meridian, 3597 E. Monarch Sky Lane #225, according to a press release from the League.

The cosmetics company is collaborating with League volunteers who will register voters and demonstrate [VOTE411.org](http://vote411.org/), the League’s nonpartisan, comprehensive voter guide, according to the release.

Early voting begins for the May 2026 primary election on April 27 in Idaho. More information on the election can be found on the Idaho Secretary of State’s [VoteIdaho.gov website](https://voteidaho.gov/), where voters can check their registration status, request an absentee ballot, or view a primary election sample ballot.

“Both LUSH and the League see an urgent need to engage Idahoans in this most important civic responsibility, especially in an election that traditionally attracts few voters,” the League’s press release said.
