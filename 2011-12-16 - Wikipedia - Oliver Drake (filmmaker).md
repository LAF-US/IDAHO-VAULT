**Clarence Oliver Drake** (May 28, 1903 – August 19, 1991) was an American film/television director, screenwriter, producer and actor who was most active in the [Western genre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_\(genre\) "Western (genre)").[^1] Though Drake began his career as an actor, he is best known as a prolific screenwriter and director of low-budget Western films (sometimes referred to as B-Westerns).[^1] Drake was most active in the 1930s and 1940s, although he continued writing and directing films until 1974.

His films include *[Today I Hang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Today_I_Hang "Today I Hang")* (1942).
