### Moments made in Silver & Light

#### FAQ About the Process:

This is a one of a kind 1850s photographic process-in camera by hand with natural light, heart, and silver. It is an imperfect process saturated with the beautiful element of humanness.

\-EMAIL ME to set up an appointment. (hellokendraelise@gmail.com)

\-You will receive the physical tin type & a digital scan of the plate

\-\*There is no negative involved so if you'd like additional images taken during your session with me you will be asked to pay $65 per additional plate past the initial fee\*

\-Each session will take about 1 hour as this is a slow in camera photographic technique I need your willingness to sit and be in the moment with me. For that is when the magic happens!

\-What to wear: I only ask for NO logos or graphic t-shirts. Please dress nicely have fun + be yourself!!
