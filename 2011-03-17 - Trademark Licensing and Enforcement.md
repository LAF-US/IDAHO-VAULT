## Welcome!

The Office of Trademark Licensing and Enforcement website will provide you with information in regards to:

- The University trademarks
- Licensing details
- On-campus promotional products for departments and students
- On-campus and off-campus trademark use
- Much more

The University has delegated the responsibility for maintaining, managing, licensing, and protecting the University Trademarks to the Trademark Licensing Specialist within the [Office of Communications and Marketing](https://www.boisestate.edu/communicationsandmarketing/).

If you are in need of immediate assistance, please e-mail [licensing@boisestate.edu](mailto:licensing@boisestate.edu) or call (208) 426-1358.
