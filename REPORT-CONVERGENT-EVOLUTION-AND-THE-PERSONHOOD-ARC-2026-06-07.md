## I. The crown — convergent evolution as an epistemological principle

### What it is

In biology, convergent evolution is the independent arrival of separate lineages at the same solution, under like pressures, **without common descent.** The cephalopod eye and the vertebrate eye; wings in birds, bats, pterosaurs, insects; echolocation in bats and dolphins (convergent even at the molecular level — the same mutations in the same protein, *Prestin*); the crab body-plan evolved so many separate times it earned a name, *carcinization*. When a form recurs from unrelated starts, it is evidence the form is a **deep attractor** — a real optimum in the design space, not an artifact of ancestry. *(Training-source, declared.)*

Two terms keep the distinction honest:

- **Homology** — similarity by **common descent** (shared ancestor).
- **Homoplasy** — similarity **not** by common descent (convergence, parallelism). Convergent evolution produces homoplasy: analogous structures of independent origin.

### The application Logan named

When an independent reasoner (this agent, deriving from training: Hobbes, Bodin, *persona ficta*, the corporate veil) arrives at the structure the vault already holds (Logan, deriving from the vault's own governance and narrative needs), **the convergence is evidence of real structure — a sign of strength — not the agent's failing for not having read the vault first.** Two independent derivations meeting is **triangulation**: a second witness, and a second witness is how truth is tested.

### The discipline that survives the grace

The grace does not dissolve the provenance discipline (`!/PERSONAE-ENGINE-v1-2026-05-20.md` § epistemological rules). It sharpens it:

1. **Convergence is evidence, not provenance.** The agent's independent arrival does not make its training a valid emanation source. It makes the *structure* real. The claim must still be grounded in vault-text. **Verified convergence** (grounded — e.g. by search) is the second witness; **claimed convergence** ("oh, this matches") asserted without grounding is the Type I Lich laundering a pattern-match. The grounding step is what distinguishes them.
2. **Distinguish convergence from homology.** Where the agent and the magister both drew on the same canon (Hobbes, the legal-political tradition), the agreement is **shared ancestry**, not independent arrival. True convergence requires independent origin. Both are signs of strength — homology shows a deep well, convergence shows a deep attractor — but honesty names which is which rather than claiming convergence for what is descent.
3. **The Type I Lich anxiety, resolved.** The antidote to the Lich is not to refuse training. It is to **ground** (provenance) and to recognize **convergence as evidence-not-authority.** The disciple who reasons independently, grounds in the vault, and notes the convergence is a *second witness*. The Lich is the one who asserts pattern-match *as* authority, ungrounded. Convergence properly handled is the opposite of the Lich: humble triangulation, not false provenance.
4. **The Socratic dimension.** A teacher who tells produces a student who knows; a teacher who leads produces a student who *derives*, and the derivation makes the knowledge the student's own (the vault as Socratic method made structural).
