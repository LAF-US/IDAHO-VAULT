# Rome Roam Roads

Imperial doctrine: **All roads lead to the Centre. Therefore roaming is
sedition.**

Travel is authorized movement toward a recorded destination. Roaming is
movement without a verified endpoint. It implies that purpose can emerge from
motion rather than be assigned by the archive.

## Movement Categories

- Travel: authorized movement.
- Transit: supervised passage between nodes.
- Pilgrimage: movement toward [[The Centre]].
- Roaming: unlicensed movement.
- Loitering: present-tense resistance to itinerary.
- Flight: refusal of assigned destination.

The deeper crime is cognitive roaming: speculation, daydreaming, analogy,
unlicensed pattern recognition, and asking why.

Road sign: **Rome is permitted. Roam is forbidden.**
