First it was a Target, then a Buc-ee’s, now a new user is interested in the large lot off Meridian Rd. and I-84.

According to a preliminary site plan obtained by BoiseDev, a large, 150,000-square-foot retail space is planned for the area with five other smaller retail spaces surrounding it.

The main retail space has loading docks located on the side of the building. Also on site is a large fuel station.

![ICCU - May 2026 - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/fb80406e-2f0f-4291-9c09-5bc7bec024d5.png)

ICCU - May 2026 - 2:1

Hawkins Pre-development Manager Ethan Mansfield confirmed that the Boise developer is in negotiations with an interested party to set up shop on the site, but could not identify which company it is.

## ‘User kicking around’

![](https://boisedev.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/large-warehouse-space-planned-on-hawkins-owned-property-north-of-i-84-Screenshot-2026-07-2.png)

Site plan for the property off Meridian Rd. and I-84. Map: Courtesy KM Engineering

“There’s definitely a user kicking around,” Mansfield said. “So there is a renewed interest in this corner, and [it’s obviously not Buc-ee’s.”](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/07/23/buc-ees-out-for-busy-meridian-interchange-but-not-giving-up-on-treasure-valley/)

Retailers with shop sizes around 150,000 square feet include Costco Wholesale, Sam’s Club, Walmart Supercenter, Target, and an offshoot of Dick’s Sporting Goods called “Dick’s House of Sport.” Of those, the retailers with large fuel stations on site are typically Costco and Sam’s Club. The site plan also shows an area designed to allow vehicles access directly to the building, similar to the tire centers found at warehouse retailers like Costco and Sam’s Club.

![Build Boise Display Ad 3 (1).png](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/226ebbd0-4265-463f-ae19-bde42b96d5f1.png)

Build Boise Display Ad 3 (1).png

Costco has submitted plans for multiple new locations in the Treasure Valley in the past several years. Currently, there is a Costco in Boise off Cole Rd., in Meridian off Ten Mile Rd., and in Nampa off the Karcher exit. Another location is [under construction in South Meridian](https://boisedev.com/news/2025/06/26/costco-meridian-kuna/) for Lake Hazel Rd. and Meridian Rd. [As BoiseDev reported,](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/03/13/costco-eagle-compplan/) another Costco location was planned in Eagle, but conflicts with the city’s comprehensive plan left the location up in the air.

Costco previously told BoiseDev it does not comment on possible store locations until three months before they are set to open.

Sam’s Club previously operated in the Treasure Valley market, with a location near the Idaho Center in Nampa. The warehouse [closed in 2010](https://www.idahopress.com/news/nampa-sams-club-to-close/article_59b73462-d08c-5463-a8c0-c6b3de8f5ff6.html), and is now a College of Western Idaho building.

![City of McCall - Visiting? 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/57592d17-0c69-4d28-a6e9-c5d629733da1.png)

City of McCall - Visiting? 2:1

One of the pads on the site plan shows a large, two-lane drive-thru, but Mansfield said this was conceptual in nature and the company does not have a specific use in mind.

Because a development agreement already exists on the site, this site plan would not have to go through the City of Meridian’s planning and zoning process or city council approval should Hawkins finalize a deal with the interested business. All needed approvals for the site will be made at a city staff level.
