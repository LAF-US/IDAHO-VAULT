# Deltora — The Gem Guardians

> A standalone reference on **the seven Gem Guardians of *Deltora Quest*** (Emily Rodda) — the keepers the **Shadow Lord** set over the scattered gems of the Belt, each in its own deadly place. Companion to `[[RESEARCH_Deltora-The-Seven-Tribes-and-their-Lands-2026-06-04]]`. This node holds **no cross-mapping** — pure source.

> **Provenance.** Web-verified 2026-06-07 against the Deltora Quest Wiki and Wikipedia. Tiers: **[canon-per-series]**. Paraphrased; no source text reproduced.

## What they are

**[canon]** After the Shadow Lord broke the Belt and **scattered the seven gems**, each was hidden in a guarded peril and kept by a **Guardian** — a monster or cursed being set to hold it. Several Guardians are not natural beasts but **victims transformed or corrupted** by the Shadow Lord (or his servant sorceress **Thaegan**). The gems are recovered in this order across the quest:

## The seven Guardians

- **[[RESEARCH_Deltora-Gorl-2026-06-07\|Gorl]] — the Topaz, Forests of Silence.** A knight in golden armour, last of three brothers, consumed by his obsession with the **Lilies of Life**; he keeps the topaz deep in the Mid Wood.
- **[[RESEARCH_Deltora-Soldeen-2026-06-07\|Soldeen]] — the Ruby, Lake of Tears.** A giant eel-like beast ruling the lake under the sorceress **Thaegan**; **not a monster by nature** — revealed to be **Nanion**, leader of the **D'Or** people, transformed by Thaegan's magic, and restored when she is defeated.
- **[[RESEARCH_Deltora-Reeah-2026-06-07\|Reeah]] — the Opal, City of the Rats.** A colossal serpent lairing in the rat-overrun ruins; when wounded it divides into multiple snakes.
- **[[RESEARCH_Deltora-The-Hive-2026-06-07\|The Hive]] (Queen Bee) — the Lapis Lazuli, Shifting Sands.** A vast collective creature of bees; the gem had been built into the wax of the **Hive's pyramid**, supporting a cell — so it had to be retrieved (a wooden bird left in its place) without collapsing the structure and rousing the swarm.
- **[[RESEARCH_Deltora-Gellick-2026-06-07\|Gellick]] — the Emerald, Dread Mountain.** A vast **Ooze Toad** who **enslaved and cruelly ruled the Dread Gnomes**, squatting amid their treasure with the emerald.
- **[[RESEARCH_Deltora-The-Glus-2026-06-07\|The Glus]] — the Amethyst, Maze of the Beast.** A huge slug-like beast that **spits sticky webs** to trap prey and hunts any who enter its maze.
- **[[RESEARCH_Deltora-The-Guardian-Fardeep-2026-06-07\|The Guardian]] (Fardeep) — the Diamond, Valley of the Lost.** A man the Shadow Lord cursed to **pose as King Endon** (to break the spirit of would-be resisters). He is bound to **four "pet" monsters — Pride, Envy, Hate, and Greed —** living growths of his own corruption, attached to him by cords of flesh. The diamond is won by a **name-guessing game** (lose, and you are trapped in the Valley); the gem **curses any who steal rather than earn it.**
