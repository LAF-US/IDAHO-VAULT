**Benito Skinner** (born November 3, 1993) is an American comedian and actor. He is known for his online persona Benny Drama, and performs celebrity impressions, skits, and original characters on platforms such as [Instagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram "Instagram"), YouTube, and [TikTok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok "TikTok").

In 2025, Skinner created and starred in *[Overcompensating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overcompensating_\(TV_series\) "Overcompensating (TV series)")* for [Amazon Prime Video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Prime_Video "Amazon Prime Video").

## Early life and education

Skinner was born and raised in [Boise, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise,_Idaho "Boise, Idaho"), and attended [Bishop Kelly High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishop_Kelly_High_School "Bishop Kelly High School"). His family is [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church "Catholic Church"), but he has said they "weren't really practicing".[^1] While a senior in high school, Skinner founded the nonprofit OATHS, which stands for Organization Assisting the Homeless Student, and was recognized at the 2011 National Philanthropy Day [[Idaho]].[^2] He also played on Bishop Kelly's football team as a wide receiver.[^3]

Some of his early comedic inspirations were [Robin Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robin_Williams "Robin Williams") in his role as *[Mrs. Doubtfire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mrs._Doubtfire "Mrs. Doubtfire")*, and [*SpongeBob SquarePants*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SpongeBob_SquarePants_\(character\) "SpongeBob SquarePants (character)"). While he would perform renditions of [Britney Spears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britney_Spears "Britney Spears") and [Destiny's Child](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Destiny's_Child "Destiny's Child") songs for his family, he has discussed repressing some of his early proclivities toward performance and comedy due to bullying and homophobic comments.[^4]

He went on to attend [Georgetown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgetown_University "Georgetown University") in Washington, D.C., where he studied acting, English, and film and media studies. In college, Skinner began experimenting with performance in the form of recorded lip syncs and other videos. He had created the @BennyDrama7 Instagram account in high school – with the "7" referencing his football jersey number – but only began to post comedic videos while in college.[^5] [^4]

While at Georgetown, Skinner was also the co-creator and host of BEAT$, a weekly campus radio show known for its pop music stylings.[^6]

## Career

After graduating, Skinner worked as a video editor while continuing to create online content. In 2016, Skinner made his social media accounts public, and this early work developed into comedy videos on [Instagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram "Instagram"), [TikTok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok "TikTok"), and YouTube.[^7] [^4] He is known for his celebrity impressions of, e.g., [Shawn Mendes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawn_Mendes "Shawn Mendes"), [Lana Del Rey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lana_Del_Rey "Lana Del Rey"), and [Kourtney Kardashian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kourtney_Kardashian "Kourtney Kardashian"), as well as for original characters, including Jenni the Hairdresser. He has described his work as fully rooted in pop culture.[^3]

In 2018, he performed his debut show "Overcompensating" at [Carolines on Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carolines_on_Broadway "Carolines on Broadway") to a sold-out audience at the [New York Comedy Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Comedy_Festival "New York Comedy Festival").[^8] He hosted the pop culture podcast *Obsessed* with [Mary Beth Barone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Beth_Barone "Mary Beth Barone") on Spotify for one season and 50 episodes.[^9] [^10] Skinner and Barone launched a pop culture podcast, *Ride*, in 2023.[^11]

Skinner gained notoriety in 2021 for filming a series of TikToks with the [White House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House_Office "White House Office"). He acted as a character nicknamed the “Gen Z intern” and urged young people to obtain [COVID-19 vaccines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_vaccine "COVID-19 vaccine").[^12] [^13] [^14] In 2023, Skinner was listed under [Forbes 30 Under 30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes_30_Under_30 "Forbes 30 Under 30") in the social media category.[^15]

In 2024, Skinner starred in [Chelsea Peretti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chelsea_Peretti "Chelsea Peretti") 's first feature, *[First Time Female Director](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Time_Female_Director "First Time Female Director").*[^11] In 2025, he hosted the press conference organized by [Spotify](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotify "Spotify") for [Lady Gaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Gaga "Lady Gaga") 's *[Mayhem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayhem_\(Lady_Gaga_album\) "Mayhem (Lady Gaga album)")* album release and interviewed the singer.[^16] The same year, Skinner created the series *[Overcompensating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overcompensating_\(TV_series\) "Overcompensating (TV series)")* for [Amazon Prime Video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Prime_Video "Amazon Prime Video").[^17] In February 2026, he appeared in the music video for [Maisie Peters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maisie_Peters "Maisie Peters") ' single "My Regards".[^18]

## Personal life

Skinner is [gay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gay "Gay") and came out in his senior year of college. He was in a relationship with director and photographer Terrence O'Connor from 2016 to 2025. They resided in Los Angeles, California. They were known for their elaborate and carefully constructed annual couples' Halloween costumes.[^19] [^20]
