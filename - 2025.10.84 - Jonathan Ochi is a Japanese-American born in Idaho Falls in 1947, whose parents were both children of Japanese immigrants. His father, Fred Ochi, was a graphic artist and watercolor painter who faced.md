title: "2025.10.84 - Jonathan Ochi is a Japanese-American born in Idaho Falls in 1947, whose parents were both children of Japanese immigrants. His father, Fred Ochi, was a graphic artist and watercolor painter who faced discrimination during World War II, including being forced to leave California's West Coast. Jonathan grew up in Idaho Falls, attended Yale University during the tumultuous late 1960s, and served in the Peace Corps in Nepal.<br /><br />Key highlights include:<br /><br />>Family history of immigration and resilience<br />>Experiences with cultural discrimination<br />>Involvement in 1960s social movements<br />>Career in graphic arts, following his father's footsteps<br />>Passion for outdoor activities like whitewater kayaking<br />>Active in Japanese American Citizens League (JACL)<br /><br />The interview provides a personal narrative of Japanese-American experience, cultural identity, and social change in mid-20th century America."
