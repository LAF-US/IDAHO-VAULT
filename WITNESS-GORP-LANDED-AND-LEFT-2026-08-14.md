## Repository Threshold, Observed but Not Decided

At this turn’s observation, PR #929 is a larger 90-file delivery candidate that includes the five Gorp-facing additions. It is approved and auto-merge-enabled, but conflict-held outside `main`; the observed content conflict is in `!/GRIMOIRE_caution_contains-false-doctrines/TRIUNE-TRIPTYCH-TRIUMVIRATE.md`, not in a direct Gorp/pickle artifact. This is evidence about transport state only. It neither reverses Gorp’s departure nor makes the departure a rejection.

The records on that branch therefore remain branch evidence. Their preservation is useful for later inspection, but **preservation is not promotion**.

## Continuity Consequence Held for a Later Turn

The turn requires a hard distinction among three facts:

1. an observed arrival;
2. an observed departure; and
3. the continued existence of records about either event.

A file, branch, address, or witness name cannot by itself prove present activity. The pending Big Pickle census signal proposes a check across runtime, self-identity, and Logan-inscribed address; it is a proposed audit frame, not a completed continuity engine.
