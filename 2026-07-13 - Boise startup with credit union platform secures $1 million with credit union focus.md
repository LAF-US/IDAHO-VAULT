A Boise startup that created a marketplace platform built for credit unions has registered as a credit union service organization (CUSO) and secured $1 million from investors.

Goodbuy connects credit union members with local small businesses, helping businesses reach new customers they may not connect with through traditional advertising. Credit unions benefit through membership growth and new revenue opportunities.

The company has secured $1 million in investment from four credit union partners: One Washington Financial (OWF), Reseda Group, Maps Credit Union, and Together Credit Union.

![Corwin Ford Nampa - July 2026 - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/02b5ccc2-e925-42c6-9e39-de8bd0223814.jpg)

Corwin Ford Nampa - July 2026 - 2:1

In addition to the funding, Goodbuy has also registered as a credit union service organization (CUSO).

“Becoming a CUSO is a foundational milestone for us,” Goodbuy CEO and co-founder Cara Oppenheimer said. “It means we’re not just a vendor to credit unions, we’re part of the ecosystem. This investment from four credit union partners signals that credit unions are ready to build this together, not just buy it.”

Credit union members are able to save up to 20% when shopping at participating businesses.

![208 Market - July 2026 - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/2de7e0a8-34ae-41b8-b550-948663e8552e.gif)

208 Market - July 2026 - 2:1

“Small businesses are the backbone of credit union communities, but most credit unions have had no real way to show up for them beyond holding the account,” Goodbuy co-founder Cary Telander Fortin said. “With Goodbuy, credit unions can acquire new business relationships, grow SMB deposits, and turn their member base into a real growth channel for local businesses.”

The $1 million investment and CUSO registration come after a series of milestones for Goodbuy, including becoming the 2024 NACUSO “Next Big Idea” winner and acceptance into Velera’s Fintech Engagement Program.
