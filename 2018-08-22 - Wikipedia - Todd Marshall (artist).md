**Todd S. Marshall** is an American illustrator and [paleoartist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paleoart "Paleoart") specializing in reconstructions of prehistoric animals, particularly [dinosaurs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinosaurs "Dinosaurs"), as well as [concept art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concept_art "Concept art") for video game companies and films. Marshall's original dinosaur art has been featured in natural history museums, scientific papers and children's books.[^2]

In [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles") in the early 1980s he painted album covers for heavy metal bands including [Mötley Crüe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%B6tley_Cr%C3%BCe "Mötley Crüe"), [W.A.S.P.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W.A.S.P._\(band\) "W.A.S.P. (band)"), and [L.A. Guns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L.A._Guns "L.A. Guns"). His interest in and production of concept art began under the mentorship of character designer Mark "Crash" McCreery, and Marshall later became well-known as a paleoartist by working on a number of new dinosaur discoveries and press releases with [paleontologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paleontology "Paleontology") [Paul Sereno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Sereno "Paul Sereno") at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago "University of Chicago").[^3] He is based in [Boise, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise,_Idaho "Boise, Idaho").[^2]

In interview with [Steve White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_White_\(comics\) "Steve White (comics)"), Marshall describes his introduction to the art of dinosaur reconstruction through the 1966 film *[One Million Years B.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Million_Years_B.C. "One Million Years B.C.")*, which inspired his first attempt at paleoart (a *[Brontosaurus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brontosaurus "Brontosaurus")*) at age four or five. Originally from [Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas "Kansas"), he moved to Los Angeles to pursue his dream of becoming a rock star, and later decided that it would be more lucrative and consistent with his talents to become an illustrator. He attended the [Art Center College of Design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_Center_College_of_Design "Art Center College of Design") in [Pasadena, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasadena,_California "Pasadena, California"), where he met "Crash" McCreery, who Marshall cites as a chief influence along with music stars such as [Elvis Presley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elvis_Presley "Elvis Presley"), [special effects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_effects "Special effects") artists such as [Ray Harryhausen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Harryhausen "Ray Harryhausen"), movie producers and directors including [George Lucas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lucas "George Lucas") and [Steven Spielberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Spielberg "Steven Spielberg"), and classic paleoartists such as [Charles R. Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_R._Knight "Charles R. Knight"). As a professional paleoartist, Marshall prefers to work in [acrylic paints](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acrylic_paints "Acrylic paints") and particularly enjoys the use of [airbrushes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbrush "Airbrush").[^3]
