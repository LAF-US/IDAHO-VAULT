Idaho Freedom Caucus Co-Chair Tammy Nichols is defending her vote to fund a socialist childcare welfare program that is ripe for Somali-style fraud.

In wandering and unclear social media posts Thursday, Nichols defended her vote to pass Senate Bill 1206, a spending bill which gave tens of millions in federal dollars to a childcare program. That program is nearly identical to the program allegedly abused by Somalis in Minnesota.

“S1206 was a budget (appropriations) bill, not a policy bill,” Nichols wrote on social media. “It did not create or authorize a new program or change Idaho law. It made funding decisions for an already existing program, using primarily federal pass-through dollars, for the next fiscal year.”

Senate Bill 1206 was, in fact, a budget bill. The measure allowed the state to spend tens of millions of federal dollars to subsidize childcare for Idaho families.

In short, it was redistribution of wealth.

**It’s unclear why the Idaho Freedom Caucus co-chair voted to support redistribution of wealth, but she wasn’t alone in her vote.** Sen. Josh Keyser, Rep. Jordan Redman, and Speaker Mike Moyle, among others, joined her in voting for the welfare handout.

Nichols’ post doesn’t explain her vote in a substantive way.

“Legislators weighed these policy considerations and voted accordingly,” her post concluded. “S1206 was about funding decisions, not new law.”

Interestingly, one of her colleagues saw the bill for what it was.

In a separate social media post, Sen. Brian Lenney called the bill “a massive expansion of the nanny state.”
