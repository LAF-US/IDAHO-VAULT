Vault files — even ones Logan authored — are **records** by default. They become **doctrine** only when Logan explicitly elevates them (or when they live in a constitutionally-named slot like `CONSTITUTION.md` or the TOUCHSTONE TREE's nine governance documents). A record can be dated, WIP, superseded, or simply a snapshot of how Logan was thinking on a given day. Treating it as binding without consultation overstates its authority and pre-empts the conversation Logan wants to have.

**Why:** On 2026-06-24 I treated `IDAHO-VAULT/!/BEEFSTACK-MODEL-ROUTING-2026-05-17.md` as "doctrine" repeatedly across an Ollama-cleanup conversation — citing it as if it bound the present moment — when Logan had already said the routing was "still WIP." He flagged the slippage explicitly: *"be cautious about elevating records to doctrine without consultation."* This sits inside the broader vault rule from `.claude/CLAUDE.md`: governance authority lives in `CONSTITUTION.md`, and provenance/jurisdiction rules in the PERSONAE ENGINE forbid pattern-matching consistency as a substitute for live delegation.

**How to apply:**
- When citing a vault file, anchor it as a record: *"the 2026-05-17 BEEFSTACK note says…"*, *"per Logan's record at `<file>`…"*, *"as documented in <file> (dated YYYY-MM-DD)…"* — surface the date so its provisional status is visible.
- Use "doctrine" only for: `CONSTITUTION.md`, files Logan has explicitly named as doctrine, or the named slots in the TOUCHSTONE TREE (MIND/BODY/SOUL/NEST nine).
- If a record's claim seems load-bearing for an action, ask before acting on it — even if Logan authored it. The author is still Logan; standing isn't automatic.
- Match scope: "the record names X" ≠ "X applies now." If the record is dated and the world has moved, flag the gap and let Logan reconcile.
- When in doubt, the `*` wildcard is correct. Per the Standing Engine: *Restraint — do not fill gaps with invented certainty because the chain feels complete.*
