A performing arts and event venue long planned on the shores of Payette Lake is on the move.

Last week, the board of directors for the [Ponderosa Center](https://ponderosacenter.org/) announced that the future venue is no longer slated to be built on land overlooking Payette Lake near Legacy Park. Instead, the nonprofit is leasing two acres off Deinhard Lane as a site for the venue.

The land is part of a 12-acre parcel owned by the [J.R. Simplot Company](https://www.simplot.com/). The land is just east of The Marketplace at McCall shopping center, which contains Ridley’s Family Markets, a liquor store, a post office, and the [future home of D & B Supply](https://valleylookout.com/2026/06/09/pretty-clear-and-clean-mccall-pz-advances-db-supply-proposal/).

“This location is everything we hoped for and more,” said Janell Strosnider, president of the nonprofit board. “Being situated in the heart of McCall, next to the Marketplace, means the Ponderosa Center will be a natural gathering place for everyone in our community — from longtime locals to first-time visitors. We are deeply grateful to Simplot for sharing our vision.”

The terms of the Simplot lease were not disclosed by the nonprofit, which said the agreement will remain confidential between the parties.

Once built, the Ponderosa Center would total nearly 15,000 square feet with a welcome center, a flexible performance hall with seating for up to 400 people, and a multi-purpose innovation lab and meeting space.

![City of McCall - Visiting? 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/57592d17-0c69-4d28-a6e9-c5d629733da1.png)

There is currently no estimated cost to build the facility, nor a timeline for construction.

Kristin Sinclair, a member of the future venue’s eight-member board, told Valley Lookout the decision to pivot away from the Payette Lake site was made to better fit the purpose of the Ponderosa Center.

“We loved the idea of being on the lake. Everyone did,” Sinclair said. “But an auditorium does not have windows. The whole point is that the stage is the focus. We realized we had been chasing a view that would not have mattered once you were inside the building. The new site is the right fit for what this place actually is”

## Downtown site agreement

The site the Ponderosa Center was formerly planned on in downtown McCall was subject to a purchase agreement with Hotel McCall owner David Carey, who agreed to sell an acre to the nonprofit in 2018 once funding was available. However, that agreement has since expired, Sinclair said.

The downtown site included about half of Depot Park and a dirt parking lot between Railroad Avenue and Legacy Park. Critics of the proposal worried that putting an event venue in that location would exacerbate parking problems and traffic in downtown McCall.

While the parking concerns are not why the board elected to choose a new site, Sinclair said better access is one benefit of the Deinhard site.

“The new site is on the transit line, bike and pedestrian pathways, and parking,” she said.

## Scaled back plan

Plans for the Ponderosa Center have also been scaled back to about half the size of the 30,000 square feet originally envisioned. The new design allows for phased construction to align building plans with fundraising progress and allow for future expansion as needed.

The nonprofit has raised about $1.1 million to build the venue, according to an [annual report for 2025](https://ponderosacenter.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/PC_Annual_Report_2025.pdf).

Besides a performing arts and meeting venue, the Ponderosa Center would serve as the new home for the McCall Area Chamber of Commerce, the McCall Music Society, and the McCall Ski Heritage Foundation.

Sheila Francis, the executive director of the McCall Chamber, called the venue a “transformative opportunity for McCall.”

“Bringing the Chamber, Music Society and Ski Heritage Foundation together in a central, accessible location will create a place where community and culture come together in every season,” Francis said.
