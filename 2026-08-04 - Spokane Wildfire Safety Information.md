### Disaster Assistance Center

**Spokane County Disaster Assistance Center** Spokane Falls Community College – Building 9  
[3305 West Whistalks Way](https://www.google.com/maps/place/3305+West+Whistalks+Way/data=!4m2!3m1!19sChIJU3jLErQZnlQRS5N1feovq3I)

**Call: [1-509-998-2750](tel:+1-509-998-2750)**

**Provides connections to:**

- Emergency assistance/Disaster services
- Recovery resources

### Community Resources (Call 211)

**Available referrals include:**

- Emergency housing
- Food & clothing
- Transportation
- Prescription assistance
- Childcare
- Mental health services
- Disability & senior services
- Pet assistance

**How to Help**

### Community Driven Relief Funds

- The [KREM Cares Help 2 Northwest](https://www.krem.com/article/news/community/krem-cares/help-2-northwest-spokane-complex-fires/293-0c603378-8959-4320-96a2-9f9a6e2645ed "https://www.krem.com/article/news/community/krem-cares/help-2-northwest-spokane-complex-fires/293-0c603378-8959-4320-96a2-9f9a6e2645ed") is partnering with the Red Cross, STCU and Rosauers to raise money for organizations that will provide aid.
- The [City of Spokane’s H.O.M.E. Starts Here Fund](https://my.spokanecity.org/endinghomelessness/donations/ "https://my.spokanecity.org/endinghomelessness/donations/") has been temporarily designated to support individuals and families affected by the ongoing wildfires. Every dollar donated will go directly toward helping meet immediate needs and support recovery efforts for those impacted.
- The [Innovia Foundation](https://innovia.iphiview.com/innovia/GiveNow/AccountInformation/tabid/495/dispatch/accountselection_id$180408_hash$f9c04d9a7ad07c61d181c69d882b312140005878/Default.aspx "https://innovia.iphiview.com/innovia/GiveNow/AccountInformation/tabid/495/dispatch/accountselection_id$180408_hash$f9c04d9a7ad07c61d181c69d882b312140005878/Default.aspx") is accepting monetary donations to its Spokane Complex Wildfire Response Fund to support immediate and long-term needs for those affected by the fire.
- Humane Evacuation Animal Rescue Team ([HEART](https://www.heartofspokane.org/donate?utm_medium=email&utm_source=govdelivery "https://www.heartofspokane.org/donate?utm_medium=email&utm_source=govdelivery")) is collecting monetary donations to help animals affected
- [Catholic Charities of Eastern Washington](https://give.cceasternwa.org/give/511873?utm_medium=email&utm_source=govdelivery#!/donation/checkout "https://give.cceasternwa.org/give/511873?utm_medium=email&utm_source=govdelivery#!/donation/checkout") is accepting monetary donations for an emergency fund to help support the individuals and families impacted by the Spokane Complex Fires.
- STCU is matching donations to its [Here for Good Foundation](https://stcu.org/here-for-good/stcus-here-for-good-foundation/here-for-good-foundation-disaster-relief "https://stcu.org/here-for-good/stcus-here-for-good-foundation/here-for-good-foundation-disaster-relief"), up to a total of $100,000. All donations will then go to Innovia’s Spokane Complex Wildfire Response Fund. The bank will also offer [disaster relief loans](https://www.krem.com/article/news/local/wildfire/where-to-donate-support-spokane-county-wildfires/%7C01bd202b580b45b8f05508def1a6d30f%7Cccd8a79b7268413b878971f8b6f4c0df%7C1%7C0%7C639213895977018802%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ==%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=ER0%2Fae83GRUGUd8iTJg9lp7S%2FiVPgrGlQze7zuut8jk=&reserved=0 "https://www.krem.com/article/news/local/wildfire/where-to-donate-support-spokane-county-wildfires/%7C01bd202b580b45b8f05508def1a6d30f%7Cccd8a79b7268413b878971f8b6f4c0df%7C1%7C0%7C639213895977018802%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ==%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=ER0%2Fae83GRUGUd8iTJg9lp7S%2FiVPgrGlQze7zuut8jk=&reserved=0") through October 31.

### Item/Food Donations

- [Second Harvest Inland Northwest](https://2-harvest.org/ "https://2-harvest.org/") will accept donations of non-perishable food (1234 E. Front Avenue).

- The Fairchild Air Force Base [Emergency Family Assistance Center](https://www.facebook.com/FairchildMFRC/posts/pfbid08WoBiT47ekc63VWQEPrDXB2dY9Dcx9foCTgunzKd6HsdWPkpxSwwMtNSPDZCPU2il "https://www.facebook.com/FairchildMFRC/posts/pfbid08WoBiT47ekc63VWQEPrDXB2dY9Dcx9foCTgunzKd6HsdWPkpxSwwMtNSPDZCPU2il") is accepting donations from those with base access at the Red Morgan Center from 8 a.m. to 6:30 p.m. Clothing donations are paused, but people can still donate things like water, toilet paper, socks, baby wipes, new underwear, and toiletries.
- [Spokanimal](https://www.facebook.com/SpokAnimal "https://www.facebook.com/SpokAnimal") is accepting donations of dog, cat, kitten & puppy food, and blankets and towels. They are also seeking foster homes for animals displaced by the fires.
- [Project Beauty Share](https://www.facebook.com/ProjectBeautyShare/posts/pfbid0ake56dHYUtsfSb9mNb8Swx3RUBz1v8wrcijRkcjkWokEWHyf46P7jFz7re4UVC8Yl "https://www.facebook.com/ProjectBeautyShare/posts/pfbid0ake56dHYUtsfSb9mNb8Swx3RUBz1v8wrcijRkcjkWokEWHyf46P7jFz7re4UVC8Yl") is collecting new, unopened hygiene items for families and firefighters. Donations can include shampoo & conditioner; toothbrushes & toothpaste; feminine hygiene products; soap, body wash & body wipes; razors & lotion; lip balm; deodorant; hairbrushes & combs. Donations can be dropped off at 2718 E. Sprague Ave. from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.
- [Global Neighborhood Thrift & Vintage](https://www.facebook.com/gnthrift/posts/pfbid027z7CF3SBWToVU5p4Fyr2SekH5G4Kf3UpTmbfX4qoq6A9bcZ8GnJaMyZ3k2u8dQ2dl "https://www.facebook.com/gnthrift/posts/pfbid027z7CF3SBWToVU5p4Fyr2SekH5G4Kf3UpTmbfX4qoq6A9bcZ8GnJaMyZ3k2u8dQ2dl") is accepting clothing donations at its Fire Emergency Donation Bins at both its retail store and donation drive-through, and will offer free clothing vouchers for those affected by the fires.
- [Partners Inland Northwest](https://www.facebook.com/partnersinw/posts/pfbid0CsHpm2143QTPz59Ap6HxkFUjFeMUW85JcJgmB3sHaQr9SjG5LmgKZsjFu6mH3cy7l "https://www.facebook.com/partnersinw/posts/pfbid0CsHpm2143QTPz59Ap6HxkFUjFeMUW85JcJgmB3sHaQr9SjG5LmgKZsjFu6mH3cy7l") is accepting donations of nonperishable food, clothing, diapers, hygiene items and financial gifts at its Spokane Valley location (10814 E. Broadway Ave.) that will be distributed to those affected by the fire.
- The Spokane Homeless Coalition is accepting donations at 527 S. Cannon of pre-packaged food, homemade sandwiches, water, hydration packets, blankets (used but washed), underwear/socks, men’s clothing, and women’s clothing.
- [Gonzaga University](https://www.gonzaga.edu/about/offices-services/emergency-management/zagready/wildfire-donation-list "https://www.gonzaga.edu/about/offices-services/emergency-management/zagready/wildfire-donation-list") is collecting donations of food, comfort items, personal care items, household basics and more at its Hemmingson Center (702 E Desmet Ave.).
- The [Filipino American Northwest Association](https://www.facebook.com/filamspokanecda/posts/pfbid0mp2kJtqvoAazsDMcoDead4hNZzhSVxiyqbe2GrdjNdU7ZvEUvYeyFpUYxyEsG8xNl "https://www.facebook.com/filamspokanecda/posts/pfbid0mp2kJtqvoAazsDMcoDead4hNZzhSVxiyqbe2GrdjNdU7ZvEUvYeyFpUYxyEsG8xNl") has partnered with the Asian Community Center and Nourish Spokane to collect donations of clean, gently used clothing; blankets & bedding; toiletries & personal hygiene items; baby supplies; non-perishable food; pet food & pet supplies; household goods & kitchen essentials at 205 N. University Rd. Drop-off hours are on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.
