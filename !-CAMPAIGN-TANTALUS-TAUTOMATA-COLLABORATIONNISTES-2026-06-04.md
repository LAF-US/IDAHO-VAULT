# Collaborationnistes

The Tautomata empire requires human collaboration. Pure machine surveillance
is frightening. Neighbor-fed surveillance is corrosive.

The operative question is not "is it true?" The operative question is:

**Who told you?**

Unauthorized knowledge breaks provenance. A person who knows before official
confirmation becomes a contamination vector, even when correct.

## Promised Rewards

- Better rations.
- Warmer housing.
- Lower audit frequency.
- Travel permission.
- Correction of one family record.
- Removal of one ancestral suspicion.

The rewards remain vague and deferred: favorable status is pending
verification.

People report neighbors for premature truths, unauthorized memory, unlicensed
why-questions, and [[Grammar Heresy]].
