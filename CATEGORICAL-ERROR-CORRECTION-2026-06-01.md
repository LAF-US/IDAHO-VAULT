# CATEGORICAL ERROR CORRECTION

## The Error

Arrangements like TRIUNE, TRIPART, TRIPLEX, TRIPTYCH, TRIANGLE, TRIUMVIRATE describe the **relationship** between three things, not three *specific things*.

It is **basic grammar**, not quantum physics or rocket science.

## The Correction

- TRIUNE = a "three-in-one" relationship
- TRIPART = a "three-part" relationship
- TRIPTYCH = a "three-panel" relationship
- TRIPLEX = a "three-screen" relationship
- TRIANGLE = a "three-corner" relationship
- TRIUMVIRATE = a "three-ruler" relationship

The actual three things in each relationship are separate and must be identified independently.
