On July 1, law enforcement in Vinton County, Ohio, made a horrifying discovery. Four adults were keeping 16 children confined to a single 12-by-12-foot room in a filthy house. The oldest child, an 18-year-old girl, has a developmental disability and could not write her name. Several of the children were “unable to speak,” [according](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/national/ap-us-news/ap-children-found-in-deplorable-ohio-home-were-part-of-same-family/) to the Associated Press, and others were so ill they had to be flown by helicopters to nearby hospitals. “They looked like almost feral animals,” said state attorney general Andy Wilson. The adults — Gary Siders Sr., Christina Siders, and Gary Siders Jr. and his wife, Elizabeth Siders — face charges of child endangerment in a case that has become a national sensation. True-crime fanatics are [breaking into](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/local-news/central-ohio-news/ohio-attorney-general-do-not-enter-and-film-siders-vinton-county-home/) the home to post footage of the cataclysm inside. Tabloids and [influencers](https://idahonews.com/criminally-obsessed/siders-house-of-horrors-this-reporter-traced-the-familys-past) are calling it the “ [house of horrors](https://www.facebook.com/NewsNationNow/videos/ohio-house-of-horrors-victim-or-monster-inside-elizabeth-siders-case/1337888491345051/) ” with a specific and lurid focus on 33-year-old Elizabeth Siders, who is the mother of the children and may have her own story to tell.

Days after the rescue, the Columbus *Dispatch* [confirmed](https://www.dispatch.com/story/news/crime/2026/07/17/ohio-child-marriage-siders-family-vinton-county-abuse-case/90899778007/?gnt-cfr=1&gca-cat=p&gca-uir=true&gca-epti=z115740p118950n00----l000450c00----e1177xxv115740d--46--b--46--&gca-ft=228&gca-ds=sophi) that Siders had married into the family when she was only 15 and her spouse, the younger Gary, was 18. She gave birth to their first child two months later and went on to bear 18 children, total, over the next 17 years. The *Dispatch* had also uncovered a pattern — three child marriages, including Elizabeth’s, in the same extended family.
