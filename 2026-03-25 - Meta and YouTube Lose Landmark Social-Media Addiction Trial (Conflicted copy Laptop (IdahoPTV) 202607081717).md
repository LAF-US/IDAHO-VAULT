LOS ANGELES—A 20-year-old woman prevailed in a landmark social-media trial against [Meta Platforms](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/META) [META 2.55%increase; up pointing triangle](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/META) and Google’s YouTube in which the companies were accused of designing their apps to be addictive and harmful to adolescents.

A jury found Instagram’s owner Meta and YouTube negligent for operating a product that harmed kids and teens and failed to warn about those dangers. The decision dealt a blow to the companies that have historically been shielded by Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act.
