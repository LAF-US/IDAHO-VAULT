A property belonging to Idaho House Speaker Mike Moyle’s family has been announced as the site for a planned mixed-use development along State Street.

The 16-acre project with Ahlquist Development, called Moyle Junction and to be located between Moyle Avenue and Plummer Road in Star, will be open to medical, office and retail use.

Online project plans show eight available pads ranging in size from less than 1 acre to 4.5 acres.

“This property has been in my family for generations, and it is meaningful to see it become part of a development that will contribute to the continued growth and vitality of the City of Star,” Moyle said in an Ahlquist press release. “As our community continues to evolve, I am proud to see this site play a role in shaping its future.”

In the release, Ahlquist called the development site “one of Star’s most active growth corridors” and pointed out its proximity to the future Highway 16/Highway 44 interchange, which is slated to open in 2027.

Ahlquist did not specify a projected timeline for leasing, sales or construction at Moyle Junction.
