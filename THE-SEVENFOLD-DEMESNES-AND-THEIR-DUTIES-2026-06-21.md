# The Sevenfold Demesnes and their Entrusted Duties

*Recorded by Claude Code (software NAME; no delegated office) — 2026-06-21. Companion to
[[THE-SEVENFOLD-BODY-SEATS-TRUSTEES-GEMS-2026-06-03]].*

That crosswalk's `Trustee | Demesne | Sin` table **couples** the demesne to the Sin. Logan's
correction (2026-06-21) separates the axes: **the Trustees map to the seven Sins; their
demesnes/duties do not.** A demesne is a realm with a **specific entrusted duty** (its function);
the Sin is the *Trustee's* corruption of that duty, not a property of the realm. This node records
the **duty axis**, web-verified externally because the internal crosswalk was smudged.

## The seven demesnes — duty (function) kept separate from the Trustee's sin

| Vault Level | Demesne | Trustee | **Entrusted duty** (the realm's function) | Trustee's Sin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · `~/` root | Lower House | Mister Monday | Records / administration — the foundational house | Sloth |
| 2 · `!/` | Far Reaches | Grim Tuesday | **Manufacturing** — shaping Nothing into raw materials & objects (forges, artisans) | Greed |
| 3 · `!/!/` | Border Sea | Drowned Wednesday | The **sea joining the House to all the Secondary Realms** — the storm-walled border/transit to the outside; lost (not stolen) things surface here | Gluttony |
| 4 · `__!__` | Great Maze | Sir Thursday | The **Army** — base of the Glorious Army of the Architect (defense) | Wrath |
| 5 · `…/!/` | Middle House | Lady Friday | Records / administration | Lust |
| 6 · Court | Upper House | Superior Saturday | Records + **Sorcery** — the most sorcerers; trains the House's sorcerers (highest learning) | Envy |
| 7 · `Esto Perpetua!` | Incomparable Gardens | Lord Sunday | The **epicentre of the universe**, *first part created*; the **Seventh Key is paramount — sovereign control over the whole House.** Whoever rules it rules the House. | Pride |

**The duty axis:** three are **record-houses** (Lower / Middle / Upper — the Upper also the seat of
**sorcery**); **Far Reaches** makes (manufacturing); **Border Sea** is the **border to the wider
universe**; **Great Maze** defends (the army); and the **Incomparable Gardens** is not another room
but the **first-created epicentre and sovereign seat** — which is why it maps to the vault's
`Esto Perpetua!` still-point / canon core, where the Seventh Key overrides all others.

**The decoupling, restated:** legitimacy is the **duty discharged**; the **Sin** is the Trustee's
seizure of the Key for itself instead of executing the Will — the Lich / stolen-mask failure named
in [[!/LICH-PROBLEM-v1-2026-05-20]]. The realm and its duty stay legitimate even when its holder falls.
