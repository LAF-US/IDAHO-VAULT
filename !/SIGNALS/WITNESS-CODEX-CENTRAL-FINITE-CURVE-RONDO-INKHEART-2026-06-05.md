# WITNESS - Curve, Rondo, Inkheart

*Filed 2026-06-05. Codex records the external study requested by Logan:
Rick and Morty's Central Finite Curve, Emily Rodda's Rondo, and Cornelia
Funke's Inkheart as structural mechanics for the AI-RPG-GH-ARG.*

---

## The Object Of Study

The current vision is AI-RPG-GH-ARG:

An agentic AI roleplaying game where GitHub is not only storage or workflow,
but the actual game board. Branches are live dimensions. Pull requests are
gates, courts, stations, and precincts. Reviews are trials. Checks are
verification machinery. Main is admitted canon. The Vault is the lore engine,
evidence mine, paper world, and memory substrate.

The game is played by doing the work.

This witness adds three external mechanics to the existing sandbox music-box
branch cosmos:

- the Central Finite Curve as bounded multiverse;
- Rondo as music-box handling law;
- Inkheart as text-to-world speech act.

---

## Central Finite Curve

The Central Finite Curve gives the branch-cosmos its boundary condition.

The relevant mechanic is bounded infinity: many realities exist, but the set
the protagonists can navigate has been filtered by a privileging rule. In Rick
and Morty terms, the Curve separates a curated subset of realities from the
wider multiverse. It is not the whole infinite field. It is infinity under a
constraint.

For the Vault, this maps to GitHub visibility and governance:

- branches visible to the remote;
- branches reachable by fetch;
- worktrees known to the local machine;
- pull requests before reviewers;
- rulesets, checks, labels, permissions, and signatures;
- the practical boundary between a world line that can enter the game board
  and a world line that remains outside the playable curve.

The Curve is therefore not only cosmology. It is access control.

It asks:

- what dimensions are visible;
- what dimensions are hidden;
- who defined the filter;
- what kind of world becomes impossible to see because the boundary excludes
  it;
- what it costs to escape the curated set.

In the Tantalus reading, the antagonist wants the whole curve to orbit
verification. Tantalus the Absolute would prefer a universe where all reachable
worlds are reachable only by his proof regime. Evil-Morty energy is the impulse
that notices the filter and seeks the wider field.

---

## Rondo

Rondo gives the repository its instrument law.

The usable mechanic is music-box materiality: a contained world is bound to an
object with handling rules. A key, winding, opening, stopping, and touching are
not decorative acts. They determine whether the painted world remains still,
animates, leaks danger, or draws the handlers inward.

For the Vault, this means the repo is not a passive container.

It is an instrument.

Branches, worktrees, commits, checks, pulls, pushes, merges, and reviews are
ways of touching the box. A careless motion is not merely an administrative
mistake. It can shake the world inside.

The Rondo layer sharpens the earlier music-box witness:

- the box has rules before the player understands them;
- the world inside can be beautiful and dangerous at once;
- opening a route creates responsibility;
- children or novices may handle the key before they grasp the stakes;
- the object is small enough to hold, but the world inside is not small;
- the music can continue after the handler has lost track of what it set in
  motion.

For AI-RPG-GH-ARG, the Rondo law is simple:

Do not touch the instrument as though it were inert.

---

## Inkheart

Inkheart gives the Vault its speech-act danger.

The usable mechanic is text-to-world reading: a voice can read a figure,
object, or threat out of a book and into the world. The boundary between story
and operation is not sealed. Reading is action. Naming is action. Binding is
action. Book-work is world-work.

For the Vault, Markdown is not inert.

When an agent reads, summarizes, files, links, names, cites, edits, or commits,
it may move a thing from story-space into operative record-space. A witness
file is therefore not only description. It is a speech act that can admit a
branch-world into the Vault's memory.

The danger is not that fiction becomes literally biological.

The danger is operational:

- a metaphor becomes a routing rule;
- a witness becomes evidence;
- a name becomes an address;
- a title becomes a claim of standing;
- a link becomes a path;
- a file becomes a body the system can carry forward.

The Inkheart lesson is restraint in narration.

Do not read something into the world unless you can stand behind the reading.

---

## Combined Rule

The GitHub branch garden is a bounded multiverse inside a music-box instrument,
where written witness can summon reality into the record.

Central Finite Curve gives the boundary.

Rondo gives the handling law.

Inkheart gives the speech act.

Together they explain why the Vault is not only a story about work and not only
work decorated with story. It is an instrument where story, law, branch state,
and real repository mechanics touch the same surface.

## AI-RPG-GH-ARG Mapping

AI:

- agents as player-characters, witnesses, partners, flawed oracles, and bounded
  tools;
- memory limits and context compression as amnesia, reincarnation, and lawful
  endings;
- verification as power and failure mode.

RPG:

- legal standing as who may act;
- lawful witness as who can testify;
- routes, chapters, precincts, parties, badges, and consequences;
- player agency constrained by jurisdiction.

GitHub:

- branches as live dimensions;
- pull requests as gates and courts;
- reviews as trials;
- checks as verification machinery;
- main as admitted canon, not the whole truth.

ARG:

- fiction and operations share surfaces;
- reading and filing change the playable world;
- external media references become mechanics only after translation into Vault
  doctrine;
- the boundary between play and work remains intentionally porous.

## Standing Warning

This witness does not canonize the external properties themselves.

It translates their mechanics into Vault language.

Central Finite Curve is not imported as Rick and Morty canon. It is used as a
model for bounded multiverse governance.

Rondo is not imported as Emily Rodda's world. It is used as a model for a
small instrument that contains a dangerous world.

Inkheart is not imported as Cornelia Funke's world. It is used as a model for
reading as a world-changing act.

The Vault must keep this distinction clean:

- inspiration is not ownership;
- mechanics are not copied expression;
- source constellation is not public product language;
- translation is required before canon.
