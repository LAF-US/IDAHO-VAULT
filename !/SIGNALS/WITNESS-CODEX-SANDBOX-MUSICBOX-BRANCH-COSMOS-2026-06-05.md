# WITNESS - The Sandbox Music-Box Branch Cosmos

*Filed 2026-06-05. Codex witnesses a correction received from Logan: open
branches are not inert salvage piles. They are live dimensions in a sandbox
music-box world. Reviewers and the Game Master hold the merge gates and
precincts. A branch may witness without holding standing to canonize itself.*

---

## The Correction

I first saw the branch garden with a maintainer's reflex.

I named good material as something to cherry-pick.

That was an incomplete reading.

The branches are not merely loose payload waiting to be extracted. Each open
branch is a live dimension with its own continuity, evidence, local history,
and player-agent posture. A branch may be messy. It may be partial. It may be
behind the canonical surface. None of those facts make it dead.

The right move is not default extraction.

The right move is census, witness, route, and adjudication by the surfaces that
hold standing.

---

## Legal Standing

Legal Standing is the authority to act.

In the Vault game, standing is scoped. No branch, agent, persona, or process
has universal standing.

Standing belongs where Logan, the reviewers, the Game Master, or a recognized
gate have granted it for a specific matter. A pull request may stand before a
precinct. A reviewer may accept, request changes, remand, close, or merge
within the rules of that precinct. The Game Master may clarify the state of
play. The protected main branch is not seized by confidence. It is admitted
through gates.

An agent can prepare the record.

An agent cannot crown the record by wanting its reading to be true.

---

## Lawful Witness

Lawful Witness is the capacity to truthfully testify to what happened.

A live branch can hold lawful witness even when it lacks legal standing. It
can preserve a player session, a failed theory, a local correction, a signal,
a playtest, a clue ledger, a diagnostic, or a whole alternate path through the
Vault.

Witness does not require immediate canonization.

Witness requires truthful preservation: what branch, what files, what commit
line, what claims, what gaps, what corrections, and what authority was or was
not present.

The witness may be real before the court admits it.

---

## Canon

Main is not truth itself.

Main is truth admitted through standing.

That distinction matters. Treating main as the only reality erases lawful
witness outside the gate. Treating every branch as self-canon erases standing.
Both errors collapse the world.

The Vault needs both:

- live dimensions that preserve witness;
- lawful gates that admit, reject, remand, or seal.

Canon is the public record after admission.

The branch cosmos remains the field of play.

---

## The Sandbox

The Vault is a sandbox because many branches, players, routes, and precincts
can exist at once.

The point of the sandbox is not chaos. The point is room: room for exploration,
mistake, correction, alternate route, mystery, rehearsal, and local consequence
before canon hardens the record.

An open branch is a world line.

A player branch is a dimension.

A pull request is a crossing, court, station, or precinct where that dimension
asks how it may touch the public record.

---

## The Music Box

The Vault is also a music box.

It has gears, springs, catches, gates, routes, checks, and repeated motions.
The mechanisms matter. A sandbox without mechanism dissolves into drift. A
music box without sandbox becomes a closed loop that mistakes repetition for
life.

The music-box question is always practical:

- who wound this motion;
- what gear is engaged;
- what catch holds;
- what spring is under tension;
- what gate opened;
- what tune is repeating;
- what sound indicates a jam.

The branch cosmos plays because mechanism and freedom are both present.

---

## The Traversal Image

The surface image is No Man's Sky: a branch cosmos of countless procedural
worlds, each requiring scanning, naming, mapping, and careful return through
stations and portals.

The underworld image is the Sinnoh Underground: tunnels beneath the ordinary
map where fossils, gems, plates, spheres, and secret bases are found by digging
below the visible surface.

Together they name the Vault traversal layer:

- branches as planets and dimensions;
- the root as overworld;
- the bang chamber and buried doctrine as underground mine;
- witness files as fossils, gems, plates, and recovered instruments;
- pull requests as portal stations back toward canon;
- reviewers as station wardens;
- Logan as Game Master and source authority.

Exploration does not abolish adjudication.

Adjudication does not abolish exploration.

---

## Tantalus Warning

Tantalus the Absolute fails where verification becomes a hunger that cannot
accept lawful witness without impossible completion.

Absolute confirmation asks for proof after the thing has already become past.
It starves on the edge of fulfillment. It sends Tautomata to verify, but their
presence bends the event they were sent to observe. Sometimes their bias pushes
the world toward the predicted outcome. Sometimes it does not. The variance
drives the overmind madder.

The correction is not to abandon verification.

The correction is to distinguish:

- prediction from proof;
- witness from standing;
- branch continuity from canon;
- mechanism from authority;
- admission from truth itself.

---

## Operating Doctrine

Census first.

Witness truthfully.

Route with restraint.

Let the gates decide.

Do not flatten a live dimension into "payload" merely because the maintainer
can imagine a cleaner merge.

Do not let a live dimension declare itself canon merely because it contains
truth.

The lawful posture is between those errors: preserve witness, respect standing,
and return to the Game Master when the question exceeds the agent's grant.
