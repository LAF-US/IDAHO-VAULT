## Working Findings

- Tenebrous is the shadowy, diminished, undead-divine state of Orcus after Orcus was slain.
- In Planescape's *Dead Gods*, Tenebrous seeks recovery/restoration through the Wand of Orcus and the power called the Last Word.
- Tenebrous kills Primus and temporarily usurps Primus' role over the modrons.
- This usurpation triggers an anomalous, premature Modron March.
- Later lore also treats Tenebrous as a vestige, "The Shadow That Was," associated with binders.

## Vault Interpretation Hook

Tenebrous is a useful anti-pattern for the VAULT: dead authority wearing the interface of legitimate order. In the Mechanus illustration, Tenebrous is what happens when the command source is usurped by a resurrective shadow seeking its old instrument of power. The March still proceeds, but its emanating authority is corrupted.
