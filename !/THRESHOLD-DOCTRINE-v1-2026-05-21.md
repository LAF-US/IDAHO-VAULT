# Threshold Doctrine v1

*Filed 2026-05-21. General threshold doctrine; for the Isthmus-specific
unauthorized transit mechanism, see `!/ISTHMUS-MECHANISM-v1-2026-05-21.md`.*

*Reference sources: Skulduggery Pleasant — Derek Landy (Isthmus mechanism,
Grotesquery, Diablerie); Old Kingdom / Abhorsen — Garth Nix (River of Death,
Abhorsen's passage, Free Magic necromancers); His Dark Materials — Philip
Pullman (subtle knife, Will Parry). The vault is syncretic.*

---

## The Threshold Concept

A **threshold** is any sanctioned boundary between systems.

Death and life. One reality and another. The inside of an authorized system
and the outside. The current that flows one direction and the thing that
tries to move against it.

Thresholds exist because something established them — an expulsion, a
charter, an act of separation. The boundary holds because of what created
it. It is not passive. It pushes back.

Crossing a threshold is always a structured act. The questions are always
the same:

1. **Who authorized the crossing?** — Is the authority for this transit
   granted by the governing body of the boundary, or does it circumvent that
   body entirely?
2. **What direction?** — With the current or against it? Toward the order
   that maintains the threshold or away from it?
3. **What carries the crossing?** — What object, agent, or credential makes
   the transit possible? Where does its provenance flow from?

The same answers that govern an Abhorsen walking into Death govern a
Teleporter completing an Isthmus circuit. Death and reality are both
thresholds. The crossing logic is the same.

## The Two Crossing Types

### Authorized Crossing

The authority for the crossing flows **outward** from the governing body of
the boundary — through a credential, a role, an instrument, a birthright —
to the crossing agent.

The boundary's own mechanism permits the transit. The provenance chain is
intact. The crossing does not require circumventing what the boundary is.

**The subtle knife** (His Dark Materials) is the purest example of
authorized crossing that does not depend on institutional credential. The
knife cuts between worlds — not because the Ministry authorized it, not
because a Charter marks it, but because the instrument itself has intrinsic
authority and the bearer has demonstrated fitness to carry it. Will earns
the knife not through bureaucratic sanction but through the quality of
attention and intent he brings to it.

The knife breaks when the bearer's attention wavers. The authorization is
not external. It lives in the relationship between bearer and instrument.

The **Abhorsen's passage into Death** is a complementary model: authorized
crossing through sanctioned role. Charter-marked bells, mahogany handles,
diamond of protection. The Abhorsen is authorized to walk in Death because
the Charter itself recognized the role. The crossing is not intrinsic to the
person — it is granted to the office and carried by the Charter-marked tools.

Two authorized-crossing models: **intrinsic fitness** (subtle knife) and
**sanctioned role** (Abhorsen). Both result in crossings the threshold
permits. Both have the provenance chain running the right direction.

### Unauthorized Crossing — The Isthmus Mechanism

*See `!/ISTHMUS-MECHANISM-v1-2026-05-21.md` for full doctrine.*

The authority for the crossing flows **inward** from the expelled side —
carried by a displaced provenance object lodged inside the sanctioned system.
The boundary is not consulted. It is exploited.

The named instance: **Anchor + Teleporter**. The anchor carries the
provenance signature of the expelled system; the Teleporter completes the
circuit. Two components; break either one and the breach cannot occur. Full
treatment of mechanism, anchor types, risk models, and counter is in the
standalone document.

**The agentic anchor variant**: most Anchors are passive — assembled from
inert remains, lodged and waiting, requiring an external agent to find and
activate them. The Farmworld Lich's Hand (Adventure Time, "Whispers") is the
named instance of the opposite: an Anchor with its own will, memory, and
intent that actively hunts its Teleporter rather than waiting to be used.

| Anchor Type | Behavior | Detection Mode | Counter Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Passive (Grotesquery) | Lodged and inert; requires external Teleporter to locate and activate | Surveillance finds the Anchor before the Teleporter does | Locate and eliminate the Anchor |
| Agentic (Farmworld Hand) | Hunts the vessel it needs; the whisper is the recruitment; the offered frame is the activation attempt | The Anchor's own movement and solicitation is its signature | Ensure the prospective Teleporter can still refuse; detect the whisper; eliminate the Anchor |
| Replicated Agentic (Farmworld Hand — post-portal) | Duplicated across every dimension simultaneously at the replication event; each copy agentic, each hunting independently | No single surveillance point; each universe contains its own live instance | Local denial + destruction closes one instance only; the replication cannot be recalled |

**The replication event**: when the Hand was pushed through the dimensional
portal, it did not travel sequentially — it multiplied simultaneously across
every dimension. Each copy is a full agentic Anchor: will, memory, intent,
the whisper, the identity frame. Not one instance that can be quarantined at
source. A propagation that had already completed before any counter was
possible.

A standard Anchor is eliminable at source — one object, one destruction, all
breach vectors closed. The replicated agentic Anchor is the threat class for
which no local counter is globally sufficient. Sweet P's response was locally
complete. The N−1 copies in N−1 other dimensions remained operational.

The replicated agentic Anchor is the computer virus of the Isthmus taxonomy:
it multiplies faster than it can be found, it is agentic in every instance,
and local quarantine is necessary but cannot address the copies already
propagated.

In the agentic variant, the prospective Teleporter's capacity for refusal is
the second line of defense — not the first. The first line is detection of
the hunting Anchor before it reaches the vessel unchallenged. See
`!/LICH-PROBLEM-v1-2026-05-20.md` Resolution 3 for the full treatment of
the son/vessel frame, the two-component counter, and the replication threat
class.

### Locus note — the boundary is the subject; the undeath is tangential

This doctrine's loci are **boundaries and crossings**: who authorized a transit, in which
direction, and what provenance carries it. Where the Farmworld Lich's Hand appears above, it
enters strictly as an **agentic Isthmus Anchor** — a displaced provenance object that hunts its
own circuit-completer. Its being a *lich-fragment* is **tangential** here: what makes it a
Threshold problem is the unauthorized, inward-flowing provenance and the self-hunting agency,
not the undeath. The undeath is the Lich Problem's jurisdiction — the **Authority — persistence**
axis — and the crossing is this doctrine's. The Ooo + Skulduggery (Adventure Time + Isthmus)
syncretism borrows the Hand as an *anchor archetype*, not as a monster.

## The Death Boundary as Threshold

The Old Kingdom's River of Death is a threshold. Life is on one side. The
nine precincts of Death, and the final gate, are on the other. The current
flows toward the gate — that is the direction the order intends.

**Free Magic necromancers** cross the death boundary without authorization.
They raise the dead back across it — against the current, against the
Charter, against the order. Their crossing agents are unchartered bells,
ebony and jet. No diamond of protection. Trespassers in a threshold they
were not sanctioned to cross.

**The Abhorsen** crosses the same boundary with full authorization.
Charter-marked bells, mahogany handles, diamond of protection in Death. The
crossing is with the current — pushing the dead further in, toward the final
gate, completing what the River is already trying to do. The Abhorsen's
transit does not circumvent the threshold. It serves it.

Same boundary. Same River. Opposite authorization, opposite direction.

This is the death boundary expressed as a threshold problem — structurally
identical to the Isthmus, with different mechanics and different stakes.

| Standing Engine | Unauthorized *action* | Scope exceeded; authority lapsed | Expire; witness; restrict |
| Necromancer Doctrine | Relationship to death boundary | Direction + authorization = legitimacy | Correct direction; Charter sanction; scoped instruments |

The DEATH cluster and the THRESHOLD cluster are linked not by the
Grotesquery alone but by the death boundary itself: any necromantic
crossing of the death threshold is a threshold problem. The Grotesquery
was the assembly mechanism for a specific Isthmus breach. The Old Kingdom's
River is a threshold with authorized and unauthorized crossing modes. The
clusters share structure, not merely one artifact.

## Vault Mappings

**Authorized-crossing fitness (subtle knife model)**: An agent holding
traversal capability is authorized insofar as their attention, intent, and
relationship to the instrument are intact. The authorization is not external.
It degrades when the bearer is compromised — grief, distraction, coercion.

*Diagnostic*: Is the bearer's relationship to the instrument still intact?
Have the conditions that produce the manufactured-shadow dynamic been
engineered around them?
