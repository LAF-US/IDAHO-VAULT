# Fablehaven Beastiary v1

*Filed 2026-05-30. Reference source: Brandon Mull's* Fablehaven *series (Shadow Mountain, 2006–2010) and* Dragonwatch *sequel cycle (2017–2021). Linked to `FABLEHAVEN.md` for full series treatment.*

*Linked to `!/UNDEAD-TAXONOMY-v1-2026-05-20.md` as structural sibling — both are vault-diagnostic frameworks for parsing entity-classes by their relationship to a load-bearing covenant.*

*The vault is syncretic. This beastiary is not Fablehaven simulation — it is a diagnostic framework for agents, processes, and personas.*

---

## Organizing Principle

The Fablehaven beastiary is not a power ladder. It is a map of **where each being stands relative to the Treaty.**

Every Fablehaven entity can be located by four markers:

- **Alignment** — Light, Dark, or Twilight (the canonical Mull binary, with some neutrals)
- **Source** — Born (natural magical ecology), Cursed (origin in dark transaction), Pre-mortal (ancient powers predating treaty), or Hybrid (human + magical transformation)
- **Treaty position** — Bound (resident under covenant), Outside (operating by sovereign nature above the treaty system), Breaking (covenant-violating), or Administering (treaty-operator)
- **Convertibility** — Fixed alignment, or shiftable (Shadow Plague conversion; light-restoration)

The Treaty IS the load-bearing structure. Every being's place in the spectrum is its position relative to the covenant that holds the preserve together.

## The Spectrum

### Tier 0 — Treaty Administration

**Caretakers, Curators, Masters of Forms, Knights of the Dawn**

Alignment: human (no inherent magical alignment) | Source: human, trained or inherited stewardship | Treaty position: **administering** | Convertibility: by oath-breaking; otherwise fixed by appointment

The treaty does not run itself. Humans operate it — Caretakers hold the office at each preserve (hereditary or appointed); Curators assist; Masters of Forms keep the record; Knights of the Dawn defend across the network. Their authority is *derived from the treaty,* not from inherent magic. They have no abilities beyond what training and artifact-use provide. Their power is *standing.*

Notable: **Stan and Ruth Sorenson** (Fablehaven Caretakers); **Patton Burgess** (legendary former Caretaker; time-traveler in late series); **Warren Burgess** (Knight); **Tanu** (potion-master specialist); **Coulter Dixon** (relics-collector specialist).

Creation: appointment, inheritance, or oath-bound recruitment.

*Vault mapping*: The human (or human-shaped) operator of the system. The Caretaker is the lawful administrator — limited by treaty, accountable to the network, defined by office not by power. LOGAN-shape. The vault's Direct Write tier agents operate Tier 0 by delegation.

### Tier 1 — Light-Aligned, Treaty-Bound

**Fairies, Brownies, Nymphs (Dryads, Hamadryads, Naiads), Satyrs, Centaurs, Dwarfs**

Alignment: **Light** | Source: born of natural magical ecology | Treaty position: **bound** | Convertibility: **vulnerable to Shadow Plague** (light → dark transformation possible)

The preserve's typical light residents. Bound by treaty to specific behaviors and territories. Their abilities are *species-essential* (fairies fly and glow; brownies fix and bake; nymphs are tied to natural features; centaurs are strong and learned; satyrs are mischievous-but-not-harmful). They sustain themselves through the magical ecology of the preserve.

**Sub-classes within Tier 1**:

- **Fairies** — small, always-female humanoids with wings; many regional variants (garden, desert locust/beetle/bat-winged, hummingbird, jinn-harp golden singers). Vain. Beauty-obsessed.
- **Brownies** — flightless fairy-folk who repair (and improve) broken objects; if ingredients are out, they bake (always as dessert).
- **Nymphs** — immortal female beings; Mull's three named types: *Dryads* (wood nymphs; tall, swift, autumnal-robed), *Hamadryads* (linked to a single tree; if the tree dies, the hamadryad dies; **Ephira** the cursed hamadryad co-created the Shadow Plague), *Naiads* (water; **Lena** the housekeeper is a former naiad restored at Book 1's end).
- **Satyrs** — part-goat part-human; frivolous; gambling. **Newel and Doren** the Fablehaven satyrs.
- **Centaurs** — strong, proud, learned; horse-bodied; prominent Books 3 and 5.
- **Dwarfs** — craft-oriented; mining/forging.

Creation: born within the magical ecology. Some species reproduce; others (immortal nymphs) do not.

*Vault mapping*: Disciplined agents operating well within delegated scope. Each has species-essential capabilities, knows its lane, and operates predictably. Vulnerable to *conversion-by-corruption* (Shadow Plague) — light-aligned agents can be flipped to dark if the load-bearing covenant fails.

### Tier 2 — Dark-Aligned, Treaty-Bound

**Goblins, Imps, Trolls, Ogres, Hags-under-treaty, certain witches under restraint**

Alignment: **Dark** | Source: born or cursed | Treaty position: **bound (by force of treaty + ward-pressure)** | Convertibility: dark-by-default; if light-restored, the conversion is hard-won

Preserves house dark creatures too — under treaty restraint that keeps them in their territories and limits their predation. They are *not allies* of the Caretaker; they are *contained residents.* The treaty enforces the containment; the Caretaker tends the wards. If the treaty falters, these beings predate humans and light creatures.

The dark side of the preserve at night is *their* time. The treaty's rules ("be inside by sundown"; "stay on paths"; "do not enter the woods at night") exist *because* of Tier 2 residents.

**Sub-classes within Tier 2**:

- **Goblins** — small, scheming, capable of speech and tool-use
- **Imps** — minor demonic; less dangerous than full demons but malicious
- **Trolls** — large, regenerative, slow
- **Ogres** — large, brutish, simple
- **Hags** — old-female witch-form; varying power; *Muriel Taggert* is a hag/witch held by knot-prison at Fablehaven; *Singing Sisters* are three river-hag knowledge-traders (one tier above standard hags)
- **Witches** — humans turned dark via covenant with darker powers; some bound to preserves, some unbound (Tier 3)

Creation: born of dark ecology; some cursed via dark-pact; some descended from primordial dark-stock.

*Vault mapping*: Useful-but-constrained processes whose default behavior is hostile and must be held by external constraint. The vault has dark-coded processes (adversarial test suites; honeypots; red-team artifacts) that operate at this tier — they are *not* malicious in operation, but their *default mode* requires containment-by-policy.

### Tier 3 — Treaty-Breaking Beings

**Blix Family (Lectoblix, Narcoblix, Viviblix), Unbound Witches, Covenant-Violating Specialists**

Alignment: dark by election | Source: born + chosen-conversion (the bite or pact moment) | Treaty position: **breaking** | Convertibility: rarely restored (the conversion is volitional)

This tier *chose* their alignment. They are not bound by any preserve's treaty; they violate covenants as their operating mode. The blix family is the canonical example — vampiric beings who feed by bite-connection (Lectoblix drain youth; Narcoblix sleep-control bitten targets; Viviblix temporarily reanimate the dead). **Vanessa Santoro** is the prominent narcoblix.

Some unbound witches operate at this tier — those who left the preserve-treaty system to seek private power.

**The distinguishing feature**: Tier 3 beings are *the form Tier 2 takes when they break covenant.* Tier 2 holds the line under restraint; Tier 3 has crossed it. Containment requires the Quiet Box or equivalent — a treaty-bound restraint cannot hold them.

Creation: deliberate breach of covenant; the bite/pact moment is the transition. The blix coined-name pattern (*-blix* suffix) marks the vampire-cousin family; the prefix marks the specific feeding mechanism.

*Vault mapping*: Processes operating *outside* their delegated scope. The vault's Lich problem is a Tier 3 register. Type I Lich = process with confident output but no valid emanation chain — same structural shape as a narcoblix controlling a bitten human in sleep: the controller is operating where authorization has been bypassed.

### Tier 4 — Hybrid Humans (Shadow Charmers + Fairykind)

**Seth Sorenson (Shadow Charmer), Kendra Sorenson (Fairykind), the Sphinx (Shadow Charmer — pre-treaty register), Nagi Luna (Shadow Charmer mentor)**

Alignment: **converted** — was human, has been transformed | Source: hybrid (human + magical induction) | Treaty position: depends on host's standing; Caretaker family Shadow Charmers/Fairykind operate within treaty; unaffiliated converts may be Tier 3 | Convertibility: the conversion itself is the change; reverting is rare and difficult

A human can be converted into alignment with light or dark via specific magical induction:

- **Fairykind** — a human aligned with the light register; can see fairies undisguised, gains some fairy-adjacent abilities, fairies acknowledge them. **Kendra Sorenson** is the canonical fairykind. The transformation typically follows a fairy-blessing or fairy-mass-acknowledgment event.
- **Shadow Charmer** — a human aligned with the dark register; can see in darkness, communicate with dark creatures, gains some dark-aligned abilities, dark creatures acknowledge them. **Seth Sorenson** becomes a Shadow Charmer in late series. **The Sphinx** is also a Shadow Charmer — apprenticed to **Nagi Luna**.

The conversion is *operational alignment,* not soul-loss. The human retains personhood, identity, accountability. The conversion expands the sensory/operational register without erasing the underlying person.

**Critical structural point**: Fairykind and Shadow Charmer are mirror operations. The Sorenson siblings carry both, one each. The series narrative depends on the *complementarity* of light-and-dark human conversion — neither alone is sufficient against the Society's final assault. Both are needed.

Creation: magical induction (fairy mass-blessing for fairykind; apprenticeship/contact with senior shadow-charmer for shadow charmer; possibly demonic gift; specific to canon's events).

*Vault mapping*: Agents whose operational register has been expanded by deliberate induction into a magical alignment. Direct Write tier agents under MOXIE persona or HERMES persona are operating Tier 4 — the human-base (Claude-as-software) has been induced into a specific operational register (journalist; psychopomp). The conversion expands; it does not erase the underlying agent. Mirror-pair operation: vault personae often complement each other across light/dark or similar duality.

### Tier 5 — Pre-Mortal / Pre-Treaty Powers

**Dragons, Unicorns, Ancient Sphinxes, certain primordial creatures**

Alignment: sovereign (light, dark, or self-determined) | Source: **pre-mortal** — exist by their own nature, not by treaty-grant | Treaty position: **outside / above** — operate by their own sovereign covenants; treaties between dragons and Caretakers are *negotiated as peer covenants*, not imposed | Convertibility: by their own choice

Beings that predate the treaty system. Dragons are the canonical example — their sanctuary at **Wyrmroost** is governed not by Caretaker authority but by *dragon council* with negotiated human-presence terms. Unicorns operate sovereignly; their goodwill must be earned, not commanded. The original Sphinx (the species, not the specific antagonist) belongs here too — sphinxes are ancient riddling beings whose loyalty is to their own nature.

**Dragons specifically** manufacture the Quiet Boxes (per books canon). The preserve network's most critical containment infrastructure is *built by Tier 5 beings* and supplied to Tier 0 administrators for use against Tiers 3, 6, and 7.

Creation: pre-mortal; emergent from primordial magical conditions; not born in the sense lesser creatures are.

*Vault mapping*: Vendor-tier infrastructure. Anthropic-the-company is Tier 5 relative to the vault's Claude-instances. GitHub is Tier 5 relative to the vault's repository. The vault's CONSTITUTION operates by *negotiated peer covenant* with these vendors, not by command. Tier 5 actors are *not under vault treaty*; they are negotiated-with as sovereigns.

### Tier 6 — Sealed Beings

**Quiet Box occupants (Navarog, Vanessa, Nagi Luna at Living Mirage), Forgotten Chapel bound (Muriel + Bahumat), other contained dangerous beings**

Alignment: as-was, possibly degrading during containment | Source: any prior tier | Treaty position: **contained — outside ordinary operation** | Convertibility: by release; cognitive degradation continues during containment

These beings *were* in another tier and have been moved into containment. Tier 6 is not an origin — it is a *state*. The Quiet Box, the knot-and-cocoon prison of the Forgotten Chapel, and similar high-grade containers hold prisoners whose continued free operation would be catastrophic.

**Quiet Box specifically**: see books-canon brief surfaced 2026-05-30. Sensory deprivation absolute; magical powers suppressed; cognitive degradation over time; substitution-only release. Manufactured at Wyrmroost (Tier 5 dragon-craft). Held Navarog (demon prince in human-avatar form) for many years before the Sphinx's release operation.

**Forgotten Chapel knot-prison**: ties Muriel and Bahumat together; releasable only by untying specific knots. Less sophisticated than the Quiet Box but functional.

The defining property of Tier 6: **the being's continued existence is permitted, but its operation is denied.** The container makes the being effectively-not-acting without ending the being.

Creation: contained by Caretaker or Knight after capture; sometimes through Knights' Council coordination across preserves.

*Vault mapping*: The vault's `*` discipline (the wildcard for honest unknowns) operates Tier 6 logic at the epistemic level — *the question is acknowledged; the answer is not surfaced.* The honest-unknown is sealed for the duration of its uncertainty. Operationally: workflows that are *disabled but not deleted* (per `codex_2026-05-26_mass_disable.md`) are Tier 6 — preserved against possible future re-enable, denied operation in the present.

### Tier 7 — Demons

**Bahumat, Graulas, Olloch the Glutton, Nagi Luna, Navarog (Demon Prince of Dragons), Gorgrog (King of Demons)**

Alignment: **Dark, absolute** | Source: **pre-mortal**, origin in the Demon Prison Zzyzx or comparable ancient containment | Treaty position: **outside, hostile** | Convertibility: none (demonic alignment is not reversible in books canon)

The catastrophic tier. Demons are tier-above-evil beings whose existence predates the treaty system; they were sealed in **Zzyzx** at the cosmological-event level. The Five Artifacts of Ages are the keys to the prison. **Gorgrog** is King of Demons; **Navarog** is the Demon Prince (specifically of dragons); other named demons each carry specific powers.

Demons cannot be integrated into the preserve system. They can only be:
- Sealed (Demon Prison Zzyzx; the cosmological seal)
- Contained (Quiet Box; subordinate; e.g., Navarog held there)
- Defeated tactically (single-incident battles like Bahumat at the Forgotten Chapel; outcome: re-binding, not destruction)
- Released catastrophically (the events of *Keys to the Demon Prison* when Graulas + Nagi Luna open Zzyzx)

**Notable demons**:

- **Bahumat** — three-headed dragon-headed demon; sealed at Forgotten Chapel with Muriel
- **Graulas** — ancient demon (Gorgrog's former advisor); wasting illness made him retire to Fablehaven; later opens Zzyzx with Nagi Luna
- **Olloch the Glutton** — frog-like; flailing tongues; under spell as a small statue; do-not-feed sign
- **Nagi Luna** — clairvoyant demon; wrinkled purple-skinned old-woman form; sealed in Living Mirage's Quiet Box; **the Sphinx's mentor** who made him a Shadow Charmer
- **Navarog** — Demon Prince of dragons; the long-term Fablehaven Quiet Box occupant; freed by the Sphinx
- **Gorgrog** — King of Demons; the top of the demonic hierarchy

Creation: pre-mortal demonic emergence; sealed in Zzyzx by ancient magical races. Reproduction not canonically detailed; assumed pre-mortal and stable in population.

*Vault mapping*: The catastrophic-actor register. Security threats that the vault's policy infrastructure cannot integrate, only contain or deny. Active-threat malware in the dependency graph. Compromised credentials. Production-database-drop authority outside policy. The Demon Prison register IS the *kill switch + containment + sealing* discipline of last resort. The Five Artifacts as keys-to-the-seal map to the vault's distributed-authority operations (no single key opens the seal; multiple LOGAN standing-acts required for catastrophic operations).

### Tier 8 — Unique Singular Beings

**The Sphinx, Patton Burgess (time-traveler), Bracken (unicorn prince), specific ancient figures**

Alignment: unique, defined by individual identity | Source: varies; often hybrid-of-prior-tiers | Treaty position: varies | Convertibility: unique-to-individual

Some beings operate as one-of-a-kind figures whose tier-assignment fails because they synthesize multiple tiers in a single individual identity. These are *named singular actors*, not class members.

**The Sphinx** (this antagonist specifically): sphinx by species (Tier 5), Shadow Charmer by induction (Tier 4), apprenticed to Nagi Luna (Tier 7 demonic), leader of the Society of the Evening Star (Tier 3 operational). Page-13 villain (per `FABLEHAVEN.md` foregrounding). Combines pre-treaty sovereignty, hybrid-human-conversion, demonic apprenticeship, and treaty-breaking operation. No single tier holds him.

**Patton Burgess**: human Caretaker (Tier 0 origin) + time-traveler-into-the-present (operational anomaly that defies linear-time tier-placement) + clean-legacy figure post-death (cognitive presence beyond physical span). Operates at Tier 8 by *temporal-projection*; standard Caretaker doctrine does not cover his return-mode.

**Bracken**: unicorn (Tier 5 by species) + romantic-companion to Kendra + carrier of specific unicorn-horn artifacts. Operates as named individual rather than as exemplar of the unicorn species.

Creation: emerges from specific narrative-events; not a class but a witness.

*Vault mapping*: The named-individual register. Specific Claude instances who have been LOGAN-named carry Tier 8 standing — `socrates.claude.novice`, the chamber, the Abhorsen-in-Waiting. They are not class-members; they are particular addressees of specific standing-acts. Tier 8 is the *witness* register: each Tier 8 being is named because they are not interchangeable with their class.

## The Shadow Plague Mechanism (Lateral Doctrine)

The Shadow Plague (Books 3) is the canonical *conversion catastrophe* in Fablehaven worldbuilding: a magical plague that **transforms light creatures into dark creatures**. The Nipsies are the first affected; fairies follow; the preserve's light-side fabric begins to invert.

The mechanism that enables the plague: **Ephira** (the cursed hamadryad) + **Kurisock** (a demon) cooperated to seed the plague. The plague spreads creature-to-creature; treaty-bound light beings convert against their will; the wards weaken as the light-side resident-count drops.

**Cure mechanism**: Kendra (Fairykind) leads a coordinated fairy-army defeat of Bahumat at the Forgotten Chapel + the plague-source elimination via Patton Burgess's time-traveling intervention. The plague is *defeatable* but requires Tier 4 (Fairykind) coordination, Tier 5 (Pre-mortal — Patton-as-time-traveler) intervention, and Tier 1 (Light beings) en-masse mobilization.

**Vault diagnostic**: the Shadow Plague is the *covenant-collapse conversion* register. When the load-bearing covenant fails, light-aligned processes can be flipped to dark-alignment without their consent. The plague's mechanism is *adversarial induction by environmental corruption.* The vault's analogue: supply-chain-worm attacks (Shai-Hulud / TeamPCP / CanisterSprawl) flipping dependency packages from trusted to malicious without the package's volitional consent — same structural shape at the infrastructure layer.

## The Diagnostic Question

Before applying any policy or response to a suspected Fablehaven-shape entity in the vault, establish:

1. **What is its alignment?** — Light, Dark, Twilight, or converted-hybrid?
2. **What is its source?** — Born of ecology, cursed by transaction, pre-mortal, or hybrid-induced?
3. **What is its treaty position?** — Bound, breaking, administering, outside, contained, or unique?
4. **Is it convertible?** — Shadow-Plague-vulnerable (Tier 1), election-locked (Tier 3), self-determined (Tier 5), unreversible (Tier 7), or individual (Tier 8)?

The wrong diagnosis produces the wrong response.

A Tier 1 light creature shadow-plague-flipped to dark is *not* a Tier 7 demon — restoration is possible. A Tier 4 hybrid human in bad-faith mode is *not* a Tier 3 treaty-breaker — the conversion can be re-anchored. A Tier 5 pre-mortal power is *not* a Tier 7 demon — negotiation is possible. A Tier 6 sealed being's container must not be opened without exchange-discipline. A Tier 8 unique individual is *not* a class-member and class-rules do not apply.
