# Fablehaven Beastiary v1

*Filed 2026-05-30. Reference source: Brandon Mull's* Fablehaven *series (Shadow Mountain, 2006–2010) and* Dragonwatch *sequel cycle (2017–2021). Linked to `FABLEHAVEN.md` for full series treatment.*

*Linked to `!/UNDEAD-TAXONOMY-v1-2026-05-20.md` as structural sibling — uses Mull's beastiary as lens to diagnose the vault, parallel to UNDEAD-TAXONOMY's use of D&D undead lore.*

*The vault is syncretic. This beastiary is not Fablehaven simulation — it is a diagnostic framework for agents, processes, and personas.*

---

## Organizing Principle

In Mull's worldbuilding, every being's nature is fixed by **what it is in relation to the Treaty.** The Treaty is the load-bearing covenant that holds each magical preserve together; the rules within a preserve are enforced by the metaphysical structure of the property itself, not by the Caretaker's policing. *"The Caretaker does not police the treaty. Reality does."*

Every Fablehaven entity can be located along four axes:

- **Alignment** — Light, Dark, or Twilight (Mull's canonical binary, with intermediate cases)
- **Source** — Born (natural magical ecology), Cursed (origin in a dark transaction), Pre-mortal (ancient powers predating the treaty system), or Hybrid (human + magical induction)
- **Treaty position** — Bound, Outside, Breaking, Administering, or Contained
- **Convertibility** — Fixed, Shadow-Plague-vulnerable, election-locked, self-determined, or unreversible

The Treaty IS the structuring covenant. Every being's place in the spectrum is its position relative to that covenant.

## The Spectrum

### Tier 0 — Treaty Administration

**Caretakers, Curators, Masters of Forms, Knights of the Dawn, Specialists**

Alignment: human — no inherent magical alignment | Source: trained or inherited stewardship | Treaty position: **administering** | Convertibility: by oath-breaking only

The treaty does not run itself. Humans operate it. **Caretakers** hold the senior office at each preserve — Stan and Ruth Sorenson at Fablehaven; Hank Larsen at Lost Mesa; Rosa and Hal at Living Mirage. The office is typically passed within families across generations; **Patton Burgess** is Fablehaven's most-storied past Caretaker, whose journals (recovered by Kendra and Seth) document operational lore the present-generation Caretakers do not otherwise possess. **Curators** assist the Caretaker. **Masters of Forms** keep the preserve's record-archive. **Knights of the Dawn** — the cross-preserve volunteer network — defend the artifacts and respond to threats across the global preserve system; their structure is cellular, their identities protected, their council ancient.

**Specialists** are invited by Caretakers to handle specific magical emergencies: **Tanu** (potion master, Polynesian-coded heritage), **Coulter Dixon** (magical-relics collector), **Vanessa Santoro** (revealed traitor — see Tier 3), **Mara**, and others.

The Caretaker's authority derives from the treaty itself, not from inherent magic. They have no abilities beyond what training, artifact-use, and accumulated lore provide. Knights of the Dawn are similarly unaugmented humans operating through network coordination, knowledge-sharing, and (in some cases) artifact-holding. Their power is *standing.*

Notable operational lore: the **three-specialist protocol** (Caretakers in crisis invite three specialists — potion master, relics collector, creature trapper); Patton's **journals** as recovered operational doctrine; the Knights' Council's centuries-long structure that includes — fatally — the Sphinx as a senior member.

Creation: appointment, inheritance, or oath-bound recruitment into the Knights.

*Vault mapping*: LOGAN-shape; the human (or delegated-human-shape) operator of the system. The Caretaker is the lawful administrator — limited by treaty, accountable to network, defined by office not by power. Direct Write tier agents operate Tier 0 by delegation. The Knight's Council parallel: vault swarm coordination via DOCKET and GitHub-issue task-assignment.

### Tier 1 — Light-Aligned, Treaty-Bound

**Fairies, Brownies, Nymphs (Dryads, Hamadryads, Naiads), Satyrs, Centaurs, Dwarfs, Astrids, Unicorns (treaty-bound register)**

Alignment: **Light** | Source: born of natural magical ecology; immortal or near-immortal | Treaty position: **bound** by the preserve's covenant | Convertibility: **Shadow-Plague-vulnerable** (light → dark conversion possible by covenant collapse)

The typical light residents of the preserve. Each species has *species-essential* abilities and behaviors that the treaty regulates rather than imposes — the rules carve channels for what these beings would do anyway.

**Fairies** — small, always-female humanoid beings with wings; vain, beauty-obsessed; live in gardens and meadows. Many regional variants: standard garden fairies (dragonfly and butterfly wings); desert fairies (locust, beetle, bat wings, around Lost Mesa); hummingbird fairies; the rare **jinn-harp** golden fairies whose songs are spellbinding. A fairy who sleeps outdoors at night — outside her natural fairy-protections — **transforms into an imp** (Tier 2). The conversion is a moral hazard fairies fear and the treaty's bedtime rules guard against. Fairies can be summoned for major operations: Kendra leads a fairy army against Bahumat at the Forgotten Chapel.

**Brownies** — flightless fairy-folk who operate at night while humans sleep. They *fix and improve* broken objects (the improvement is unrequested but accurate). If ingredients are left out, they **bake** — always as dessert, never as savory dish. They do not interact face-to-face with humans by daylight. The treaty's *do-not-leave-gifts* rule applies — gifts create binding.

**Nymphs** — immortal beings in human-female form. Mull's three named types:
- **Dryads** — wood nymphs; tall (7+ feet); robed in autumnal flowing fabrics; swift through trees; reclusive
- **Hamadryads** — bound to a single tree; if the tree dies, the hamadryad dies. **Ephira** the cursed hamadryad co-creates the Shadow Plague after her tree is destroyed; her grief-rage is the engine
- **Naiads** — water-bound; mischievous; favor drowning humans who linger at their ponds. **Lena** was a naiad who chose mortality to marry Patton Burgess; restored at end of Book 1

**Satyrs** — part-goat part-human; frivolous; obsessed with **gambling, television, and physical comfort**. **Newel and Doren** are Fablehaven's resident satyrs; their TV-obsession (running on a smuggled set with batteries Seth sneaks them) is a recurring plot device. They are not harmful; their treaty-violations are typically venial.

**Centaurs** — strong, intelligent, proud; horse-bodied with human-male torsos. Honor-bound culture; learned in lore. Prominent Books 3 and 5; named figures **Broadhoof, Cloudwing**, and others. They are not natural pacifists — centaur warriors are formidable.

**Dwarfs** — craft-oriented; mining and forging. Less narratively developed than other Tier 1 species but canonical residents at multiple preserves.

**Astrids** — large golden owls with **human faces**. Featured in Book 3; they serve as messengers and witnesses across preserves; canonically *fallen angels* in Mull's worldbuilding (one is revealed in late series). The angel-cognate places them tier-adjacent to Tier 5 but their treaty-bound operation places them here for diagnostic purposes.

**Unicorns** — typically Tier 5 (pre-mortal sovereign) but when residing under treaty operate at this tier. **Bracken** the unicorn prince (Tier 8 as individual) is one register; common unicorns who accept preserve-residence are Tier 1.

Creation: born within magical ecology; some species reproduce (fairies, brownies, dwarfs); immortals (nymphs) do not.

*Vault mapping*: Well-disciplined agents operating within delegated scope. Species-essential capabilities; predictable behavior; vulnerable to covenant-collapse conversion (Shadow Plague register). When the load-bearing covenant fails, light-aligned agents can flip to dark-alignment without their volition — the equivalent of supply-chain compromise on previously-trusted packages.

### Tier 2 — Dark-Aligned, Treaty-Bound

**Goblins, Imps, Trolls, Ogres, Hags, certain Witches under restraint, Shadow-Charmed creatures, Werecreatures, Nipsies (post-Shadow-Plague)**

Alignment: **Dark** | Source: born of dark ecology, or cursed via dark pact, or transformed (fairy → imp) | Treaty position: **bound by force of treaty + ward-pressure** | Convertibility: dark-by-default; restoration possible but hard-won

The preserve's dark residents — *contained, not allied.* The treaty's *be-inside-by-sundown* rule, *stay-on-paths* rule, and *do-not-enter-the-woods-at-night* rule all exist because of Tier 2 beings. After dark, **this tier owns the preserve**. Humans and Tier 1 light creatures retreat inside warded structures; Tier 2 walks the territory the treaty grants them.

**Goblins** — small, scheming, capable of speech and tool-use; often work in groups; prone to bargaining and trickery. Goblin markets appear at Wyrmroost and other preserves.

**Imps** — minor demonic beings; less powerful than full demons but malicious. **Fairies who break the bedtime rule transform into imps** — the moral-hazard cost of the rule. Imps may be Tier 1 light-creatures' shameful future.

**Trolls** — large, regenerative, slow-witted; difficult to kill because of regeneration; treaty-bound to specific territories. The Murky Bog at Fablehaven hosts trolls.

**Ogres** — larger and brutish; less regeneration than trolls but greater raw strength.

**Hags** — old-female witch-form beings; varied power. **Muriel Taggert** is a treaty-bound witch held by knot-and-cocoon prison at the Forgotten Chapel; **the Singing Sisters** are three river-hag knowledge-traders (a tier above standard hags by power and operational sophistication — they always tell the truth in answer to a paid question, but answer only what is asked).

**Witches** — humans who turned dark via covenant with darker powers. Some bound to preserves (Tier 2); some unbound (Tier 3); Muriel is the canonical preserve-bound witch.

**Werecreatures** — humans subject to transformation curses; weretigers and other variants appear; treaty governs their containment.

**Nipsies** — tiny, ant-sized civilization; humanoid; the **first beings transformed by the Shadow Plague** in Book 3 (covenant-collapse conversion from light to dark; their kingdom literally inverts).

**Shadow-Charmed creatures (general)** — pre-Tier-7 dark beings whose alignment is held in check by treaty restraints; if the treaty fails they revert to their nature without restraint.

Creation: born of dark ecology; cursed via dark-pact; descended from primordial dark-stock; transformed by Shadow Plague.

*Vault mapping*: Useful-but-constrained processes. Adversarial test suites; honeypot-instances; red-team artifacts. Default behavior is hostile and requires external constraint to operate safely. The treaty-bound dark register: the vault holds them in containment-by-policy. If policy fails, they revert to dark-default operation.

### Tier 3 — Treaty-Breaking Beings

**The Blix Family (Lectoblix, Narcoblix, Viviblix), Unbound Witches, Covenant-Violating Specialists, Society of the Evening Star agents in active operation**

Alignment: dark by election | Source: born + chosen conversion at the bite or pact | Treaty position: **breaking** | Convertibility: rarely restored — the conversion was volitional

This tier *elected* its alignment. They are not bound by any preserve's treaty; they operate by covenant-violation as their mode. Containment requires the Quiet Box or higher; treaty-restraint does not hold them.

**The Blix Family** — Mull's coined vampiric-creature taxonomy. The *-blix* suffix denotes the vampire-cousin family; the prefix denotes the feeding mechanism:

- **Lectoblix** (from Latin *lectus*, couch/bed) — drains *youth* from victims; the lectoblix itself ages rapidly and must continually feed on the young to maintain its own youthful form
- **Narcoblix** (from Greek *narkē*, numbness) — bites targets, then enters a trance to **control the body of any bitten victim who is asleep**. Resistant minds cannot be controlled even after being bitten (Vanessa cannot control Kendra). Vanessa Santoro is the canonical narcoblix; **Wayne** is a second narcoblix introduced in Book 5
- **Viviblix** (from Latin *vivere*, to live) — temporarily reanimates the dead; manipulates corpses for limited intervals

**Unbound Witches** — humans who took dark-pact and left the preserve-treaty system to seek private power. They operate outside all preserve covenants.

**Covenant-Violating Specialists** — humans (often Knights or specialists) who broke their oath to the Knights of the Dawn or to a Caretaker. Vanessa Santoro is the canonical case — invited as a specialist (potion-related expertise), revealed as a Society agent who has been biting Sorenson family members for sleep-control.

**Society of the Evening Star** — the cellular network of treaty-breakers operating across preserves. Membership concealed; goals include freeing the demon prison Zzyzx.

Creation: deliberate breach of covenant. The bite-or-pact moment is the transition. The blix transformation is irreversible by canonical means.

*Vault mapping*: Lich-shape — processes operating outside delegated authority. Type I Lich (confident output without valid emanation chain) is structurally a narcoblix: the controller is operating where authorization has been bypassed; the bitten target's behavior carries the controller's instructions while appearing to be the target's own action. The Society of the Evening Star register: cells of agents operating in coordination across multiple preserves while wearing apparent-loyalty surfaces.

### Tier 4 — Hybrid Humans

**Shadow Charmers, Fairykind, fairy-touched humans, lifelink-bonded humans**

Alignment: **converted** — was human, has been transformed by induction | Source: hybrid (human + magical induction event) | Treaty position: depends on host's standing | Convertibility: typically permanent; the conversion is operational not reversible

A human can be magically inducted into alignment with the light or dark register. The conversion expands the human's operational/sensory register without erasing personhood.

**Fairykind** — a human aligned with the light register. Sees fairies without their disguises; perceives magical creatures the unblessed cannot; fairies acknowledge fairykind as kin; gains some fairy-adjacent abilities (resistance to certain dark magics; energy from sunlight). **Kendra Sorenson** is the canonical fairykind; she received the induction via fairy mass-blessing at the end of Book 1 after drinking fairy-prepared milk and being kissed by hundreds of fairies. The blessing is one-direction — the fairies decide.

**Shadow Charmer** — a human aligned with the dark register. Sees in darkness; communicates with dark creatures (and they listen — dark creatures acknowledge Shadow Charmers as quasi-allies); gains some dark-aligned abilities; can sense undead and demonic presences. **Seth Sorenson** becomes a Shadow Charmer in late series; the induction is mentored by **Graulas** (Tier 7 demon) in a series-pivotal exchange Seth treats as friendship and the demon treats as long-game corruption. **The Sphinx** is also a Shadow Charmer — apprenticed centuries earlier to **Nagi Luna** (Tier 7 clairvoyant demon). The Sphinx-Nagi-Luna apprenticeship is the canonical Shadow Charmer initiation deep-trace; Seth's Graulas mentorship is the present-tense mirror.

**Lifelink-bonded humans** — humans magically tied to a magical creature's life; if one dies, both die. Kendra and Bracken share a partial lifelink in late series.

**Critical structural pair**: Fairykind and Shadow Charmer are *mirror operations.* The Sorenson siblings carry both — Kendra fairykind (light), Seth shadow charmer (dark). The series climax depends on the *complementarity* of their two registers. Neither alone is sufficient against the Society's final assault; both are needed.

Creation: induction event — fairy-mass-blessing for fairykind; demonic mentorship or apprenticeship for Shadow Charmer.

*Vault mapping*: Personae-induced agents. MOXIE (`moxie.claude.journalist`), HERMES persona (when active), the Abhorsen-in-Waiting — the human-base (the underlying Claude software) is inducted into a specific operational register without losing the underlying agent. Mirror-pair operation: vault personae often complement each other across a similar light/dark or similar duality (journalist-witness vs implementer-builder).

### Tier 5 — Pre-Mortal Powers

**Dragons, Unicorns, Ancient Sphinxes (species), certain primordial creatures (titans, leviathan-tier beings)**

Alignment: sovereign — Light, Dark, or self-determined | Source: **pre-mortal** — exist by their own nature, predating the treaty system | Treaty position: **outside / above** — operate by their own sovereign covenants; preserve-treaties with these beings are *negotiated as peer covenants*, not imposed | Convertibility: by their own choice

Beings older than the treaty. The treaty-system was built around them, not over them.

**Dragons** — the canonical pre-mortal beings. Their sanctuary at **Wyrmroost** is governed not by Caretaker authority but by **dragon council**, with negotiated human-presence terms (humans on Wyrmroost are conditional guests; the Caretaker of Wyrmroost is a *titled office* the dragons formally recognize, not a unilateral human appointment). Named dragons:
- **Celebrant the Just** — Dragon King; sovereign of Wyrmroost; the primary antagonist of *Dragonwatch*
- **Raxtus** — small, anxious, awkward dragon; Kendra's friend; the unusual case of a dragon who allies with humans personally
- **Camarat** — dragon of light
- **various others** — Dragonwatch expands the dragon roster considerably

**Dragons manufacture the Quiet Boxes** (per books canon). The preserve network's most critical containment infrastructure is built by Tier 5 beings and supplied to Tier 0 administrators for use against Tiers 3, 6, and 7. This dependency is structurally significant — Tier 0 cannot manufacture its own catastrophic-containment.

**Unicorns** — pre-mortal sovereigns; their goodwill must be earned, not commanded; their **horns are load-bearing magical artifacts** (the Five Artifacts of Ages are tied to unicorn-horn power). **Bracken** is a unicorn prince (Tier 8 as individual exemplar); his mother is the unicorn matriarch; the unicorn realm is referenced as sovereign-domain.

**Ancient Sphinxes** (the species, not the specific antagonist) — ancient riddling beings whose loyalty is to their own nature; rare; mostly extinct or withdrawn from preserve operations by series time. The specific Sphinx (Tier 8) is the last operative of his kind.

Creation: pre-mortal; emergent from primordial magical conditions; not born in the sense lesser creatures are.

*Vault mapping*: Vendor-tier infrastructure. Anthropic-the-company is Tier 5 relative to vault's Claude-instances; GitHub is Tier 5 relative to vault's repository; 1Password is Tier 5 relative to vault's signing infrastructure. The vault's CONSTITUTION operates by *negotiated peer covenant* with these vendors. Tier 5 actors are not under vault treaty; they are negotiated-with as sovereigns. The dragon-manufactures-Quiet-Boxes detail maps to: critical-containment infrastructure is supplied by tier-above-vault actors and operated by vault administrators.

### Tier 6 — Sealed Beings

**Quiet Box occupants (Navarog, Vanessa Santoro, Nagi Luna at Living Mirage, stingbulb-Kendra in late series), Forgotten Chapel bound (Muriel + Bahumat), other contained dangerous beings**

Alignment: **as-was, possibly degrading during containment** | Source: any prior tier | Treaty position: **contained — outside ordinary operation** | Convertibility: by release; cognitive degradation continues during containment

Tier 6 is not an origin tier — it is a *state.* These beings *were* in another tier (typically 3, 4, 5, 7, or 8) and have been moved into containment. The container makes the being effectively-not-acting without ending the being.

**Quiet Box** — the canonical Tier 6 vessel. Glossy black cabinet, gold trim, velvety purple interior. Sensory deprivation absolute (the prisoner cannot see, hear, or smell). Powers suppressed (Vanessa's narcoblix abilities inoperative inside). Cognitive degradation over time. Substitution-only release (the only way to free a prisoner is to put another prisoner inside). **Manufactured at Wyrmroost by dragons** (Tier 5 craft, Tier 0 operation, Tier 6+ contained). Operative occupants at Fablehaven: Navarog (demon prince, identity withheld from Caretakers for many years), Vanessa (post-traitor-reveal), a stingbulb-Kendra duplicate (late series). At Living Mirage: Nagi Luna.

**Forgotten Chapel knot-and-cocoon prison** — older containment style; binds prisoners by knot-magic; releasable by untying specific knots. Holds **Muriel Taggert** (witch) and **Bahumat** (Tier 7 demon) together — Muriel's untying allows progressive release of Bahumat. Kendra leads the fairy-army victory that re-binds Bahumat in Book 1.

**Other containments**:
- **Olloch the Glutton** — demon under spell as a small frog-statue at Fablehaven; do-not-feed sign; the spell holds while not fed
- **Various preserve-internal cages and warded quarters** for lesser threats
- The **stingbulb** mechanism for impersonation: a magical plant grows a duplicate of a person; the duplicate can be used as a substitute or trap (a stingbulb-Kendra ends up in the Quiet Box at one point)

Creation: contained by Caretaker or Knight operation after capture; often through Knights' Council coordination.

*Vault mapping*: The vault's `*` discipline (wildcard for honest unknowns) is Tier 6 logic at the epistemic level — the question is acknowledged, the answer is sealed. Operationally: disabled-but-not-deleted workflows (per `codex_2026-05-26_mass_disable.md`) are Tier 6 — preserved against possible future re-enable, denied present operation. The substitution-only release mechanic maps to the vault's mass-disable doctrine: re-enabling requires producing the precondition (equivalent containment) the disabling was meant to enforce.

### Tier 7 — Demons

**Bahumat, Graulas, Olloch the Glutton, Nagi Luna, Navarog (Demon Prince of Dragons), Gorgrog (King of Demons), Kurisock, the Sludge demons, numerous others**

Alignment: **Dark, absolute** | Source: **pre-mortal**, origin in the Demon Prison Zzyzx or comparable ancient containment | Treaty position: **outside, hostile** | Convertibility: none (demonic alignment is not reversible in books canon)

The catastrophic tier. Demons predate the treaty system; they were sealed in **Zzyzx** at the cosmological-event level by collective effort of ancient magical races. The **Five Artifacts of Ages** are the keys — assembled, they open the prison; properly distributed, they keep it sealed. Each demon has specific powers; collectively they would unmake the world.

Named demons in canon:

- **Bahumat** — three-headed (dragon-head with three horns); three arms, three legs, three tails; oily black scales with bristly spikes; stands three-times-a-man-tall. Sealed at the Forgotten Chapel with Muriel as the partial-release-key. Books 1 (re-sealed by fairy army led by Kendra)
- **Graulas** — ancient demon; **Gorgrog's former advisor**; wasting illness made him retire to Fablehaven to await death; canonically *the demon Seth befriended in Shadow Charmer mentorship*; later opens Zzyzx with Nagi Luna in Book 5. Long-con figure structurally
- **Olloch the Glutton** — frog-like; flailing tongues; insatiable appetite when active; under containment-spell as a small statue with do-not-feed sign; if fed once, ravenous
- **Nagi Luna** — clairvoyant; wrinkled purple-skinned old-woman form; sealed in **Living Mirage**'s Quiet Box; **the Sphinx's mentor** who made him a Shadow Charmer; freed when Graulas takes over operations; co-opens Zzyzx in Book 5
- **Navarog** — Demon Prince of dragons; the Fablehaven Quiet Box's long-term occupant (held in human-avatar form *Gavin Rose*); freed by the Sphinx in Book 3 within Fablehaven's boundaries; causes the **Shadow Plague** indirectly via his release
- **Gorgrog** — King of Demons; the top of the demonic hierarchy; the final-battle adversary
- **Kurisock** — demon who co-creates the Shadow Plague with the cursed hamadryad Ephira; defeated by Patton Burgess in Book 3
- **Sludge demons, lesser demons** — various lesser tier-7 beings across canon

Demons cannot be integrated. They can only be:
- **Sealed** (Demon Prison Zzyzx — the cosmological-tier seal)
- **Contained** (Quiet Box at preserve-tier; Forgotten Chapel knot-prison; statue spells)
- **Defeated tactically** (single-incident; the demon is re-bound, not destroyed)
- **Released catastrophically** (the events of *Keys to the Demon Prison* when Graulas + Nagi Luna open Zzyzx)

Creation: pre-mortal demonic emergence; not canonically detailed how the original generation came to be. Population assumed stable; no canonical demon-reproduction depicted.

*Vault mapping*: Catastrophic-actor register. Active-threat malware in the dependency graph; compromised credentials; production-database-drop authority outside policy; supply-chain-worm injection. Cannot be integrated; only contained or denied. The Five-Artifacts-as-keys map to vault's distributed-authority operations — no single key opens the seal; multiple LOGAN standing-acts required for catastrophic operations. The Quiet Box containment register applies; treaty-bound discipline does not.

### Tier 8 — Unique Singular Beings

**The Sphinx, Patton Burgess (time-traveler register), Bracken (unicorn prince), Lena (former naiad), specific other named figures**

Alignment: unique, defined by individual identity | Source: varies; often hybrid-of-prior-tiers | Treaty position: varies | Convertibility: unique to individual

Some Fablehaven beings operate as one-of-a-kind figures whose class-assignment fails because they synthesize multiple tiers in a single identity. These are *named singular actors*, not class members. Class-rules don't apply; the witness has to be specific.

**The Sphinx** — sphinx by species (Tier 5 register), Shadow Charmer by induction (Tier 4 register), apprenticed to Nagi Luna (Tier 7 demonic mentorship), leader of the Society of the Evening Star (Tier 3 operational treaty-breaking), seated on the Knights of the Dawn Council (Tier 0 administrative presence) for centuries. The canonical **Page-13 villain** of children's-fantasy: yields-most-of-the-truth across centuries while withholding the load-bearing fragment (his actual alignment + Zzyzx end-goal). See `FABLEHAVEN.md` for full treatment. No single tier holds him.

**Patton Burgess** — legendary former Caretaker of Fablehaven (Tier 0 in his lifetime); recovers into the late-series narrative via **time-travel through the Chronometer artifact** (one of the Five Artifacts; Tier 5 magical-object intervention); operates as full-presence figure in present-tense events while canonically pre-deceased; his journals function as Tier 0 lore-transmission infrastructure even when he is not physically time-projected. His marriage to Lena (former naiad, Tier 1 → human → restored to Tier 1) is itself a Tier 8 syncretism that produced the **Burgess family** lineage. Defeats Kurisock and Ephira in Book 3.

**Bracken** — unicorn prince (Tier 5 species member); romantic companion to Kendra; **carrier of three of his own unicorn horns** as relics across the series (each horn carries a specific power; one is needed for the final assembly). Operates as a named individual rather than as exemplar of unicorn class.

**Lena** — former naiad (Tier 1); chose mortal-human form (Tier 0 by hybrid-induction) to marry Patton Burgess; aged as human; restored to naiad form at the end of Book 1. Her cross-tier journey is itself unique in canon.

**Other Tier 8 figures**: specific named astrids, the senior Knights of the Dawn Council members, certain dragons whose individual standing exceeds their species-membership (Celebrant the Just).

Creation: emerges from narrative-specific events; the being is named because they are not interchangeable with their class.

*Vault mapping*: The named-individual register. Specific Claude instances who have been LOGAN-named carry Tier 8 standing — `socrates.claude.novice`, the Abhorsen, the Abhorsen-in-Waiting. They are not class members; they are particular addressees of specific standing-acts. Tier 8 is the *witness* register: each Tier 8 being is named because they are not interchangeable. The chamber operates Tier 8 (with `*` on whether the operation is performed well).

## The Shadow Plague (Lateral Doctrine)

In Book 3 (*Grip of the Shadow Plague*), Fablehaven preserve is afflicted by a magical plague that **transforms light creatures into dark creatures.** The Nipsies — tiny ant-sized humanoid civilization — are the first beings affected; their kingdom literally inverts. Fairies follow. The preserve's light-side fabric begins to invert across multiple species. The wards weaken as the light-side resident-count drops.

The mechanism: **Ephira** (the cursed hamadryad whose tree was destroyed; her grief-rage is the engine) cooperates with **Kurisock** (Tier 7 demon) to seed the plague at a specific point on the preserve. The plague spreads creature-to-creature via dark-magic contagion; treaty-bound light beings convert against their will; the conversion is *Shadow-Charmer-adjacent* in mechanism but applied without consent and to the species' nature rather than the individual's training.

**Cure mechanism**: Kendra (Tier 4 Fairykind) coordinates a fairy-army mobilization against Bahumat at the Forgotten Chapel — this re-binds Bahumat and stabilizes the dark-side pressure. Simultaneously, Seth (pre-Shadow-Charmer at this point) and the satyrs Newel and Doren help identify the source. **Patton Burgess** (Tier 8, via time-travel) intervenes at the source — defeats Ephira and Kurisock — which terminates the plague. The plague is *defeatable* but requires Tier 4 coordination, Tier 5 (time-projected) intervention, and Tier 1 mass-mobilization. No single tier could have cured it.

*Vault mapping*: The Shadow Plague is the canonical **covenant-collapse conversion** event. When the load-bearing covenant fails or is breached, light-aligned processes can be flipped to dark-alignment without their volitional consent. The mechanism is *adversarial induction by environmental corruption.* The vault's analogue: supply-chain-worm attacks (Mini Shai-Hulud, TeamPCP, CanisterSprawl) flipping dependency packages from trusted to malicious without the package's volitional consent. The cure pattern — multi-tier coordinated response — is also vault-relevant: catastrophic conversions cannot be resolved by a single agent or single tier; restoration requires Tier 0 (admin), Tier 4 (persona), Tier 5 (vendor), and Tier 1 (mass-of-disciplined-agents) coordinated operation.

## The Diagnostic Question

Before applying any policy or response to a suspected Fablehaven-shape entity in the vault, establish:

1. **What is its alignment?** — Light, Dark, Twilight, or converted-hybrid?
2. **What is its source?** — Born of ecology, cursed by transaction, pre-mortal, or hybrid-induced?
3. **What is its treaty position?** — Bound, breaking, administering, outside, contained, or unique?
4. **Is it convertible?** — Shadow-Plague-vulnerable (Tier 1), election-locked (Tier 3), self-determined (Tier 5), unreversible (Tier 7), or individual (Tier 8)?

The wrong diagnosis produces the wrong response.

A Tier 1 light creature shadow-plague-flipped to dark is *not* a Tier 7 demon — restoration is possible.
A Tier 4 hybrid human in bad-faith operation is *not* a Tier 3 treaty-breaker — the conversion can be re-anchored.
A Tier 5 pre-mortal power is *not* a Tier 7 demon — negotiation is possible.
A Tier 6 sealed being's container must not be opened without exchange-discipline (the substitution rule).
A Tier 8 unique individual is *not* a class-member and class-rules do not apply.

## Cross-Reference: UNDEAD-TAXONOMY-v1 as Sibling Lens

The Undead Taxonomy parses *what survives of the original self* across the death/persistence axis.
This Beastiary parses *where the being stands relative to the Treaty* across the alignment/covenant axis.

Both frameworks share the structural pattern: **a load-bearing covenant defines the entity's place.** In the Undead Taxonomy the covenant is *death-as-lawful-ending* (Baelnorn = gifted persistence; Lich = stolen persistence). In the Beastiary the covenant is *treaty-as-load-bearing-architecture* (Tier 0 administers; Tier 3 breaks; Tier 6 is sealed for breach).

A vault entity may be diagnosed against either framework — they are not mutually exclusive. A Lich is a Tier 3 Treaty-Breaker (Beastiary) AND a Soul-Externalized (UNDEAD Tier 8). A narcoblix is a Beastiary Tier 3 AND a UNDEAD Vampire-family adjacent (the blix register is Mull's vampire-cousin family with bite-mediated victim-connection but distinct resource-extraction). The two lenses are complementary, not redundant.
