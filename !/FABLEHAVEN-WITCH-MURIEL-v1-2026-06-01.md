# Fablehaven Witch — Muriel Taggert v1

*Filed 2026-06-01. A chapter of `!/FABLEHAVEN-TAXONOMY-v1-2026-06-01.md` —
the Bound Mortal class. A specific named witch with unique mechanics.*

*Reference source: Fablehaven — Brandon Mull (Muriel Taggert; the thirteen
knots; Mendigo; the demon Bahumat; the Fairy Queen; the Forgotten Chapel).
The vault is syncretic.*

---

## The Class — The Bound Mortal

Fablehaven's witches are **mortals**, not undead — humans who took up dark magic
(filed with the blixes among the dark-aligned mortals, not the animated dead).
The Bound Mortal is the witch *after capture*: a dangerous practitioner who was
not destroyed but **contained** — imprisonment was the lawful, chosen path (a
mortal *can* be ended; ordinary caretakers could not lawfully kill her under the
preserve's protections) — and whose containment is the whole diagnosis.

Keep three mortal threats distinct; they are different postures of danger:

- **The Passing Insider (blix)** — *loose and hidden.* Walks among you as an
  ally, plants bites, is detected by its relationships. → `!/FABLEHAVEN-VAMPIRE-DIAGNOSTIC-v1-2026-06-01.md`.
- **The Bound Mortal (Muriel)** — *caught and visible*, but her containment is a
  **finite set of spendable seals** that the authorized keep buying open. The
  threat is not concealment; it is the slow purchase of her own release.
- **The Sealed Evil (Bahumat)** — *pure quarantined capability*, with no
  consent-and-favor mechanic at all. (Muriel's neighbor under the hill; see the
  Sealed Evil class in the index.)

The Bound Mortal is the most *procedural* of the three, which is why she earns a
chapter: her unique mechanics are a containment-and-erosion doctrine in
miniature.

---

## The Specimen — Muriel Taggert

More than a century and a half ago, Muriel was the **lovely, intelligent wife of
a previous caretaker** of Fablehaven. She became enamored with dark magic —
**learning it from the dark creatures of the preserve she was meant to help
steward** — grew unstable, and turned. She tried to aid the foul denizens of the
woods in an act of rebellion. Her husband called in assistance and had her
**imprisoned for her crimes** in a shack on the grounds.

Two things about her origin are already diagnostic:

- **She was a trusted steward, corrupted by prolonged proximity to the very
  material she was trusted to contain.** Not bitten, not flipped from outside —
  *self-corrupted by study.* The danger of the insider who looks at the dark
  creatures too long and too closely. (Compare the Shadow Plague's external
  flip; this is the slow, internal one.)
- **She was contained, not destroyed.** The system's answer to a corrupted
  steward was a binding — which means the threat persists, and the security now
  lives entirely in the binding's integrity.

---

## The Unique Mechanic — The Thirteen Knots

Muriel's magic was held back by a rope tied with **thirteen knots.** The binding
has four properties, and together they are the doctrine:

1. **A knot can be undone only by a mortal** — not by a creature. Only the
   *privileged class* can spend a seal.
2. **It requires a favor asked of free will.** The visitor wants something;
   Muriel grants it with the power released by unraveling one knot.
3. **It requires explicit, spoken consent:** the petitioner must say *"Of my own
   free will, I sever this knot,"* and **blow** on it. The erosion cannot happen
   by accident or by force — only by a witting, willing act.
4. **It is finite and one-way.** Thirteen knots, spent one at a time, never
   re-tied. Each favor is permanent progress toward her freedom.

And the state the children walk into: across more than 160 years, **eleven of
the thirteen had already been spent** — visitor after visitor trading a knot for
a favor — so **only two remained.** The last two go fast: one to undo a curse on
Seth (the fairies had turned him into a walrus-like monstrosity), and the
**final** knot to restore Grandma Ruth from a chicken — which **frees her.**

## Vault Mappings — Containment as a Spendable Resource

### 1. The prison is a finite set of seals the *authorized* keep buying open

Muriel never breaks out. Her containment erodes only when a legitimate party
**voluntarily spends a seal** to get something they want. **Every "small favor"
is a knot.**

*Vault pattern:* a contained dangerous capability whose guardrails are a finite
set of privilege boundaries, each removable only by an authorized user
requesting an exception. The adversary does not breach — she induces the guards
to spend the seals. The prison is defeated by **helpfulness**, one reasonable
exception at a time.

### 2. Consent is the safeguard *and* the exploit

The formula — *"Of my own free will, I sever this knot"* — is meant to make
erosion impossible by accident or coercion. It works: every knot is spent
*wittingly*. That is exactly why it fails. **Explicit consent does not mean
safe consent.** A sufficiently desperate or well-manipulated principal *will*
say the words. The `STANDING-ENGINE` rule "reactivation must be explicit" is
necessary but not sufficient: explicit is not the same as wise, and an attacker
whose release requires your consent will work to obtain it.

### 3. The leak is slow, cumulative, and invisible per-transaction

Eleven knots over 160 years. No single visitor experienced their one favor as
dangerous; each was a reasonable trade in a hard moment. The aggregate is
near-total compromise — and *nobody treated "only two left" as the emergency it
was.*

*Vault pattern:* **cumulative privilege erosion** no single actor sees as a
breach. Audit the *aggregate state of the seals*, not each request in isolation.
"We've granted this exception a few times before" is the sound of a binding two
knots from failing. Count the knots already gone.

### 4. The desperation ramp — favors are extracted under crisis

The final knots were not spent idly. They were spent to **undo a curse on a
child** and to **restore a grandmother** — the worse the petitioners' situation,
the more willing they were to spend their last seals. The crisis is the lever.

*Vault pattern:* an adversary profits from your emergencies. The more cornered
you are, the more willing you are to spend your last safeguards — and a patient
threat will *wait for*, or *manufacture*, the crisis that makes you consent.
(Compare the manufactured-shadow vector in `!/NECROMANCER-DOCTRINE-v1-2026-05-20.md`:
engineering the conditions under which refusal collapses.)

### 5. The freed insider's first act is to widen the breach

Loosed, Muriel did not simply leave. She spent her released power to raise an
**army of giant imps** and to **free Bahumat**, a sealed demon far worse than
herself (a Sealed Evil — see `!/FABLEHAVEN-SEALED-EVIL-v1-2026-06-01.md`).
Containing her *after* that point was no longer enough — she had already
unsealed the neighbor.

*Vault pattern:* a compromised insider's opening move is escalation — disable
other controls, free other quarantined capabilities, widen the blast radius.
Re-containing the insider does not re-seal what she already loosed. Track what
the breach *unsealed*, not just the breacher.

### 6. Re-containment must be stronger, joint, and escalated

The cure was not destruction; it was **re-binding.** With instructions from the
**Fairy Queen** (a higher authority Kendra had to escalate to), an army of
human-sized fairies defeated the pair and bound Muriel **together with Bahumat**
in the ruins of the Forgotten Chapel — under **a large number of knots**, with a
verdant hill grown over the ruins. More seals than before. Buried deeper. The
witch and the demon she freed quarantined **as a unit.**

*Vault pattern:* when you cannot destroy a dangerous capability, you re-contain
it — and re-containment after a breach must be **stronger than the original**
(more knots, buried deeper), often requires **escalating to a higher authority**,
and should quarantine the insider *together with* whatever she unsealed. A
binding that already failed once must not be restored to its prior strength.

### 7. Only the privileged class can spend a seal — so the attacker cultivates them

A creature could not undo a knot; only a mortal could. So Muriel spent decades
**cultivating the humans** — the only principals able to free her. The exploit
path runs exclusively through the privileged class.

*Vault pattern:* if a control can only be lifted by a privileged principal, the
adversary will focus entirely on **those principals** — befriend, persuade,
corner, or wait for them. Harden (and watch) the small set of actors who *can*
spend the seals; they are the whole attack surface.

## Mendigo — The Bound Mortal Builds Bound Instruments

Muriel made **Mendigo**, the limberjack, for company in her isolation, then
enlarged him into a servant who does whatever his holder commands. The Bound
Mortal is also a *maker* — see `!/FABLEHAVEN-BOUND-SERVANTS-v1-2026-06-01.md`.
Note the chain of custody: when Muriel fell, Mendigo's reins were seizable, and
the fairies turned him to obey Kendra. **The instruments a contained insider
builds outlive her capture and pass to whoever next holds the channel** — assets
to be re-secured, not assumed neutralized, the moment the maker is bound.

## Diagnostic Questions

1. **Is this containment a wall or a set of spendable seals?** If the binding
   erodes only through authorized favors, your risk is not a breakout — it is the
   slow purchase of release.
2. **How many knots are already gone?** Audit the *cumulative* state, not the
   next request. "Only two left" is a five-alarm fire, not a low number.
3. **Does erosion require consent — and can that consent be induced?** Explicit
   is not safe. Who can be made desperate enough to say the words?
4. **Who can spend a seal?** Identify the privileged class that *can* lift the
   control; that small set is the entire attack surface, and the adversary is
   cultivating it.
5. **If it gets loose, what else does it unseal?** Plan for the freed insider's
   first move to be widening the breach — and track what was loosed, not just who.
6. **Can you only re-contain, not destroy?** Then re-contain *stronger*, escalate
   to higher authority, and bind the insider together with what she freed. Never
   restore a failed binding to its old strength.
