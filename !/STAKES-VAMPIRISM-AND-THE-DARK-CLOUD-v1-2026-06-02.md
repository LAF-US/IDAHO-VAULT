# Stakes — Vampirism & the Dark Cloud

*Filed 2026-06-02. Reference: Adventure Time, **Stakes**. Syncretic — a
diagnostic framework, not a simulation. Claims marked **[canon]** /
**[mapping]**. Where canon is silent on an "origin," this page says so rather
than inventing one.*

## How vampirism works — canon

- **[canon]** **It spreads by the bite** — a vampire turns the living by biting
  them. But conversion appears to be **deliberate/conscious**, not an automatic
  consequence of every bite: the Vampire King *could* turn people and animals,
  but turning is something a vampire seems to *choose.*
- **[canon]** **The locus is the soul/essence, not the body.** A vampire's
  powers — and its true death — live in its **essence.** Staking the body is not
  always enough (the **Moon** self-heals any wound and is only ended when her
  **soul is sucked out**). To truly end a vampire, and to take its power, you
  reach the **soul.**
- **[canon]** **Marceline's soul-absorption is a *demon* power** (see the
  Marceline note), which is why she — a tiefling — could both kill vampires
  permanently and inherit their abilities.
- **[mapping]** Canon does **not** give a single "first vampire" origin myth.
  This page does **not** claim one. `*`

## The cure, and its by-product — canon

- **[canon]** Princess Bubblegum builds a **machine that extracts vampiric
  essence.** Marceline asks to be cured; the extraction restores her mortality
  — **but releases the five vampires she had absorbed**, who reconstitute and
  escape. *Removing the essence did not destroy it.*
- **[canon]** When the **Vampire King's** essence is extracted, he **splits in
  two**: his mortal self reverts to a mundane **lion**; his essence becomes a
  monster — the **Dark Cloud.**

## The Dark Cloud — canon

- **[canon]** A **tar-like monstrosity of "pure, concentrated vampirocity."** It
  **engulfs and traps living beings *en masse*** (cows, villagers) and generates
  powerful gales — but **engulfment is not conversion**: the captured **fall back
  out, unturned, when Marceline absorbs it.** The **only** vampirizing the episode
  shows is the **neck-bite on Marceline herself** during her absorption. The
  contagion, distilled and unbound from any single body.
- **[canon]** It **bites Marceline**, turning her back into a vampire. She ends
  it by flying **inside** and **soul-draining it from within** — at the cost of
  **becoming the Vampire Queen again.** Afterward the Vampire King persists as a
  **mental projection** in her mind.

## Diagnostic Criteria — the vault mapping

**[mapping]** The Dark Cloud is the **concentrated-essence** problem, and it ties
straight to `!/LICH-PROBLEM-v1`:

1. **The threat lives in the essence, not the body.** Attack outputs/symptoms and
   it heals or reconstitutes. Reach the **core** (the soul-store) or you have not
   ended it. (This is the phylactery logic of the Lich Problem, in vampire form.)
2. **Extraction ≠ destruction.** Pulling a concentrated capability out of its
   vessel does not annihilate it — it can become **diffuse and contagious** (the
   cloud), and it **engulfs en masse.** *The cure breeds the worse form.* Plan
   custody of the by-product before you extract. (Cf. the **Fablehaven nail** —
   *unmerged, PR #400, not yet on `main`* — and the Vampire King page.)
3. **Targeted spread has a discrete turning-choice; the diffuse form does not.**
   For an individual **bite**, conversion is the vampire's *deliberate choice to
   turn* — a bite alone does not convert (canon above). That makes targeted spread
   **gated and interruptible**: the operative counter is **preventing or
   interrupting the bite-and-turn** — and, on the maker's own side, the would-be
   maker **declining to perpetuate** (cf. `!/LICH-PROBLEM-v1` Resolution 3, *Active
   Refusal*) — **not** a victim-side veto you can rely on. The **Dark Cloud**, by
   contrast, **engulfs and traps *en masse*** — mass *capture* with **no
   single turning-gate** (the captured fall back out unturned; the only actual
   turning still rode one bite, on Marceline). For the diffuse by-product the operative counter is **containment
   before diffusion** (point 2). Diagnose which regime you are in before reaching
   for either.
4. **Ending it re-incurs it, and leaves a sub-layer.** The one who absorbs the
   cloud to end it **takes the condition back into themselves** (Marceline
   re-Queened), and the absorbed essence **persists as an internal projection** —
   exactly the **sub-layer** architecture of `!/LICH-PROBLEM-v1` Resolution 2
   (the Lich-in-Sweet-P speaking from underneath). No one closes this loop clean;
   the cost is carrying it afterward.

## Diagnostic Questions

1. Does the threat live in an **essence/core** that survives attacks on the body?
2. Will **extracting** it leave a **diffuse, contagious** by-product? (Then secure
   the by-product as part of the plan, not after.)
3. Does its spread require a **chosen** conversion — a decision point that can be
   **refused**?
4. Who absorbs it to end it — and what **sub-layer** do they carry afterward?
