## Working Findings

- The Great Modron March is a periodic mass expedition of modrons out of Mechanus and through the Outer Planes.
- Common timing: once every 289 years, described as 17 Mechanus cycles of 17 years.
- Primus orders the March.
- The purpose is intentionally uncertain in the lore; common interpretations include reconnaissance, information gathering, recalibration of Mechanus, and temporary imposition or measurement of order.
- The March is lawful and procedural, but hazardous. It does not negotiate once underway.
- Modron hierarchy matters: orders descend rank by rank, and lower modrons understand only adjacent authority layers.
- The 2e adventure The Great Modron March centers on an anomalous March triggered early after Primus was usurped by Tenebrous.

## Vault Interpretation Hook

For VAULT doctrine, the Modron March is a useful image for scheduled audit/process traversal: a lawful census or synchronization sweep that observes the whole system, follows predetermined routing, and can become dangerous when procedure outruns judgment. In the Mechanus illustration, Primus maps to Logan; the modron machinery does not originate authority.
