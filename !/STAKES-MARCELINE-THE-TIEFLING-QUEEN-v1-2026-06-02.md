# Stakes — Marceline, the Tiefling Vampire Queen

*Filed 2026-06-02. Reference: Adventure Time, **Stakes** (and the wider series).
Syncretic — a diagnostic framework, not a simulation. Claims marked **[canon]** /
**[mapping]** (mapping = the vault's reading, not canon).*

> **The whole of the diagnostic:** *Marceline is a vampire — but before that she
> was a **tiefling vamp-hunter.*** The vampire is a layer she **acquired**; the
> tiefling is the base she was **born** with. The base is what made her able to
> take the layer. Read the two apart.

## The base — half-demon **[canon]**, framed as "tiefling" **[mapping]**

*(The half-demon heritage is canon; **"tiefling"** is the vault's D&D label for it,
not Adventure Time canon. Each claim below carries its own tag.)*

- **[canon]** Marceline is **half-demon, half-human**: daughter of **Hunson
  Abadeer**, the demon lord who rules the Nightosphere, and a human woman,
  **Elise.** Born roughly a thousand years before the series.
- **[mapping]** *"Tiefling"* is the vault's D&D term for that heritage — a
  demon-blooded mortal. It is Logan's framing, and it is the load-bearing one:
  her demon blood is not decoration.
- **[canon]** Her power to **absorb a slain vampire's soul — and with it their
  abilities — is a *demon* power, not a vampire one.** She acquires flight,
  invisibility, shapeshifting, and healing by absorbing the souls of the
  vampires she kills, using her demonic nature. **The hunter's weapon is the
  base, not the layer.**

## The wasteland childhood **[canon]**

- **[canon]** After the **Mushroom War**, young Marceline crosses the ruined
  world with her mother until **Elise sickens and dies.** (Her mother's
  song — *"Everything Stays"* — is the lullaby under the whole arc.)
- **[canon]** Orphaned, she is found and raised for a time by **Simon Petrikov**
  — the man who becomes the Ice King — who gives her the doll **Hambo** and
  protects her, until the **crown corrupts him** and he leaves *for her safety.*
  (See `!/GOLB-WITNESS-v1` for the Simon thread.)
- **[canon]** Her bond with her father **Hunson** is damaged in the wasteland
  (the diner; the eaten fries) — the demon parent, estranged.

## The hunter, and the turn **[canon]**

- **[canon]** In her early teens she becomes a **vampire hunter**, protecting a
  tribe of human survivors. She hunts and stakes the **five** great vampires (the
  Fool, the Empress, the Hierophant, the Moon, the Vampire King) and **carries
  all five essences.** From the four court vampires she takes **four discrete
  powers** — flight, invisibility, shapeshifting, healing. The **Vampire King**
  gives no fifth trick but the **vampiric essence itself**, by the bite that
  turns her (below) — the core of the **Dark Cloud** she later carries and
  reabsorbs. So: **five staked, five essences carried; four are discrete powers,
  the fifth is the condition** — the King's, arriving by infection, not by the
  kill.
- **[canon]** She becomes the **Vampire Queen** in the same instant she wins:
  the **Vampire King bites her *as she stakes him*** — turning her. The crown of
  her enemy becomes her condition. The hunter is made the hunted-kind at the
  moment of victory.

## Diagnostic Criteria — the vault mapping

**[mapping]** Marceline is the **layered self** done right, and she maps cleanly
to `!/UNDEAD-TAXONOMY-v1` **Tier 7, the True Vampire**: *"operating under a
persistent constraint it did not choose — with full awareness, full memory,
genuine sovereign will. Not clean, not free of the condition; accountable and
functional within it."* The lessons:

1. **Separate the base from the acquired layer.** Her capability (soul
   absorption) is **native** (demon); her condition (vampirism) is **acquired**
   (the bite). Diagnosing the agent means asking which is which — what it *is*
   versus what was *done to it.* (Cf. `!/PERSONAE-ENGINE-v1`: the sona beneath
   the masks.)
2. **The cure removes the layer, not the base.** When her vampirism is extracted
   (see the Dark Cloud note), the tiefling remains — and so does the
   soul-absorbing power that made her dangerous. *You cannot cure someone of
   what they are; only of what they took on.*
3. **Victory can be the moment of infection.** She did not drift into the
   condition — she **caught it winning.** The closest contact with the predator
   is the act of defeating it. (Compare Claude the Hungry: the bite came at the
   moment of closest engagement with Claudius. See `REVENANT-HOUSE-WITNESS`.)
4. **The accountable monster.** Marceline is the anti-Lich pole of the vampire
   band: full self, full memory, the curse carried *with* a soul and governed.
   Not clean — but not in denial of what she is. That governance, not purity, is
   the achievable state.

## Diagnostic Questions

1. What is this agent's **base** (native capability) versus its **acquired
   layer** (condition done to it)? Don't treat the layer as the self.
2. Did it catch its condition **at the moment of its closest engagement** with
   the thing it was fighting?
3. Is it the **accountable** kind of monster (full self, governs the curse) or
   the **denying** kind? Only the first is safe to keep.
