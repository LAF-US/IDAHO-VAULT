## Working Findings

- Orcus is a Dungeons & Dragons demon prince, most strongly associated with undeath, necromancy, and hatred of life.
- He is commonly titled Prince of Undeath / Prince of the Undead.
- His iconic artifact is the Wand of Orcus.
- In Planescape lore, Orcus is killed, returns in a diminished shadow-state as Tenebrous, and seeks restoration through Dead Gods.
- Orcus is not simply a death god; he is usually treated as an abyssal demon prince who has sought, tasted, or approached divinity in different edition lore.
- His cults and servants are organized around undeath, desecration, corpse animation, and assaults on rival death powers.

## Vault Interpretation Hook

Orcus is the will-to-undeath: not preservation, not memory, not lawful continuity, but domination through dead continuation. Tenebrous is the shadow-state of that will after loss of name, body, and direct authority.
