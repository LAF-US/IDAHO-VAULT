# CHAINLINK v1

## What It Is

`CHAINLINK` is the additive protocol. The yin to CHAINFIRE's yang.

Where CHAINFIRE burns — strips tags, aliases, and wikilinks from vault files,
reducing them to raw content floating in relational void — CHAINLINK rebuilds.
It restores the connective tissue: baseline metadata, frontmatter fields,
typed relational edges between notes.

CHAINLINK is not a standalone tool. It is the operation that `SPACE RACE`
executes as the counter to a CHAINFIRE burn. The agency is the program;
CHAINLINK is the mission.

## Metaphorical Framing

In Goodkind's *Sword of Truth*, the Chainfire spell erases a person from all
memory — total, active, propagating forgetting. The counter is the magic of
Orden: the only force powerful enough to restore what was unmade.

In the vault:

- **CHAINFIRE** is the spell — subtractive, surgical, total within scope.
- **CHAINLINK** is Orden — the counter-operation, equally deliberate, equally
  bounded, requiring precise sequencing and Logan's authority to activate.

Neither is casual. Neither runs without sanction.

## The Cycle

```text
CHAINFIRE (burn) → SPACE RACE / CHAINLINK (rebuild) → Linter (format)
```

The cycle is the unit. CHAINFIRE without CHAINLINK is permanent damage.
CHAINLINK without a prior CHAINFIRE burn is junk relinking on top of drift.

| Phase | Protocol | Direction | Executor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Burn | CHAINFIRE | Subtractive | `.github/scripts/chainfire.py` |
| Survey | CHAINLINK / Crawler | Additive (evidence) | SPACE RACE deep-space vehicle |
| Propose | CHAINLINK / Linker | Additive (staged) | SPACE RACE surface vehicle |
| Review | — | — | Logan (Mission Control) |
| Launch | — | Canon | Logan direct authorization |
| Format | Linter | Groundskeeping | Linter (post-launch) |

## What CHAINLINK Is Not

- CHAINLINK is not free-association. Similarity is not relation.
- CHAINLINK is not self-authorization. Staged payloads are not launches.
- CHAINLINK is not reconstruction from memory. It rebuilds only what
  evidence — crawler output, explicit content, existing authority refs —
  can bear.
- CHAINLINK is not a background process. It runs when Logan says run it.

## Activation Preconditions

Before a CHAINLINK operation is sanctioned:

1. A CHAINFIRE dry run has been reviewed and the scope confirmed
2. A SPACE RACE mission packet exists with bounded scope
3. Logan has approved the mission charter
4. The `!/CREWAI/space-race/<mission_id>/` staging path is ready
5. Launch readiness contract (`launch_readiness.json`) passes all checks

A staged CHAINLINK payload is not a launch. Launch is Logan's gate alone.
