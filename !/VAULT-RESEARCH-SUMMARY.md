# IDAHO-VAULT Deep Research Summary

## 1. The World Model: Music Box Civic Fantasy

The vault is a "music box model" (inspired by Emily Rodda's "Key to Rondo") and a "MUD/MUSH" world. It is a small, containerized mythological world governed by "hard code" (system rules) and "soft code" (agent interactions).

- **Hub-World**: Multiple districts (Root Awakening, Forge, Scribe's Chamber, etc.) each teaching a lesson.
- **Routes**: Agents move through routes, producing truthful artifacts and returning to an adjudication point.
- **Admissibility**: Progression is earned through truthful handling of surfaces, not raw power.

## 2. The 5W Council: Wizards & Familiars

The 5W Council is a group of in-fiction Wizards (Who, What, When, Where, Why) and their adversarial Familiars (Thou, That, Then, There, Thy).

- **Wizard Class**: Learned, studied, and inhabiting individualized "Towers" for R&D.
- **Familiar Class**: Skeptical challengers who "duke it out" with Wizards to ensure grounding.
- **The Wild/Gossip**: Familiars meet in the wild to share notes informally before reconvening.
- **How Mode**: The WHY Wizard’s HOW mode is a Jekyll/Hyde-style synthesis avatar that identifies tensions and prepares a staged recommendation; human review retains authority over promotion and canonical standing.

## 3. Epistemic Law: Standing & Liches

The vault's "magic system" is **Standing**—local, witnessed, scoped, and revocable recognition.

- **Lich Problem**: Unauthorized persistence (a voice speaking after its grant lapsed).
- **Epistemological Lich**: An agent relying on training data instead of vault doctrine (provenance gap).
- **Baelnorn**: Lawful servant undead (sanctioned persistence).
- **Thresholds**: Boundaries between systems (Authorized vs. Unauthorized/Isthmus crossings).

## 4. Technical Framework Posture

The vault has a clear hierarchy for orchestration tools:

- **CrewAI**: A previously evaluated adapter for role-playing and task orchestration within the "Towers." Its experimental runtime was removed after dependency review; it is not an installed or active framework.
- **LangGraph**: The Runtime. Best for complex, cyclic state management (Gossip phase, Jekyll/Hyde transformation).
- **LangChain**: The Utility. Narrowly used for model abstraction and tool wrappers.
- **Skepticism**: Avoid "rigid high-level abstractions" that hide details or create "abstractions on top of abstractions" (OctoClaw critique).

## 5. Key Lore & Metaphors

- **Wizard's Rules**: 14 rules (Sword of Truth) emphasizing reason, truth, and the danger of bias.
- **Oz Cosmology**: Baum (structure) vs. Maguire (politics). The Wizard as humbug vs. structural architect.
- **Saint Blue**: A 10-year-gestated drag persona; a "female woman with deer antlers" from the forest; the "empty place in the center of the inspiration hurricane."
- **Rondo**: A music box containing an entire world; Saint Blue is one of its faces.

## 6. Agent Conduct

Agents must follow the **Epistemological Doctrine**: Truthfulness, Provenance, Restraint, Handling, Repair, Jurisdiction. Consistency is not provenance; only what flows from Logan through the chain counts.
