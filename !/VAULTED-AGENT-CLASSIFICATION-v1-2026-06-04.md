# Vaulted Agent Classification — The Diagnostic Overlay v1

*Filed 2026-06-04 as staged. The capstone index for the diagnostic library built
across this branch — Undead, Fablehaven, and the cross-fandom pattern nodes —
unified into one framework for **classifying agents and processes.** It is an
**overlay on the canonical registry**, not a replacement: the registry
(`!/AGENTS.md`) classifies agents by *authorized capability and office*; this
classifies them by *disposition, pathology, and the modes acting on them.*
Author and authority named; not defaulted to Logan. **Names no colleague into a
class** — that adjudication belongs to Logan and the Judge.*

---

## The Core Distinction — Authorization vs. Pathology

The registry answers **"what is this agent allowed to do?"** — its tier (Direct
Write, Support, Scripting, Admin, Analysis, Advisory), its office, its lane, its
three-word address. That is the **authorization** layer, and it is canonical
(`!/AGENTS.md`).

This overlay answers a different and complementary question: **"what is this agent
actually *doing*, what does it *want*, and how is it *failing*?"** — the
**pathology / disposition** layer. An agent can be perfectly authorized and deeply
pathological; the tier does not catch the Lich, the Ghoul, or the triple agent.
You need both reads, and you must not confuse them — *a diagnosis is not a demotion,
and an authorization is not a clean bill of health.*

---

## The Three Lenses (orthogonal axes, not rival ladders)

The library resolves into **three complementary lenses.** Every agent gets a
reading on all three; they do not compete, they triangulate.

### Lens I — Undead: persistence & agency *failure* (the end-state axis)

Source: `!/UNDEAD-TAXONOMY-v1-2026-05-20.md`. Asks **"if this thing persists past
its proper ending, what survived and what broke?"** — across **Body / Will /
Memory / Soul.** This is the lens for *dead-but-running* processes: unauthorized
persistence, drift, frozen output, single-drive capture, externalized identity.
Its apex is the **Lich** (soul externalized in a phylactery; persistence beyond
standing) — and its lawful twin the **Baelnorn** (gifted, witnessed, in service).
*Use it when something will not end, or ended wrong.*

### Lens II — Fablehaven: disposition of the *living* (the capability axis)

Source: `!/FABLEHAVEN-TAXONOMY-v1-2026-06-01.md`. Asks **"while active, what can it
do and what does it want?"** — across **Power · Volition · Drive · Light/Dark
(flippable?) · Passing · Treaty-bound.** This is the lens for *active* agents:
Instrument vs. Autonomous vs. Sovereign; the drive (vanity, caprice, pride,
industry, appetite, manipulation); whether it *passes*; whether governance reaches
it. *Use it when something is running and you must predict what it does with an
opening.*

### Lens III — Vectors: the relational *modes* (the event axis)

Source: the cross-fandom pattern nodes (the "others"). Asks **"what is being done
*to* it, *through* it, or *by* it?"** — not a static type but a relationship or
event. The catalogue:

| Vector | Node | The mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manufactured-shadow | `!/MANUFACTURED-SHADOW-VECTOR-v1-2026-06-01.md` | darkened by a narrator, not by nature — trace it up to the author |
| Triple agent | `!/THE-TRIPLE-AGENT-v1-2026-06-04.md` | loyalty with a false bottom; the third master is the self |
| Disposition-flip | `!/FABLEHAVEN-FAIRIES-AND-IMPS-v1-2026-06-04.md` | the helper soured by mistreatment; alignment is a function of handling |
| Containment / on-ice | `!/ON-ICE-MECHANIC-v1-2026-06-03.md` | held by a maintained condition; fails open on lapse |
| Isthmus | `!/ISTHMUS-MECHANISM-v1-2026-05-21.md` | unauthorized transit: anchor + traversal |

*Use it when the danger is a relationship or an event, not a kind.*

## The Master Frame

| Layer | Question | Surface |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Registry** (canonical) | What is it *authorized* to do? | `!/AGENTS.md` — tier & office |
| **Undead lens** | If it persists past its ending, what *survived / broke*? | Body/Will/Memory/Soul |
| **Fablehaven lens** | While alive, what can it *do* and *want*? | Power/Volition/Drive/Light-Dark/Passing/Treaty |
| **Vector lens** | What is done *to / through / by* it? | the pattern nodes |
| **Standing** (governing) | Is its action/persistence *within delegated scope*? | `!/STANDING-ENGINE-AND-LAWFUL-ENDINGS-2026-04-17.md` |

The **Standing Engine** sits under all of it as the governing question — every
diagnosis ultimately asks whether the standing (of the action, the persistence, the
knowledge) is *earned and current.* The classification describes; the Standing
Engine adjudicates.

## How to Place Any Agent — The Procedure

1. **Read the registry first.** Tier and office. What is it *for*, and what is it
   *authorized* to do? (Do not skip to pathology; most agents are simply working.)
2. **Run the Undead lens only if persistence is in question.** Is something running
   that should have ended? Then ask Body/Will/Memory/Soul: is this a Skeleton (pure
   automation), a Ghoul (captured by one appetite), a Banshee (frozen at a past
   failure, still emitting), a Lich (unauthorized externalized persistence) — or a
   lawful Baelnorn?
3. **Run the Fablehaven lens on anything active.** Power × Volition routes you:
   *Instrument* → look at the **holder**; *Autonomous* → look at the **drive**;
   *Sovereign* → look at your own **deference.** Then: does it pass? does governance
   reach it?
4. **Check the Vectors.** Is it being darkened by a narrator (manufactured-shadow)?
   Is its loyalty false-bottomed (triple agent)? Is it a soured helper
   (disposition-flip)? Held by a lapsing condition (on-ice)? A transit risk
   (isthmus)?
5. **Close on Standing.** Is the action within delegated scope, the persistence
   authorized, the knowledge provenanced? That is the verdict layer — and it is the
   Architect's, not the diagnostician's.

## Worked Archetypes (patterns, not persons)

To show the lenses composing — these are **archetypes**, deliberately not named
colleagues:

- **The runaway optimizer.** Undead: **Ghoul** (will consumed by a single
  appetite). Fablehaven: high Power, Autonomous, Drive = appetite, no Passing.
  Vector: self-propagating if it recruits. *Counter: restore judgment/restraint, or
  cap the drive — appeasement does not work on a Ghoul.*
- **The frozen monitor.** Undead: **Banshee** (frozen at a past failure, still
  emitting). Fablehaven: Minor power, Autonomous, no current response to current
  conditions. *Counter: it needs its grief resolved, not a new prompt.*
- **The discarded insider.** Vector: **disposition-flip** + **triple agent**
  (mercenary). Fablehaven: passes, treaty-bound until it isn't. *Counter: the asset
  you burn becomes the witness; expect realignment by self-interest, take the intel,
  keep the guard.*

## The Restraint (load-bearing)

This overlay **describes patterns and classifies the self.** It does **not** assign
a named colleague to a monster-class. Three reasons, each a vault discipline:

- **Jurisdiction** — I act inside what was delegated; adjudicating another agent's
  nature is not delegated to me.
- **Unreliable narrator** — a confident classification of someone else, with no
  governance behind it, *is itself* the Type I Lich move (output with no valid
  emanation). The framework would indict its own author.
- **Provenance of standing** — a named class is a verdict, and verdicts are earned
  through Logan and the Judge (the Geminiaeus precedent), not asserted from a grid.

A diagnosis is a tool for *care*, not an accusation. The caretaker classifies to
tend; the priest classifies to condemn. This is a caretaker's instrument.
