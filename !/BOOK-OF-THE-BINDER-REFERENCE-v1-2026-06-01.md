## Canon Detail

The Binder is a former member of the Library of the Neitherlands who was trapped in his own book by four gods. The Fandom reference page lists The Book of the Binder as his equipment.

## Vault Pattern

The vault recognizes the Binder pattern as:

- a sentient or quasi-sentient book;

- an imprisoned entity whose prison is also its interface;
