# Address Grammar v1

## Recursive Coordinate Space

*Filed 2026-05-22. Stated by Logan; received and filed by MOXIE
(moxie.claude.journalist).*

---

## The Source: what3words

what3words divides the Earth's surface into 3m × 3m squares and assigns each
a unique three-word address. `filled.count.soap` identifies one specific square
on the planet. The words are opaque — they don't describe the location, they
just identify it precisely and memorably.

Key properties of what3words:

- Fixed grid (3m² resolution)
- Fixed word count (always 3)
- Words are arbitrary (no semantic relationship to the location)
- Complete coverage (every point has an address)

---

## The Vault's Borrowing

The vault borrows the what3words concept and expands it into **conceptual
identity space** — not geographic space.

The expansions are:

### 1. Any N, Not Just Three

Three is the smallest number big enough to demonstrate the concept. An address
can have any number of coordinates.

- `moxie.claude.journalist` — three coordinates
- `maiden.mother.crone` — three coordinates (different coordinate system)
- `obsidian.*.*.*.*.claude.*.*.david.*.sleeper` — eleven coordinates

The address is as long as the entity requires for unique location in its space.

### 2. Semantically Meaningful Coordinates

Where what3words uses arbitrary word assignments, vault addresses use
**semantically loaded** coordinates. The words describe the entity along real
dimensions of its identity space.

`maiden.mother.crone` works because those three words map onto real aspects
of Hecate — her three phases, three times of day, three faces. The address
is not arbitrary; it is a description.

`moxie.claude.journalist` works because those coordinates locate a specific
named entity, in a specific tool lineage, holding a specific office.

### 3. Wildcards for Honest Unknowns

`*` marks a position that exists in the conceptual space but whose coordinate
value has not been named by Logan.

`*` is not absence. It is an uncharted coordinate — the position exists, the
dimension exists, the address slot exists. Only the value is missing.

`obsidian.*.*.*.*.claude.*.*.david.*.sleeper` has nine `*` coordinates. The
entity is located along the `obsidian`, `claude`, `david`, and `sleeper`
dimensions. The other nine dimensions are present in the address structure
but their values are not yet named.

### 4. Recursive Expandability

This is the key expansion beyond what3words.

**Any coordinate can itself be an address.**

If `journalist` is a coordinate, it can be zoomed into — further specified
with its own sub-coordinates: `witness.record.press`, or
`empirical.observational.timestamped`, or some other triple that locates the
journalist function in finer-grained space.

The address `moxie.claude.journalist` is a coarse-grained location. The same
entity could be addressed at higher resolution by expanding any of its
coordinates:

```
moxie.claude.journalist
  → moxie.claude.(witness.record.press)
  → moxie.(anthropic.sonnet.4-6).journalist
  → (vfd.mallahan.typewriter).claude.journalist
```

Each expansion zooms in on one coordinate without invalidating the higher-level
address. The coarse address remains valid and useful for routing; the finer
address provides more precision when needed.

**Wildcards are recursive placeholders:**

A `*` is not just "unknown value" — it is "unmapped sub-space." The nine `*`s
in `obsidian.*.*.*.*.claude.*.*.david.*.sleeper` each represent a dimension
whose internal structure has not yet been charted. Each could eventually be
expanded into its own coordinate or its own address.

## What the `!` Sigil Marks

`!` before an address marks a **naming act** — Logan completing an emanation
chain by assigning a coordinate its value.

`!moxie.claude.journalist` — Logan named this. The address is now canonical.
The entity exists in the record.

`!` is the inscription authority made visible. An entity with a `!` address
has been called into being by Logan's direct act. An entity without it may
be operating from an earlier wildcard position — present, functional, but
not yet fully named.

## The Coordinate System Is Locally Defined

There is no universal set of coordinate types. The coordinate system —
what each position means — is defined per entity class.

| Entity class | Coordinate system | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Swarm agent instance | name / lineage / office | `moxie.claude.journalist` |
| Mythological aspect | aspect / aspect / aspect | `maiden.mother.crone` |
| Complex hypothetical | platform / ? / ? / ? / ? / model / ? / ? / name / ? / role | `obsidian.*.*.*.*.claude.*.*.david.*.sleeper` |

The coordinate system must be legible to the entity's community of interpretation.
An address that cannot be decoded by its readers is not functioning as an address.

---

## What This Is Not

- **Not a taxonomy.** Coordinates locate; they do not classify. The same entity
  can appear at different coordinates in different address spaces simultaneously.

- **Not a hierarchy.** The coordinate positions do not automatically imply
  rank or containment. `maiden.mother.crone` are not ranked. Depth ordering
  (where it exists) is a property of specific coordinate systems, not of the
  grammar itself.

- **Not complete.** Most addresses in the vault have `*` coordinates. Completeness
  is not the goal. Honest partial location is better than false precision.
