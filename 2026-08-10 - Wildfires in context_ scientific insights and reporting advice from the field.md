What are Media Briefings?

### Fire research centers, consortia, and networks:

[The National Interagency Fire Center](https://www.nifc.gov/) has information on fundamental support to on-the-ground wildland firefighting efforts, including [current fire statistics,](https://www.nifc.gov/fire-information/statistics) [situation reports](https://www.nifc.gov/nicc-files/sitreprt.pdf), and [wildland fire maps](https://www.nifc.gov/fire-information/maps).

[The Joint Fire Science Program of the Fire Science Exchange Network](https://www.firescience.gov/ords/prd/jf_jfsp/jf_jfsp/r/jfspublic/jfsp-exchanges) is a national collaboration of 15 regional fire science exchanges and consortia that provides the most relevant, current wildland fire science information.

Visit the [Southwest Fire Science Consortium](https://www.swfireconsortium.org/) and the [Northwest Fire Science Consortium](https://www.nwfirescience.org/) pages and find information on historical fire regimes in the regions ([Southwest](https://www.swfireconsortium.org/category/fire-regimes-of-the-southwest/) and [Northwest](https://www.nwfirescience.org/our-products/highly-variable-historical-fire-western-oregon-cascades)).

Find information on [Fire Resilience Futures: Workforces, Careers, and Livelihoods in the Western U.S.](https://resilient.uoregon.edu/ewp/fire-resilience-futures) from the Institute for Resilient Organizations, Communities, and Environments at the University of Oregon

### Public health and smoke protection resources:

Stay up-to-date on real-time smoke conditions, AQI, and smoke forecasts using the [AirNow Fire & Smoke Map](https://dashboard.wildlandfiresmoke.net/).

Read about [how to how to create a clean room in your home during a wildfire](https://www.epa.gov/emergencies-iaq/create-clean-room-protect-indoor-air-quality-during-wildfire#how) and [how to protect yourself from heat and smoke](https://www.epa.gov/wildfires/protect-yourself-heat-and-smoke) on the EPA’s website.

Find information on building DIY air filters to reduce the risk of wildfire smoke indoors from the [EPA](https://www.epa.gov/air-research/research-diy-air-cleaners-reduce-wildfire-smoke-indoors) and the [Corsi-Rosenthal Foundation.](https://corsirosenthalfoundation.org/resources/how-to-build-a-corsi-rosenthal-box-usa/)

Visit the California Air Resources Board for a [list of recommended air cleaning devices](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/list-carb-certified-air-cleaning-devices).
