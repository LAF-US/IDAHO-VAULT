**James Castle** (September 25, 1899 – October 26, 1977) [^1] was an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans "Americans") artist born in [Garden Valley, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garden_Valley,_Idaho "Garden Valley, Idaho"). Although Castle did not know about the art world outside of his small community, his work ran parallel to the development of [20th-century art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20th-century_art "20th-century art") history.[^2] His works have been collected by major institutions. The [Philadelphia Museum of Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia_Museum_of_Art "Philadelphia Museum of Art") organized a retrospective of Castle's work which toured nationally in 2008–09. Castle's work entered the international arena with a substantial exhibition in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid "Madrid"), [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain "Spain") at the [Museo Nacional Centro de Arte Reina Sofía](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museo_Nacional_Centro_de_Arte_Reina_Sof%C3%ADa "Museo Nacional Centro de Arte Reina Sofía") in 2011 and was included in the 2013 [Venice Biennale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice_Biennale "Venice Biennale") exhibition *The Encyclopedic Palace.* In 2014 The [Smithsonian American Art Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smithsonian_American_Art_Museum "Smithsonian American Art Museum") featured their recent acquisition in the exhibition *Untitled: The Art of James Castle* and the [Whitney Museum of American Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_Museum_of_American_Art "Whitney Museum of American Art") included their acquired collection of Castle's work in the 2017 exhibition *Where We Are.*

## Biography

![James Castle at home in boise, Idaho](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5c/James_at_the_Front_of_His_Home_in_Boise%2C_idaho..webp/250px-James_at_the_Front_of_His_Home_in_Boise%2C_idaho..webp.png)

James Castle seated in his studio in Boise, [[Idaho]]

James Castle was a self-taught artist who created drawings, assemblage and books throughout his lifetime. Castle was born profoundly [deaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deaf "Deaf") and for at least some time attended the Gooding School for the Deaf and the Blind in [Gooding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gooding,_Idaho "Gooding, Idaho"), Idaho, but it is not known to what extent he could read, write, or use [sign language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sign_language "Sign language").[^3] Castle's artworks were created almost exclusively with found materials such as papers salvaged from common packaging and mail, in addition to food containers of all types. Castle mixed ink using [soot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soot "Soot") from the woodstove with saliva and applied it with tools of his own making, including sharpened sticks, and other found objects. His drawings sensitively depict interiors, buildings, animals, landscapes and people based on his family's rural [Garden Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garden_Valley,_Idaho "Garden Valley, Idaho") homestead as well as the architecture and landscapes of the places he lived and visited. His former home in Boise, Idaho is now a cultural center devoted to his work and includes an [artist-in-residence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artist-in-residence "Artist-in-residence") venue.[^4]
