The Arts Endowment provides public access to data and analyses about the arts and cultural sector, in addition to data and analyses about the agency’s own operations.

For access to a growing repository of arts and cultural datasets, documentation, analytical tools, and related literature, please visit the agency’s [National Archive of Data on Arts & Culture](https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/web/pages/NADAC/index.html "(opens in a new window)"). In addition, the Arts Endowment maintains a series of “ [Arts Data Profiles](https://www.arts.gov/impact/research/arts-data-profile-series),” with data tables, research briefs, interactive data visualizations, and other resources to educate the public about arts-related datasets. Finally, [NEA Research publications](https://www.arts.gov/impact/research/publications) often link to public data sources containing arts variables.

For access to knowledge about the agency’s administrative data holdings, please see our comprehensive (JSON-formatted) [inventory of datasets](https://www.arts.gov/data.json) at the Arts Endowment.

The NEA’s Chief Data Officer chairs a Data Governance Board (DGB), with representatives across the agency. The DGB meets quarterly to review and coordinate data management for the NEA.
