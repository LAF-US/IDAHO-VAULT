#### Zohran Mamdani booed off stage at New York City police event

New York City socialist Mayor Zohran Mamdani was heckled heavily at a recent police event. (Credit: Obtained by Fox)

New York City socialist Mayor [Zohran Mamdani](https://www.foxnews.com/category/person/zohran-mamdani) is being mocked after many say he was humiliated by being booed off the stage while attending one of the city's biggest pro-police celebrations on Staten Island.

Mamdani attempted to address about 3,500 residents and families attending the [New York Police Department’s](https://www.foxnews.com/category/us/crime/police-and-law-enforcement) 43rd Patrol Borough National Night Out Against Crime event on Tuesday. His remarks, however, lasted less than a minute as loud boos, jeers and chants of "get out of here" and "nobody wants you" drowned out his voice.

The mayor attempted to continue speaking for a short time, but the jeers drowned him out. After the boos continued, Mamdani thanked the crowd with his signature hand-over-his-heart gesture, waved and walked offstage smiling. As he exited, the crowd continued to berate him, with some yelling expletives. An organizer attempted to regain control of the event, but the crowd persisted, booing and shaking their fists at Mamdani.

"Mamdani tried to deliver a speech at a [New York Police](https://www.foxnews.com/category/us/new-york-city) event in Manhattan but was VICIOUSLY booed and jeered off stage," reacted conservative pundit Eric Daugherty on X. "He walked off in SHAME."

Mamdani has faced criticism over his relationship with police dating back to his mayoral campaign, which was dogged by questions about his past support for defunding the police. After his election, critics renewed those attacks when his budget included cuts to police funding and canceled [plans to hire 5,000 new NYPD officers](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/mamdani-proposes-cutting-nypd-budget-canceling-5k-new-officer-hires).

He also took criticism for his handling of an incident in February in Washington Square Park, dubbed "Snowballgate," where a mob of roughly 100 people pelted NYPD officers with snowballs, leaving two officers injured.

Rather than condemning the assault, Mamdani appeared to downplay the violence, referring to the perpetrators as "kids" taking part in a snowball fight.
