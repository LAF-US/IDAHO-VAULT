## Overview

**Blaine** (Japanese: カツラ *Katsura*) is the **Gym Leader of Cinnabar Island's Gym** (later relocated to the Seafoam Islands in Generations II and IV), known officially as the **Cinnabar Gym**. He specializes in **Fire-type Pokémon** and awards the **Volcano Badge** to trainers who defeat him in battle.

- **Full Name**: Blaine
- **Japanese Name**: Katsura (カツラ)
- **Role**: Gym Leader (Gen I, III, VII) / Seafoam Islands (Gen II, IV)
- **Specialty**: Fire-type Pokémon
- **Badge**: Volcano Badge
- **First Appearance**: Pokémon Red and Blue (Generation I)
- **Distinction**: Seventh Gym Leader players face in the Kanto region

## Game Appearances

### Generation I (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow)

- **Location**: Cinnabar Gym, Cinnabar Island
- **Team**:
  - Growlithe (Lv. 42) - Bite, Roar, Take Down, Fire Blast
  - Ponyta (Lv. 40) - Stomp, Growl, Fire Spin, Ember
  - Rapidash (Lv. 42) - Stomp, Growl, Fire Spin, Fire Blast
  - Arcanine (Lv. 47) - Bite, Roar, Take Down, Fire Blast
- **Reward**: Volcano Badge, TM38 (Fire Blast), 4,620 Poké Dollars
- **Notable**: Gym door locked; requires Secret Key from Pokémon Mansion

### Generation II (Gold, Silver, Crystal)

- **Location**: Seafoam Islands (Gym relocated after Cinnabar Island volcano eruption)
- **Team**:
  - Magcargo (Lv. 45) - Flamethrower, Rock Throw, Harden, Curse
  - Magmar (Lv. 45) - Flamethrower, Fire Punch, Confuse Ray, Smokescreen
  - Rapidash (Lv. 50) - Fire Blast, Fury Attack, Quick Attack, Agility
- **Reward**: Volcano Badge, TM38 (Fire Blast)
- **Notable**: Cinnabar Island destroyed by volcanic eruption; Gym moved to Seafoam Islands. Fields a new Kanto postgame team (with Gen II additions Magcargo/Magmar), not his Gen I lineup. TM50 Overheat did not exist in Generation II

### Generation III (FireRed, LeafGreen)

- **Location**: Cinnabar Gym, Cinnabar Island
- **Team**:
  - Growlithe (Lv. 42) - Bite, Roar, Take Down, Fire Blast
  - Ponyta (Lv. 40) - Stomp, Bounce, Fire Spin, Fire Blast
  - Rapidash (Lv. 42) - Stomp, Bounce, Fire Spin, Fire Blast
  - Arcanine (Lv. 47) - Bite, Roar, Take Down, Fire Blast
- **Reward**: Volcano Badge, TM38 (Fire Blast), 5,120 Poké Dollars
- **Notable**: Updated movesets with Bounce

### Generation IV (HeartGold, SoulSilver)

- **Location**: Seafoam Islands
- **Team**: Same as FireRed/LeafGreen
- **Reward**: Volcano Badge, TM50 (Overheat)
- **Notable**: Gym remains at Seafoam Islands

### Generation VII (Let's Go, Pikachu! and Eevee!)

- **Location**: Cinnabar Gym, Cinnabar Island
- **Team**:
  - Growlithe (Lv. 42)
  - Ponyta (Lv. 40)
  - Rapidash (Lv. 42)
  - Arcanine (Lv. 47)
- **Reward**: Volcano Badge, TM46 (Fire Blast)
- **Notable**: Secret Key no longer required to enter

### Other Appearances

- Pokémon Stadium 2: Gym Leader Castle

### Battle Strategy

Blaine's battle strategy is characterized by:

1. Fire-type STAB offense (Fire Blast, Fire Spin, Ember)
2. Physical attacks (Bite, Take Down, Stomp)
3. Status-inducing moves (Roar, Bounce)
4. High Attack stats across team
5. Intimidate ability on Growlithe/Arcanine (lowers opponent Attack)

### Recommended Counters

- Water-types: Super effective against Fire (Surf, Water Gun, Hydro Pump)
- Ground-types: Super effective against Fire (Earthquake, Dig)
- Rock-types: Super effective against Fire (Rock Slide, Rock Throw)
- Dragon-types: Resist Fire moves
- Flash Fire users: Absorb Fire attacks and boost own Fire moves

## Personality and Design

### Personality

- Highly intelligent: Creates challenging riddles and puzzles
- Patient: Willing to teach and guide trainers
- Protective: Deeply cares for his Pokémon and Gym
- Playful: Enjoys mental challenges
- Professional: Takes Gym Leader duties seriously

### Design Evolution

- Generation I & III: Older man with glasses, red outfit
- Generation II & IV: Similar design at Seafoam Islands
- Generation VII: Modernized appearance
- Consistent elements: Glasses, red/black color scheme
- Anime: Gray hair, glasses, red outfit

### Name Origin

- **English**: Blaine - from Old English "blæc" (black/dark) or possibly "blaze"
- **Japanese**: カツラ (Katsura) - from "katsura" (桂), a type of tree, or possibly related to "katsu" (勝, victory)
- Fits his Fire-type specialty and intelligent personality
