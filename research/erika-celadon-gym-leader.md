## Overview

**Erika** (Japanese: エリカ *Erika*) is the **Gym Leader of Celadon City's Gym**, known officially as the **Celadon Gym**. She specializes in **Grass-type Pokémon** and awards the **Rainbow Badge** to trainers who defeat her in battle.

- **Title**: The Nature-Loving Princess
- **Specialty**: Grass-type Pokémon
- **Badge**: Rainbow Badge
- **First Appearance**: Pokémon Red and Blue (Generation I)
- **Distinction**: Fourth Gym Leader players face in the Kanto region

## Game Appearances

### Generation I (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow)

- **Location**: Celadon Gym, Celadon City
- **Team**:
  - Victreebel (Lv. 29) - Razor Leaf, Wrap, Poison Powder, Sleep Powder
  - Tangela (Lv. 24) - Constrict, Bind
  - Vileplume (Lv. 29) - Petal Dance, Poison Powder, Mega Drain, Sleep Powder
- **Reward**: Rainbow Badge, TM21 (Mega Drain), 2,230 Poké Dollars
- **Notable**: Gym requires Cut to navigate; all trainers are female

### Generation II (Gold, Silver, Crystal)

- **Location**: Celadon Gym (Kanto postgame)
- **Team**:
  - Jumpluff (Lv. 42) - Mega Drain, Stun Spore, Sleep Powder, Leech Seed
  - Bellossom (Lv. 46) - Petal Dance, Giga Drain, Sweet Scent, Solar Beam
  - Tangela (Lv. 44) - Giga Drain, Stun Spore, Bind, Mega Drain
  - Victreebel (Lv. 46) - Razor Leaf, Acid, Sleep Powder, Giga Drain
- **Reward**: Rainbow Badge, TM19 (Giga Drain)
- **Notable**: Kanto postgame team with Generation II additions (Jumpluff, Bellossom), not her Gen I Victreebel/Tangela/Vileplume lineup. Can be challenged after obtaining 8 Johto badges

### Generation III (FireRed, LeafGreen)

- **Location**: Celadon Gym
- **Team**:
  - Victreebel (Lv. 29) - Stun Spore, Acid, Poison Powder, Giga Drain
  - Tangela (Lv. 24) - Poison Powder, Constrict, Ingrain, Giga Drain
  - Vileplume (Lv. 29) - Sleep Powder, Acid, Stun Spore, Giga Drain
- **Reward**: Rainbow Badge, TM19 (Giga Drain), 2,900 Poké Dollars
- **Notable**: Updated movesets with new moves like Giga Drain

### Generation IV (HeartGold, SoulSilver)

- **Location**: Celadon Gym
- **Team**: Same as FireRed/LeafGreen
- **Reward**: Rainbow Badge, TM19 (Giga Drain)
- **Notable**: Rematch available on Saturday/Sunday 3:00-5:00 PM by Celadon City fountain

### Generation VII (Let's Go, Pikachu! and Eevee!)

- **Location**: Celadon Gym
- **Team**:
  - Vileplume (Lv. 30)
  - Florgess (Lv. 30)
  - Bellossom (Lv. 30)
- **Reward**: Rainbow Badge, TM53 (Mega Drain)
- **Notable**: Updated team with different Grass-type Pokémon

### Other Appearances

- Pokémon Stadium: Uses Victreebel, Tangela, Vileplume
- Pokémon Stadium 2: Appearance in Gym Leader Castle
- Pokémon Puzzle League: Opponent in 1P and 2P Stadium modes
- Pokémon Masters EX: Sync pairs with Vileplume, Comfey, Tangela, Leafeon, Lurantis

### Battle Strategy

Erika's battle strategy is characterized by:

1. Status-inducing moves (Sleep Powder, Poison Powder, Stun Spore)
2. Grass-type STAB offense (Razor Leaf, Giga Drain, Mega Drain)
3. Defensive play with Tangela's Bind and Constrict
4. High-risk Petal Dance from Vileplume (multi-turn attack)

### Recommended Counters

- Fire-types: Super effective against Grass (Growlithe, Vulpix, Charizard)
- Flying-types: Super effective against Grass (Pidgeotto, Pidgeot, Butterfree)
- Ice-types: Super effective against Grass (Lapras, Jynx)
- Bug-types: Super effective against Grass (Butterfree, Beedrill)
- Poison-types: Resist Grass moves (but watch for Poison-type moves)
- Status prevention: Pokémon with Insomnia or Vital Spirit abilities

## Personality and Design

### Personality

- Graceful: Embodies traditional Japanese elegance
- Patient: Willing to teach and guide trainers
- Artistic: Student of flower arranging
- Protective: Deeply cares for her Pokémon
- Professional: Takes Gym Leader duties seriously

### Design Evolution

- Generation I & III: Traditional Japanese kimono (pink in RBY, yellow in FRLG/HGSS)
- Generation VII: Green kimono in Let's Go
- Consistent elements: Long black hair, red headband, obi (sash), zōri (sandals), tabi (socks)
- Anime: Royal blue hair, red headband

### Name Origin

- **English**: Erika - from "Erica," a genus of flowering plants
- **Japanese**: エリカ (Erika) - same as English, referencing flowers
- Fits the plant/flower naming theme of many Grass-type specialists

## Trivia

### Game Trivia

- Only female Gym Leader in Generation I Kanto
- Gym requires HM01 (Cut) to access central area
- All trainers in Celadon Gym are female
- Only Gym in Kanto with exclusively female trainers
- In Pokémon Yellow, overworld sprite has different hair color
- In Let's Go, Lass Kay requires showing a "cute Pokémon" to enter

### Anime Trivia

- First appearance in Episode 26
- Banned Ash for calling perfume "just a means of making guys fawn over girls"
- Team Rocket's fire destroyed Celadon Gym
- Only stolen item was Gloom extract (perfume ingredient)
- Rebuilt Gym appeared in later episodes
- Recognized Ash in "Suffering the Flings and Arrows!"

### Cultural Impact

- Represents traditional Japanese beauty and elegance
- Popular character in Pokémon merchandise
- Featured in Pokémon Masters EX with multiple sync pairs
- Appears in various spin-off games and media
