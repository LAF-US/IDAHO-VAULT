## Core Series Regions

These are the primary regions from the main series Pokémon games, each introducing new Pokémon, Gym Leaders, Elite Four, and Champions.

| # | Region | Generation | Debut Game(s) | Based On | Notes |
| --- | -------- | ------------ | -------------- | ---------- | ------- |
| 1 | **Kanto** | I | Red, Blue, Green (Japan), Yellow | Kantō, Japan | The original region. Features 8 Gyms. |
| 2 | **Johto** | II | Gold, Silver, Crystal | Kansai & traditional Japanese culture | West of Kanto. Features 8 Gyms. |
| 3 | **Hoenn** | III | Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald | Kyūshū, Japan | Southern region. Features 8 Gyms. |
| 4 | **Sinnoh** | IV | Diamond, Pearl, Platinum | Hokkaidō, Japan | Northern region. Features 8 Gyms. |
| 5 | **Unova** | V | Black, White, Black 2, White 2 | New York City, USA | Features 8 Gyms. Only region with seasonal cycles. |
| 6 | **Kalos** | VI | X, Y | France | Features 8 Gyms. Introduces Mega Evolution. |
| 7 | **Alola** | VII | Sun, Moon, Ultra Sun, Ultra Moon | Hawaii, USA | No traditional Gyms; uses Island Trials and Kahunas. |
| 8 | **Galar** | VIII | Sword, Shield | United Kingdom | Features 8 Gyms. Introduces Dynamaxing and the Wild Area. |
| 9 | **Paldea** | IX | Scarlet, Violet | Iberian Peninsula (Spain/Portugal) | Features 8 Gyms. Open-world design. Introduces Terastallization. |

## Notes on Epochs

Each core region appears across multiple **epochs** (generations, remakes, and sequels):

- **Kanto**: Gen I (RBGY), Gen III (FRLG), Gen VII (LGPE)
- **Johto**: Gen II (GSC), Gen IV (HGSS)
- **Hoenn**: Gen III (RSE), Gen VI (ORAS)
- **Sinnoh**: Gen IV (DPPt), Gen VIII (Legends: Arceus - as Hisui)
- **Unova**: Gen V (BW, B2W2)
- **Kalos**: Gen VI (XY)
- **Alola**: Gen VII (SM, USUM)
- **Galar**: Gen VIII (SwSh)
- **Paldea**: Gen IX (SV)

Each epoch may feature:

- Different Gym Leaders
- Updated teams and levels
- New story elements
- Graphical/technical changes
