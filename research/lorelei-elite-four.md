## Overview

**Lorelei** (Japanese: カナ *Kana*) is the **first member of the Kanto Elite Four**. She specializes in **Ice-type Pokémon** and is known for her logical, calculated, and cool style of battling.

- **Full Name**: Lorelei
- **Japanese Name**: Kana (カナ)
- **Anime Name**: Prima
- **Role**: Elite Four (first member)
- **Specialty**: Ice-type Pokémon (with Water-type secondary)
- **First Appearance**: Pokémon Red and Blue (1996-1998)
- **Distinction**: First Elite Four member trainers face in Kanto

## Game Appearances

### Generation I
- **Games**: Red, Blue, Green (JP), Yellow
- **Location**: Indigo Plateau (Chamber 1)
- **Team**: Dewgong (Lv. 52), Cloyster (Lv. 51), Slowbro (Lv. 52), Jynx (Lv. 54), Lapras (Lv. 54)
- **Reward**: None

### Generation II
- **Games**: Gold, Silver, Crystal
- **Status**: Absent. Lorelei does **not** appear in the Generation II Elite Four. The Indigo Plateau lineup in Gold/Silver/Crystal is Will, Koga, Bruno, and Karen; Will (Psychic-type specialist) takes the first Elite Four position Lorelei held in Generation I. There is no Generation II Lorelei battle.

### Generation III (Remakes)
- **Games**: FireRed, LeafGreen
- **Team**: Dewgong (Lv. 52), Cloyster (Lv. 51), Slowbro (Lv. 52), Jynx (Lv. 54), Lapras (Lv. 54)
- **Reward**: 5,400 Poké Dollars
- **Notable**: Uses Hail, Safeguard, Spikes

### Generation IV (Remakes)
- **Games**: HeartGold, SoulSilver
- **Team**: Same as FRLG
- **Reward**: 5,400 Poké Dollars

### Generation VII (Reimagining)
- **Games**: Let's Go, Pikachu!, Let's Go, Eevee!
- **Team**: Same as Gen I
- **Location**: Indigo Plateau

### Signature Pokémon
- Lapras (childhood companion)
- Dewgong (lead, weather effects)
- Cloyster (defensive, Spikes)
- Slowbro (only non-Ice type)
- Jynx (Ice/Psychic, status moves)

### Strategy
- Ice-type offense
- Water-type support
- Weather effects (Hail)
- Status moves (Safeguard, Protect, Amnesia, Yawn)
- Defensive play

### Counters
- Electric-types (4/5 are Water)
- Grass-types
- Fighting-types
- Rock-types (watch for Water moves)
- Steel-types
