# House Precedence

**Plain words first:** this is *reference lore*, entered at Logan's direction ([FEAT] issue #664). It records the precedence order of the House in Garth Nix's *The Keys to the Kingdom* series (2003–2010). It does **not** create, rank, or seat any vault office, agent, or persona. Per [[CONSTITUTION]] § I, offices are appointments, not inheritances — a rank in a novel's register is not a rank in this vault. No mapping from House Precedence to vault agents or offices exists; if one is ever wanted, that is Logan's to declare (`*`).

## What it is

House **Precedence** is the rank an inhabitant holds inside the House — and the House specifically; an inhabitant's precedence may change in the Secondary Realms. The names of all, or at least most, House inhabitants are inscribed in the *Register of Precedence*.

Source: [House Precedence — Keys to the Kingdom wiki](https://keystothekingdom.fandom.com/wiki/House_Precedence), as cited by Logan in issue #664.

## Vault context

- **The House** is already a vault term: [[CONSTITUTION]] § I defines it as the Living Structure of the Vault and the Architecture & Infrastructure of the Union Sovereign's work in service of The Architect's Will and Vision. This note records the literary register behind that language.
- **The Architect** stands first in the register above; the vault's own usage (e.g. `VAULT-CONVENTIONS.md` § "Blessed Working Surfaces") echoes the same figure.
- Scope is held deliberately narrow, per the pattern of [[WITNESS-WIZARDS-RULES-INCORPORATED-2026-06-21]]: this note records the source as reference. It ratifies no downstream gloss, and it seats no one.

### Prior attempted registers

Logan notes (2026-07-17, in-session, following up issue #664) that prior vault artifacts **attempted to be such a device** — vault-side analogs of the *Register of Precedence*, inscribing the names of the House's inhabitants:

- **`swarm.json`** — self-described "machine-readable structured registry of all swarm elements" (`registry_version: 2026-06-10, durable registration, no liveness inference`). It inscribes names, lineages, dotfolders, capability tiers, and labels — but each entry expressly asserts *no* office occupancy ("Specific offices and duties require Logan appointment"). A register of names, not of precedence.
- **`!/agents.json`** — treated by [[!README]] as a *generated discovery index, not executable startup*; it did not exist when probed on 2026-07-17 (`*`).
- **`AGENTS.md`** and the referenced `!/AGENTS.md` — the human-readable registry surfaces; the latter likewise absent when probed on 2026-07-17 (`*`).

The distinction these attempts kept, and this note keeps: a register may inscribe *names*; **precedence and office flow only from Logan's appointment** ([[CONSTITUTION]] § I, Identity Decoupling in `VAULT-CONVENTIONS.md`). None of these artifacts — nor this note — ranks the inhabitants.
