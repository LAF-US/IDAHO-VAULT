Album caption and index card: Topographical work. Chittenden and Holmes. 1874.

General collection album caption: Topographic station on a high point of the Wyoming Bad Lands. Jackson no. 1112, large & small negs.

Correlation List of New and Old Numbers of Descriptive Catalog of the Photographs of the United States Geological Survey of the Territories, “8x10 enlargement of new number 1112.” Refer to jwh01112.

Typed information on back of duplicate photograph: Hayden Survey topographic activity. Topographer is George B. Chiddenden and W.H. Holmes is sketching. Photo by W.H. Jackson. ca. 1874. Credit:  USGS (Neg. No. 1112a in the USGS Photographic Collection and National Archives).

Handwritten information on back of duplicate photograph:  There is a similar water color by W.H. Holmes.  He omits himself, with his feet dangling over the rocks and substitutes Gardiner acting as recorder in the USGS Reston Library Holmes sketchbooks.  He changes the name Gardiner to W.H. Holmes in a duplicate water color in the National Collection of Fine Arts.  W.H. Holmes was the first director, but the figure is still Gardiner not Holmes. N. Carico 6/14/74.
