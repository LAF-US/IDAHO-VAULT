# Mormonism

**Mormonism** — the religious tradition founded by **Joseph Smith** and centered
today on the **Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (LDS Church)** — earns
its place in this cluster through one specific, well-documented channel: its
**temple endowment ceremony**, whose ritual grammar of **tokens, signs, words,
and aprons** is closely parallel to that of [[Freemasonry]]. Of all the branches
off the Masonic template, this is the most direct transmission of the **coded
fraternal language** into a religion. This node focuses on that symbolic and
ritual dimension; it is not a complete account of Latter-day Saint theology or
history.

This is an original synthesis grounded in the sources listed at the end,
written with care for a living faith tradition, and is a synthesis rather than a
verbatim copy.

## The documented timeline

The connection is unusually well dated:

- **March 15–16, 1842** — Joseph Smith was initiated as a **Freemason** in the
  newly chartered **Nauvoo Lodge** in Illinois, advancing through the degrees
  rapidly.
- **May 4, 1842** — within weeks, Smith introduced the **endowment** ordinance
  to a small group of Latter-day Saint men, several of them fellow Masons.

The temporal proximity is the heart of the historical discussion: the endowment
emerged in its developed form immediately after Smith's Masonic initiation.

## The shared ritual language

The endowment and the Masonic degrees share a striking set of formal elements —
the same "the gesture is the message" structure that defines [[Freemasonry]]:

- **Tokens** (ritual handclasps), **signs** (gestures), and **words**
  (passwords) conferred in stages;
- **Penalties** attached to the obligations of secrecy (the explicit penalties
  were **removed from the LDS ceremony in 1990**);
- A ritual **raising of the arms**;
- An **apron** worn during portions of the ceremony;
- The **square and the compass** marked on the **temple garment**, the
  emblematic marks most plainly shared with Masonry.

The structural resemblance — graded initiation, secret recognition-tokens,
obligations — is close enough that historians broadly regard the endowment as
having **drawn on the Masonic ritual Smith had just received**, adapting it to
an entirely new theological purpose (eternal covenants, the plan of salvation,
progression toward exaltation) rather than borrowing its meaning.

## Two readings

As with every node in this cluster, the evidence supports a careful split
between fact and interpretation — and here two good-faith readings coexist:

- **The historical reading.** The forms were adapted from Freemasonry; the
  timing and the specificity of the parallels (tokens, signs, penalties,
  aprons, the square and compass) make independent invention implausible.
- **The faithful reading.** Joseph Smith and early Latter-day Saint leaders held
  that the endowment and Masonry flowed in part **"from the same ancient
  spring"** — that Masonry preserved, in corrupted form, fragments of an
  original temple ordinance that the Restoration was recovering whole. On this
  view the resemblance is not borrowing but **common descent** from an ancient
  original.

This is the cluster's recurring shape — a venerable-origin claim set against a
documentary record — handled here with the respect a living tradition is owed:
the parallels are factual; their *ultimate meaning* is a matter of faith on
which this note takes no side.

## Relationship to the cluster

Mormonism is a **branch off [[Freemasonry]]**, a sibling of
[[Freemasonry and American politics]] and [[Collegiate Greek life]] — the three
American inheritors of the Masonic fraternal form. Its distinctive contribution
is to show the template crossing fully into **religious ritual**: the coded
language of grip, sign, and emblem becomes the vehicle of sacred covenant. It is
the cluster's clearest instance of a *symbolic recognition-system* being carried
intact from a fraternal order into a faith.

## Why this is in the vault

The endowment is a ritual symbolic language — meaning carried through tokens,
signs, words, and emblems rather than plain statement — and its documented
relationship to [[Freemasonry]] is a clean case of how such coded systems
transmit and transform, which is the vault's *per sona* concern (see
[[GRIMOIRE]] and `VAULT-CONVENTIONS.md`). It is filed here as cultural and
historical reference, handled respectfully, on the record per
[[VAULT-CONVENTIONS]].
