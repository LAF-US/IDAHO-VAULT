**Randy Lee Davison** (born (1971-05-17)May 17, 1971) is an American actor who appeared in the films *[The United States vs. Billie Holiday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_United_States_vs._Billie_Holiday "The United States vs. Billie Holiday")* (2021) as [Joseph McCarthy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_McCarthy "Joseph McCarthy"), *[Mank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mank "Mank")* (2020), *[Hemet, or the Landlady Don't Drink Tea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hemet,_or_the_Landlady_Don't_Drink_Tea "Hemet, or the Landlady Don't Drink Tea")* (2023), *[Not This Part of the World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Not_This_Part_of_the_World "Not This Part of the World")* (1995), and *[Touch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Touch_\(2022_film\) "Touch (2022 film)")* (2022). In the 1990s, Davison appeared in the television show *[America's Funniest People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/America's_Funniest_People "America's Funniest People")* as [Edith Bunker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edith_Bunker "Edith Bunker") and as [Senex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senex "Senex") in [Boise State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise_State_University "Boise State University") 's production of *[A Funny Thing Happened on the Way to the Forum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Funny_Thing_Happened_on_the_Way_to_the_Forum "A Funny Thing Happened on the Way to the Forum")*.

## Early life

Davison graduated from [Capital High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_High_School_\(Boise,_Idaho\) "Capital High School (Boise, Idaho)") and is a theatre major out of [Boise State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise_State_University "Boise State University").[^1] In 1992, Davison appeared on *[America's Funniest People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/America's_Funniest_People "America's Funniest People")* as [Edith Bunker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edith_Bunker "Edith Bunker") and as [Popeye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popeye "Popeye") being [Elvis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elvis_Presley "Elvis Presley").[^2] The following year, he participated in the [Irene Ryan Acting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irene_Ryan "Irene Ryan") competition at the [Kennedy Center American College Theatre Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kennedy_Center_American_College_Theater_Festival "Kennedy Center American College Theater Festival") regional conference for his monologue in the play *The Four Bagger*.[^3] In 1994, Davison starred as Phil in *[Boys' Life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boy's_Life_\(play\) "Boy's Life (play)")* at BSU,[^4] tied for top honors at the Student Programs Board's stand-up comedy competition *Just Another One Night Stand.*[^5]

## Career

Davison's journey into acting was inspired during his grade school years when he saw a boy playing a bear in the youth production of *[The Jungle Book](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Jungle_Book "The Jungle Book")* [^6] and catching a performance of *[The Tempest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Tempest "The Tempest")* in 7th grade.[^7]

Early in his career, Davison co-starred in the film *[Not This Part of the World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Not_This_Part_of_the_World "Not This Part of the World")*.[^8] Before joining [SAG-AFTRA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAG-AFTRA "SAG-AFTRA"), his work experience came from being an extra on movies sets and doing voice-over projects. Davison relocated to [San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Diego "San Diego") after booking a commercial for [Southwest Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest_Airlines "Southwest Airlines").[^7] His acting career set him up with work at [UC San Diego School of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UC_San_Diego_School_of_Medicine "UC San Diego School of Medicine").[^9] Davison co-starred with [Jayma Mays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jayma_Mays "Jayma Mays") in a production of *[The Ruling Class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ruling_Class_\(play\) "The Ruling Class (play)")* at The Knightsbridge Theatre in Los Angeles, California.[^10]

In 2017, he contributed his voice to *Uncharted Regions*, a [radio drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_drama "Radio drama") by [Neal Hallford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neal_Hallford "Neal Hallford") [^11] featuring [Gigi Edgely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gigi_Edgley "Gigi Edgley"), [John Billingsley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Billingsley "John Billingsley") and [Larry Nemeck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Nemecek "Larry Nemecek").[^12] Davison worked on a film called *[Carbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbon_\(2019_film\) "Carbon (2019 film)")* in 2018, shot in San Diego.[^13] In the 2020s, he portrayed the *[maitre d'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maitre_d "Maitre d'")* of the [Cafe Trocadero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trocadero_\(Los_Angeles\) "Trocadero (Los Angeles)") in *[Mank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mank "Mank")*, [Joseph McCarthy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_McCarthy "Joseph McCarthy") in *[The United States vs. Billie Holiday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_United_States_vs._Billie_Holiday "The United States vs. Billie Holiday")*,[^1] a sheriff in *[Hemet, or the Landlady Don't Drink Tea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hemet,_or_the_Landlady_Don't_Drink_Tea "Hemet, or the Landlady Don't Drink Tea")*,[^14] and acted in *[Touch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Touch_\(2022_film\) "Touch (2022 film)")*.[^9] Davison was one of the last people photographed with [Tom Sizemore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Sizemore "Tom Sizemore") before his death in 2023.[^15] He was nominated at the [Pacific Southwest Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Southwest_Emmy_Awards "Pacific Southwest Emmy Awards") for hosting the 2024 San Diego Film Awards.[^16]
