For $950,000 you or someone you know could be the owner of 45 acres and a former mushroom farm.

[According to a property listing from Colliers](https://www.colliers.com/en/properties/vale-mushroom-farm-foreclosed-commercial-property-for-sale/usa-1000-washington-st-e-vale-or-97918-usa/usa1127178), two properties that housed a mushroom farm in Vale, Oregon, are for sale. Oregon Trail Mushrooms formerly operated the mushroom farm, but the farming activity has since ceased, and the property went into foreclosure. The property has been abandoned for over 15 years.

![](https://boisedev.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/mushroom-farm-for-sale-Screenshot-2026-07-3-2.jpeg)

A mushroom farm in Vale, Oregon is for sale. Photo: Courtesy Colliers

[According to the Associated Press,](https://apnews.com/general-news-4ba0fbf8c4b440159888f730d1d62380) the mushroom farm’s property was considered by a Canada-based mushroom farm in 2017 as a potential site for its first U.S. farm, but ultimately it did not meet the company’s needs.

Baraboo - Aug/Sep - 2:1

The listing is split between a 29-acre parcel on Fulleton Rd. and an approximately 16-acre parcel on Washington St. The flyer says the acreages are estimates because the recorded acreages differ from parcel measurements.

![](https://boisedev.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/mushroom-farm-for-sale-Screenshot-2026-07-3-5.jpeg)

A mushroom farm in Vale, Oregon is for sale. Photo: Courtesy Colliers

There is also a geothermal well on the Washington St. property.

There are five homes on the Washington St. property that are abandoned and vandalized. According to a 2004 [Oregon Institute of Technology article](https://techcommons.oit.edu/record/6650?p=keyword%3A%22VALE%22&v=pdf) on the mushroom farm’s use of geothermal technology, the homes were abandoned after Oregon Trail obtained its water rights when well water levels and temperatures declined after the mushroom farm started up.

The Fulleton property features 165,000 square feet of office and warehouse space, according to the property listing. The listing says the building has been “inspected by a general contractor, demolition contractor, and mechanical engineer who can provide further information on the current condition of the building and equipment.”

The Fulleton property also has a large concrete collection pad, and grain storage areas.
