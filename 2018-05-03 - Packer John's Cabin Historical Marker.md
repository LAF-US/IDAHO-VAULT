New Meadows in Adams County, Idaho — *The American West (Mountains)*

## Packer John's Cabin

Inscription.  
One fourth mile north. Built 1862. First Democratic Territorial Convention held in Cabin Fall of 1863  
  
Erected 1936 by Sons and Daughters of Idaho Pioneers.  
  
Topics and series. This historical marker is listed in this topic list: [Government & Politics](https://www.hmdb.org/Results.asp?Search=Topic&CategoryID=67). In addition, it is included in the [Sons and Daughters of Idaho Pioneers](https://www.hmdb.org/results.asp?Search=Series&SeriesID=424) series list. A significant historical year for this entry is 1862.  
  
Location. 44° 57.348′ N, 116° 13.476′ W. Marker is in New Meadows, Idaho, in Adams County. It is at the intersection of State Highway 55 at milepost 155 and Packer John Road on State Highway 55. [Touch for map](https://www.hmdb.org/map.asp?markers=119372,37957,110379,110416,119494,119491,109934).
