# Grace (Sword of Truth)

## Summary

A Grace is a magic symbol from Terry Goodkind's *Sword of Truth* representing all of existence. It is built from four nested/overlaid elements — an outer circle, a square, an inner circle, and an eight-pointed star — each drawn in a fixed order that itself carries meaning, from the boundary of the underworld inward to the Creator's light.

## Key Facts

**Geometry:**

- A small circle sits inside a square, with one point of the circle touching the midpoint of one side of the square.
- The square is inscribed inside a larger circle; each of the square's four corners touches that larger circle.
- Inside the smaller circle is an eight-pointed star.
- Lines radiate outward from each of the star's eight points, piercing through both circles and the square. Each line bisects one of the square's four corners as it passes through.

**Drawing order and meaning** (a Grace is always drawn in this sequence):

1. **Outer circle, first** — represents the beginning of the underworld, beyond which is nothing. Starting here symbolizes creation: beginning from nothing.
2. **Square, second** — represents the Veil, the boundary between the world of the living and the world of the dead.
3. **Inner circle, third** — represents the world of the living.
4. **Eight-pointed star, last** — represents the light of the Creator. The lines radiating from the star through the rest of the Grace represent that light transcending all boundaries.

**Status in the source material:** described as a very powerful and very common magic symbol within the Sword of Truth setting.
