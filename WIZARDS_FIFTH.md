# Wizard's Fifth Rule

## Summary

The fifth of the numbered Wizard's Rules stated across Terry Goodkind's *Sword of Truth* series, one per book. The Fifth Rule concerns judging by actions rather than words. This is also the book (*Soul of the Fire*) in which the Fatal Grace spell-form is used — see `Grace (Sword of Truth).md`.

## Key Facts

**Source book:** *Soul of the Fire* (book 5)

**Text:**

> Mind what people do, not only what they say, for deeds will betray a lie.
