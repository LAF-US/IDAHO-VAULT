# Old loganfinney.com Design (April 2026 Archive)

## Key Design Elements

### Hero Section
- **Background**: Full-width hero image (Idaho Capitol building interior with people)
- **Text overlay**: White text on dark/semi-transparent background
- **Headline**: "IDAHO MULTIMEDIA JOURNALIST" - bold, large, white text
- **Subheading**: Professional bio text in white
- **Tagline**: "The world is quiet here." (italicized, green accent)

### Navigation & Buttons
- **Primary buttons**: White background with dark text (RESUME, WORK)
- **Accent color**: Bright lime/neon green (#00FF00 or similar)
- **Navigation style**: Clean, minimal button-based navigation

### Color Scheme
- **Primary**: White text on dark backgrounds
- **Accent**: Bright lime green
- **Background**: Dark/image-based hero
- **Overall**: High contrast, bold

### Typography
- Bold, sans-serif headlines
- Professional, clean layout
- Minimal text, maximum impact

### Overall Aesthetic
- Professional and bold
- Editorial/journalistic feel
- Image-driven hero
- Minimal text, maximum visual impact
- Green accent color (similar to The Gemstone but green instead of blue)
- Clean, organized button-based navigation
