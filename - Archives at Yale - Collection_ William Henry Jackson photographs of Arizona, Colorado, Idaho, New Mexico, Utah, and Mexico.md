## Abstract

Photographs taken by William Henry Jackson and published by W.H. Jackson & Co. of Arizona, Colorado, [[Idaho]], New Mexico, Utah, and Mexico, circa 1880s.

## Bibliography

William Henry Jackson (1843-1943), American photographer, opened a studio in Denver, Colorado, in 1879. He incorporated himself as W.H. Jackson Photograph and Publishing Company, Inc. in 1883.
