## Idaho Republicans advance stricter immigration laws

(Photo courtesy of the Idaho Legislature)

In Idaho, a group of Republican lawmakers are pushing for stricter immigration laws, even as some national leaders suggest the party take a softer approach to avoid alienating voters in the midterm elections.

State Rep. Dale Hawkins (R-Fernwood) revealed a list of goals for the 2026 legislative session along with other lawmakers. One major plan is to encourage local police to sign special agreements with federal agents to help enforce immigration laws. Senator Brian Lenney (R-Nampa) and others at the meeting argued that immigration is “damaging our way of life” and even mentioned the theory of “demographic replacement,” which suggests local populations are being pushed out by newcomers, according to [*Idaho Statesman.*](https://www.idahostatesman.com/news/politics-government/state-politics/article314581701.html)

They also want to conduct audits to see how many illegal immigrants are in Idaho’s jails and schools, and they are trying to bring back a bill that would make it a crime to hide or shelter illegal immigrants. A similar bill stalled last year after local sheriffs and church leaders argued it would do more harm than good.
