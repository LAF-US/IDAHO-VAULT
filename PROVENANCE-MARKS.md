**Provenance marks** — every claim carries the source-class it flows from:

- `[handed]` — Logan's given words, carved as spoken. The highest authority present in a note.
- `[read]` — a vault surface read at a cited head or date; consistency is not provenance — cite the surface.
- `[verified]` — an external source fetched and checked on a stated date.
- `[witnessed]` — the writer's own recorded conduct, offered as factual record under the Repair axis, not as rhetoric.
- `[lore]` — recounted from memory of a tradition or canon, not from a fetched text; shape sound, wording not quotable.
- `[reading]` — the writer's interpretation; ruled by no one unless Logan adopts it.
- `[inferred]` — a welding of sources into a claim; offered as proposal, never declared as canon.
- `*` — provenance absent or position ungranted; the honest mark, available and to be used.
