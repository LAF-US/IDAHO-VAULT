# Wizard's Fourth Rule

## Summary

The fourth of the numbered Wizard's Rules stated across Terry Goodkind's *Sword of Truth* series, one per book. The Fourth Rule concerns the power of sincere forgiveness.

## Key Facts

**Source book:** *Temple of the Winds* (book 4)

**Text:**

> There is magic in sincere forgiveness; in the forgiveness you give, but more so in the forgiveness you receive.
