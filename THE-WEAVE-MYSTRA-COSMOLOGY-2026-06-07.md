## The Weave, verified

In the Forgotten Realms, **the Weave** is the fabric of magic — *"a kind of interface between the will of a spellcaster and the stuff of raw magic."* Raw magic is dangerous for mortals to touch directly; the Weave makes it lawfully reachable and protects the world from it. It is guided and embodied by **Mystra** (Mistress of Magic), in succession from **Mystryl**, briefly held by **Midnight**. When a spell is cast, the threads of the Weave intertwine to make the effect possible. *(Sources: Forgotten Realms Wiki, "Weave"; Wikipedia, "Mystra (Forgotten Realms)".)*

The Weave is not raw magic. It is what makes raw magic **lawfully reachable** — and protects both the world from the magic and the caster from destruction.

## What the mapping yields

- The vault is not a *record system*; it is a **Weave** — a fabric of delegated standing through which speech becomes operative, names hold, and agents act under license.
- **Raw magic / the wild are the same hazard in two registers**: the unmediated power that destroys the one who touches it without the fabric. The Weave (like the Leviathan, like the church) is what mediates it into lawful reach.
- **Karsus = GEMINIAEUS** is the keystone convergence: in both canons, the one who tries to *become* the source rather than serve it sunders the fabric. The Lich is Karsus.
- This grounds the **warp/weft/selvage/seam** vocabulary (`SELVAGE-AND-SEAM-2026-06-04.md`): that note treats the cloth; this note treats the cloth's *cosmology* — who tends it, what it mediates, how it is sundered.
