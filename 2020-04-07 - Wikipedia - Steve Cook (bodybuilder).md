**Steven Cook** [^3] (born December 10, 1984),[^4] known professionally as **Steve Cook**, is an American professional [bodybuilder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bodybuilder "Bodybuilder") and two-time [Mr. Olympia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mr._Olympia "Mr. Olympia") top ten finisher.[^5]

He is also known for his work on [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube "YouTube"). In July 2020, his channel had 1.3 million subscribers.

## Early life

Cook was born on December 10, 1984. He was raised in [Boise, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise,_Idaho "Boise, Idaho"), where his father worked as a high school athletic director. He grew up doing various sports, including wrestling.[^6]

Cook attended Dixie State University, now named [Utah Tech University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_Tech_University "Utah Tech University"), where he was a [linebacker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linebacker "Linebacker") for the [NCAA Division II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCAA_Division_II "NCAA Division II") [Dixie State Trailblazers football team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_Tech_Trailblazers_football "Utah Tech Trailblazers football").[^7] [^8] [^9] He completed his bachelor of science in Integrated Studies—biology/psychology.[^4]

## Career

After college, Cook worked as a waiter at [Texas Roadhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Roadhouse "Texas Roadhouse").[^10]

He got his start in professional bodybuilding at a *[Muscle & Fitness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscle_&_Fitness "Muscle & Fitness")* event during Mr. Olympia weekend in 2010. After winning the *M&F* Male Model Search, he soon became a bodybuilding.com and Optimum Nutrition spokesmodel.[^10]

He took part in the Gold's Classic Treasure Valley Natural Bodybuilding Championship, and won. He has won the Fit Body Competition, which Bodybuilding.com organizes. A year later in 2011, Cook also won the Naturally Bodybuilding Championship hosted by the NPC Iron Man magazine. He has a title from a NPC Junior National Championship.

Cook has won the IFBB Houston Pro [^11] twice, in 2012 and 2014.

Cook is a co-owner of the fitness app Fitness Culture and gym of the same name in [St. George, Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._George,_Utah "St. George, Utah").[^12] [^7]

Cook was a coach on [season 18 of *The Biggest Loser*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Biggest_Loser_\(season_18\) "The Biggest Loser (season 18)") on [USA Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USA_Network "USA Network").[^10]

- 2010 ABFF 5th Annual Golds Classic Treasure Valley Natural Bodybuilding, Fitness, Figure, and Bikini Championships 1st
- 2010 Bodybuilding.com Fit Body Competition 1st [^4]
- 2011 NPC Iron Man Magazine Naturally Bodybuilding, Figure and Bikini Championships 1st [^4]
- 2011 NPC Junior USA Championships 3rd [^4]
- 2011 NPC Junior National Championships 1st [^4]
- 2012 IFBB Houston Pro 1st [^4]
- 2013 IFBB Olympia Weekend 8th [^13]
- 2014 IFBB Dallas Pro 1st
- 2014 IFBB Olympia Weekend 5th [^14]

## Personal life

Cook got married at 21 and divorced at 23.[^6]

In 2021, Cook proposed to fellow Gymshark athlete, Australian Morgan Rose-Moroney, after having been dating since late 2019. They have been married since May 2022.[^16]
