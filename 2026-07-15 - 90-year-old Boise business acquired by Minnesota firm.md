A Minnesota company has acquired a 90-year-old Boise business.

Loffler Companies of St. Louis Park, Minnesota, purchased Fisher’s Technologies of Garden City, the companies announced. Terms of the deal were not disclosed.

Fisher’s Office Equipment first opened in Downtown Boise with an office on 9th St., later moving to the Union Block building on Idaho St. in the 1940s. Tom Fisher started the business and started selling typewriters. The business expanded and offered a wide array of office equipment and furnishings.

The business expanded over the decades and has had business in a variety of office-related industries. In 1963, Fisher’s marked 25 years in business, and told the Idaho Statesman he had a new way to serve customers. Document shredding.

“The idea for this came to me one day when I was going down the street and found a check which was torn but had the signature intact and could have been a forgery,” Fisher told the paper. “And many times, I have seen discarded records blowing up and down alleys.”

![](https://boisedev.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/90-year-old-boise-business-acquired-by-minnesota-firm-Screenshot-2026-07-1.png)

A 1950’s ad for Fisher’s Office Equipment. Screenshot: Via Newsbank

It was one of many pivots Fisher’s made over the years, from typewriters to office furniture to stationery to document printing and more. In 1985, Gary Mahn bought Fisher’s and pushed into copier and printer sales and service. In 1990, Fisher’s moved back to 9th Street with a large store. By 1995, Boise Cascade had purchased Fisher’s office products division, and the company focused more and more on technology. Mahn sold a controlling stake in the business to three Fisher’s executives, Chris Taylor, Eric Strand, and JT Jones, in 2012, and the three remain listed as principals according to the Idaho Secretary of State. Mahn [died in 2025](https://www.fisherstech.com/gary-mahn-tribute/).

Today, the company is known as Fisher’s Technology and helps businesses manage their information technology and printing solutions, operating across a four-state region of Idaho, Montana, Washington, and Utah.

Fisher’s has acquired a string of businesses in the last 15 years across the region.

Loffler started in a Minneapolis suburb in 1986, and says it will continue to operate Fisher’s under its current branding.

“This partnership is especially meaningful because Fisher’s and Loffler have built a trusted friendship over more than 20 years,” Loffler President and CEO James Loffler said. “Both organizations have built their success by investing in their people, helping customers achieve their goals, and making a positive impact in the communities they serve. We are far more alike than we are different.”

Taylor said that in looking for a buyer, they wanted to find a strong fit. Fisher’s will continue to be led by current employee Ty Grigsby.

“After building Fisher’s for decades, it was incredibly important to find a partner who values our people, our customers, and our culture as much as we do,” Taylor said. “With Ty’s continued leadership and Loffler’s shared values, this merger creates exciting opportunities for our employees and brings even more capability and value to the customers and communities we collectively serve.”
