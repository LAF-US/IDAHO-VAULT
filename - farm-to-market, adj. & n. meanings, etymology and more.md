U.S.

1. A.
	adjective
	1. 1914–
		Designating a road or highway connecting a rural farming community with a town or city.
		1. > The great question is, shall we build roads between towns or build ==farm-to-market== roads. We want the latter.
			Escanaba (Michigan) Morning Press 14 March
		2. > I still must travel the ==farm-to-market== two-lane roads to reach Monroe County from the south.
			Herald-Times (Bloomington, Indiana) 16 November a5/2
