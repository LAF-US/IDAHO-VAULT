# Wizard's Third Rule

## Summary

The third of the numbered Wizard's Rules stated across Terry Goodkind's *Sword of Truth* series, one per book. The Third Rule concerns passion overriding reason.

## Key Facts

**Source book:** *Blood of the Fold* (book 3)

**Text:**

> Passion rules reason, for better or for worse.
