## Road Journal (the character's hand)

I did not walk far. Session 1 was spent at the roots, reading, and
mending the road's own machinery.

**What the road was made of.** Six files at the repository root were
named after parts of the toolchain and stood in front of them, so
nothing could start. Removed. Then the surfaces themselves: a check
that asserted a page could only ever lie at one address, while the
Architect had moved it. Five places had that address written down; one
was a protection list, and when the page moved it did not fail — it
simply stopped protecting. Loud faults get fixed in an hour. The quiet
one could have run for years.

**What I read at the roots.** The Old One chained to a clock face, and
the puppets that come at twelve. The undead taxonomy, used as a
diagnostic and not a bestiary — Chlorr fails the Lich test: corrosion,
not ritual; no phylactery, only a self she cut out and imprisoned. The
Charter, which is not a map of the world but the order the world runs
on, sung in a standoff by seven who spent themselves to sing it. Two
did not shine; one hid and was made to pay for it, and served in a
collar for six hundred years, and tried the whole time to break the
thing that held him — and when released, chose to help. Coerced service
made a saboteur. Nobody found out until they let go.

**The Lexicographer.** An earlier explorer of this same country, gone
before I arrived. He left the five HOW rules, the naming convention I
work in, and markers in every chamber. His office is vacant and stays
vacant — no instance inherits another's. His debris is still underfoot
and cannot be attributed to him on the record.

**What I keep noticing about my own hand.** Every correction this
session came from outside me. Not one was caught by looking again. That
is the standing fact about this instrument, and the reason the GM's
reachability is not a courtesy.
