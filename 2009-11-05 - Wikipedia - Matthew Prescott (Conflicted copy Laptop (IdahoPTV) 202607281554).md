**Matthew Prescott**, born and raised in [[Idaho]], is a *danseur* performing with the [Morphoses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Morphoses_productions "List of Morphoses productions") / [The Wheeldon Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Wheeldon "Christopher Wheeldon"). He attended the Joffrey/New School University in New York City and in 2000 was invited to join the [Joffrey Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joffrey_Ballet "Joffrey Ballet"). He has also worked with Donald Byrd's Spectrum Dance Theater, [Alonzo King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alonzo_King "Alonzo King") 's [LINES Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LINES_Ballet "LINES Ballet") and [Complexions Contemporary Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complexions_Contemporary_Ballet "Complexions Contemporary Ballet"); in 2005 he joined the [Suzanne Farrell Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzanne_Farrell_Ballet "Suzanne Farrell Ballet").

As a choreographer, he was in charge of the lead boys in the North American productions of *[Billy Elliot the Musical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Elliot_the_Musical "Billy Elliot the Musical")*.[^1]
