TWIN FALLS, Idaho — An off-duty Idaho State Police trooper and an armed citizen exchanged gunfire with the man [who opened fire Saturday at](https://www.ktvb.com/article/news/local/twin-falls-idaho-mass-shooting-in-n-out-burger-visitors-center-investigation/277-0311b78f-1c41-4b8c-9eb3-90c3c39f229f) a Twin Falls In-N-Out Burger, reportedly driving him from the scene and helping prevent additional casualties, authorities said in a Sunday press conference..

The suspect, identified as 24-year-old Chad Williams, later died by a self-inflicted gunshot wound, police chief Matthew Hicks confirmed.

Hicks says the Williams family was notified and are working with law enforcement to identify a motive.

Multiple reports misidentified another Twin Falls man as the suspect, but police clarified Williams is the only suspect at this time and it's believed he acted alone.

Three people were killed and at least seven others were injured in the shooting at the newly opened In-N-Out Burger on Blue Lakes Boulevard North. The gunman also fired near the neighboring Twin Falls Visitor Center.

"I want to extend my deepest thanks to the off-duty trooper and the good citizen that engaged the shooter," Twin Falls County Sheriff Jack Johnson said. "These people are true heroes in our community, and their actions diverted the shooter and no doubt saved many lives."

The identities of the deceased have not been released yet. We do know an unnamed employee of the restaurant was among those killed, according to In-N-Out owner Lynsi Snyder.

Of the seven injured in the shooting, two people have been treated and released from the hospital. Three others are in what officials described as "stable" condition and two more are in critical condition.

One of the injured was identified as Terry Dudley, a driver with Northstar Taxi, according to co-workers and [a social media post](https://www.facebook.com/permalink.php?story_fbid=pfbid024p64nK4qN9R3xebWW1d7MqTGaGWhbPTTFNmbZQXDQKPFDwvLoMGuaYEZRragG7pQl&id=61582280015137) shared by the company. Dudley was hospitalized after he was shot twice while charging his Tesla, a representative of the taxi company told KTVB.

A second officer-involved shooting also happened during the response, which is now being reviewed by Jerome Police Department. Details on this incident were limited by officials.

Reports of [another shooting in Twin Falls on Saturday night](https://www.ktvb.com/article/news/crime/police-investigate-drive-by-shooting/277-0445a1fb-6602-4ee7-b425-59bae82ee471) were confirmed, but officials said that shooting is unrelated to the In-N-Out shooting.
