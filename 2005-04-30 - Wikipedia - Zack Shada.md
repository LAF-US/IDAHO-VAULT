**Zachary David Shada** (born November 25, 1992) is an American actor. He is best known for the *[Jane Doe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Doe "Jane Doe")* movies (2005-2008).

## Life and career

Shada was born Zachary David Shada on November 25, 1992 in [Boise, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise,_Idaho "Boise, Idaho"). His brothers are actors Josh and [Jeremy Shada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Shada).[^1]

He appeared as Thin Boy in *[Charlie's Angels: Full Throttle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie's_Angels:_Full_Throttle "Charlie's Angels: Full Throttle")* (2003), and as Nick Davis in the 2005 television film *[Jane Doe: Vanishing Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Doe:_Vanishing_Act "Jane Doe: Vanishing Act")* and its sequels. He has appeared in two episodes of [*Lost*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost_\(2004_TV_series\) "Lost (2004 TV series)"), three episodes of *[Wizards of Waverly Place](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wizards_of_Waverly_Place "Wizards of Waverly Place"),* and voiced Comet in *[Space Chimps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Chimps "Space Chimps")*.

Shada voiced the character Pen in the pilot for the [Cartoon Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartoon_Network "Cartoon Network") series *[Adventure Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adventure_Time "Adventure Time")*.[^2] [^3] His brother Jeremy was later cast in the role, with the character being renamed [Finn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finn_the_Human "Finn the Human").[^4] [^5] [^6]

Jeremy and Zack Shada are members of the pop-punk band [Make Out Monday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Make_Out_Monday "Make Out Monday").[^5] [^7]
