**Ryan Hamilton** (born 1976) is an American [stand-up comedian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stand-up_comedian "Stand-up comedian") who uses [observational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Observational_comedy "Observational comedy"), [sarcastic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarcasm "Sarcasm"), and [self-deprecating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-deprecation "Self-deprecation") humor. He is known as a clean comedian and his material focuses on his own experiences, including his single life, [skydiving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skydiving "Skydiving"), [hot air balloons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_air_balloon "Hot air balloon"), and his huge smile. He was named one of *[Rolling Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone "Rolling Stone")* 's Five Comics to Watch in 2012,[^1] and also has made standout appearances on *[The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Tonight_Show_Starring_Jimmy_Fallon "The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon")*, *[The Late Show with Stephen Colbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Late_Show_with_Stephen_Colbert "The Late Show with Stephen Colbert")*, *[Conan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conan_\(talk_show\) "Conan (talk show)")*, *[The Late Late Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Late_Late_Show_\(U.S.\) "The Late Late Show (U.S.)")*, *[Last Comic Standing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Last_Comic_Standing "Last Comic Standing")*, [Comedy Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comedy_Central "Comedy Central"), and [Showtime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Showtime_\(TV_network\) "Showtime (TV network)").[^2] [^3] [^4] [^5]

## Early life

Hamilton was born in 1976 in [Ashton, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashton,_Idaho "Ashton, Idaho"), a small potato farming community.[^6] [^7] He attended [BYU-Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BYU-Idaho "BYU-Idaho"), receiving an associate degree in journalism and public relations.[^6] [^8] He then transferred to [Brigham Young University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigham_Young_University "Brigham Young University") and earned a bachelor's degree in public relations.[^9]

## Stand-up comedy career

Ryan started performing in [Salt Lake City, Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_Lake_City,_Utah "Salt Lake City, Utah") clubs. He also lived and performed in [Seattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle "Seattle") and performed often in [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston "Boston"), all of which he considers his "comedy homes".[^10] He has appeared in several festivals and competitions including winning the [Sierra Mist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Mist "Sierra Mist") "America's Next Great Comic" search in 2005 and joining their national tour.[^3] [^4] In 2011 he won The Great American Comedy Festival.[^11] He has been a regular performer at the [Just for Laughs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Just_for_Laughs "Just for Laughs") Comedy festivals, including Montreal and Sydney, and was a two-time semi-finalist on *[Last Comic Standing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Last_Comic_Standing "Last Comic Standing")*.[^12] [^13] He has also performed stand-up on [*Conan*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conan_\(talk_show\) "Conan (talk show)"), [Comedy Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comedy_Central "Comedy Central"), and [Showtime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Showtime_\(TV_network\) "Showtime (TV network)").[^14] Recently he opened for [Jerry Seinfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerry_Seinfeld "Jerry Seinfeld") on his 2019 Live tour.

His one-hour special, *Happy Face*, became available on [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") on August 29, 2017.[^15] On June 23, 2026, his stand-up special, *This Just Hit Me*, was released on Netflix.[^16] [^17]

## Personal life

Hamilton resides in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City").[^10]

In January 2022, he was hit by a shuttle bus as a pedestrian in Los Angeles at LAX airport, and suffered ten broken ribs, a compound fractured arm that required delayed emergency surgery, and a punctured collapsed lung.[^18] He recovered at his family home in Idaho.
