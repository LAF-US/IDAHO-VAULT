Some fans hoping to see the Banana Ball event at Albertsons Stadium at Boise State bought tickets to seats they could not have, and some were told the tickets they bought won’t be available tonight.

The event was plagued by complaints of several issues during the first game Friday, and [KTVB reports seven people](https://www.ktvb.com/article/news/local/large-number-of-patients-heat-related-incidents-banana-ball-boise/277-caefa692-4f74-4472-b24c-464ec070a377) were taken to the hospital with heat-related issues. Fans complained online about insufficient water stations. During the game broadcast on ESPN, the commentator claimed temperatures on the field reached 170 degrees, though the official temperature at the Boise Airport was 101.

Boise State spokesperson Chris Kutz did not respond to phone or email messages from BoiseDev, but a [statement attributed to](https://www.instagram.com/p/DbglzfkATna/?igsh=MXU5YzNlMWVqc2M3dg==) him to KTVB said they had “over 140” calls for assistance during the Friday event.

Some fans with tickets to tonight’s event received an email from Banana Ball about ticket issues.

“After last night’s game, we identified an issue with the seating configuration we used to assign tickets for the event that resulted in fans receiving incorrect seat assignments,” the email from Banana Ball read. “As soon as we became aware of it, our team worked to reassign every affected party as fairly as possible. Whenever we could, we kept groups together or within “high-five distance” by seating them in consecutive rows. When no seated option was available, we reassigned those parties to the standing room only section.”

The TV broadcast showed scores of empty seats throughout the stadium during Friday’s game. The impacted fans with tickets for Saturday’s events will see changes.

![Weather with Sarah - Idaho Power](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/54fe6a7b-4e48-4c07-b9ad-1f79c34fb57e.gif)

Weather with Sarah - Idaho Power

“Your seats have been reassigned, and your new tickets are now available in your Fans First Ticket account… Some fans have been reassigned to standing-room-only tickets, and assigned seats may not be in the same zone as the original purchase. We know this isn’t the experience you expected, and it’s certainly not the one we wanted to provide.”

Fans impacted will get both a ticket refund and new seats, according to the email, as well as a merchandise gift card that can be used online or in person. Impacted fans will also skip the ticket lottery and get first dibs on tickets to next year’s events.

Kutz did not respond to BoiseDev’s inquiry about how many fans were impacted Friday or Saturday by the deadline for this story. Boise State saw issues with the Mountain West Championship game in late 2024, [selling tickets to seats that didn’t exist,](https://boisedev.com/news/2024/12/03/boise-state-tickets-canclled/) though that issue was caught before the game. In that instance, Ticketmaster placed blame on Boise State, while Boise State said it was a Ticketmaster issue.

Boise State publicly noted a raft of changes for Saturday’s events, including [adding additional water stations](https://x.com/N_burk/status/2083601294812692811?s=20) and [messaging encouraging fans to wear light clothing, bring portable fans](https://x.com/extramilearena/status/2083627200926872032?s=20) and hydrate before the event.

> Please visit any of our cooling stations today! Free water available across the building. Come hydrated, wear loose fitting light-colored clothing, and text our fan assist line for any assistance at 208-427-3227 with your location and your request! [pic.twitter.com/r4R4bYSR8n](https://t.co/r4R4bYSR8n) — Nathan Burk (@N\_burk) [August 1, 2026](https://x.com/N_burk/status/2083647063653536197?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw)

The schedule for the events also changed, with the game starting 30 minutes earlier at 5:30 pm. During Friday’s event, fan meet-and-greet events with players were held before the game started at 2 pm, and the game did not begin until 7 pm.

Because fans could not leave the parking lot and reenter, they had to spend an extended amount of time in the parking lot before the game. The school cut this timeframe down for Saturday and moved player meet-and-greets to after the game when conditions are expected to be cooler.
