A planned Costco for Eagle is in a holding pattern, but not all is silent.

[As BoiseDev reported,](https://boisedev.com/news/2025/09/03/another-costco-retail-giant-eyes-site-on-idaho-55/) the company is considering a location at the intersection of Highway 55 and Hill Rd. in Eagle. The location would be Costco’s fifth location in the Treasure Valley, and would be more than 160,000 square feet in size.

Permits for the location were filed with the City of Eagle last winter, but a public hearing to move the project forward has not happened.

![Spitfire Tacos - 2:1 - July](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/54ed1bde-1f1b-4a28-b2f9-6a9a0f55a1c5.jpg)

Spitfire Tacos - 2:1 - July

City of Eagle Public Information Officer Laura Williams said the city is waiting on Costco to request a meeting with the city’s Planning and Zoning Commission.

In March, [BoiseDev reported](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/03/13/costco-eagle-compplan/) on an unpublished change the city made in 2021 that limited the size of commercial retail projects on the former Quarry Village site to 120,000 square feet, smaller than Costco’s proposal for a 160,000-square-foot building. The story incorrectly implied Costco could not move forward on the site, but in the process of reporting this story, BoiseDev learned that the earlier story was incorrect. Williams told BoiseDev Costco applied for a zoning amendment with its application, which would allow the project to move forward.

## Infrastructure work

While the company has not requested a public hearing, some required background work on the project has taken place in recent months. The company has taken steps to investigate the location further, including working with both state and local transportation agencies on potential infrastructure projects.

![Sundance Company](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/a95b6100-2267-485a-87f4-0eb633a38c0e.png)

Idaho Transportation Department Public Information Officer Jill Youmans said the department and Costco had worked together on getting a Sales Tax Anticipated Revenue Program agreement in place, should Costco get approved by the City of Eagle.

Through the program, a developer pays for agreed-upon public infrastructure improvements upfront and is later reimbursed through sales tax deferrals.

![](https://boisedev.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/its-official-costco-seeks-approval-for-new-store-along-idaho-55-Screenshot-2026-01-0.jpeg)

Site plan for proposed Costco Wholesale along Idaho 55 in Eagle. Site plan: Via KM Engineering

Youmans said the STAR agreement will be a quick process because the department has worked with Costco before and they have “been a good partner with STAR agreements in the past.”

![Corwin Ford Nampa - July 2026 - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/02b5ccc2-e925-42c6-9e39-de8bd0223814.jpg)

After reviewing a submitted traffic impact study, the Ada County Highway District sent a memo to the city in June about the planned Costco’s traffic impacts and the district’s requested mitigation efforts.

According to the memo, the Eagle Costco is anticipated to generate 7,081 vehicle trips per day on all days except Saturday, which is expected to see 8,826 vehicle trips.

## ACHD conditions

ACHD said in the memo that should the project be approved, it would like to see Costco held to several conditions of approval. These include constructing a multi-lane roundabout with four lanes going east/west and two lanes going north/south at the Hill Rd. and Horseshoe Bend Rd. intersection, another roundabout at Hill Rd., and widening Hill Rd. to five lanes between Highway 55 and Horseshoe Bend Rd.

![Spitfire Tacos - 2:1 - July](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/54ed1bde-1f1b-4a28-b2f9-6a9a0f55a1c5.jpg)

The roundabouts were part of Costco’s initial proposal, [as BoiseDev reported.](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/01/07/costco-eagle-plan/)

Additionally, the district is requesting a condition for Costco to widen Horseshoe Bend Rd. between Arnold Ave. and Optimist Park to three lanes.

The next step in the process is for Costco to go before the City of Eagle’s Planning and Zoning Commission for a public hearing. After that, the project would go before the city council for approval.

![SERVPRO Boise - Jan25](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/5b2a85ae-933e-4c98-82a5-e207d65304e9.png)

Until Costco requests the public hearing and tells the city it is ready to move forward, Williams said the project is on hold.

BoiseDev reached out to Costco for comment but was told by a spokesperson it is against company policy to comment regarding future Costco warehouses until two to three months before a new location opens.

Costco is currently at work on [a fourth location in South Meridian](https://boisedev.com/news/2025/06/26/costco-meridian-kuna/) at the corner of Meridian Rd. and Lake Hazel Rd. [As BoiseDev reported,](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/07/27/large-warehouse-retailer-space-planned-on-hawkins-owned-property-north-of-i-84/) an unbranded site plan proposed for I-84 and Meridian Rd. also potentially matches the typical footprint of the wholesale retailer’s other locations.
