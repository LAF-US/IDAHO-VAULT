Jackson, Photographer, Second Edition, Illustrated, 1872 Series, page 46, Nos. 511,512:  "The Diamond Hitch," or the operation of "packing" a mule. The entire transportation of the survey was effected by packing, each mule carrying on an average about 250 pounds, and so arranged upon the pack-saddle or aparejo by a system of "hitches" as to withstand all the vicissitudes of mountain traveling.
