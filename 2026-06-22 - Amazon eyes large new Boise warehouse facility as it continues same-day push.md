Another large Amazon facility, with features to help speed same-day delivery, appears poised for land south of the Boise Airport.

Asilia Investments of Salt Lake City, Utah, applied to the city to build a 250,000-square-foot building on a piece of land currently owned by Adler Industrial. Adler is in the midst of a long-range master-planned industrial development dubbed Pleasant Valley across nearly 2,000 acres near the intersection of Orchard St. and Lake Hazel Rd.

Amazon is not listed on the documents, which only say the facility is for a “fulfillment center and distribution” facility.

![Morrison Center - July 2026 - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/37e7ef09-855c-4668-ba54-b9eb50afe557.png)

Morrison Center - July 2026 - 2:1

However, the site plan provided to the City of Boise shows an area of the property built out for “Flex,” which is [an Amazon program](https://flex.amazon.com/blog/navigating-the-package-pickup-process-with-ease) that allows delivery drivers to use their own vehicles to quickly deliver items, similar to DoorDash or Uber Eats. Asilla lists six clients on its real estate page, including Amazon, and touts [its role in building out four industrial projects](https://www.asiliainvestments.com/strategies/real-estate) for the retail giant across the region.

The site plan also includes several features consistent with Amazon’s “sub same-day delivery” facilities, including specialty ramps for grocery delivery. The facilities allow for a range of products to be stored on site and picked up by drivers in their own cars and delivered in just a few hours. Amazon [operates a similar facility in Nampa](https://boisedev.com/news/2024/04/17/nampa-amazon-warehouse/), as BoiseDev reported in 2024.

Amazon rolled out same-day delivery across a range of items earlier this year to some residents in SW Idaho.

Morrison Center - July 2026 - 2:1

“This project will feature a concrete tilt building designed to house a diverse range of goods destined for distribution,” Asilia wrote in an application letter. “The site layout includes three separate drive aisles, each serving a specific purpose: associate parking, delivery vehicles, and line-haul trucks. The site plan outlines various access points to ensure seamless operation and maintain a balance of traffic flow across different areas of the site. This also creates increased safety for all parties navigating their own portion of the site.”

![](https://boisedev.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/amazon-eyes-large-new-boise-warehouse-facility-as-it-continues-same-day-push-Screenshot-2026-06-2.png)

Site plan submitted for a planned warehouse south of the Boise Airport. Site plan: Via CESO

Separately, Adler has applied to the City of Boise to change the zoning on the site of the proposed warehouse from I-1 to I-2. The key difference in I-2 zoning is that operations are allowed to run 24 hours per day. Most Amazon distribution centers operate around the clock.

It’s not the first time Amazon has eyed a facility on Pleasant Valley Holdings’ land in the area. In 2022, BoiseDev [reported on Project Dove](https://boisedev.com/news/2021/11/30/amazon-project-dove-boise/), a 1.33-million-square-foot facility to the south of the newly proposed project. That project was awarded an $86.4 million building permit from the City of Boise, but the permit was later voided, and Project Dove did not move forward.

Amazon has a large number of facilities across the Treasure Valley. It first entered the market with a [facility it dubbed Project Bronco](https://boisedev.com/news/2018/11/12/amazon-nampa-yeah-probably-heres-the-evidence/) near I-84 in Nampa, which takes up 2.5 million square feet of space. It has branched out in the years since with smaller facilities in [Boise](https://boisedev.com/news/2019/08/02/amazon-boise/), [Meridian](https://boisedev.com/news/2020/11/16/amazon-distribution-meridian/), and [Nampa](https://boisedev.com/news/2020/11/16/amazon-distribution-meridian/). It has also worked on [an air cargo hub](https://boisedev.com/news/2021/11/19/boise-inks-deal-with-amazon-for-air-cargo-facility-with-millions-in-infrastructure-improvements/) at the Boise Airport. A wireless networking [subsidiary set up shop in Downtown Boise](https://boisedev.com/news/2025/07/01/amazon-eero-boise/). It is even working to add a [drone delivery facility in Nampa](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/05/27/nampa-greenlights-drone-shipments-final-approval-delivery-areas-still-pending/).
