## Working Notes

- `Codex` begins in wood: trunk, block, boards, wax tablets.
- It becomes book-form: bound leaves replacing the scroll for many uses.
- It becomes authority-form: law-code, standard collection, canonical compiled
  body.
- It becomes abstraction-form through `code`: signals, rules, cipher, machine
  instructions.
- In the Vault, `Corpus` is the safer governance term; `Codex` remains an
  agent/tool lineage term.
