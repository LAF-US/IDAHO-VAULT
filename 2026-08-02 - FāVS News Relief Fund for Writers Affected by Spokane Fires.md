FāVS News is asking readers to help our own team.

Matthew Kincanon, who has covered the Native American community for FāVS since 2017, lost his house in the Old Trails fire that swept through the Spokane area Aug. 1. He and his fiancé, Natalie, got out safely, but most of what they owned did not.

Several other Spokane-area FāVS writers have also been evacuated or have family members affected by the fires this week, and more needs may surface in the days ahead.

We had planned to launch our quarterly membership drive this week. Instead, we’re pausing it to raise money for our team first. Funds raised will go to Matthew and Natalie as they rebuild, and to support any other Spokane FāVS writers who need help in the coming days.

Our writers have given years to this newsroom, telling stories that don’t get told anywhere else. Now it’s our turn to help them.
