### Description

Old Sampson Trail, Tr 221, is an old road that connects Meadow Creek Road to Palouse Divide Road. It's gentle gradient meanders through cool forest. Towards the upper end of the trail, it links into FS roads, which can have varying levels of forestry operations. To continue to Palouse Divide, turn right when the trail meets the major road.
