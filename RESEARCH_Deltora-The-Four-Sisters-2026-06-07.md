# The Four Sisters

> A buried fable and the slow-poison engines named after it — the spine of *Dragons of Deltora*. Pure source.
>
> **Provenance.** Web-verified 2026-06-07 (Deltora Quest Wiki, Wikipedia). **[canon-per-series].** Paraphrased.

## The original Sisters (the fable)
- **[canon / Architect's note]** While **sailing** in exile, **[[RESEARCH_Deltora-The-Shadow-Lord-2026-06-07\|Malverlain]]** **came upon an island** where **four sisters sang.** Their song, unknown to him, **soothed and sealed a great beast** in the land's heart. Hating the singing, he imprisoned and **killed the original Sisters** — and so **released the beast**: it rose, crushed the island's guardians, and sank the isle, forcing Malverlain to flee into the **Silver Sea.**

## The Shadow Lord's Four Sisters (the engines)
- **[canon]** Long after, the Shadow Lord built **four creatures/engines of his own** and hid them at **Deltora's four corners** — **named after the women he had killed** (a detail he found amusing). They **sing songs of death**, poisoning the land so that **crops wither and famine spreads.**
- **[canon]** They are known only by **direction** (no personal names):
  - **Sister of the East** — hidden at **Dragon's Nest**
  - **Sister of the North** — hidden at **Shadowgate**
  - **Sister of the West** — hidden on the **Isle of the Dead**
  - **Sister of the South** — hidden in the city of **Del** itself (beneath Lief's own home)

## Their destruction
- **[canon]** Each Sister can be undone only **with the aid of a dragon**; the last **Topaz, Ruby, Emerald, and Amethyst** dragons destroy them in turn (→ `[[RESEARCH_Deltora-The-Dragons-and-Ak-Baba-2026-06-07]]`).
