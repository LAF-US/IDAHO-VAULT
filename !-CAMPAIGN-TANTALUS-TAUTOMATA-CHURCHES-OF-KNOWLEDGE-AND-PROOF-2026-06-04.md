# Churches of Knowledge and Proof

The apparent schism between the Church of Knowledge and the Church of Proof is
a managed conflict planted by [[Tantalus the Absolute]]. Both Churches are
Tautomata institutions. The conflict keeps the ranks busy and hides the
observer-effect wound.

## Church of Knowledge

Patron image: Tautomaton Dr. Albertus Magnus Universalis.

Knowledge exists before proof. The universe is legible. What can be known must
be collected, ordered, repeated, and universalized.

Sin: omniscience without agency.

## Church of Proof

Patron image: Automaton Dr. Thomas Aquinas of the Church.

Assertion is not truth. Prediction is not proof. Proof requires completion.
Completion sanctifies certainty.

Sin: certainty after usefulness has expired.

The Brazen Head schism is a debugging harness that forgot it was fake.
