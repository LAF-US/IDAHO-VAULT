## Folder

## EUGENE O. LEONARD folder

#### DC

Description:

The collection consists of Leonard’s business and financial records that pertain to agriculture and his business, the Leonard Chemical Laboratory, as well as his work as Pocatello City Chemist. The agricultural business records deal specifically with the local dairy industry and include government publications, milk plant-producer inspection forms, milk producer distributor inspection forms, retail raw milk reports, and commercial milk and water reports. Other reports focus on local drinking establishments, including reports of whiskey samples. Personal and business correspondence and ephemera (1901- 1963), publications, teaching files from his tenure at Idaho State College, and over 250 photographs, negatives and postcards that focus mainly on regional natural features, landscapes (including Yellowstone National Park and Craters of the Moon National Monument), Pocatello, and family make up the remainder of the collection.
