**Delaney Marie Rowe** is an American social media personality, actress, and comedian. She is known for her [cringe comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cringe_comedy "Cringe comedy") videos on [TikTok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok "TikTok") in which she parodies character tropes in media, such as the "absolutely insufferable female lead of an [indie movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_film "Independent film")."

## Life and career

Rowe was raised in [Boise, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise,_Idaho "Boise, Idaho"). Inspired by the 2004 film *[Confessions of a Teenage Drama Queen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confessions_of_a_Teenage_Drama_Queen "Confessions of a Teenage Drama Queen")* to pursue acting, she regularly performed in the [Idaho Shakespeare Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idaho_Shakespeare_Festival "Idaho Shakespeare Festival") growing up. She moved from [[Idaho]] to [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles") to attend the [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California "University of Southern California") [School of Dramatic Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USC_School_of_Dramatic_Arts "USC School of Dramatic Arts"), where she earned a [BFA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Fine_Arts "Bachelor of Fine Arts") in acting in 2017.[^2] [^3] She worked as a hostess at a restaurant while auditioning for roles. She then started working as a personal chef after making flyers, on which she lied about attending culinary school, and putting them in mailboxes around [Beverly Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beverly_Hills "Beverly Hills").[^4] Rowe appeared in the 2020 short film *The Wine* as Cara.[^5]

Starting in 2020, Rowe started posting comedy sketches—which she writes, performs in, and edits—on [TikTok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok "TikTok").[^4] [^6] Her first video on the platform to go viral was a comedy sketch in which she gave a monologue about rain from the perspective of the "absolutely insufferable female lead of an [indie movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_film "Independent film") ".[^2] She continued to play this character in later videos that also went viral on the platform.[^5] [^7] Her videos typically feature [cringe comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cringe_comedy "Cringe comedy") and parodies of other media tropes, such as female characters written by men and [Manic Pixie Dream Girls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manic_Pixie_Dream_Girl "Manic Pixie Dream Girl").[^8] [^9] She appeared in the 2023 film *The List*.[^6] As of 2023, she is signed to [United Talent Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Talent_Agency "United Talent Agency").[^4]

## Personal life

As of 2023, Rowe lives in [Echo Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Echo_Park "Echo Park"), Los Angeles.[^2] She was in a relationship with [B. J. Novak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B._J._Novak "B. J. Novak").[^10]
