**Janet Holmes** is an American poet and professor.

## Education and career

Holmes earned her B.A. from [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University "Duke University") and her M.F.A. from Warren Wilson College.

She was the director of [Ahsahta Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahsahta_Press "Ahsahta Press").[^1]

She taught at Boise State University in the Department of Theatre, Film and Creative Writing from 1999 [^2] through 2019.[^3]

## Published works

She is the author of six poetry collections, most recently *The ms of m y kin* [^4] (Shearsman Books, 2009). Her poems were published in literary journals including *American Poetry Review*, *[Beloit Poetry Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beloit_Poetry_Journal "Beloit Poetry Journal")*, *Boulevard*, *Carolina Quarterly*, *Georgia Review*, *Michigan Quarterly Review*, *MiPoesias*, *Nimrod*, *Pleiades*, *Poetry*, *Prairie Schooner*, and in anthologies including *[The Best American Poetry 1994](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Best_American_Poetry_1994 "The Best American Poetry 1994")* and *[The Best American Poetry 1995](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Best_American_Poetry_1995 "The Best American Poetry 1995")*.

**Full-length collections**

- *The ms of m y kin* (Shearsman Books, 2009)
- *F2F* ([University of Notre Dame Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame_Press "University of Notre Dame Press"), 2006)
- *Humanophone* (University of Notre Dame Press, 2001)
- *The Green Tuxedo* (University of Notre Dame Press, 1998)
- *The Physicist at the Mall* ([Anhinga Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anhinga_Press "Anhinga Press"), 1994)

**Chapbooks**

- *Paperback Romance* (State Street Press, 1984)

## Honors and awards

Her honors include the Minnesota Book Award [^5] and fellowships from [Yaddo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaddo "Yaddo") and the [MacDowell Colony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacDowell_Colony "MacDowell Colony").[^6] [^2]

- 1999 Chad Walsh Poetry Prize from *[Beloit Poetry Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beloit_Poetry_Journal "Beloit Poetry Journal")*
- 1999 Minnesota Book Award
- 1998 Poetry Book of the Year from *ForeWord Magazine*
- 1997 Pablo Neruda Award from *Nimrod International Journal of Prose & Poetry*
- 1996 Ernest Sandeen Prize in Poetry
- 1993 Anhinga Prize
- 1984 State Street Press Chapbook Award
