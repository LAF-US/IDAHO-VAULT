BOISE, Idaho — *Editor's note: KTVB has removed photos of the individual that were provided by Boise School District.*

Boise police say a man whose photo was circulated after reports of suspicious activity at Riverside Elementary School is not considered a suspect or threat.

In a message sent to families and staff Monday, the Boise School District said the individual contacted the Boise Police Department after seeing his photo in media coverage. Police interviewed the man and verified that he was on the Riverside campus to take photographs for an art exhibit.

"The individual in the photos reached out to Boise Police after seeing his photo in the news," Boise police said in a statement shared by the district. "He explained he was taking photos for an art showcase and police do not consider him a suspect or threat."

School district officials said staff reported the activity to police "out of an abundance of caution" based on what was observed at the time.

Following the investigation, Boise police determined the individual does not pose a threat to Riverside Elementary, its students or staff.

District leaders thanked the man and others who provided information to law enforcement, as well as the Boise Police Department for its response.

"The safety of our students and staff remains our highest priority," the district said. "We appreciate our community's continued partnership as we prepare to welcome students back to school on Wednesday."

In an earlier letter to parents and staff, the Boise School District said staff reported seeing a person not associated with the school photographing the exterior of the campus on July 6 and again on Aug. 4

"At this time, we are not aware of any specific threat to Riverside Elementary students or staff," the district superintendent, Wendy Johnson, wrote in a letter to families. "However, with the first day of school on Wednesday, August 12, we wanted our school community to be informed."
