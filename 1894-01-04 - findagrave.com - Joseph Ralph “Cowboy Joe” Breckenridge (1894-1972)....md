Joseph R. (Cowboy Joe) Breckenridge, 77, 1035 Emma St., died November 16 at Kootenai Memorial Hospital.  
He was born in Ione (sp? likely Ionia) Michigan, January 4, 1894, coming to Kootenai County in 1928.  
Keeping Coeur d'Alene as a home base, he traveled all over the United States, Mexico and Canada as a free lance artist. He also participated in many rodeos.  
During past years, Breckenridge could be seen painting in the big building the Kootenai County fairgrounds during the Kootenai County Fair.  
Survivors include his daughter, Mrs, James (Marlene) Welch, Pinehurst; a brother, William, Post Falls; a sister, Lillian Peterson with whom he resided in Coeur d'Alene; six grandchildren.  
  
The funeral will be Wednesday at 1:30 p.m. at Yates Funeral Home with the Rev. Walter Pegram officiating. Burial will be at Forest Cemetery.  
He served in the Marine Corps during World War I.
