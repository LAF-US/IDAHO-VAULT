**William Louis Petersen** (born February 21, 1953) is an American retired actor. He is best known for his role as [Gil Grissom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gil_Grissom "Gil Grissom") in the [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS "CBS") drama thriller series *[CSI: Crime Scene Investigation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSI:_Crime_Scene_Investigation "CSI: Crime Scene Investigation")* (2000–2015), for which he won a [Screen Actors Guild Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award "Screen Actors Guild Award") and was nominated for a [Golden Globe Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Awards "Golden Globe Awards"); he was further nominated for three [Primetime Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Awards "Primetime Emmy Awards") as a producer of the show.[^1] He reprised his role as Gil Grissom in the sequel *[CSI: Vegas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSI:_Vegas "CSI: Vegas")*, which premiered on October 6, 2021.

He also starred in the films *[To Live and Die in L.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/To_Live_and_Die_in_L.A._\(film\) "To Live and Die in L.A. (film)")* (1985), *[Manhunter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhunter_\(film\) "Manhunter (film)")* (1986), *[Young Guns II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Guns_II "Young Guns II")* (1990), *[Fear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fear_\(1996_film\) "Fear (1996 film)")* (1996), *[The Contender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Contender_\(2000_film\) "The Contender (2000 film)")* (2000), *[Detachment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detachment_\(film\) "Detachment (film)")* (2011), and *[Seeking a Friend for the End of the World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seeking_a_Friend_for_the_End_of_the_World "Seeking a Friend for the End of the World")* (2012).[^2]

## Early life

William Louis Petersen was born on February 21, 1953 in [Evanston, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evanston,_Illinois "Evanston, Illinois"), the youngest of six children of June (née Hoene; 1909–2006) and Arthur Edward Petersen (1907–2004), who worked in the furniture business.[^3] [^4] Of [Danish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_Americans "Danish Americans") and [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Americans "German Americans") descent, he was raised in the Roman Catholic faith of his mother.[^5] [^6] He has two brothers, Arthur Jr. and Robert, and three sisters, Anne, Mary Kay, and Elizabeth.[^7]

He graduated from [Bishop Kelly High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishop_Kelly_High_School "Bishop Kelly High School") in [Boise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise,_Idaho "Boise, Idaho"), [Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idaho "Idaho"), in 1972. He was accepted to [Idaho State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idaho_State_University "Idaho State University") on a [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_football "American football") scholarship. While at [[Idaho]] State, Petersen took an acting course, which changed the direction of his life.

In 1974 he and his wife Joanne left college [^8] and followed a [drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drama "Drama") professor to the [Basque country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basque_Country_\(autonomous_community\) "Basque Country (autonomous community)"). There he studied as a [Shakespearean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shakespearean "Shakespearean") actor. Petersen also became interested in [Basque culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basque_culture "Basque culture"): He studied the [Basque language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basque_language "Basque language") and gave his daughter the Basque name "Maite Nerea" ("My Beloved"); she was born in [Arrasate/Mondragón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mondrag%C3%B3n "Mondragón") in 1975.[^9]

Petersen returned to Idaho with the intention of being an actor. But not wanting to work a nonacting job there, he returned to the Chicago area, living with relatives. He became active in the theater and earned his [Actors' Equity card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actors'_Equity_card "Actors' Equity card"). He performed with the [Steppenwolf Theatre Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steppenwolf_Theatre_Company "Steppenwolf Theatre Company"), of which he has been an ensemble member since 2008.[^10] He was a co-founder of the Remains Theater Ensemble,[^8] which also included other prominent Chicago actors [Gary Cole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Cole "Gary Cole") and [Ted Levine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Levine "Ted Levine").

## Career

Petersen made his film debut with a bit part in *[Thief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thief_\(film\) "Thief (film)")* (1981), directed by [Michael Mann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Mann "Michael Mann"). The actor received his first break when he played a Secret Service agent gone rogue to avenge his mentor in [William Friedkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Friedkin "William Friedkin") 's 1985 action film *[To Live and Die in L.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/To_Live_and_Die_in_L.A._\(film\) "To Live and Die in L.A. (film)")*[^11] In 1986, he played FBI agent [Will Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Graham_\(character\) "Will Graham (character)") in the first [Hannibal Lecter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Lecter "Hannibal Lecter") film, *[Manhunter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhunter_\(film\) "Manhunter (film)")*.[^12]

Because his role was so emotionally exhausting, he did everything he could to rid himself of Graham after finishing [principal photography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_photography "Principal photography"). He [shaved](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaving "Shaving") off his [beard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beard "Beard"), cut his hair, and bleached it blond. He also claims to have done this because, while rehearsing for a play in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago "Chicago"), he found he was speaking like Graham. He dyed his hair so he could look in the mirror and see a different person.[^13]

He declined a part in [Oliver Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Stone "Oliver Stone") 's *[Platoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platoon_\(film\) "Platoon (film)")*, as it would have kept him in the [Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippines "Philippines"), away from his family. Instead, he worked on the 1987 HBO [made-for-TV movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Made-for-TV_movie "Made-for-TV movie") *Long Gone* as a minor league baseball player and manager named Cecil "Stud" Cantrell.[^14] Petersen was offered the role of [Henry Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Hill "Henry Hill") in the film *[Goodfellas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodfellas "Goodfellas")*, but turned it down. An exposé about the film in the May 2015 issue of *[Playboy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Playboy "Playboy")* claims that Petersen turned down the audition altogether.[^15]

In a 1990 ABC three-part miniseries, *[The Kennedys of Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Kennedys_of_Massachusetts "The Kennedys of Massachusetts")*, Petersen played U.S. President [John F. Kennedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_F._Kennedy "John F. Kennedy") 's father, Ambassador [Joseph P. Kennedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_P._Kennedy_Sr. "Joseph P. Kennedy Sr.").[^16] The film won an Emmy and a Golden Globe from eight and two nominations, respectively. Also in 1990, Petersen portrayed the infamous Patrick Floyd "Pat" Garrett in *[Young Guns II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Guns_II "Young Guns II")*.[^17]

In 1993, Petersen appeared in a CBS TV miniseries, *[Return to Lonesome Dove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Return_to_Lonesome_Dove "Return to Lonesome Dove")*, as former Ranger Gideon Walker.[^18] He played Steven Walker, a father who stops at nothing to break up the relationship between his daughter and her vicious boyfriend in *[Fear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fear_\(1996_film\) "Fear (1996 film)")* (1996).[^19] Petersen played Governor Jack Hathaway, an unscrupulous candidate for vice president following the death of the incumbent, in *[The Contender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Contender_\(2000_film\) "The Contender (2000 film)")* in 2000.[^20]

He appeared uncredited in the noir thriller *[Mulholland Falls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mulholland_Falls "Mulholland Falls")* as a character who finds himself on the violent receiving end of a Los Angeles police squad's tactics. In 1999, he starred in *[Kiss the Sky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiss_the_Sky_\(film\) "Kiss the Sky (film)")* as "Jeff." [^21] [^22] He appeared as part of an all-star cast in the 1997 remake of *[12 Angry Men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12_Angry_Men_\(1997_film\) "12 Angry Men (1997 film)")* (as Juror #12, a.k.a. "the Snob").[^23]

From 2000 to 2009, he played Dr. [Gil Grissom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gil_Grissom "Gil Grissom") in the [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS "CBS") crime drama *[CSI: Crime Scene Investigation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSI:_Crime_Scene_Investigation "CSI: Crime Scene Investigation")*.[^24] Petersen took a break from *CSI* in 2006 to appear in a five-week run of the [Trinity Repertory Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_Repertory_Company "Trinity Repertory Company") production of *[Dublin Carol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin_Carol "Dublin Carol")* by [Conor McPherson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conor_McPherson "Conor McPherson"), in [Providence, Rhode Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Providence,_Rhode_Island "Providence, Rhode Island").[^25] Petersen renewed his contract with CBS to appear on *CSI* for the 2008–09 season, reportedly for $600,000 per episode.[^26] On July 15, 2008, the [Associated Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associated_Press "Associated Press") reported that Petersen was leaving the show as a regular following Season 9's tenth episode, in order to pursue more stage-acting opportunities, but that he might return for guest spots.[^27] He remained an executive producer of the show.[^27] He reprised his role of Gil Grissom in the eleventh-season episode "The Two Mrs. Grissoms" (aired February 3, 2011). He came back in 2015 as a guest in the series finale, "Immortality."

In February 2020, Petersen was announced to return for a *CSI* sequel series along with [Jorja Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorja_Fox "Jorja Fox"), before the series was picked up as *[CSI: Vegas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSI:_Vegas "CSI: Vegas")*. In October 2025, Petersen stated he was retired from acting and was not seeking any new roles.[^28]

## Personal life

In 1975, Petersen and his wife Joanne Brady had a daughter, Maite. They later divorced.

In June 2003, Petersen married his longtime girlfriend Gina Cirone. On July 5, 2011, Petersen and Cirone had twins, a daughter and son, via surrogate.[^29]

In 2004, he described to *[Playboy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Playboy_Magazine "Playboy Magazine")* a [near-death experience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Near-death_experience "Near-death experience") he had in the 1980s which gave him "assurance" that there is an [afterlife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afterlife "Afterlife").[^30] In August 2021, he was taken to the hospital via ambulance after feeling unwell on the set of *[CSI: Vegas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSI:_Vegas "CSI: Vegas")*. He was experiencing symptoms of exhaustion after 12 weeks of shooting, and was released from the facility soon after.[^31]

## Honors

On February 3, 2009, Petersen received a star on the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame "Hollywood Walk of Fame").[^32]
