**Building History**

The German social and physical fitness group, the Turnverein, had its clubhouse on the north side of Bush Street, between Stockton and Powell Streets in 1861. (See *The San Francisco Directory for the Year 1861*, \[San Francisco: Henry G. Langley, 1861\], p. 334.) The Turnverein moved their San Francisco clubhouse to 534 Kearny in 1862. (See *The San Francisco Directory for the Year 1862*, \[San Francisco: Henry G. Langley, 1862\], p. 341.)
