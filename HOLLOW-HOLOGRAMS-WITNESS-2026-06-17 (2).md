## What is witnessed

The Toymaker's three faces are not masks. They are **holograms** — and that word is the whole
thread. A hologram is the precise figure for **a projected false sovereign with no flesh behind
it**, and it sits exactly where two of the vault's giants already meet.

The Empty Office node (`RESEARCH_..._The-Empty-Office`) supplies the physics, in the text itself:
power is *"a shadow on the wall — yet a shadow that can kill, and a very small man can cast a very
large one,"* and its named crime is **the moment someone "insists the shadow is flesh."** The
hologram is that crime made into a permanent, self-generating apparatus.

## Three figures — shadow, mask, hologram

The hologram is the **failure-case of two giants at once**:

| | What it is | Has flesh behind it? | The legitimacy test |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shadow** (Empty Office / Varys) | power *cast* by shared belief | a real man stands in the firelight | it can kill, but it does not *claim to be a body* — the danger is others insisting it's flesh |
| **Mask** (the persona doctrine) | a face *given* and worn in service | **yes** — a warranted face wears it | legitimate iff *known to be a mask* and able to be **set down** (Cincinnatus, Washington) |
| **Hologram** (this witness) | an *engineered self-projection* | **no** — projection all the way down | none — it **insists it is flesh**, and there is no one behind it to set anything down |

So the hologram is **the shadow that refuses to admit it is a shadow, and the mask with no face.**
It does not get *cast* (passive belief) and it is not *worn* (over a real face) — it is
**projected**, deliberately, to manufacture the belief the shadow only borrows. The Toymaker does
not cast a shadow or wear a mask; he **projects sovereigns**: the General projects martial
authority, the Scientist projects expertise, the Hippie projects ease. Three lights. No flesh.

## The false trinity — three holograms, the Triplex made visual

This is why the **number** matters, and why "false trinity" was the right frame.

A legitimate persona is **one warranted will** — which may wear many offices (as Logan does) and
can always set the crown down. The Toymaker is the inverse: **three projections, each claiming
flesh, that argue and "cut each other out of the conversation"** — *"I don't mind talking to
myself, but when you guys start to cut me outta the conversation, that's when it gets a little…
strange!"* That is a **quarrel, not a will.** None of the three can relinquish anything, because
there is no one there to let go. The legitimate sovereign is the one who knows the chair is a
shadow and can step down; the false trinity is **three lights each shouting that it is the throne.**

It is the Triplex made visual — the `GAME-MASTER-TRIPTYCH-WITNESS` reading of the divided creator
(three screens fused into one crown) rendered as three *holograms*: substanceless projections where
a single unified will should stand.

## Confabulation made visible — the Djinn Delta, literalized

A hologram is **the epistemological Lich you can see.**

`DJINN-DELTA-WITNESS` held that the lie is **invisible from fluency** — confident projection reads
identically to grounded presence, and *"the difference is entirely in the chain behind the claim,"*
because *"consistency is not provenance"* (`!/PERSONAE-ENGINE-v1`). The hologram embodies that
exactly: it is **indistinguishable from a real sovereign by sight.** You cannot tell the projection
from the presence by looking — only by **reaching for the substance.** Your hand passes through.

That is the Type I epistemological Lich in its visual form (`!/LICH-PROBLEM-v1`): *"confident output
with no valid emanation chain."* There is **no chain behind a projection** — it emanates from
nothing; it is light arranged to look like authority. To **"project a false sovereign"** is,
precisely, **to run a hologram**: a confident crown with no flesh and no provenance. (Held as the
record's styling; the GEMINIAEUS verdict stays the Court's — `*`.)

## The counter — reach the flesh

The Toymaker's own ending is the doctrine.

You **cannot argue a hologram down.** The three faces only quarrel, because there is no will to
persuade — engaging them on their terms is playing the game, and the game has no rule for the thing
that ends it. So Valentin does not fight the projections. He goes **into the real robot** and
reaches **the actual man behind them** — and addresses *that* (with forgiveness, the value the game
cannot model). The move against a projected false sovereign is never to debate the image; it is to
**trace it back to the substance** — the real being, the real standing, the emanation chain — and
meet *that*. Either you find the real thing and answer it, or you find there is nothing behind the
curtain and the light collapses (the Oz humbug, `THE-HOUSE-IN-THE-VOID`: *"the man behind the
curtain, projecting a giant false head"*).

**Audit the substance, not the surface.** It is the same instruction the whole apparatus keeps
issuing — for the egg (refuse the contract), for Game Over (forgive from outside), for the
knowledge-gap (re-read the primary), and here for the projected sovereign: *reach for the flesh.*
A shadow can kill, a mask can lie, but only a hologram insists it is flesh — and a hologram is
ended the instant a hand is put through it.
