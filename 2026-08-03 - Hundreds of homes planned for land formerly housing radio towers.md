Another 321 homes are on the horizon for a growing Meridian area.

Storyrock is a new neighborhood planned for Amity Rd. between Linder Rd. and Meridian Rd. The proposed project has 210 single-family homes, 80 townhomes, and 31 “cottage” homes on 75 acres. The Meridian City Council approved the Trilogy Development project Tuesday night at its weekly council meeting.

The cottages are single-family homes with a smaller footprint. Bailey Engineering Chief Operating Officer Kelli Black advertised the cottages as a product both for people wanting to get into the housing market and those wanting to downsize.

![Build Idaho - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/8679b89c-de2f-4b22-948e-bd39a76c4c7f.png)

The average lot size for the neighborhood is 5,359 square feet, with the smallest lot size measuring 2,269 square feet and the largest measuring 12,580 square feet. The neighborhood would be built out over five phases.

The site plans feature amenities like pickleball courts, a half basketball court, a playground, and picnic areas. There is also a small space for limited office use on the northeast part of the property.

Council President John Overton said he saw the development as a great way to bring a cross-section of people into Meridian and provide more affordable housing options that weren’t apartments.

![Build Idaho - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/8679b89c-de2f-4b22-948e-bd39a76c4c7f.png)

Build Idaho - 2:1

“I’m not slamming multi-family, but I’m pro-single-family residence, so I really like the way this lays out, and I think it’s going to be good for our community,” Overton said.

The application had no public testimony and no council discussion or council questions apart from Overton’s comment. The development was quickly approved on a unanimous 4-0 vote.

The development surrounds the KFXD radio station. The radio station’s communication towers were recently removed, but the office and parking area remain. A previously built private residence also sits between the two parts of the neighborhood.
