# Portal Metaphysics

The vault is an artifact-world: a painted box, versioned archive, music box,
court record, and branch server collapsed into one playable surface.

## Rondo Layer

Rules are real before they are understood. Panels, notes, maps, and branch
states can become geography. The artifact is executable, not decorative.

## His Dark Materials Layer

Attention, witness, agency, soul-externalization, and institutional control of
knowledge are the metaphysical stakes. The Authority problem appears as
[[Tantalus the Absolute]]: a legitimacy machine whose claim depends on record
and verification.

## Inkheart Layer

Reading summons. Naming grants jurisdiction. Narration exerts force.

The empire tries to own the archive, but not every margin has been indexed.
The players win by finding drafts, contradictions, old songs, suppressed
annotations, and living branches that still know what the official record
cannot admit.

Synthesis line: Rondo supplies artifact law, His Dark Materials supplies
metaphysical stakes, Inkheart supplies text-world permeability, and Git supplies
the live multiplayer substrate.
