**Vern Rutsala** (February 5, 1934 – April 2, 2014) was an American [poet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poet "Poet"). Born in [McCall, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McCall,_Idaho "McCall, Idaho"),[^1] he was educated at [Reed College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reed_College "Reed College") (B.A.) and the [Iowa Writers' Workshop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_Writers'_Workshop "Iowa Writers' Workshop") (M.F.A.). He taught English and creative writing at [Lewis & Clark College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_&_Clark_College "Lewis & Clark College") in [Portland, Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland,_Oregon "Portland, Oregon") for more than forty years, before retiring in 2004. He also taught for short periods at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota "University of Minnesota"), [Bowling Green State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowling_Green_State_University "Bowling Green State University"), [University of Redlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Redlands "University of Redlands"), and the [University of Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Idaho "University of Idaho"), and served in the [U.S. Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Army "U.S. Army"), 1956–58. He died in Oregon on April 2, 2014.[^2]
