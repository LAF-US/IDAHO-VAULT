Boise-based Micron Technology is no longer a trillion-dollar company, after several punishing days on Wall Street.

The Boise-based chipmaker has seen several days of losses, with the stock dropping nearly ten percent Wednesday alone. The stock closed at $739 a share Wednesday, but kept sliding in after-hours trading. Micron’s total valuation stands at $834 billion as of 2:30 pm Wednesday, after first slipping below the trillion-dollar mark Tuesday.

![](https://boisedev.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/MU-2026-07-29-14-35--MU-2026-07-29-14-35-.png)

Graph: Courtesy Trading View

Micron is seeing its stock dragged down by competitor SK Hynix of South Korea, which saw record earnings for the latest quarter — but the numbers were far below what analysts expected, [according to Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/nvidia-micron-amd-sink-after-sk-hynix-results-fail-to-impress-ai-trade-unwinds-130352334.html).

Pressure from a Chinese chipmaker is also adding uncertainty to traditional memory makers. [CNBC reported that](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/28/china-chipmaking-duv-tool-asml-explained.html) a Chinese company has figured out how to make an imerion deep ultraviolet lithography machine – the hulking devices that Micron and other chipmakers use as a key step in chipmaking. The machines were previously exclusive to a Micron supplier known as ASML.

If China has been able to crack the technology, it could allow the communist nation’s business sector to ramp up chip production. [CNBC reported](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/28/china-chipmaking-duv-tool-asml-explained.html) there are still doubts about China’s overall capability with the technology.

[As BoiseDev reported](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/07/27/report-apple-says-micron-is-price-gouging-micron-wants-trump-to-stop-apple-from-using-chinese-chips/), Micron and Apple are also in a back-and-forth with the Trump administration over Apple’s potential use of Chinese chips in devices the tech giant sells overseas.
