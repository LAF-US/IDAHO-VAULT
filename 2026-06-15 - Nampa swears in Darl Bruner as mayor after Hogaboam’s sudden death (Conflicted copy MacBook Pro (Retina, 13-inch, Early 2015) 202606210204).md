NAMPA, Idaho (CBS2) — Nampa has a new mayor following the sudden death of Mayor Rick Hogaboam earlier this spring.

Darl Bruner has been sworn in as Nampa's new Mayor ahead of the regularly scheduled City Council meeting.

The appointment comes after Hogaboam’s “sudden and tragic passing” on March 18, 2026, just weeks after he was sworn into office. Under Idaho Code 50-608, the Nampa City Council was responsible for filling the vacancy.

Bruner will serve as mayor through the next general city election in November 2027, when Nampa voters will have the opportunity to elect a mayor.

City leaders said opportunities for the community to meet and visit with Mayor-appointed Bruner are expected to occur later this month. They also said, “We appreciate the community’s patience and understanding through this emotional process.”
