Last week, the U.S. Department of Justice [withdrew](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/23/business/media/new-york-times-subpoenas-withdraw.html) subpoenas that would have required reporters from the New York Times to turn over phone records and testify in court about their sources on security concerns related to President Trump’s new Air Force One. The move came during a hearing in which a federal judge sharply criticized the department for violating its own rules limiting prosecutors’ use of subpoenas and other tools to demand information from journalists.
