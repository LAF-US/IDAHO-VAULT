## Summary

A Miskatonic University expedition to Antarctica discovers a mountain range
higher than the Himalayas, concealing the ruins of a prehuman civilization.
The Elder Things — a star-headed alien species — built cyclopean cities on the
Antarctic plateau millions of years before humanity. They created the
shoggoths: formless biological servants programmed for any task. Over
millennia the shoggoths gained independence and destroyed their masters.

Dyer and his graduate student Danforth are the only survivors who know the
full truth. The story is Dyer's forced testimony — written to prevent further
expeditions from returning to the site. Danforth goes insane after glimpsing
something in the mountains beyond the city that Dyer himself did not see.

## The Snow Fields

The snow fields are the Antarctic ice cap — the blank white surface that
covers what lies beneath. The expedition drills through ice to find fossils.
They fly over the mountains and see the city's outer wall. The snow fields
are the *surface* — the visible desolation that promises nothing, above the
cyclopean architecture that holds everything.

In the vault register, the snow fields are any surface that appears empty
but covers a buried civilization.
