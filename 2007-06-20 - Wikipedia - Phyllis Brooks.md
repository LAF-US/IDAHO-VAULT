**Phyllis Brooks** (July 18, 1915 – August 1, 1995) was an American actress and model.[^2] She was born Phyllis Seiler in [Boise, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise,_Idaho "Boise, Idaho").[^3] Some sources have also inaccurately cited 1914 as her year of birth, but 1915 is the correct year according to Social Security records.

## Career

### Modeling

![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/1c/Phyllis_Brooks_wearing_a_fur-lined_coat_and_fedora.jpg/250px-Phyllis_Brooks_wearing_a_fur-lined_coat_and_fedora.jpg)

Brooks wearing a coat and fedora (1937)

Brooks was a model for two years before progressing to a career in film. She stated, "I started posing for photographers as a lark, and it was a lot of fun." [^4]

She had been known as the " [Ipana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ipana "Ipana") Toothpaste Girl", due to her work for that product.[^1]

### Film

Initially known as **Mary Brooks**, she began her career in films in 1934 [^1] at age 20, in *[I've Been Around](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I've_Been_Around_\(film\) "I've Been Around (film)")*.[^5] Brooks, who had about 30 performances in films, was a B-movie leading lady during the 1930s and 1940s, with roles in such films as *[In Old Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_Old_Chicago "In Old Chicago")* (1937), *[Little Miss Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Miss_Broadway "Little Miss Broadway")* (1938) and *[The Shanghai Gesture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Shanghai_Gesture "The Shanghai Gesture")* (1941).

![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8c/Cesar_Romero_and_Phyllis_Brooks.jpg/250px-Cesar_Romero_and_Phyllis_Brooks.jpg)

Phyllis Brooks with actor Cesar Romero, c. 1940

She appeared in [Sidney Toler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidney_Toler "Sidney Toler") 's *[Charlie Chan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Chan "Charlie Chan")* series, in the [Shirley Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shirley_Temple "Shirley Temple") films *[Rebecca of Sunnybrook Farm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebecca_of_Sunnybrook_Farm_\(1938_film\) "Rebecca of Sunnybrook Farm (1938 film)")* and in *[Little Miss Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Miss_Broadway "Little Miss Broadway")*.[^6]

### Stage

On [Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broadway_theatre "Broadway theatre"), Brooks appeared in *Stage Door* (1936–37), *[Panama Hattie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panama_Hattie "Panama Hattie")* (1940–42), *The Night Before Christmas* (1941), and *Round Trip* (1945).[^7]

## Wartime activities

![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/53/Robert_Lowery-Phyllis_Brooks_in_High_Powered.jpg/250px-Robert_Lowery-Phyllis_Brooks_in_High_Powered.jpg)

Robert Lowery and Brooks on High Powered (1945)

![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f0/Gary_Cooper_Phyllis_Brooks_Una_Merkel%2C_Brisbane_1943_%281%29.jpg/250px-Gary_Cooper_Phyllis_Brooks_Una_Merkel%2C_Brisbane_1943_%281%29.jpg)

Phyllis Brooks (middle) with Gary Cooper and Una Merkel at a Brisbane press conference on their way to entertain the troops (1943)

Brooks was reported (UK *Sunday Telegraph* December 1942) as being president of Parties Unlimited Inc. in an article about [Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinema_of_the_United_States "Cinema of the United States") at war. Along with actress [Una Merkel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Una_Merkel "Una Merkel") and accompanied by film star [Gary Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Cooper "Gary Cooper"), Brooks was the first civilian woman to travel to the [Pacific theater of war during World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Ocean_theater_of_World_War_II "Pacific Ocean theater of World War II") on a [USO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USO "USO") tour.

## Personal life

Brooks was engaged at one time to [Cary Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cary_Grant "Cary Grant").[^8] She married [Torbert Macdonald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torbert_Macdonald "Torbert Macdonald") on June 23, 1945, in [Tarrytown, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarrytown,_New_York "Tarrytown, New York").[^9]

Brooks moved east to [Cambridge, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge,_Massachusetts "Cambridge, Massachusetts") with her new husband in 1945 so that he could complete his studies at [Harvard Law School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Law_School "Harvard Law School"). He had been a Harvard football captain and a decorated [PT boat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PT_boat "PT boat") captain in [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II "World War II"). Macdonald died in office in 1976.[^10] [^11] they had 4 children.

## Death

Brooks died on August 1, 1995, in [Cape Neddick, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Neddick,_Maine "Cape Neddick, Maine"), aged 80.[^5]
