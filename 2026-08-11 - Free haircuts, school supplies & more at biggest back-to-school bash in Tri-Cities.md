Tri-Cities families can get a break on the cost of getting ready to send the kids back to school.

Organizations across the Tri-Cities are hosting giveaways, including the biggest [back-to-school bash](https://www.facebook.com/photo/?fbid=1472796168218345&set=gm.2973381203053882) in the region Monday with free services and supplies to get kids ready for the upcoming school year.

[Lutheran Community Services Northwest](https://lcsnw.org/office/tri-cities/), [Communities in Schools Benton-Franklin](https://bentonfranklin.ciswa.org/) and [SOZO Church](https://sozochurch.com/) are putting on an event at the HAPO Center in Pasco Monday, Aug. 17, offering haircuts, healthcare, school supplies and more. In its seventh year running, the event is expecting to serve 2,500 students.

No registration or identification is needed, and students of all ages are encouraged to attend. The services will run from 4 p.m. to 6:30 p.m.

Lutheran Community Services and Communities in Schools Benton-Franklin both work with youth to provide access to safe, equal education and lifestyles. The back-to-school bash ensures children and their families receive necessary services without worrying about the financial burden.

![https://img.particlenews.com/image.php?url=1M9DWE_1E92xyjE00](https://img.particlenews.com/image.php?type=thumbnail_580x000&url=1M9DWE_1E92xyjE00)

Lutheran Community Services Northwest have held similar fundraisers in the past for youth in the community. Courtesy Lutheran Community Services Northwest

Amber Gonzalez, Associate Director at CISBF, and Amy Marinoni, Regional Advancement Manager, told the Tri-City Herald in an email that this year’s major supporters are SOZO Church, HAPO Event Center, the [Paul Mitchell School](https://paulmitchell.edu/), [Benton-Franklin Health District](https://www.bfhd.wa.gov/) and [Molina Healthcare](https://www.molinahealthcare.com/). Local businesses have also donated school supplies, funding and volunteer time to help make the event possible.

“The cost of school supplies is increasing, and families need more support to pay for the requirements needed to participate in all school activities,” organizers said. “We don’t want the cost of a sports physical to be the reason a youth in our community cannot play football, volleyball or any other sport that our school districts offer.”

The event offers free backpacks, school supplies, sport physicals, haircuts, dental care and screenings, vaccine health, book giveaways, activities, resource booths and popcorn.

“Most of the time, the barrier is the cost. However, other barriers can include lack of time or knowledge of how to access other reduced cost resources or even knowing where to go,” they said. “The back-to-school bash acts as a one-stop shop for those needs.”

## Other events

Several other organizations in the area are hosting events as well, including Columbia Center Mall and Tri-Cities Community Health.

Domestic Violence Services of Benton and Franklin Counties is hosting a back-to-school festival Wednesday from 3:30 to 5:30 p.m. with free school supplies, community resources, games and more.

The event will be in the Mariposa Thrift Boutique parking lot, at 3311 W. Clearwater Ave., Ste. B120.

[Tri-Cities Community Health](https://mytcch.org/event/back-to-school-fair-2026/) is handing out over 2,500 free backpacks filled with school supplies for students at their event on Aug. 14 in Pasco. There will also be sports physicals offered for a flat fee unless covered by insurance.

There will be local booths providing informative details for families heading into the school year and providing activities for children. It will run from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. at 715 W. Court St. Pasco.

Saturday, Aug. 15, [the mall](https://www.simon.com/mall/columbia-center/stream/its-a-back-to-school-thing-6285746) is inviting students and families for shopping, interactive games and activities and free treats from select stores and vendors. The first 100 guests to arrive at Center Court inside the mall will receive a free water bottle. The event runs from 1 p.m. to 3 p.m.

The bash will also celebrate incoming 2027 stores like Five Below and Toynime as well as JD Sports which is now open.
