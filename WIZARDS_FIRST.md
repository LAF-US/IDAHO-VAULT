# Wizard's First Rule

## Summary

The first of the numbered Wizard's Rules stated across Terry Goodkind's *Sword of Truth* series, one per book. The First Rule concerns the ease with which people can be led to believe falsehoods.

## Key Facts

**Source book:** *Wizard's First Rule* (book 1)

**Text:**

> People are stupid; given proper motivation, almost anyone will believe almost anything. Because people are stupid, they will believe a lie because they want to believe it's true, or because they are afraid it might be true. People's heads are full of knowledge, facts, and beliefs, and most of it is false, yet they think it all true. People are stupid; they can only rarely tell the difference between a lie and the truth, and yet they are confident they can, and so are all the easier to fool.
