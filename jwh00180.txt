Album caption:  Sawtell's ranch, at the northern end of Henrys Lake. Messrs. Sawtell and Wurtz, the pioneers of this region, have built themselves very comfortable quarters by the side of a very fine, large spring. They catch large quantities of fish from the lake, for which they find a ready market in Virginia City and the mining towns. Large game of all kinds is abundant. Fremont County, Idaho. 1872.
