# Capitol Correspondents Association CCA Letter March 2026\_\_src\_scratch-folder\_\_9c09aba1

On March 4, 2026, [[Capitol Correspondents Association]] president [[Kevin Richert]] and [[Idaho Press Club]] president [[Melissa Davlin]] sent a formal open-meetings complaint letter to [[Joint Finance-Appropriations Committee|JFAC]] co-chairs [[Scott Grow]] and [[Josh Tanner]], triggering a statutory response deadline of approximately March 18, 2026.

## The Letter

- **Date:** March 4, 2026
- **Signatories:** [[Melissa Davlin]] ([[Idaho Press Club]] president), [[Kevin Richert]] ([[Capitol Correspondents Association|CCA]] president)
- **Addressees:** [[Scott Grow]] and [[Josh Tanner]] (JFAC co-chairs)
- **CC:** [[Keith Bybee]] ([[Legislative Services Office|LSO]])
- **Statutory citations:** [[Idaho Code §74-201]], [[Idaho Code §74-207]], [[Idaho Senate Rules|Senate Rule 20]], House Rule 26

## Statutory Timeline

Under [[Idaho Code §74-208]]:

- 14-day cure period begins from written notice
- Statutory response deadline: ~**March 18, 2026**
- Silence = denial
- Civil penalties: $250 (unknowing), $1,500 (knowing), $2,500 (repeat knowing within 12 months)
- Penalties are personal to each member
- AG enforcement only — no private right of action

## CCA Membership Context

Approximately a dozen CCA members were briefed ahead of the letter. The [[Capitol Correspondents Association]] is the press credentialing body for the Idaho Legislature. [[Kevin Richert]] is CCA president; [[Melissa Davlin]] is not a CCA officer but signed as IPC president.
