# The Kingslayer Scene — Jaime, Aerys, and the Iron Throne

> Ned Stark's defining memory of the end of **Robert's Rebellion**: a sworn knight of the **Kingsguard** found seated upon the **Iron Throne**, the king he was sworn to protect dead at its foot. Pure ASOIAF source.
>
> **Provenance.** Web-verified 2026-06-08 (A Wiki of Ice and Fire, Wikipedia, Fandom). Tiers: **[canon]**, ⚠️ **[show]** where noted. Paraphrased; no source text reproduced.

## The setting
- **[canon]** At the close of **Robert's Rebellion (282 AC)**, **Tywin Lannister** sacked **King's Landing**. The **Mad King, Aerys II Targaryen**, held up in the **Red Keep**, raving his standing order: **"Burn them all."**

## The Mad King's wildfire plot
- **[canon]** Aerys had caches of **wildfire** hidden beneath the city, and gave **Jaime Lannister** — his own Kingsguard, then about **seventeen** — two final commands: **bring him his father Tywin's head**, and **set the city alight**, burning King's Landing and every soul in it.

## The killing
- **[canon]** Jaime **slew the pyromancer Rossart** first (to stop the wildfire), then **turned his blade on Aerys**, killing him **at the foot of the Iron Throne** — breaking the holiest vow of the Kingsguard, to protect the king. For this he is forever called the **"Kingslayer."**

## On the throne
- **[canon]** When **Eddard "Ned" Stark** entered the throne room moments later, he found **Jaime seated upon the Iron Throne**, his golden sword still wet with the king's blood, Aerys's corpse in a pool below. Jaime **rose** and remarked he was only **"keeping it warm"** for Robert.

## The name and the misjudgment
- **[canon]** The image **sealed Jaime's reputation** — Ned judged him a man who had murdered his king and **coveted the throne for himself**, and never learned *why* Jaime had really struck (the wildfire plot that would have killed thousands).
- **⚠️ [show]** Years later Jaime gives the fuller account (to **Brienne of Tarth**, at Harrenhal), bitter that he was condemned on sight: *"By what right does the wolf judge the lion?"*
