# Gellick — Guardian of the Emerald

> Keeper of the **Emerald** on **Dread Mountain**, tyrant of the **Dread Gnomes**. One of seven (→ `[[RESEARCH_Deltora-The-Gem-Guardians-2026-06-07]]`). Pure source.
>
> **Provenance.** Web-verified 2026-06-07 (Deltora Quest Wiki, Wikipedia). **[canon-per-series].** Paraphrased.

## Identity & nature

- **[canon]** A vast **Ooze Toad** that secretes a **poisonous slime** — and is **immune to his own poison.**

## Origin & service

- **[canon]** Before King Alton's reign, Gellick offered the **Dread Gnomes** his poison for their **arrowheads** in exchange for service — then **enslaved** them. He served the **Shadow Lord** by supplying the poison for the Grey Guards' **Blisters**, and was set as guardian of the **Emerald**, squatting amid the gnomes' treasure.

## Encounter & fate

- **[canon]** Spying via the **Dreaming Spring**, Lief learns of the gnomes' servitude. His companions' captured Blisters are useless (Gellick is immune); instead **Lief throws his flask of Dreaming Spring water into Gellick's mouth.** Because he is **not pure of heart**, the magic water **transforms him into a tree**, killing him. The freed gnomes (via **Fa-Glin**) give the Emerald for the Belt.
