More than a few Idaho Republicans vote with Democrats more than 75% of the time, according to a new scorecard released this week.

That new measure is part of Stop Idaho RINOs’ Idaho Republican Platform Scorecard. The data may be startling for many who believe Idaho to be a reliably conservative state. [You can visit the new Votes With Democrats Scorecard here](https://idgopscorecard.com/voting-with-dems/).

Consider that Idaho Senate President Pro Tem Kelly Anthon, the top Republican in the Idaho Senate, voted with Democrats roughly 75% of the time during the 2025 legislative session.

More bothersome for some may be the larger context. According to the scorecard data, 71% of Idaho Republicans vote with Democrats 50% of the time or more.

Just as alarming is the voting record of Sen. Jim Guthrie, a Republican from McCammon. He voted with Democrats on nearly 86% of votes in 2025. Guthrie, who chairs the powerful Senate State Affairs Committee, has long had a reputation as a killer of conservative bills.

Republican Rep. Mark Sauter of North Idaho’s District 1 ranked first among House Republicans for voting with Democrats. Sauter, elected in what is considered one of Idaho’s most conservative legislative districts, voted with Democrats on nearly 84% of his 2025 votes.

The scorecard, which shows the voting habits of all statehouse Republicans, also highlights lawmakers who least often side with Democrats.

First-term Republican Rep. Lucas Cayler of Caldwell voted with Democrats on 31% of his votes in 2025. Magic Valley representatives David Leavitt and Clint Hostetler followed, voting with Democrats 32% and 33% of the time, respectively.

Sen. Glenneda Zuiderveld, also from the Magic Valley, posted the lowest score in the Idaho Senate. She voted with Democrats on roughly 38% of votes in 2025. Sen. Christy Zito had the second-lowest Senate score at 37.8%.

To make the data user-friendly and accessible, the scorecard includes district averages and allows users to download a printable version. For transparency, [it also explains its methodology](https://idgopscorecard.com/voting-with-dems/) at the bottom of the page.

Finally, the scorecard serves as a useful validator for other accountability tools, including the Idaho Freedom Index and the Idaho Republican Platform Scorecard. The new data suggests that, with some exceptions, the less often a lawmaker votes with Democrats, the more aligned they tend to be with the Idaho Freedom Index and the Idaho Republican Party Platform.
