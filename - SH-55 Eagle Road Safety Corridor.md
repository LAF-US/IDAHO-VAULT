## Overview

The Idaho Transportation Department has launched a safety pilot to evaluate potential safety improvements and their effects along Eagle Road (State Highway 55), from Interstate 84 to State Highway 44. Eagle Road is Idaho's busiest non-interstate highway.

This multi-year initiative supports ITD's ongoing efforts to reduce crashes and improve traffic flow through targeted, data-driven upgrades.

To date, ITD has removed several unsignalized left-turn movements along the corridor and will activate a variable speed limit system in August 2026.

## Update – VSL Signs

## August 2026 Update

The new Variable Speed Limit (VSL) signs turn on Thursday, August 6! Speed limit changes begin August 17. Read below for more information.

During the first several days of operation, the signs will remain at the current speed limit (55 mph) to give drivers time to become familiar with the new signs and allow the old signs to be removed.

**Starting August 17,** the VSL signs will:

- **Reduce speed limits from 55 mph to 45 mph** during weekday peak travel times from **7 to 9 a.m. and from 4 to 6 p.m.**
- Activate a **flashing beacon** when lower speeds are in effect so drivers know when to slow down.

Additional LED speed feedback signs will alert drivers who are over the speed limit.

The new signage has been installed at key locations between Franklin Road and Chinden Blvd. following a recent safety study and coordination with local stakeholders, community leadership and law enforcement. ITD will monitor traffic flow and crash rates over the two-year pilot project.

## Safety Improvements

This project has included:

- Installation of additional safety signage
- A variable speed limit (VSL) corridor on Eagle Road between Franklin Road and Chinden Boulevard during periods of heavy congestion
	- The VSL system will reduce speed limits from 55 mph to 45 mph during peak congestion times from 7 a.m. to 9 a.m. and from 4 p.m. to 6 p.m**.**
- Removal of three mid-block left turns identified as high-risk areas:
	- Southbound U-turn at Bourbon Street (near Ustick Road)
		- Northbound left turn lane at Baldcypress Street
		- Two-way left turn lane near the Boise River in Eagle

## Community Input

To help spread awareness of the upcoming project, ITD held two public open houses in August 2024 to gather feedback and answer questions from the community.
