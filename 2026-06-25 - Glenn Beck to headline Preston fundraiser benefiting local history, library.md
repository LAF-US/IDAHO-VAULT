PRESTON, Idaho (KIFI) — A special fundraiser featuring Author Glenn Beck supporting two local nonprofits preserving history and expanding educational opportunities.

The fundraising event will benefit two local projects: the restoration of the historic Oneida Stake Academy, preserving an important piece of southeastern Idaho's history, and the construction of a new addition to the Larsen-Sant Public Library, expanding educational opportunities for future generations.

The event will take place Saturday at 3:30 p.m. and will raise money for the restoration of the historic Oneida Stake Academy and the construction of a new addition to the Larsen-Sant Public Library.

During the event, Beck will share stories from American history while showcasing rare historical artifacts and discussing the people and events that helped shape the nation.
