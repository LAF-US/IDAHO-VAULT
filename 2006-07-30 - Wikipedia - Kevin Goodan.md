**Kevin Goodan** is an American poet and professor. His most recent book is *Spot Weather Forecast* ([Alice James Books](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alice_James_Books "Alice James Books"), 2021).[^1] His first book, *In the Ghost-House Acquainted,* won a New England/New York Award [^2] from Alice James Books, as well as the 2005 L.L. Winship/ [PEN New England Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PEN_New_England_Award "PEN New England Award").[^3] His poems have been published in *[Ploughshares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ploughshares "Ploughshares"),[^4] [Colorado Review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_Review "Colorado Review"), Crazyhorse, [Mid-American Review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mid-American_Review "Mid-American Review"), American Poet Magazine, Cutbank,* and other journals.

Raised on the [Flathead Indian Reservation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flathead_Indian_Reservation "Flathead Indian Reservation") in Western Montana, Goodan began working for the [U.S. Forest Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Forest_Service "U.S. Forest Service") at a young age. He has lived in Northern Ireland and western Massachusetts. He received his M.F.A. degree from the [MFA Program for Poets & Writers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MFA_Program_for_Poets_&_Writers "MFA Program for Poets & Writers") at the [University of Massachusetts Amherst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Massachusetts_Amherst "University of Massachusetts Amherst") and his B.A. degree from the [University of Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Montana "University of Montana"). He has taught at the University of Connecticut and as Visiting Writer at [Wesleyan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesleyan_University "Wesleyan University"). He was formerly employed at [Lewis-Clark State College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis-Clark_State_College "Lewis-Clark State College"),[^5] and is currently an adjunct professor at [Colby-Sawyer College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colby-Sawyer_College "Colby-Sawyer College").[^6]
