The Milepost 81 Fire burning along Idaho 55 near Banks. Photo: Courtesy Idaho Transportation Department

Idaho 55 was closed near Banks shortly after midnight on Thursday due to a wildfire burning along the highway, according to the Idaho Transportation Department.

There was no immediate timeline for the closure to be lifted as of Thursday morning. Barricades are placed just north of Banks and near Smiths Ferry.

“SH-55 will reopen once conditions allow us to safely reopen the highway to the traveling public,” said Anna McLean, an ITD spokesperson.

The Milepost 81 fire was detected at about 3 a.m. on Aug. 12, about 1.5 miles northeast of Banks. It has since grown to about 330 acres, according to the Idaho Department of Lands.

![](https://valleylookout.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/08/valleylookout.com-banks-fire-closes-idaho-55-as-containment-timeline-pushed-back-screenshot-2026-08-13-at-9.20.28-am.png)

The Milepost 81 Fire is burning between Idaho 55 and the Banks Lowman Road. Map: Via Watchduty.org

The highway closure comes as firefighters stopped the fire from spread to the north and to the east, instead allowing it to back down toward Idaho 55 to the west and Banks Lowman Road to the south, said Jonathan Luhnow, an IDL spokesperson.

“That will likely be modified as firefighting continues but I can’t say as to when or what that would look like,” Luhnow said of the closure.

![City of McCall - Visiting? 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/57592d17-0c69-4d28-a6e9-c5d629733da1.png)

Containment of the Milepost 81 fire was originally expected by noon on Thursday, but that timeline has since been pushed back to noon on Friday due to “steep terrain with burning snags and rolling material.”

[The National Weather Service has issued a Red Flag Warning](https://forecast.weather.gov/showsigwx.php?warnzone=IDZ011&warncounty=IDC085&firewxzone=IDZ402&local_place1=17%20Miles%20ENE%20Donnelly%20ID&product1=Red+Flag+Warning&lat=44.7855&lon=-115.7394) for the area from 3 p.m. on Thursday to 6 a.m. on Friday, with dry air, thunderstorms, and wind gusts up to 40 miles per hour expected.

There are currently about 70 firefighters battling the fire, as well as two helicopters using water buckets that are being refilled from the Payette River.

![St. Luke's Health Plan - 2026 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/3cffa70b-bd30-43bc-8a9e-62008bbc7dff.png)

IDL is advising rafters, kayakers, and other recreational users to avoid the Payette River near Banks to allow free access for two helicopters to fill water buckets. Drone should also not be flown in the area to avoid disrupting firefighting operations.

The cause of the fire has not yet been determined and remains under investigation.
