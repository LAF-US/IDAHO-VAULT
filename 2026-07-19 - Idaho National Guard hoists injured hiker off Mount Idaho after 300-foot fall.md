BOISE, [[Idaho]] (CBS2) — A climber who fell about 300 feet on Mount Idaho in Custer County was rescued and flown to a hospital Friday afternoon after suffering a head injury.

On July 18 at 12:18 p.m., the Custer County Search and Rescue coordinator was notified of a call received by Custer County Dispatch reporting that an individual had fallen on Mount Idaho in the Lost River Range.

Custer County Search and Rescue enlisted help from Air St. Luke’s 1 out of Boise and Idaho National Guard TALON70. TALON70 hoisted the individual off the mountain and brought him to waiting Custer County SAR ground teams, along with Air St. Luke’s helicopter and medics.

The individual was then transported to Eastern Idaho Medical Center.

Rescuers credited the Idaho National Guard and Air St. Luke’s for completing the rescue in “spontaneous inclement weather conditions over the Lost River Range,” and noted that Custer County SAR is a volunteer organization.
