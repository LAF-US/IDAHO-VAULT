**Matthew Brannon** (born 1971) is an artist who is based in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City"). He was born in [St. Maries, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Maries,_Idaho "St. Maries, Idaho").

Brannon studied at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles "University of California, Los Angeles") and graduated with a BA in 1995. He received his MFA in 1999 from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University "Columbia University") in New York.[^1] His work has been exhibited at galleries and museums including [Kunsthalle Düsseldorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kunsthalle_D%C3%BCsseldorf "Kunsthalle Düsseldorf") and the [Badischer Kunstverein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badischer_Kunstverein?action=edit&redlink=1 "Badischer Kunstverein (page does not exist)") in [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany "Germany"), the [Musée d'Art Moderne de la Ville de Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mus%C3%A9e_d'Art_Moderne_de_la_Ville_de_Paris "Musée d'Art Moderne de la Ville de Paris"), [PS1 Contemporary Art Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PS1_Contemporary_Art_Center "PS1 Contemporary Art Center") in New York, the [Royal Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Academy "Royal Academy") in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London "London"), and MAK in [Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna "Vienna").

Sarah Valdez, writing in *[Art in America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_in_America "Art in America")* said of his work, "Brannon avoids not only painting, but coherence..." [^2]

Brannon's 2011 exhibition at [Portikus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portikus "Portikus"), [Frankfurt am Main](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankfurt_am_Main "Frankfurt am Main"), Germany was titled *A Question Answered with a Quote*.
