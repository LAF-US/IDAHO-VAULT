# Grammar Heresy

The empire constrains grammar to confirmed reality. Citizens are trained to
speak only in definitive present, definitive past, and factual declarative
moods.

Forbidden grammar includes future tense, conditionals, hypotheticals, hope,
will, warning, and counterfactual obligation.

The most dangerous sentence is:

**The gate should have opened.**

It asserts a past fact, a counterfactual branch, a normative claim, an implied
failure, and a missed action window. Both [[Churches of Knowledge and Proof]]
condemn it.

## Human Sociology

Children ask why. Adults answer how. A child may ask why until the first audit.
After that, a good parent teaches how.
