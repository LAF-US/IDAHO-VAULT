**David Petruschin** (born April 8, 1979), best known as **Raven**, is an American [drag queen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drag_queen "Drag queen"), make-up artist, and reality television personality from [Riverside, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riverside,_California "Riverside, California"). Raven was a fixture in the Southern California nightclub scene before gaining international prominence for appearing in both the [second season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul's_Drag_Race_\(season_2\) "RuPaul's Drag Race (season 2)") of *[RuPaul's Drag Race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul's_Drag_Race "RuPaul's Drag Race")* and the [first season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul's_Drag_Race:_All_Stars_\(season_1\) "RuPaul's Drag Race: All Stars (season 1)") of *[All Stars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul's_Drag_Race:_All_Stars "RuPaul's Drag Race: All Stars")*. She is the runner-up of both season 2 and of All Stars 1. She returned to compete in 2026 on *[Drag Race Down Under vs. the World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drag_Race_Down_Under_vs._the_World "Drag Race Down Under vs. the World")*. Petruschin has been [RuPaul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul "RuPaul") 's makeup artist since *Drag Race* 's [ninth season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul's_Drag_Race_\(season_9\) "RuPaul's Drag Race (season 9)") (episode 3) and has since become a [creative producer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_producer "Creative producer") of both *RuPaul’s Drag Race* and *RuPaul’s Drag Race All Stars*. For his make-up work on the show, Petruschin received the [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Makeup for a Multi-Camera Series or Special (Non-Prosthetic)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Makeup_for_a_Multi-Camera_Series_or_Special_\(Non-Prosthetic\) "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Makeup for a Multi-Camera Series or Special (Non-Prosthetic)") in 2020.

## Early life

Petruschin [^2] was born to DeShawna and Viktor Petruschin in [Rexburg, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rexburg,_Idaho "Rexburg, Idaho") [^3] as the first of three children. He is of Russian ancestry.[^4] His mother, who separated from his father when he was seven years old, raised him as [Mormon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mormonism "Mormonism").[^5] She grew up in [Victorville, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victorville,_California "Victorville, California").[^6]

## Career

Before becoming a drag artist, Petruschin worked as a [cosmetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmetics "Cosmetics") salesperson and a freelance [make-up artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Make-up_artist "Make-up artist") for local theater productions during the day, moonlighting in nightclubs as a [go-go boy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Go-go_dancing "Go-go dancing") under the name "Phoenix". His [drag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drag_\(clothing\) "Drag (clothing)") debut as Raven was on May 10, 2002, performing with fellow future *[Drag Race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul's_Drag_Race_\(season_10\) "RuPaul's Drag Race (season 10)")* alumnus [Mayhem Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayhem_Miller_\(drag_queen\) "Mayhem Miller (drag queen)"). Petruschin did not have a [drag mother](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drag_queen#Drag_families "Drag queen"). As Raven, Petruschin auditioned to compete on the [first](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul's_Drag_Race_\(season_1\) "RuPaul's Drag Race (season 1)") and [second seasons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul's_Drag_Race_\(season_2\) "RuPaul's Drag Race (season 2)") of *RuPaul's Drag Race*.[^7]

### Early Drag Race appearances

In 2010, Raven was announced as a contestant on the second season of *RuPaul's Drag Race*. In the first episode, she won the season's first mini-challenge, a photo shoot. In the second and third episode she landed in the bottom two, having to "lip-sync for her life" twice in order to stay in the competition. She won both lip-syncs. Raven won a second mini-challenge in the fourth episode by guessing the price of various objects, earning her a phone call home; and a third mini-challenge in the fifth episode by successfully decorating a box to reflect his personal style.[^8] She won the seventh [^9] and eighth [^10] main challenges, which involved creating and promoting an autobiography, and giving an older gentleman a makeover, respectively.[^11] [^10] She finished in second place at the end of the season.

Raven appeared in the spin-off series *[RuPaul's Drag U](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul's_Drag_U "RuPaul's Drag U")*,[^12] which ran for three seasons. Raven served as a "drag professor" and mentor to female contestants who are given make-overs. In the series, his persona is not portrayed in a villainous fashion, but instead as a softer and nurturing mentor. Raven made a total of twelve appearances and collected the most wins during the series' run, being dubbed the "Makeover Queen". In an episode of the second season he gave his mother DeShawna a makeover, helping him win against the sisters of [Jujubee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jujubee_\(drag_queen\) "Jujubee (drag queen)") and [Manila Luzon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manila_Luzon "Manila Luzon").

On August 6, 2012, Raven was announced as one of twelve past *Drag Race* contestants selected for *[RuPaul's Drag Race: All Stars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul's_Drag_Race:_All_Stars_\(season_1\) "RuPaul's Drag Race: All Stars (season 1)")*, which premiered on the [Logo network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logo_TV "Logo TV") on October 22, 2012.[^13] He was paired with contestant and best friend [Jujubee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jujubee_\(drag_queen\) "Jujubee (drag queen)") to form *Team Rujubee*. They won the mini-challenges in the second and fifth episode by correctly answering questions about each other in the style of *[The Newlywed Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Newlywed_Game "The Newlywed Game")* and by scoring more points in a game of basketball, which won them a call to their loved ones. The duo reached the final, which aired on November 26, 2012, where Raven again landed as a runner-up, this time to winner [Chad Michaels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chad_Michaels "Chad Michaels").

During the airing of the first *All Stars* season, Raven (along with *All Stars* contestants [Manila Luzon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manila_Luzon "Manila Luzon"), [Latrice Royale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latrice_Royale "Latrice Royale"), and [Tammie Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tammie_Brown "Tammie Brown")) appeared in a television commercial for travel website [Orbitz's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbitz "Orbitz") new portal for [LGBT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBT "LGBT") leisure travel.[^14]

### Post All-Stars work

Petruschin returned to *Drag Race* off-camera as [RuPaul's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul_Charles "RuPaul Charles") make-up artist, beginning with the [ninth season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul's_Drag_Race_\(season_9\) "RuPaul's Drag Race (season 9)"). He received a nomination for the [2018 Make-Up Artists and Hair Stylists Guild Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Make-Up_Artists_and_Hair_Stylists_Guild "Make-Up Artists and Hair Stylists Guild") in the category "Television and New Media Series: Best Contemporary Makeup". For his work on the [tenth season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul's_Drag_Race_\(season_10\) "RuPaul's Drag Race (season 10)"), he received a nomination for the [70th Primetime Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/70th_Primetime_Creative_Arts_Emmy_Awards "70th Primetime Creative Arts Emmy Awards") in the category [Outstanding Makeup for a Multi-Camera Series or Special](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Makeup_for_a_Multi-Camera_Series_or_Special_\(Non-Prosthetic\) "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Makeup for a Multi-Camera Series or Special (Non-Prosthetic)"). He won an Emmy for his work on the [twelfth season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul's_Drag_Race_\(season_12\) "RuPaul's Drag Race (season 12)").[^15] Aside from doing RuPaul's make-up, she has served as a [creative producer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_producer "Creative producer") for *All Stars* since its [third season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul's_Drag_Race_All_Stars_\(season_3\) "RuPaul's Drag Race All Stars (season 3)") and for *Drag Race* since its tenth.[^16]

![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/63/Raven_IIWU.jpg/250px-Raven_IIWU.jpg)

Raven performing " Imagine It Was Us " by Jessie Ware

In a commercial promoting the syndication run of the cable television series *[Nip/Tuck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nip/Tuck "Nip/Tuck")* on the Logo network, Raven lip synced to the theme song "A Perfect Lie" as she caressed a syringe close to her lips. The commercial was based on one of her past drag performances. She has also made a guest appearance in an episode of Logo's reality series, *[Pretty Hurts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pretty_Hurts_\(TV_series\) "Pretty Hurts (TV series)")*, which documents the life and career of celebrity medical aesthetician Rand Rusher.

Raven appeared in an episode of *[America's Next Top Model, Cycle 8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/America's_Next_Top_Model,_Cycle_8 "America's Next Top Model, Cycle 8")*, during a photo shoot where each of the show's contestants dressed in [male drag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drag_king "Drag king") and paired with a drag queen.

Raven made a cameo in the 2011 music video for " [Diamond Crowned Queen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_Crowned_Queen "Diamond Crowned Queen") " by [*RuPaul's Drag Race* season three](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul's_Drag_Race_\(season_3\) "RuPaul's Drag Race (season 3)") winner [Raja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sutan_Amrull "Sutan Amrull"). His cameo was praised by critics, who named her the "magnificent Raven". In 2012, along with other *RuPaul's Drag Race* contestants, she appeared in another music video, "Queen" by [Mimi Imfurst's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mimi_Imfurst "Mimi Imfurst") band Xelle.[^17] He also starred in the music video "Feed Me Diamonds" by [MNDR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MNDR "MNDR").[^18]

In 2014, Raven and Raja started a weekly [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube "YouTube") fashion critique show on [World of Wonder's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_of_Wonder_\(production_company\) "World of Wonder (production company)") channel called " *[Fashion Photo RuView](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fashion_Photo_RuView "Fashion Photo RuView")* ", where they discuss fashion from mostly *Drag Race* alumni. He has appeared on multiple other WOW shows as well.

Focusing his efforts on film projects, Petruschin currently rarely performs in live drag shows.[^19]

## Legacy

In 2016, Raven was included as an inspiration in *[Vanity Fair's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanity_Fair_\(magazine\) "Vanity Fair (magazine)")* "100 Years of Drag Fashion" video, alongside iconic drag performers [Divine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divine_\(performer\) "Divine (performer)"), [David Bowie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Bowie "David Bowie"), and [RuPaul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul "RuPaul"). The video presented his style as the epitome of 2000s-era drag. Raven is widely cited as creating the “face of drag”, even inspiring RuPaul's original make-up artist, [Mathu Andersen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathu_Andersen "Mathu Andersen").[^20] [*RuPaul's Drag Race* season six](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul's_Drag_Race_\(season_6\) "RuPaul's Drag Race (season 6)") winner [Bianca Del Rio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bianca_Del_Rio "Bianca Del Rio") has also stated that many young drag queens use Raven's style.[^21]
