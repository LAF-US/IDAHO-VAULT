# The Guardian (Fardeep) — Keeper of the Diamond

> Keeper of the last gem, the **Diamond**, in the **Valley of the Lost** — a cursed man bound to his own vices. One of seven (→ `[[RESEARCH_Deltora-The-Gem-Guardians-2026-06-07]]`). Pure source.
>
> **Provenance.** Web-verified 2026-06-07 (Deltora Quest Wiki, Wikipedia). **[canon-per-series].** Paraphrased.

## Identity & origin

- **[canon]** Once **Fardeep**, owner of the **Champion Inn at Rithmere.** The **Shadow Lord** transformed him into the **Guardian** of the Diamond and **cursed him to pose as King Endon** — so that would-be resisters who found him would believe the king had **joined the Shadow Lord** and despair.

## The four "pets"

- **[canon]** He is bound to **four monsters — Pride, Envy, Hate, and Greed —** living growths **attached to him by cords of flesh.** They are literally **parts of himself**: the vices that grew in him, made manifest.

## The game & the gem

- **[canon]** To win the **Diamond** one must **guess the Guardian's true name**; lose, and you are **trapped in the Valley.** The Diamond **curses any who steal it** rather than win it fairly.
- **[canon]** **Lief** answers **"Endon"** — the pose-name. Earlier, **Doom** had guessed "Endon" too; believing the king had defected to the Shadow Lord, Doom **left without the Diamond** and went on to found the **resistance.**
