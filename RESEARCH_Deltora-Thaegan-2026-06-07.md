# Thaegan — Sorceress of the Lake of Tears

> One of the Shadow Lord's most powerful servants — the sorceress who drowned a city and bound the Guardian **[[RESEARCH_Deltora-Soldeen-2026-06-07\|Soldeen]]**. Mother of the **[[RESEARCH_Deltora-Thaegans-Brood-2026-06-07\|brood]]**. Pure source.
>
> **Provenance.** Web-verified 2026-06-07 (Deltora Quest Wiki, Wikipedia). **[canon-per-series].** Paraphrased.

## Identity & allegiance

- **[canon]** A sorceress in the service of the **Shadow Lord**, who **increased her magical power tenfold.** Infamous above all for her **curses**, "limited only by the sorceress's imagination and cruelty."

## Powers

- **[canon]** **Teleportation** within her territory, **telekinesis** (striking through the air), **levitation**, and **transfiguration** — and a vast repertoire of curses of wildly varying strength.

## The Lake of Tears curse

- **[canon]** Over **a hundred years** before the Ruby's recovery, Thaegan — unable to bear the **beauty of the city of D'Or** — transformed it into the **Lake of Tears**, turning its people into writhing sea-creatures and forcing the city's leader **Nanion** to serve as the serpent **[[RESEARCH_Deltora-Soldeen-2026-06-07\|Soldeen]]**. When the **Ralad** people protested, she **robbed them and all their descendants of their voices.**

## Death

- **[canon]** Thaegan is killed when **Kree** (Jasmine's raven) strikes the one spot her magic does not armour — the **little finger she casts with** — drawing blood. With her death, **all her spells break**: the Ralads regain their voices, and the Lake and its denizens revert to the **city and citizens of D'Or.**
- **[canon]** She also sets **Lief a riddle** — an arithmetic problem reckoning herself and her children in a cave — with their lives the forfeit.

## The brood

- **[canon]** Mother of **thirteen monstrous children** (→ **[[RESEARCH_Deltora-Thaegans-Brood-2026-06-07]]**), who pursue Lief's company for vengeance across the quest.

> ⚠️ **Adaptation note:** in the anime the Shadow Lord **revives Thaegan through her ring** after a rat carries it to him — a screen continuation, not the books'.
