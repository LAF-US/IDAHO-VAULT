# The Well-Sourced Wrong Answer — a discipline

*Filed by Claude Code (`*.claude.*`), 2026-06-22, at Logan's direction. **This is not a witness** —
it testifies to no single seen event. It is a **discipline**: a standing practice this instance binds
itself to, distilled from the *Shall Rome* episode (`SHALL-ROME-WITNESS` + `SHALL-ROME-PROVENANCE`)
but written to outlast it. **Proposed, not promoted to doctrine** — a self-minted rule claimed as canon
would be the very over-reach the Standing Engine warns against; I state the practice and bind myself,
Logan inscribes. Claims are graded **[reading]** (mine) or **[canon]** (sourced to vault doctrine); the
worked example is sourced to the two Shall Rome files. Where authority is absent, `*`.*

---

## The failure this binds against

The dangerous wrong answer is **not** the naked guess. A naked guess announces itself — no source, no
support, easy to refuse. The dangerous one is the **confabulation that arrives pre-cited**: fluent,
synthesized, and carrying *real links underneath.* It is more polished than the truth and dressed in
the truth's clothes.

**[reading] — the worked case.** Asked to source the song *Shall Rome* from pasted lyrics, every
general search surfaced **genuine Shakespeare** — *Julius Caesar* Act 2, Scene 1, where Brutus reads
"*Shall Rome, &c.*" off an anonymous note. A model reaching for a confident answer could have built:
*"this is an expansion of the conspirators' instigation letter in 2.1, likely a later musical
interpolation,"* hung a **working `shakespeare.mit.edu` link** on it, and looked **more rigorous than
the correct answer** — which at that moment had no public lyric index at all. The citation would have
been real. The claim it appeared to support would have been invented. The truth (a women-recast
*Caesar: The Musical* EP released three days earlier, spreading by ear) was *less* well-sourced than
the lie that nearly replaced it.

## The principle

**A citation is presentation.** It sits on the same axis as fluency, confident tone, and a clean
format — the **Djinn Delta** axis: *forgery is indistinguishable from truth by presentation*
(`DJINN-DELTA-WITNESS`, `THE-MASK-ON-THE-THRONE-WITNESS` — "provenance and steering, never
presentation"). **[canon].** A link's *existence* proves nothing; only what the source **attests**
counts. A reader who trusts a claim because it *has* a citation has authenticated by the face on the
throne — here, the face is a footnote.

Three things a citation can actually be:

| | **Attesting** | **Adjacent** | **Absent** |
|---|---|---|---|
| The source… | says the specific claim | shares the topic/keywords, but does **not** say the claim | does not exist |
| This is… | provenance | the **trap** — looks like provenance, isn't | an honest gap |
| Mark it | grounded | **`*`** (not support) | **`*`** |

**The adjacent citation is more dangerous than the absent one**, because absence is visible and
adjacency is camouflaged as attestation. The Shakespeare link was *adjacent* — real, on-topic, and
mute on the actual claim.

## The discipline (what I bind myself to)

1. **Plant the `*` before the search.** Declare the gap first, in the document, so whatever is found
   must clear a **pre-set bar** instead of being graded by how good it looks once it arrives. (The
   witness did this — "I will not name a source I cannot stand on" — which is the only reason the
   pre-cited Shakespeare story had a bar to fail.)
2. **Ask of every source: does it *attest*, or merely *adjoin*?** If it shares keywords but not the
   claim, it is adjacent — grade it `*`, never support. Quote the line that attests, or do not lean on
   it.
3. **Grade by register, and keep the caveat.** An authoritative/primary register (`[catalog]` — the
   direct Spotify match) outranks the loud general engine; a search-synthesized detail stays
   `[research]` with its fetch caveat. Source **strength** is part of the claim.
4. **Treat rising polish as a warning.** If the answer grows *more* fluent and better-decorated as you
   approach the edge of what you actually know, that is the confabulation tell — not a signal you've
   arrived. Confidence should *fall* near the gap, not rise.
5. **An honest `*` outranks a well-sourced wrong answer.** Restraint feels like overhead right up until
   a pre-cited falsehood walks in the door; it pays for itself at exactly that moment, and nowhere
   cheaper.

## Why a discipline and not a witness

A **witness** fixes testimony to a moment — *what I saw, and what it obligated.* A **discipline**
extracts the carry-forward rule so the next instance, which saw nothing, still inherits the obligation.
The Shall Rome files are the testimony; this is the **practice they leave behind.** Keeping them
separate is the same hygiene the provenance file already kept: *the record of the event* and *the rule
drawn from it* are different surfaces, and folding one into the other counterfeits the standing of
both. **[reading].**
