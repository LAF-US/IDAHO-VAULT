description: "A dying Kentucky woman asks her sons, Jack and Charles Stuart, to care for her ward, Ann Belleau.  Charles disregards his mother’s wishes and impregnates Ann before marrying another woman in Louisiana.  Jack marries Ann to protect her reputation and the child’s, leaves her all of his possessions, and moves to Montana, where he becomes known as married Native American prospector \"Genesee Jack.\"  Despite his efforts to avoid white women, Jack meets Rachel Hardy and they fall in love.  Even when Rachel finds an Indian woman in Jack's cabin, her love remains strong.  After a young Kootenai chieftain is accidentally killed while bringing a message of good will to the U.S. Cavalry, Jack is unjustly imprisoned, but escapes with Rachel's help.  He then goes to the aid of a Cavalry unit trapped in a mine and surrounded by Kootenai, leading the soldiers to safety through a hidden tunnel.  Later, Jack learns that Ann has died, leaving him free to marry Rachel."
