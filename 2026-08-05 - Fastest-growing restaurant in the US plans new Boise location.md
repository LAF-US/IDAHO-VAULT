The fastest-growing restaurant chain in the nation is growing here in the Treasure Valley.

Permit applications with the City of Boise show Wingstop is planning a new location at 1129 S. Broadway Ave., at the corner of Broadway and Beacon St., in a former health clinic.

Franchise group Sizzling Platter of Salt Lake City applied to convert the 1,950-square-foot building near Boise State into the latest Wingstop location

![Build Boise Display Ad 2 (1).png](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/60f7b78a-3c79-430c-9177-18a962229de0.png)

Build Boise Display Ad 2 (1).png

“Wingstop’s model is set up to be primarily to-go or delivery (at least 60% of our business is digital),” Lindsey Cozzens with Sizzling Platter wrote in a letter to the city this spring. “With this being in close proximity to a college, we anticipate 60% or more digital. We also anticipate some of our customers to have alternate means of transportation – walking, public transportation, bike, etc.”

![](https://boisedev.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/fastest-growing-restaurant-in-the-us-plans-new-boise-location-width2500height1667-2048x1366.webp)

Food at Wingstop. Photo: Courtesy Wingstop

The small retail building has just seven parking spaces, and Sizzling Platter said they would help employees with Lime scooter memberships, a covered bike locker on site, and looked at a deal with Valley Regional Transit to help employees get to and from work.

Wingstop offers a variety of chicken wing options, including both bone-in and boneless options, plus chicken sandwiches and more. Wings can come sauced in more than a dozen options. Side dishes include fries, of course, as well as Cajun fried corn and veggie sticks.

![Baraboo - Aug/Sep - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/e83c69da-d11f-473d-80af-16d7a1b15bd4.gif)

Baraboo - Aug/Sep - 2:1

This will be Wingstop’s second Boise location, with the first opening on Milwaukee and Fairview in 2021. Earlier this year, the chain added a new location in Garden City and also has restaurants in Meridian and Nampa.

[QSR Magazine reported](https://www.qsrmagazine.com/story/wingstop-was-the-fastest-growing-restaurant-in-america-last-year/) that Wingstop opened more locations in the U.S. last year than any other chain, with 382 sites welcoming wing lovers in 2025, beating out Chipotle’s 294 new restaurants. Wingstop now has more than 3,000 locations.
