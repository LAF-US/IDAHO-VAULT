What is Experts on Camera?

Water systems across the U.S. are aging, and communities in rural areas with shrinking populations are struggling to fund and maintain their drinking water infrastructure. In many regions, water systems that need repairs also face climate change-related impacts from flooding, wildfires, and droughts. **On June 16, 2026, SciLine interviewed** [Dr. Zia Lyle](https://www.linkedin.com/in/zia-lyle/), a postdoctoral researcher at the at the Institute for Risk Management and Insurance Innovation at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.
