# House Targaryen

> The bloodline: a minor Valyrian family that survived the apocalypse on a prophecy, conquered a continent on dragonback, ruled it ~300 years, and broke on its own madness. One of a trilogy with `[[RESEARCH_A-Song-of-Ice-and-Fire-The-Dragons-2026-06-02]]` (the weapon) and `[[RESEARCH_A-Song-of-Ice-and-Fire-Aegons-Conquest-2026-06-02]]` (the war).
>
> **Provenance.** Tiers flagged: **[canon]**, **[legend]**, **[theory]** (incl. fan theories the books seed but do not confirm in print), **[show]**, **[reading]**. ⚠️ awoiaf/Wikipedia blocked direct fetch; claims rest on search-extracts — verify before publication.

## Origins

- **[canon]** One of ~40 Valyrian **dragonlord families** — and *"far from the most powerful."* Seat: **Dragonstone**, the Freehold's western outpost in the narrow sea.
- **[canon]** They alone of the dragonlords survived the Doom **long-term** because **Daenys the Dreamer** foresaw it and her father **Lord Aenar** moved the family and five dragons to Dragonstone in **114 BC**, twelve years before the cataclysm. Rivals called it cowardice. *(A few dragonlords outside Valyria outlasted the cataclysm only briefly — e.g. Aurion of Qohor; see the Doom note.)*

## Heraldry, words, and the look

- **[canon]** **Sigil:** a **red three-headed dragon on black.** **Words:** *"Fire and Blood."* The three heads are commonly linked to **Aegon and his sister-wives Visenya and Rhaenys**, who conquered on three dragons.
- **[canon]** **The Valyrian look:** silver-gold / platinum hair and **purple/violet eyes** — *"few but the blood of the dragon have purple eyes."* ⚠️ **Not universal**: e.g., Queen Alysanne had blue eyes; children of mixed marriages (e.g., Baelor, of a Martell mother) varied.

## Incest & the Doctrine of Exceptionalism

- **[canon]** Targaryens wed **brother to sister** (and close kin) for generations to keep the dragonlord blood "pure" — an abomination to the **Faith of the Seven**, a friction that helped spark the **Faith Militant uprising** under Aenys I / Maegor.
- **[canon]** The **Doctrine of Exceptionalism** (worked out by **King Jaehaerys I** with **Septons Oswyck and Barth**, **54 AC**) resolved it: incest is an abomination for ordinary men *"born of Andalos,"* but Targaryens **"are not like other men"** — dragonriders of Valyrian descent — and so an **exception** applies. ⚠️ Often misattributed to Barth alone; he assisted, Jaehaerys drove it.

## Madness — "the coin"

- **[canon]** The proverb — spoken to Barristan Selmy by **Jaehaerys II** (Daenerys's grandfather): *"every time a Targaryen is born the gods flip a coin"* — the gloss that **madness and greatness are two sides of one coin.** ⚠️ This is **Jaehaerys II**, not Jaehaerys I the Conciliator named above.
- **[canon]** Cases: **Maegor the Cruel**; **Aerion Brightflame** (believed himself a dragon, drank wildfire to "transform," died); **Aerys II "the Mad King"** (paranoia, voices, burning men alive — ⚠️ "schizophrenia" is a modern fan gloss, not GRRM's text).
- **[theory/unresolved]** **Daenerys's** sanity is an **open question in the published novels** (Barristan watches her for "the taint"); the show's "mad queen" turn is **not yet print canon.**

## Dragon dreams & prophecy

- **[canon/legend]** **Dragon dreams** — prophetic visions in some of the blood (Daenys's were said to fill a lost book, *Signs and Portents*).
- **[theory-laden]** **Rhaegar** was obsessed with prophecy — the **prince that was promised** / **Azor Ahai** reborn, and *"the dragon must have three heads."* Maester Aemon once thought Rhaegar the promised prince; Rhaegar later fixed on his son Aegon. Identities remain unresolved in canon. (→ the religions note, R'hllor.)

## The dynasty (≈300 years)

- **[canon]** **Aegon I the Conqueror** (the Conquest); **Maegor the Cruel** (tyranny, war with the Faith); **Jaehaerys I the Conciliator** & **Good Queen Alysanne** (the long stabilizing golden age — the era of Septon Barth as Hand); the **Dance of the Dragons (129–131 AC)** — civil war between **Aegon II** (greens) and **Rhaenyra** (blacks), Viserys I's named heir — which **killed nearly all the dragons** and began the decline; **Aegon III the Dragonbane** (under whom the last dragon died, 153 AC); a long twilight to **Aerys II the Mad King.**

## The fall — Robert's Rebellion

- **[canon]** **Rhaegar** crowned **Lyanna Stark** queen of love and beauty over his wife **Elia Martell**; Lyanna's disappearance with Rhaegar lit the war. ⚠️ *"Abduction"* is the victors' framing; the books strongly imply she went willingly, and **R+L=J** (Jon Snow's parentage) is heavily seeded but **not explicitly confirmed in the published books.**
- **[canon]** Aerys II had **Rickard and Brandon Stark** killed; the rebellion escalated. At the **Sack of King's Landing**, Lannister forces turned on the Targaryens; **Gregor Clegane and Amory Lorch murdered Elia and Rhaegar's children**; **Jaime Lannister ("Kingslayer") killed Aerys II.** Rhaegar had already died to Robert at the **Trident.**
- **[canon]** Survivors smuggled off Dragonstone to the Free Cities: **Viserys III** (the self-styled **"Beggar King"**) and the newborn **Daenerys** (**"Stormborn,"** her mother **Rhaella** dying in the birth-storm) — the seed of the restoration arc and the saga's title-bearing "last dragon[s]."
