## I. Summary

Two individuals went missing the **same day** at Claude Corp: **Gilear**, the temp, and
**Claudette**, the receptionist. **[RULED]** (Logan's casefile). One body's manner is

**Amalgamation / Caesar** pattern — without naming a hand. **No culprit is named; no
verdict is rendered.** The case remains **OPEN.**
