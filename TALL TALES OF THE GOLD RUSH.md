**__TALL TALES OF THE GOLD RUSH__**
Reports of gold in the 1860s drew thousands of people into the deep Idaho mountains -- and the fact early promoters often exaggerated the details of frontier life certainly helped. But it often turns out the real people behind the myths are even more fascinating than their legends. In vignettes of characters like a trespassing prospector (Elias Pierce), tireless packers ("Packer John" Welch, Lloyd Magruder, Jesus Urquidez), a wandering artist (Charles Ostner) and his patient wife (Julia Ostner), frontier politicians (Israel Cowan, James Hawley), a fearsome lawman (Orlando "Rube" Robbins) and his suffragette wife (Corilla Robbins), *Idaho Experience* uncovers the larger-than-life tall tales and true stories of the legendary pioneer men and women behind Idaho's rough-and-tumble gold rush era and its romantic reputation.
