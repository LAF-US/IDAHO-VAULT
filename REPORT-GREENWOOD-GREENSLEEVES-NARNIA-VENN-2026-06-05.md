# Report: Greenwood, Greensleeves, Narnia, and the Venn of Standing

## Executive Summary

The greenwood braid now has enough overlapping circles to behave like a usable campaign map rather than a loose mood-board.

The core finding is:

> Power-bearing objects, songs, bloodlines, offices, and greenwood forms can all carry force, but force does not settle standing.

This report distinguishes the major circles:

- **Greenwood**: living material before final authorization.
- **Greensleeves**: green sign carried by garment, desire, print, and tune.
- **Cooper's Old Ones**: authorized office under the Light.
- **Cooper's Wild Magic**: nonaligned natural sovereignty, distinct from Nix's Free Magic.
- **Nimmo's Children of the Red King**: inherited endowment, capacity without automatic office.
- **Narnia**: sung creation, Aslanic authority, Deep/Deeper Magic, and lawful kingship under the Emperor-over-the-Sea.

The useful center of the Venn diagram:

> A green, wooden, musical, inherited, or cosmic object may carry power. The decisive question remains: who has standing to use it, shape it, claim it, sing it, inherit it, or rule by it?

## Circle One: Greenwood

Greenwood is not merely "green."

Greenwood is living material before final authorization. It is branch before plank, sap before lumber, tree before instrument, wood before house, scaffold, staff, bow, mask, cage, or pyre.

The Greenwood Gospel identified a polymorphic sequence:

- **Green Man**: the forest given a human face.
- **Wicker Man**: woven wood made into sacrificial architecture.
- **Greenwitch**: made green body that wakes into will.
- **Emerald Lumber**: green material industrialized into secret architecture.
- **Green Violin**: greenwood refined into voice and escape.
- **Fiddler's Green**: green music-country beyond labor and death.

The standing question:

> Who may cut the branch, shape the body, build the house, tune the instrument, light the fire, or claim the song?

Greenwood is the material axis of the map.

## Circle Two: Greensleeves

Greensleeves shifts the green from wood to cloth and music.

The earliest surviving reference is not a birth certificate but a paper threshold: Richard Jones's 3 September 1580 Stationers' Register license for a now-lost ballad, paired that same day with Edward White's answer ballad. The existence of an answer on the same day supports the better reading that "new" is print-market language, not proof of first composition.

The Greensleeves Gospel defined the central phrase:

> Greensleeves is green worn at the threshold of consent.

The singer desires. The Lady withholds. The song petitions, complains, spends, repeats, and circulates. None of that grants standing over the beloved.

The tune then becomes a reusable vessel:

- courtly complaint,
- answer ballad,
- moralized version,
- Shakespearean public reference,
- Christmas carol through "What Child Is This?",
- Cooper's threshold tune on an old flute beside the Sign of Wood.

The standing question:

> Who may name the wearer, print the song, answer the song, moralize the song, sanctify the tune, or use the melody at a threshold?

Greensleeves is the music and social-signal axis of the map.

---

## Circle Three: Cooper's Old Ones

In Susan Cooper's *The Dark Is Rising* sequence, the Old Ones are not simply gifted children and not merely nature spirits.

They are office-bearers under the Light.

Will Stanton's discovery is therefore unlike the common "child has a power" pattern. He learns that he belongs to an immortal, time-loose order charged with standing in a cosmic conflict. Merriman is not merely a mentor; he is an elder office-holder in that same order.

The Old Ones gather and guard the Things of Power:

- the Six Signs,
- the Grail,
- the Harp,
- the Sword.

These are not ordinary magic items. They are material offices: objects whose use depends on right timing, right bearer, right provenance, and right cosmic alignment.

The standing question:

> Who may hold a Thing of Power physically, and who may wield it lawfully?

Cooper's Old Ones are the office-under-Light axis of the map.

---

## Circle Four: Cooper's Wild Magic

Cooper's Wild Magic must remain distinct from Nix's Free Magic.

Cooper's Wild Magic is nonaligned natural sovereignty. It is not evil merely because it is not Light. It is not servant merely because the Light may need it. It belongs to older nature, local power, sea, green ritual, creaturely will, and relation.

The Greenwitch embodies this distinction. She is made from leaves and branches, cast into the sea, and awakened into danger and grief. She can be approached, offended, pitied, or manipulated, but she is not simply owned by Light or Dark.

This circle is important because it prevents the map from flattening into a two-party war.

The standing question:

> What powers are not under your office even when your cause is righteous?

Cooper's Wild Magic is the third-standing natural-sovereignty axis of the map.

---

## Circle Five: Nimmo's Children of the Red King

Jenny Nimmo's *Children of the Red King* adds a different model: inherited endowment.

The endowed children possess powers through descent from the Red King. Charlie Bone's power, for example, is capacity through lineage, not office under a cosmic order.

That makes Nimmo useful as a contrast to Cooper:

- Cooper's Will has office under the Light.
- Nimmo's endowed children have inherited capacity.

Capacity is real. It can be dangerous, useful, beautiful, or coercive. But capacity is not the same as standing. A child may be able to do something without being authorized to do it. A family may inherit force without inheriting wisdom.

The standing question:

> What does bloodline grant, and what does it fail to grant?

Nimmo is the inheritance/endowment axis of the map.

---

## Circle Six: Narnian Cosmology

Narnia adds a theological and royal axis.

The crucial features:

- Aslan sings Narnia into being in *The Magician's Nephew*.
- Aslan is son of the Emperor-over-the-Sea.
- The Deep Magic is law from the dawn of time.
- The Deeper Magic is older still, capable of overturning the Witch's legalistic claim when innocent self-sacrifice enters the pattern.
- Sons of Adam and Daughters of Eve are called to kingship and queenship.
- Narnia is not merely governed by power but by right rule under Aslan.

C.S. Lewis's own better category is "supposal," not strict allegory. Aslan is not a flat symbol standing for Christ inside a puzzle. He is the imagined answer to what Christ might be like in a world like Narnia.

Narnia therefore gives a distinct model of standing:

> Authority is not merely inherited, possessed, or discovered. Authority is participation in a created order under Aslan.

This is not the same as Cooper:

- Cooper's Old Ones serve the Light through office and Things of Power.
- Narnia's kings and queens rule under Aslan in a world sung into being.

Nor is it Nimmo:

- Nimmo's endowment is bloodline capacity.
- Narnia's kingship is vocation under a creator-king.

Nor is it Greenwood:

- Greenwood asks who may shape living material.
- Narnia asks who may rule living creation.

The standing question:

> Does power serve the song that made the world, or does it claim the world as its own possession?

Narnia is the sung-creation and lawful-kingship axis of the map.

## Venn Center

The shared center is not "magic."

The shared center is **power under contested standing**.

Each circle gives a different way power may appear:

- material power,
- musical power,
- office power,
- wild power,
- inherited power,
- creator-law power.

Each circle then asks whether that power is lawful in context.

The central campaign principle:

> The presence of power does not answer the question of standing. It only makes the standing question urgent.

---

## Relationship To Tantalus

This report also clarifies the enemy.

Tantalus the Absolute cannot abide living free will because it breaks prediction. He treats living choice as entropy. His Tautomata march to overwrite the territory with the mental model-map he worships but cannot fully accept as truth.

Against that, the Venn offers living distinctions he cannot tolerate:

- Greenwood can object to shaping.
- Greensleeves can refuse the singer.
- Wild Magic can refuse Light and Dark alike.
- Endowment can exist without office.
- Things of Power can require timing and right bearer.
- Narnian kingship can depend on Aslanic vocation rather than mere control.

Tantalus wants the map to settle all standing in advance.

These stories refuse.

They insist that standing is relational, timed, witnessed, authorized, and sometimes unavailable even to the powerful.

That is why they matter.

## Campaign Use

This Venn can be used to classify artifacts, characters, and conflicts without collapsing them:

- A green instrument may be Greenwood, Greensleeves, or Narnia depending on whether the emphasis is material, song, or creator-law.
- A child with power may be Nimmo-style endowed, Cooper-style office-bearing, or Narnia-style called to kingship.
- A nature power may be Green Man/Greenwitch third-standing, not automatically a servant of Light.
- A lawful artifact may be a Thing of Power rather than a loot item.
- A sacred song may accompany a threshold without owning it.
- A king may rule lawfully only if rule participates in the deeper order.

Practical diagnostic:

1. Identify the power.
2. Identify its form.
3. Identify its provenance.
4. Identify who claims it.
5. Identify who authorized the claim.
6. Identify what the claim costs the living.
7. Identify whether completion is being mistaken for standing.

## Closing Finding

The overlapping Venn diagrams are not clutter. They are the point.

Greenwood teaches that living material cannot be shaped lawfully without standing.

Greensleeves teaches that longing cannot sing itself into possession.

Cooper teaches that office, Wild Magic, and Things of Power must not be confused.

Nimmo teaches that inheritance grants capacity, not automatic authority.

Narnia teaches that creation itself may be sung, but rule within creation remains answerable to the singer.

Together:

> The world is not made true by being mapped. It is made lawful by right relation between power, bearer, object, timing, witness, and authority.
