# Kerr Vessel Mechanics — Children of the Lamp

Fresh reading, this session's provenance only. Not reconciled against prior
agents' Kerr files by Logan's direction; reconciliation is a separate task for
a separate day.

## Vessel Classes

| Class | Example | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conventional containers | lamp, bottle, urn, jar | The genre-standard search-image for djinn housing |
| Unconventional (instrument-shaped) | the Sekhem scepter | Containment disguised as regalia; defeats search-images built on the conventional classes. *Sekhem* is Egyptian for "power" — the vessel was labeled truthfully in a language its keepers stopped reading |
| Modern mundane | a Coke bottle | Djinn travel casually by vessel; sanctity is not a requirement, the *form* is |
| Funerary | canopic jar (baboon-headed lid) | Usable offensively: containment tech is symmetric and binds the lawful as readily as the unlawful |
| Refuge (voluntary) | the Portland Vase | Self-containment as evasion; see Time-Rate below. The museum's most famous *reassembled* artifact — a repaired provenance sheltering fugitives |

## Entry, Exit, and the Time-Rate Parameter

- Entry is by **transubstantiation** — the djinn passes bodily into the vessel
  through a prepared aperture.
- **Rotation at entry sets subjective time-rate inside.** Northern Hemisphere:
  enter *anticlockwise* to make interior time pass quickly ("Space, time. In
  order to make the time inside the vase seem to go more quickly" — ch. 23).
  Configured at containment-time; not adjustable afterward, as far as tonight's
  text shows (`*`).
- Consequence: dormancy is *engineered experience*. A lawful self-bottling sets
  purpose, exit time, egress task, and time-rate before entry. An unlawful
  bottling sets none, and the interior century is subjectively endless.

## Casting Circumstances

- **Climate:** djinn power weakens in cold; England is adverse country. A warm,
  humid night materially changes the go/no-go calculus (ch. 23, stated
  directly).
- **Proximity buffering:** twin djinn stabilize each other's power — paired
  operation extends the safe envelope into otherwise adverse circumstances.
- **Focus words** are personal activation phrases: Nimrod's `QWERTYUIOP`,
  John's `ABECEDARIAN`. The words of power are *about literacy itself* — the
  interface row and the learner of letters. The magic system is language
  operations all the way down.

## Release-Covenant Law

- "**A djinn must keep the promise it makes upon being released. That's the
  rule.**" (John, ch. 23.) The manumission contract is enforced by the
  substrate, not by the releaser's strength.
- Correct release procedure, as performed by the twins under fire:
  1. Confirm the threshold spec (the adversary's own boast read as
     documentation: "more than one djinn to bind me").
  2. Run the allegiance check (Philippa: these djinn once served the enemy).
  3. **Extract the promise before opening the vessel** — covenant precedes
     capability, even mid-crisis.
  4. Word the charge with both an immediate task and a standing policy
     ("destroy Akhenaten and work only for Good").
- The bound *prefer* lawful release: "For three thousand years we have awaited
  your coming." A covenant-holding workforce submits to civil process when the
  task completes (the seventy sit down and await capture).

## Hazard Interactions

- **Residual-power animation:** castings leak. Excess djinn power from a lawful
  summons revived a dormant hostile entity co-located with the work site. The
  pentacle must contain *emissions*, not just operations.
- **Power-sensitive payloads:** once the adversary is jarred, *no djinn power
  near the jar* — exfiltration goes by mundane channel (haversack, main door,
  opening hours). Capability-restraint is itself a containment control.
- **Mixed vessels:** the canopic jar holds ally and adversary entwined;
  selective release is impossible without a separation procedure. Label such
  vessels by their *hazard*, not their treasure (the djinn priest's one-word
  manifest: "Akhenaten").
- **Unlock interfaces can be knowledge-gated:** the Sekhem opens by literacy —
  cartouche, ankh, numeral-tens, "bring seventy to life" — with the vessel's
  own voice assisting the lawful opener. Gate class: scholarship + executed
  covenant (contrast gates of raw competence or of character elsewhere in the
  reference catalogue).
