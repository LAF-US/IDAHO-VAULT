A group of volunteers next to the truck donating 40,000 pounds of food from The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

POCATELLO — The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints has donated 40,000 pounds of food in honor of America’s 250th anniversary celebration.

The donation was made Wednesday, June 17, at the Bishop’s Storehouse on 580 Roosevelt Ave. Michelle Barrott, communications director for the local LDS Church, said the food was delivered in an America250 truck — one of 250 trucks delivered to locations across the country.

“We distributed that to food banks around town,” Barrott said. “It stayed local. We had the missionaries come to help separate it and load all the pantries.”

Food pantries that received a portion of the donation included My Brother’s Table, Valley Mission, Gate City Baptist, Benny’s Pantry, Aid for Friends, the Idaho Food Bank and Pocatello/Chubbuck School District 25.

“It’s a really good thing for food pantries,” Barrott said. “With all the food insecurities right now, this is an amazing thing for Eastern Idaho.”

The food donated included rice, salsa, spaghetti sauce, applesauce, diced tomatoes and peanut butter.

“All this food is shelf-stable,” Barrott said. “Everyone’s fired up about the peanut butter.”

Barrott said the atmosphere when the food was dropped off was positive and everyone was happy that families in need throughout the community would be able to receive it.

“It’s going to people right here in our town,” Barrott said. “That’s what makes me happy about it.”

The best part of the day was watching everyone come together to divide the food among the pantries. Barrott said they made sure each group received a good portion.

“We have a lot of great people in this town who have big hearts and love to serve and help their neighbors,” Barrott said. “Everyone’s here and everyone’s just helping each other.”
