# The Verificar

The **Verificar** is the imperial vicar of verification: a cleric-warlock
office coded into Tautomata species identity.

The Verificar does not decide what happened. The Verificar decides whether
happening has entered truth.

## Playable Archetype

- Species: [[Tautomata]].
- Cleric pole: Proof, Archive, Record, Completion.
- Warlock patron: [[Tantalus the Absolute]].
- Implement: ledger, audit seal, censor-spear, witness drone, or brass stylus.

## Spell Flavor

- Bless: seal of admissibility.
- Bane: notice of contradiction.
- Command: procedural imperative.
- Zone of Truth: chamber of record.
- Hold Person: pending verification.
- Hex: audit mark.
- Counterspell: claim rejected.

Battle cry: **Verificandum est.**
