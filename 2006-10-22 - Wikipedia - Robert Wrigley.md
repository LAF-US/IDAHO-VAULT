**Robert Wrigley** (born 1951 in [East St. Louis, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_St._Louis,_Illinois "East St. Louis, Illinois")) is an American poet and educator.

## Biography

In 1971 Wrigley was inducted into the [army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Army "United States Army"), filing for discharge as a [conscientious objector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conscientious_objector "Conscientious objector").[^1] He received his M.F.A. in Poetry from the [University of Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Montana "University of Montana") in 1976, where he studied under poets [Richard Hugo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Hugo "Richard Hugo"), [Madeline DeFrees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madeline_DeFrees "Madeline DeFrees"), and [John Haines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Haines "John Haines"). From 1987 to 1988 he served as writer-in-residence for the state of [Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idaho "Idaho"), and has received fellowships from the [National Endowment for the Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Endowment_for_the_Arts "National Endowment for the Arts"), the Idaho State Commission on the Arts, and the [Guggenheim Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Simon_Guggenheim_Memorial_Foundation "John Simon Guggenheim Memorial Foundation").

His poems have been published in a number of journals, including *[Poetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poetry_magazine "Poetry magazine")*, *[The Atlantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Atlantic "The Atlantic")*, *[Barrow Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrow_Street_\(magazine\) "Barrow Street (magazine)")*, and *[The New Yorker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Yorker "The New Yorker")*.[^2] In 2003 and 2006 he had poems published in *[Best American Poetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_American_Poetry "Best American Poetry")*, and in 2013, his poem "Religion" appeared in *The Best of the Best American Poetry: 25th Anniversary Edition*, selected by [Robert Pinsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pinsky "Robert Pinsky"). Wrigley is also the recipient of seven [Pushcart Prizes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pushcart_Prize "Pushcart Prize"). *Reign of Snakes* won the [Kingsley Tufts Poetry Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingsley_Tufts_Poetry_Award "Kingsley Tufts Poetry Award"); *Lives of the Animals* won the 2005 [Poets' Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poets'_Prize "Poets' Prize"). *In the Bank of Beautiful Sins* won the San Francisco Poetry Center Book Award. *Box* won a 2017 Pacific Northwest Book Award.

Wrigley retired from teaching (in 2016) at the M.F.A. program in creative writing at the [University of Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Idaho "University of Idaho"), where his wife, [Kim Barnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Barnes "Kim Barnes"), a memoirist and writer, also taught until her retirement in 2020.
