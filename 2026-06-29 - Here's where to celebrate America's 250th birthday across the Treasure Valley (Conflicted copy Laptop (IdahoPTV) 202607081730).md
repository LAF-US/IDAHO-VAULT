The United States of America’s 250th birthday celebration officially kicks off this Fourth of July, and communities across the Treasure Valley are marking the milestone with parades, concerts, fireworks, museum exhibits, baseball and family-friendly activities.

Whether you’re looking for a traditional parade, live music, or one of the state’s official America250 events, here’s a roundup of celebrations happening across the valley.

## Boise

**United We Celebrate: A Community Day at the Idaho State Museum**

Saturday, July 4, from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.

The Idaho State Museum is offering free admission as part of the nation’s 250th anniversary celebration. Visitors can explore [two new America250 exhibitions](https://history.idaho.gov/event/united-we-celebrate-a-community-day/): *United We Celebrate: The Traditions That Bring America Together* and *Four Score and Seven Years Before*, along with hands-on activities for all ages throughout the day.

**America250 for $2.50 Weekend at Zoo Boise**

Saturday, July 4 – Sunday, July 5

[Zoo Boise](https://zooboise.org/events/all-ages-events/) is celebrating with $2.50 admission all weekend in honor of America’s 250th birthday. It’s one of the best family bargains of the holiday weekend.

**Idaho 4th of July Parade**

Saturday, July 4 | Parade begins at 10 a.m.

[Downtown Boise’s annual Independence Day Parade](https://america250.idaho.gov/) returns to Capitol Boulevard as part of Idaho’s America250 celebration. The morning also includes hot air balloons, a community pancake breakfast, and a pet parade before the main event.

**Idaho America250 Capitol Celebration**

Saturday, July 4, from 11:30 a.m. to 3 p.m.

Following the parade, the [celebration continues on the Idaho State Capitol steps and Cecil D. Andrus Park](https://america250.idaho.gov/capitol-celebration/) with one of the state’s signature America250 events.

![Watkins Carter - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/f002ca13-0527-4831-9254-99ce5fa33461.jpg)

Watkins Carter - 2:1

The free celebration includes:

- Live performances from Josh Ritter, Afrosonics, Jeff Crosby, Chad Marvin & EllieMae and the 25th Army Band
- Capitol tours and historical displays
- Food trucks and local vendors
- Interactive activities for kids and families
- Parade float viewing

Admission is free.

## Meridian

**Meridian Independence Day Festival**

Saturday, July 4 | 4 p.m. to 10:30 p.m.  
  
Celebrate America’s 250th anniversary at [Meridian’s annual Independence Day Festival](https://meridiancity.org/parks/specialevents/independence-day/) at Storey Park (205 E. Franklin Rd., Meridian). The free community celebration begins at 4 p.m. with food trucks serving local favorites, snacks, and refreshments, followed by live music starting at 5 p.m. The evening wraps up with what organizers are calling Meridian’s “biggest and brightest” fireworks show yet, launching at approximately 10:20 p.m. from east of Meridian Speedway. Limited ADA parking will be available near the Meridian Pool.

## Kuna

**Kuna Fourth of July Celebration**

Saturday, July 4, from 6 to 11 p.m.

[Kuna’s 4th of July America250 Celebration](https://america250.idaho.gov/events-calendar/city-of-kuna-4th-of-july-celebration-celebrating-250-year-of-independence/) is happening at Bernie Fisher Park from 6 to 11 p.m. There will be over 15 food trucks and a D.J. spinning tunes for everyone to enjoy. Fireworks kick off after dusk near the 2nd St. baseball fields at 10:15 p.m.
