**Bridget Powers** (born Cheryl Murphy [^2]; October 11, 1980) is an American [pornographic actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pornographic_actress "Pornographic actress") with [dwarfism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwarfism "Dwarfism"). She is often credited as " **Bridget the Midget** " [^3] or with the alternate spelling " **Bridget Powerz** ".[^3] Besides her adult work, she has also appeared in many films in either comedic or dramatic roles, such as *[Big Money Rustlas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Money_Rustlas "Big Money Rustlas")*, *[Tiptoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiptoes "Tiptoes")*, and *[S.W.A.T.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S.W.A.T._\(2003_film\) "S.W.A.T. (2003 film)")*. She also is the singer in the [punk band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punk_band "Punk band") Bridget and the Nameless.

## Early life

Powers was born in [Boise, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise,_Idaho "Boise, Idaho") to a Guatemalan mother and Irish father.[^4] Her family moved to Burbank, California, when she was one year old, after which her parents divorced.[^5] She had a number of operations between the ages of three and fourteen to correct [bowed legs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowed_legs "Bowed legs"); the results left her with one straight leg and one that points to the left. Powers has to wear a knee brace.[^1] Powers has an older sister, Vanessa, who she hasn't seen or spoken to since she was 18.[^6]

## Career

Powers was discovered at a [goth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goth_subculture "Goth subculture") nightclub by a man who was working as a make-up artist for a gothic vampire [adult movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pornographic_film "Pornographic film").[^1] She made her first porn film in 1999.[^7] By her own count, she appeared in 65 to 70 original films, with compilations bringing that up to over 110.[^8] She left the business, as she wished to do condom-only scenes yet many of the male performers would remove the condoms.[^8] Disillusioned with the exploitation of the porn film industry, she then set up her own [video on demand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_on_demand "Video on demand") website.[^3]

She was a co-host on [Ed Powers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Powers "Ed Powers") ' radio show *Bedtime Stories*. She was co-host of the Johnny Nerotic Live Show from 2002-2024 Appearing first in the pilot for the show in Nov,10th 2001.She is also in a band named Blakkout. In addition to her erotic film roles, she has appeared in several mainstream films, such as *[8mm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8mm_\(film\) "8mm (film)")*, *[Confessions of a Dangerous Mind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confessions_of_a_Dangerous_Mind_\(film\) "Confessions of a Dangerous Mind (film)")*, *[S.W.A.T.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S.W.A.T._\(2003_film\) "S.W.A.T. (2003 film)")*, *[Wristcutters: A Love Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wristcutters:_A_Love_Story "Wristcutters: A Love Story")*, *[I Hope They Serve Beer in Hell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Hope_They_Serve_Beer_in_Hell_\(film\) "I Hope They Serve Beer in Hell (film)")* and independent films such as *Almost Amateur*, *[Tiptoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiptoes "Tiptoes")*, and *Lynsey does Jody 2 – Extreme Edition*.

She appeared as herself in the 2002 [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO") [documentary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Documentary_film "Documentary film") series *[Cathouse: The Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathouse:_The_Series "Cathouse: The Series")*, which explored the lives of the owners, management, staff and customers of the [Moonlite BunnyRanch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moonlite_BunnyRanch "Moonlite BunnyRanch") brothel in [Mound House, Nevada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mound_House,_Nevada "Mound House, Nevada"). As "Bridget the Midget", she was featured in an episode of the [History Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_\(American_TV_channel\) "History (American TV channel)") series *Wild West Tech*. In 2006, she appeared on an episode of [VH1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VH1 "VH1") 's celebrity reality show, *[The Surreal Life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Surreal_Life "The Surreal Life")*. She has also appeared on *[The Howard Stern Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Howard_Stern_Show "The Howard Stern Show")* on [E!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E! "E!")[^9] She also played the role of Tink, Sheriff Sugar Wolf's love interest in the 2010 film *[Big Money Rustlas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Money_Rustlas "Big Money Rustlas")*.

In 2022, Powers formed the [punk band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punk_band "Punk band") Bridget and the Nameless.[^10]

Since 2024, Powers has been touring the country with the "Micro Maidens", the world's smallest [burlesque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burlesque "Burlesque") show, and "MicroMania" [midget wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midget_wrestling "Midget wrestling").[^11]

## Personal life

She has one son, who was born when Powers was 28.[^12] [^13] Powers has stated she remains on friendly terms with the father of her son, who has since remarried [^14]. On September 18, 2019, she was arrested and booked into the [Clark County Detention Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clark_County_Detention_Center "Clark County Detention Center") after stabbing her boyfriend Jesse James [^15] in the leg at her home in [Las Vegas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Vegas "Las Vegas"), after discovering him in bed with another woman.[^16]
