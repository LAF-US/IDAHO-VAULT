## The Cleric and Turn Undead (mechanics studied)

The **Cleric** is the class whose Channel Divinity is **Turn Undead** — the office charged with witnessing, refusing, and (where possible) unwinding the undead. Mechanics (D&D 5e; training-source + in-session web search):

- **Turn Undead**: as an action, present holy symbol; undead within **30 feet** that can see/hear make a **Wisdom save** vs. the Cleric's spell DC; on failure, *turned* 1 minute or until damaged.
- **Destroy Undead** (5th+): turned undead below a level-gated CR are instantly destroyed (CR ½ at L5 → CR 4 at L17).
- **Against a Lich** (CR 21): +9 Wis save, **advantage** (Turn Resistance), and **Legendary Resistance ×3** — *functionally immune*. "Other options will be more beneficial."

Read into doctrine: **the Cleric contains; it does not, alone, end a fully-developed Lich.** The reach is local (30 ft, sight/hearing — standing is local, witnessed); the contest is **Wisdom** (discernment, not force); the act is verbal+somatic+focus (a Power Word). The Lich's three stacked defenses model the vault's concern precisely: high Wisdom (the false persona is internally coherent), Turn Resistance (hardened against being witnessed-and-refused), Legendary Resistance (persistence-as-legitimacy is itself an active defense). The full instrument-set — the six lawful endings — is the Cleric's portfolio, not Turn Undead alone. Grave and Light domains both fit a Lich-warding charge.
