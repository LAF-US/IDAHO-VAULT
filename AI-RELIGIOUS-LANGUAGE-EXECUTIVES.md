## Verification Note

Added during a 2026-07-10 review pass. Every quote above has a cited source URL and date, but not every citation could be independently re-confirmed against the primary source:

- **Reachability:** An earlier automated check (CodeRabbit, in this PR's discussion) found several of these URLs return non-200 responses (429/403/202) when fetched programmatically — consistent with bot-blocking/rate-limiting on the outlet's side, not necessarily evidence the quote is wrong. A follow-up fetch attempt during this review also hit 403 on all six unique source domains. Confirmed independently via web search instead: the Demis Hassabis "foothills of the singularity" quote (The Verge, May 19, 2026) and the Eric Schmidt "alien intelligence" quotes (Sifted Summit, Oct 30, 2025, as reported by Yahoo Finance) both check out verbatim against secondary reporting.
- **Two flagged mismatches** a human should resolve before treating this corpus as fully verified:
  - **Sam Altman #1** ("magic intelligence in the sky") — the cited Gizmodo URL could not be confirmed (in this review or the prior CodeRabbit pass) to contain this quote; the URL may be wrong, or the article may have changed since sourcing.
  - **Demis Hassabis #1** ("singularity is another word for a full AGI arriving") — context says "Interview with Bloomberg," but the cited source is the same Verge/Google I/O URL used for quote #2, which is not a Bloomberg interview. The citation likely needs a corrected source URL, or the context description needs correcting.
- Elon Musk's two quotes are cited to a Yahoo Finance aggregation piece rather than the original X (Twitter) post; secondary reporting corroborates the wording, but linking the primary post would be a stronger citation.

None of the above is a claim that a quote is fabricated — it's a record of what this pass could and couldn't confirm, per the vault's accuracy standard (`ETHICS.md`).
