Photographers, production companies, and advertising agencies now have a new studio to create.

Visionkit, a creative studio in Garden City — designed by producers for producers — opened its doors in May, with daily studio time available for rent.

The space at 111 W. 34th Street is built to handle everything from big commercial shoots to detailed product photography.

![208 Market - July 2026 - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/2de7e0a8-34ae-41b8-b550-948663e8552e.gif)

208 Market - July 2026 - 2:1

“We built this space to be the kind of studio we always wanted to work in,” Visionkit Studio Producer and Co-Owner Stephani Norell said. “Whether you are a local photographer needing more space or an out-of-town production company looking for a turnkey solution, we have the infrastructure to make your shoot run smoothly.”

The studio is 8,000 sq. ft., featuring a massive 4,300 sq. ft. production floor with one of the area’s largest cyc walls measuring 30′ wide x 30′ deep x 16′ tall, according to Norell.

In addition to renting the space, creators have access to on-site gear rentals and production and coordination services.
