Claim:

Starting July 1, 2026, Idaho will use the firing squad as its primary method of execution.

Rating: [![True](https://mediaproxy.snopes.com/width/200/https://media.snopes.com/2018/03/rating-true.png) True](https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/rating/true)

[About this rating](https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/rating/true)A [claim](https://www.facebook.com/thehodgetwins/posts/pfbid0rvjSmKCMX6AioLpXm1HHsKidXCmM2y6bFz25XvqJXxnbJeY9mhccaKtUq5Jx36nAl) ([archived](https://perma.cc/FZ24-YHW4)) circulated online in June 2026 that, starting July 1, Idaho would use the firing squad as its primary method of execution for death-row prisoners.

One Facebook user wrote, "Idaho is officially moving away from lethal injection."

According to that claim and others [on X](https://x.com/DailyLoud/status/2069672642311749641) ([archived](https://perma.cc/8RN9-MV7G)) and [Threads](https://www.threads.com/@michekyakeymi/post/DZ9DE0WHK1K) ([archived](https://perma.cc/S54M-Q8XD)), the firing squad would also be the chosen execution method for people convicted of raping a child. Snopes readers also [contacted](https://www.snopes.com/contact/) us about the claim.

The claim was true. Idaho [House Bill 37](https://legislature.idaho.gov/wp-content/uploads/sessioninfo/2025/legislation/H0037.pdf), which Gov. Brad Little [signed](https://legislature.idaho.gov/sessioninfo/2025/legislation/H0037/) into law in March 2025, made the firing squad the state's primary method of execution beginning July 1, 2026. The state would still allow lethal injection to be used if it was not possible to use the firing squad.

People convicted of first-degree murder or "aggravated lewd conduct with a minor" age 12 or under can face the death penalty in Idaho, according to [state law](https://law.justia.com/codes/idaho/title-19/chapter-25/section-19-2515/). At the time of this writing, the state held [eight](https://www.idoc.idaho.gov/content/prisons/death-row) prisoners on its death row and last attempted an execution by lethal injection in [February 2024](https://apnews.com/article/idaho-execution-creech-murders-serial-killer-91a12d78e9301adde77e6076dbd01dbb). It was not successful.

All eight of the state's death-row prisoners were convicted of murder.

According to the Death Penalty Information Center, a national nonprofit group that shares information about the death penalty in the U.S. but takes no position on the practice, Idaho is [the only state](https://deathpenaltyinfo.org/state-and-federal-info/state-by-state/idaho#:~:text=2025%E2%80%89%E2%80%94%E2%80%89Governor%20Brad%20Little%20signs%20a%C2%A0bill%20on%20March%2012%20autho%C2%ADriz%C2%ADing%20the%20fir%C2%ADing%20squad%20as%20Idaho%E2%80%99s%20pri%C2%ADma%C2%ADry%20method%20of%20exe%C2%ADcu%C2%ADtion%2C%20effec%C2%ADtive%20July%201%2C%202026.%20Only%20four%20oth%C2%ADer%20states%20autho%C2%ADrize%20the%20fir%C2%ADing%20squad%2C%20and%20none%20as%20their%20pri%C2%ADma%C2%ADry%20execution%20method.%C2%A0) in the U.S. to authorize the firing squad as its primary method of execution. Idaho [introduced](https://www.idahostatesman.com/news/local/crime/article316066906.html) the firing squad as an alternate method of execution to lethal injection in 2023.

Since the passage of House Bill 37, the Idaho Statesman [reported](https://www.idahostatesman.com/news/local/crime/article316066906.html#:~:text=Work%20to%20retrofit,than%20%241.2%20million.) that work to retrofit an existing execution chamber for a firing squad has cost more than $1.2 million.

Under Idaho's [new system](https://media.snopes.com/2026/06/idaho_execution_procedures.pdf) (Page 8), volunteers who have held the state's Peace Office Standards and Training [certification](https://post.idaho.gov/about-post/) for at least three years are eligible to be selected for a firing squad. Qualified volunteers include law enforcement, felony probation and parole, correction or detention and probation officers.

According to the Idaho Department of Correction's [standard operating procedures](https://media.snopes.com/2026/06/idaho_execution_procedures.pdf) (Page 8), qualified volunteers must also pass a target shooting test. Members of the firing squad may not have had disciplinary action in the past 12 months regarding use of a firearm or use of force. They also may not have a blood or legal relationship to any victims or the condemned person.

Idaho has carried out three executions since it changed its death penalty statute in 1977. The state used lethal injections for those executions, [according](https://deathpenaltyinfo.org/facts-and-research/data/executions?state=Idaho&federal=No) to the Death Penalty Information Center.
