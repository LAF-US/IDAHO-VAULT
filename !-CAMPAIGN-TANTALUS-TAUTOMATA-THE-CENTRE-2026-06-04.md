# The Centre

**The Centre** is the deepest mine under the Fatherland: forge, archive,
labyrinth, throne, tomb, and mind-palace.

Tautomata folklore says [[Tantalus the Absolute]] descended into brass and
bronze veins and extracted the hidden potential of truth. The metal was not
merely ore. It was record-bearing possibility.

## Mythic Functions

- Hidden Hephaestus: the buried forge-divinity.
- Daedalus: architect imprisoned by his own labyrinth.
- Nome King: subterranean tyrant of mineral possession.
- Uruk-hai image: soldiers drawn from industrial darkness.

The Fatherland may not have been built on the mine. It may have been built to
hide the mine.

Inscription at the lowest gate: **Descent is proof of descent.**
