**Torrie Anne Wilson** (born July 24, 1975) is an American retired [professional wrestler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestling "Professional wrestling"), [model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_\(person\) "Model (person)") and [fitness competitor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fitness_competitor "Fitness competitor"). She is best known for her tenures in [WWE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE "WWE") and [WCW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WCW "WCW").

As a fitness competitor, Wilson won the Miss Galaxy competition in 1999.[^1] [^8] Shortly after, she was signed by [World Championship Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Championship_Wrestling "World Championship Wrestling") (WCW), where she stayed from 1999 until 2000.[^2] [^7] [^8] In 2001, she made her debut on World Wrestling Federation (WWF) television as part of [The Invasion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Invasion_\(professional_wrestling\) "The Invasion (professional wrestling)") (by WCW, of the \[then-\] WWF) storyline.[^4] Her most high-profile storylines took place in 2002 when she [feuded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feud_\(professional_wrestling\) "Feud (professional wrestling)") with [Dawn Marie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dawn_Marie "Dawn Marie"),[^4] [^9] and 2003 with [Sable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sable_\(wrestler\) "Sable (wrestler)"). Wilson was part of the all-female [stable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestling_stable "Professional wrestling stable") (i.e., a group of storyline-associated characters) known as [Vince's Devils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vince's_Devils "Vince's Devils"), which ended its run in 2006.

Wilson has been on the cover of several magazines, including *[FHM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FHM "FHM")* and *[Playboy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Playboy "Playboy")*, which Wilson posed for twice, once along with Sable.

Wilson was inducted into the [WWE Hall of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Hall_of_Fame "WWE Hall of Fame") in 2019.[^10]

## Early life

Wilson was born and raised in [Boise, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise,_Idaho "Boise, Idaho").[^1] [^11] She says that, while growing up, she was painfully shy.[^8] [^9] [^12] Wilson found her niche in many school activities, including [cheerleading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheerleading "Cheerleading"), dance, and [track and field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Track_and_field "Track and field").[^1] [^8]

During her sophomore year of high school, after Wilson's interest in modeling grew more serious, her mother urged her to pursue it.[^1] They visited an agency and were informed that Wilson would have to lose weight to be considered for jobs.[^1] In the process of losing weight, she began a bout with [anorexia nervosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anorexia_nervosa "Anorexia nervosa") and then [bulimia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulimia "Bulimia") that lasted from around the age of 15 to 19.[^1] [^8]

After recovering from the disorder, Wilson became involved in fitness.[^1] [^7] She began eating six meals a day and working out.[^8] She placed third in her first competition,[^1] and she later won the Miss Galaxy competition in 1998.[^1] [^8] She then spent some time on the Extreme Fitness Team.[^13] As part of the Women's Tri-Fitness Championships in 1998, Wilson won first place in the Grace and Physique round.[^14]

Later in 1998, Wilson moved to [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles") to pursue acting.[^1] [^9] She began acting classes and was introduced to an agent who helped her gain a few jobs.[^1] Wilson was approached by [World Championship Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Championship_Wrestling "World Championship Wrestling") through an agent in 1999 for a gig of only a couple of months. However, her popularity snowballed her character into a full-time role.[^15]

## Professional wrestling career

### World Championship Wrestling (1999–2000)

Wilson's start in wrestling came when she attended a [World Championship Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Championship_Wrestling "World Championship Wrestling") (WCW) show with her boyfriend in 1999.[^7] [^8] [^9] After going backstage, she was asked to walk out to the ring with [Scott Steiner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Steiner "Scott Steiner").[^7] Later, [Kevin Nash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Nash "Kevin Nash") expressed an interest in doing a storyline with her.[^4] [^7] [^9] She first appeared on the February 8, 1999, episode of Nitro (later named "Samantha"). It was revealed, in the storyline, that Wilson was brought in by the [New World Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_World_Order_\(professional_wrestling\) "New World Order (professional wrestling)") (nWo) to seduce [David Flair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Flair "David Flair") into [turning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turn_\(professional_wrestling\) "Turn (professional wrestling)") against his father, [Ric Flair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ric_Flair "Ric Flair").[^4] [^16] They appeared together on February 21 at [SuperBrawl IX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SuperBrawl_IX "SuperBrawl IX") when Wilson slapped Ric, and David zapped him with a [taser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taser "Taser").[^17] After Ric won the [WCW World Heavyweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WCW_World_Heavyweight_Championship "WCW World Heavyweight Championship") from [Hollywood Hogan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Hogan "Hollywood Hogan") at [Uncensored](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uncensored_\(1999\) "Uncensored (1999)") inside a Barbed Wire Steel Cage match, David and Wilson took some time off from WCW television in April before returning in May, when Wilson would accompany David to the ring for matches. At the [1999 Bash at the Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bash_at_the_Beach_1999 "Bash at the Beach 1999"), Wilson acted as a [valet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valet_\(professional_wrestling\) "Valet (professional wrestling)") for Flair as he successfully defended the [WCW United States Heavyweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WCW_United_States_Heavyweight_Championship "WCW United States Heavyweight Championship") against [Dean Malenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_Malenko "Dean Malenko").

She continued to accompany David to the ring until September when David was on the telephone with Wilson and later found out that Wilson was in the Filthy Animals' locker room flirting with [Billy Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Kidman "Billy Kidman"), which resulted in a feud between Flair and Kidman. On the October 18, 1999, episode of [Nitro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WCW_Monday_Nitro "WCW Monday Nitro"), Wilson turned on him, resulting in Flair losing the match and the Animals beating on him; after the Animals stopped assaulting David, Wilson left with the Filthy Animals. It was around this time that Wilson began managing Kidman and his [Filthy Animals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filthy_Animals "Filthy Animals") teammates.[^4] As a result, she found herself involved in the Animals continuing [feud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feud_\(professional_wrestling\) "Feud (professional wrestling)") with [The Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolution_\(professional_wrestling\) "Revolution (professional wrestling)").[^4] On the November 22, 1999, episode of *Nitro*, during a tag team match between Kidman and [Konnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konnan "Konnan") facing Creative Control for the [WCW World Tag Team Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WCW_World_Tag_Team_Championship "WCW World Tag Team Championship"), Kidman looked up at the Turnertron and saw that Wilson and [Eddie Guerrero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eddie_Guerrero "Eddie Guerrero") were shown flirting inside his locker room on the "Kidcam", a hidden camera segment, which led to a storyline feud between Kidman and Guerrero.[^4] Following this, Wilson once again disappeared from television. Wilson however returned on the January 19, 2000, episode of *[Thunder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WCW_Thunder "WCW Thunder")* managing Kidman, and in April 2000, Kidman and Wilson joined a [stable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestling_stable "Professional wrestling stable") known as [the New Blood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Blood "The New Blood"), making them both villainous characters. As part of their newest storyline, Kidman became jealous of the attention Wilson began to give to fellow New Blood member [Horace Hogan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horace_Hogan "Horace Hogan"). In June, Wilson gave Kidman a hit at the [Great American Bash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_American_Bash_2000 "Great American Bash 2000"), causing him to lose to Horace's uncle, Hulk Hogan.[^4]

Wilson was taken off of television following the encounter, reappearing at July's [Bash at the Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bash_At_The_Beach_2000 "Bash At The Beach 2000") during a match between [Shane Douglas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Douglas "Shane Douglas") and [Buff Bagwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buff_Bagwell "Buff Bagwell"). After the match, Wilson and Douglas left together, which led to a feud between Kidman and Douglas.[^18] At September's [Fall Brawl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_Brawl_2000 "Fall Brawl 2000"), Wilson and Douglas defeated Kidman and [Madusa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madusa "Madusa") in a mixed tag team [match with scaffolding erected above the ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scaffold_match "Scaffold match").[^2] Wilson was released from WCW in December 2000.[^2]

### World Wrestling Federation/Entertainment (2001-2008)

#### The Invasion and feuding with Dawn Marie (2001–2003)

Wilson made her television debut as a heel on the June 28, 2001, episode of *[SmackDown!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SmackDown! "SmackDown!")* as part of [The Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Alliance_\(professional_wrestling\) "The Alliance (professional wrestling)") during the Invasion [angle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angle_\(professional_wrestling\) "Angle (professional wrestling)") in 2001.[^4] In her first storyline with the company, she portrayed [Vince McMahon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vince_McMahon "Vince McMahon") 's latest affair.[^19] She also regularly teamed with fellow WCW performer [Stacy Keibler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stacy_Keibler "Stacy Keibler"). The duo made their wrestling debuts in a [Bra and Panties match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bra_and_Panties_match "Bra and Panties match") against [Lita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Dumas "Amy Dumas") and [Trish Stratus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trish_Stratus "Trish Stratus") at the [InVasion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/InVasion "InVasion") pay-per-view, which Stratus and Lita won by stripping Keibler and Wilson to their yellow and white [bikinis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bikinis "Bikinis"). The following night on *Raw*, Wilson defeated Stratus in a [Paddle on a Pole match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paddle_on_a_Pole_match "Paddle on a Pole match").[^2] Despite her relative lack of in-ring experience, Wilson, alongside Keibler and [Ivory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivory_\(wrestler\) "Ivory (wrestler)"), regularly feuded with the [Divas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWF_Diva "WWF Diva"),[^20] as well as interfering in matches on behalf of other wrestlers in The Alliance.

She became a face during the Invasion storyline when her character began an on-screen romance with [Tajiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoshihiro_Tajiri "Yoshihiro Tajiri").[^4] This new romance caused the heel Stacy Keibler to turn against Wilson, and as a result of this, Wilson defected to the WWF. Wilson defeated Keibler in the first [lingerie match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lingerie_match "Lingerie match") at [No Mercy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Mercy_\(2001\) "No Mercy (2001)").[^2] When the first [brand separation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Brand_Extension "WWE Brand Extension") of the company roster took place in March 2002, Wilson was [drafted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_draft "WWE draft") to the [SmackDown!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SmackDown_\(WWE_brand\) "SmackDown (WWE brand)") brand.[^21] Shortly after this, as part of the storyline, Tajiri soon became jealous over the increased attention that Wilson received from other men, so he forced her to wear a [geisha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geisha "Geisha") outfit,[^4] [^22] and he also appeared to mistreat her in matches.[^2] She depantsed [Rico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rico_Constantino "Rico Constantino") on the April 25, 2002, version of *SmackDown!*.[^23] Wilson finally grew tired of this, and during Tajiri's match against [The Hurricane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregory_Helms "Gregory Helms"), she got on top of the announcer's table and stripped off her clothing. The distraction allowed The Hurricane to pick up the win.[^2] [^24] At [Rebellion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebellion_\(2002\) "Rebellion (2002)"), Wilson and her real life boyfriend at the time [Billy Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Kidman "Billy Kidman") defeated [John Cena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Cena "John Cena") and [Dawn Marie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dawn_Marie "Dawn Marie") in an intergender tag team match. After the match, Wilson and Kidman kissed.[^2] Wilson then got her first [WWE Women's Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Women's_Championship_\(1956%E2%80%932010\) "WWE Women's Championship (1956–2010)") match on SmackDown! after Women's Champion [Molly Holly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molly_Holly "Molly Holly") invaded the show interrupting Wilson's photoshoot and challenging her to a match in which Wilson was unsuccessful in winning.

Wilson started a controversial [feud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feud_\(professional_wrestling\) "Feud (professional wrestling)") with [Dawn Marie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dawn_Marie "Dawn Marie") in September 2002,[^4] [^9] in which Dawn would start a relationship with Wilson's real-life father Al Wilson.[^7] [^9] [^25] On the October 17 episode of *SmackDown!*, Wilson teamed up with Rikishi in a losing effort to the team of Dawn and [Matt Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Hardy "Matt Hardy") [^26] In response, Wilson defeated Dawn at [No Mercy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Mercy_\(2002\) "No Mercy (2002)") in October.[^27] Soon afterwards, Dawn would get engaged to Al, and told Wilson that she would break it off if she spent a night in a hotel room with her, though Dawn would later reveal this was a lie. Dawn would eventually show footage of this at [Armageddon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armageddon_\(2002\) "Armageddon (2002)").[^4] [^7] The feud continued into 2003 when Dawn and Al got married on the January 2 episode of *SmackDown!* in their underwear.[^28] One week later, Al then, in storyline, died from a [heart attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_attack "Heart attack") after having rigorous sex numerous times in succession on their honeymoon.[^4] [^29] [^30] Wilson defeated Dawn again to end the feud at the [Royal Rumble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Rumble_\(2003\) "Royal Rumble (2003)") in what was billed as a *Stepmother vs. Stepdaughter match.*[^4] [^30]

#### Playboy and various feuds (2003–2005)

In May 2003, Wilson appeared in a pictorial and on the cover of *[Playboy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Playboy "Playboy")* magazine.[^4] A few months earlier, when her pictorial was announced, a short storyline feud with [Nidia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nidia "Nidia") developed, with the latter being jealous of the fact that Wilson was chosen for the cover and spread in the magazine.[^31] In weekly [vignettes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vignette_\(wrestling\) "Vignette (wrestling)") aired on *SmackDown!*, both Nidia and her on-screen boyfriend [Jamie Noble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamie_Noble "Jamie Noble") made trips to [The Playboy Mansion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Playboy_Mansion "The Playboy Mansion") to complain to [Hugh Hefner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Hefner "Hugh Hefner") over his choice.[^31] On the *SmackDown!* after [WrestleMania XIX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_XIX "WrestleMania XIX"), during a segment labeled as Wilson's *Playboy Coming Out Party* former [Diva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Diva "WWE Diva") and *Playboy* covergirl [Sable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sable_\(wrestler\) "Sable (wrestler)") made her return to WWE after a four-year absence.[^4] This began a storyline between the two women where for weeks, Sable played mind games with Wilson, being friendly one moment and unfriendly the next.[^32] This all led up to a bikini contest between the two at [Judgment Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Judgment_Day_\(2003\) "WWE Judgment Day (2003)"), which Wilson won.[^33] After the contest, Wilson kissed the former [Women's Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Women's_Championship_\(1956%E2%80%932010\) "WWE Women's Championship (1956–2010)") Sable on the lips, showing her that there were "no hard feelings" due to Wilson's win over the WWE [veteran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veteran "Veteran").[^34] Continuing her feud with Noble and Nidia from earlier in the year, Wilson managed [Billy Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Gunn "Billy Gunn") upon his WWE return in the summer of 2003.[^4] The feud came to an end after Nidia and Noble both became fan favorites and sided with Gunn and Wilson. After the formation of this alliance, Wilson, Nidia, and Dawn Marie feuded with [Shaniqua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linda_Miles "Linda Miles") for a short period of time, which led to Shaniqua physically dominating Wilson and Nidia in a [handicap match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Handicap_match "Handicap match").[^2]

![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6d/Torrie_Wilson_2.jpg/250px-Torrie_Wilson_2.jpg)

Wilson in December 2004

Wilson teamed with Sable in March 2004 to feud with *[Raw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Raw "WWE Raw")* Divas [Stacy Keibler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stacy_Keibler "Stacy Keibler") and [Miss Jackie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackie_Gayda "Jackie Gayda").[^4] The feud's storyline centered on the latter duo's jealousy of Wilson and Sable as *Playboy* cover girls, as well as their recently being named cover girls yet again, as the two Divas were announced to be featured together in an upcoming *Playboy* March 2004 issue.[^4] After weeks of rivalry, Wilson and Sable defeated Keibler and Miss Jackie in a *[Playboy Evening Gown match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evening_gown_match "Evening gown match")* at [WrestleMania XX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_XX "WrestleMania XX") by stripping their opponents of their evening gowns.[^35] Wilson also wrestled Rene Dupree to a draw on the April 29 edition of *SmackDown!*.[^36] In May, Wilson began a second feud with Dawn. As part of the feud, the two had a match with Wilson's career on the line when the *SmackDown!* General Manager at the time, [Kurt Angle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Angle "Kurt Angle"), made the stipulation for the match at [Judgment Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Judgment_Day_\(2004\) "WWE Judgment Day (2004)"), which Wilson defeated Dawn to keep her career.[^37] Later in the year, Wilson engaged in a short feud with Sable, who defeated her at [The Great American Bash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Great_American_Bash_\(2004\) "The Great American Bash (2004)").[^38] [^39] On the July 1 edition of *SmackDown!*, Sable was defeated by Wilson in a rematch. In November, Wilson began feuding with [Hiroko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiroko_Suzuki "Hiroko Suzuki").[^4] On the December 2 episode of *SmackDown!*, Wilson teamed up with [Rey Mysterio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rey_Mysterio "Rey Mysterio") and [Rob Van Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Van_Dam "Rob Van Dam") in winning effort against Hiroko, [Kenzo Suzuki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenzo_Suzuki "Kenzo Suzuki") and [Rene Dupree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rene_Dupree "Rene Dupree").[^2]

On February 10, 2005, on a special episode of *SmackDown!* (recorded on February 4 in [Saitama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saitama,_Saitama "Saitama, Saitama"), [Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan "Japan")), Torrie defeated Hiroko in a [kimono match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kimono_match "Kimono match") by removing her kimono first.[^40] Torrie was then involved in a short-lived feud with [MNM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MNM_\(professional_wrestling\) "MNM (professional wrestling)") valet [Melina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melina_Perez "Melina Perez"). On July 24 at [The Great American Bash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Great_American_Bash_\(2005\) "The Great American Bash (2005)"), the two of them faced in a bra and panties match with [Candice Michelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candice_Michelle "Candice Michelle") as the special guest referee, in which Wilson was defeated.[^41] [^42] A rematch between both of them took place on the July 28 episode of *SmackDown!*, where Melina was once again victorious.[^43]

#### Vince's Devils (2005–2006)

![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/32/Torrie_Wilson_in_Chicago.jpg/250px-Torrie_Wilson_in_Chicago.jpg)

Wilson during a WWE house show in Chicago in January 2005

It was revealed on August 22, 2005, that Wilson and [Candice Michelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candice_Michelle "Candice Michelle") had been moved to the [Raw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raw_\(WWE_brand\) "Raw (WWE brand)") brand as a part of a trade in exchange for [Stacy Keibler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stacy_Keibler "Stacy Keibler") and [Christy Hemme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christy_Hemme "Christy Hemme").[^44] On that night, Wilson and Candice turned heel by attacking 2005 [Diva Search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diva_Search "Diva Search") winner [Ashley Massaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashley_Massaro "Ashley Massaro").[^4] Her and Candice's storyline feud with Massaro continued over the next couple weeks, as the duo, along with their [enforcer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enforcer_\(professional_wrestling\) "Enforcer (professional wrestling)") [Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Marie_Varon "Lisa Marie Varon") (dubbed as the "Skankateers" by [Trish Stratus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trish_Stratus "Trish Stratus")), made things difficult for Massaro.[^4] On the September 5 episode of *Raw*, Wilson defeated Massaro after interference from Victoria and Candice.[^2] Massaro evened the score on the September 12, 2005, episode of *Raw* when she brought out the returning Stratus, and the duo proceeded to attack the heels.[^45] Later that night, Wilson teamed up with Victoria in a losing effort to Stratus and Massaro.[^2] This led to a tag team match at [Unforgiven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unforgiven_\(2005\) "Unforgiven (2005)"), where the team of Stratus and Massaro defeated Wilson and Victoria.[^46] The following night on *Raw*, Wilson was defeated by Stratus. After the match, Wilson, Victoria and Candice attacked Massaro and Stratus.[^2] The feud continued into *[WWE Homecoming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Homecoming "WWE Homecoming")*, where Stratus and Massaro defeated Wilson, Candice, and Victoria in the first three on two [Bra and Panties match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bra_and_Panties_match "Bra and Panties match") by stripping all three of their opponents.[^47]

Wilson was absent from WWE television for quite some time after this, leading to rumors and false reports that she had been released from WWE.[^48] [^49] In actuality, she had taken a leave of absence to tend to some "personal issues".[^48] Wilson was surprised by the rumors after some media personnel offered to help her find work.[^48] WWE issued a statement on their website, in which Wilson also commented, denouncing the rumors.[^48] In the statement, she joked she had to "call the WWE office and ask them if they forgot to fire me".[^48]

On October 17, Wilson made her return to *Raw*, where she, Candice and Victoria faced off against the team of Stratus, Massaro, and their new ally [Mickie James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mickie_James "Mickie James") in a winning effort after Wilson pinned James.[^2] On the November 28 episode of *Raw*, Wilson teamed up with Victoria and Candice Michelle in losing effort to Stratus, Massaro and James.[^50] On the February 13 episode of *Raw*, Wilson was accompied by Candice in a losing effort to Massaro.[^2] Wilson soon turned face once again, after Candice and Victoria both turned against her. On the March 13 episode of *Raw*, Wilson helped Stratus retain the Women's Championship against Victoria who was accompanied by Candice.[^2] The following week on *Raw*, Wilson teamed up with Stratus to defeat Victoria and Candice.[^2] The storyline feud between Candice and Wilson led to a match where Wilson faced *[Playboy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Playboy "Playboy") '* s newest cover girl, Candice, at [WrestleMania 22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_22 "WrestleMania 22") in a *Playboy Pillow Fight*.[^4] [^51] Wilson won the match,[^4] but the feud still continued on-and-off for some time. On the June 12, 2006, episode of *Raw*, Wilson defeated Candice in the first " *Wet and Wild match* ", a wrestling match involving water balloons and squirt guns, in which the winner of the match would be on the cover of the *WWE 2006 Summer Special* magazine.[^5]

Wilson also made a special appearance on WWE's [ECW brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ECW_on_Sci_Fi "ECW on Sci Fi") on August 22, 2006, representing Raw in a bikini contest against ECW Diva [Kelly Kelly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Kelly "Kelly Kelly").[^52] A winner was not decided, however, and a six-person mixed tag match began pitting Wilson, [Tommy Dreamer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Dreamer "Tommy Dreamer"), and [The Sandman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sandman_\(wrestler\) "The Sandman (wrestler)") against the team of Kelly Kelly, [Mike Knox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Knox "Mike Knox"), and [Test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_\(wrestler\) "Test (wrestler)"). Wilson's team was victorious.[^5] [^52]

In the final months of 2006, Wilson entered into an on-screen relationship with [Carlito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carly_Col%C3%B3n "Carly Colón").[^2] On the November 27 episode of *Raw*, Wilson participated in a #1 Contenders Diva Battle Royal that was won by Victoria.[^2] On the December 11 episode of *Raw*, Wilson was defeated by Victoria and after the match [Chris Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Masters "Chris Masters") proceeded to put Wilson in a Masterlock until she was saved by Carlito.[^2] During this time, [Ric Flair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ric_Flair "Ric Flair") took an interest in Carlito, and Wilson accompanied the duo in a series of tag matches.[^2] On the March 12 episode of *Raw*, Wilson was defeated by then Women's Champion [Melina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melina_Perez "Melina Perez"), after the match Melina attacked Wilson but was stopped by [Mickie James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mickie_James "Mickie James"), who was then stopped by Victoria, Melina then attempted to escape but was stopped by [Ashley Massaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashley_Massaro "Ashley Massaro").[^2] In May 2007, however, Carlito turned against Flair and dumped Wilson when he became a heel.[^53] For laughing at Vince McMahon, he decided to put her in a match teaming up with Carlito, in which she lost on the June 4, 2007, episode of *Raw*.[^54]

#### SmackDown and retirement (2007–2008)

The storyline came to a halt when on the June 11 episode of *Raw*, Wilson was drafted to the SmackDown! brand as part of the [2007 WWE Draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Brand_Extension "WWE Brand Extension").[^55] As she returned, she acted as a valet for [Jimmy Wang Yang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Wang_Yang "Jimmy Wang Yang") and the duo was placed in feuds against Victoria and [Kenny Dykstra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenny_Dykstra "Kenny Dykstra").

At [SummerSlam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SummerSlam_\(2007\) "SummerSlam (2007)"), Wilson participated in a number one contenders Battle Royal which was won by [Beth Phoenix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beth_Phoenix "Beth Phoenix").[^2] On the September 21 episode of *SmackDown!*, Wilson was one of the bridesmaids at the Theodore Long – Kristal Marshall wedding.[^2] On the September 28 episode of *SmackDown!*, after losing to Victoria, she was attacked in the ring by the debuting villainess [Krissy Vaine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krissy_Vaine "Krissy Vaine"),[^56] but the feud was prematurely dropped when Vaine left the company.[^57] On the October 29 episode of *Raw*, Wilson competed in the Halloween Divas Battle Royal eliminating Victoria before being last eliminated by [Kelly Kelly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Kelly "Kelly Kelly"), who defeated her with a strike to the groin.[^2] On the November 16 episode of *SmackDown!*, Wilson teamed up with [Mickie James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mickie_James "Mickie James") in a losing effort to Phoenix and [Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Marie_Varon "Lisa Marie Varon").[^2] She was on James's team at [Survivor Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivor_Series_\(2007\) "Survivor Series (2007)"), which was victorious over Phoenix's.[^2] Wilson's last on-screen appearance was during the November 23 episode of *SmackDown!*, defeating long-time rival Victoria.[^2] In November, Wilson took time off from wrestling to undergo [physical therapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_therapy "Physical therapy") in relation to a previous back injury.[^58] Torrie then made several final appearances as part of the company in February 2008, for autograph signings during the WrestleMania Fan Axxess tour – such as February 9 and 11. Following this, she took more time off. On May 8, 2008, Wilson was released from her WWE contract and she subsequently retired.[^59]

#### Sporadic appearances (2009, 2012, 2018)

Eleven months after her release, Wilson returned on April 5, 2009, at [WrestleMania 25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_25 "WrestleMania 25"), competing on the 25-Diva Royal to crown Miss WrestleMania, which was won by [Santina Marella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santino_Marella "Santino Marella").[^60] Wilson was shown in the audience at [WrestleMania XXVIII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_XXVIII "WrestleMania XXVIII") with her then-boyfriend, [Alex Rodriguez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Rodriguez "Alex Rodriguez"), of the [New York Yankees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Yankees "New York Yankees").[^61]

On the January 22, 2018, episode of *[Raw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Raw "WWE Raw")*, Wilson was honored being "one of the greatest female superstars in the history of WWE" and made a special appearance as part of the 25th anniversary of Raw along with fellow wrestlers and former co-workers; [The Bella Twins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bella_Twins "The Bella Twins"), [Maryse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maryse_Ouellet "Maryse Ouellet"), [Kelly Kelly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Kelly "Kelly Kelly"), [Lilian Garcia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilian_Garcia "Lilian Garcia"), [Michelle McCool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_McCool "Michelle McCool"), [Terri Runnels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terri_Runnels "Terri Runnels"), [Maria Kanellis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Kanellis "Maria Kanellis"), and fellow [Hall of Famers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Hall_of_Fame "WWE Hall of Fame") [Trish Stratus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trish_Stratus "Trish Stratus") and [Jacqueline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacqueline_Moore "Jacqueline Moore").[^62] On January 28 at [Royal Rumble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Rumble_\(2018\) "Royal Rumble (2018)"), Wilson made a surprise entrance at number 9 during the first [women's Royal Rumble match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Rumble_\(2018\) "Royal Rumble (2018)"), in which she eliminated [Dana Brooke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dana_Brooke "Dana Brooke") before ultimately being eliminated by [Sonya Deville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonya_Deville "Sonya Deville").[^63] On October, 28, Wilson took part on a Battle Royal during the first all-women's pay–per–view, in which the winner would receive a future WWE Women's Championship match, but was unsuccessful as she was eliminated by [Mandy Rose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandy_Rose "Mandy Rose").[^64]

#### Hall of Fame and sporadic appearances (2019–present)

On March 4, 2019, it was announced that Wilson would be inducted into the [WWE Hall of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Hall_of_Fame "WWE Hall of Fame") class of 2019.[^10] At the ceremony on April 6, she was inducted by [Stacy Keibler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stacy_Keibler "Stacy Keibler").[^65] The next night at [WrestleMania 35](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_35 "WrestleMania 35"), she appeared during intermission while she was introduced as part of the [2019 Hall of Fame class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Hall_of_Fame_\(2019\) "WWE Hall of Fame (2019)").[^66] She made another appearance during the Raw Reunion on July 22, 2019, and on Raw Legends night on January 4, 2021, where she appeared in backstage segments.[^67]

On January 31, 2021, Wilson participated in the [Royal Rumble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Rumble_\(2021\) "Royal Rumble (2021)") at the women's Royal Rumble match being the 17th entrant and the only Hall of Famer in the match.[^68] Ultimately, she was eliminated by [Shayna Baszler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shayna_Baszler "Shayna Baszler").[^68] Later in April, WWE Network listed her as one of the many female performers who made an impact in WWE outside the ring.[^69]

On March 31, 2023, Wilson inducted Stacy Keibler into the [WWE Hall of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Hall_of_Fame "WWE Hall of Fame") class of 2023.[^70]

In April 2024, Wilson appeared at the [2024 WWE Draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_WWE_Draft "2024 WWE Draft") alongside Michelle McCool to announce the 2nd round draft picks.

## Personal life

Wilson says she did not watch wrestling when she was younger,[^7] [^12] [^16] but says that since becoming involved in the industry, she has become a fan of [Hulk Hogan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hulk_Hogan "Hulk Hogan").[^12] While working for [WWE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE "WWE"), Wilson was on the road up to 300 days per year.[^16] She had a [Maltese dog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maltese_dog "Maltese dog") named Chloe, who traveled with her.[^16] While on the road, Wilson worked out at least four days a week.[^9] Usually, her workout consisted of an hour of cardiovascular workouts and a half-hour of lifting weights.[^9] Wilson also has a close friendship with [Stacy Keibler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stacy_Keibler "Stacy Keibler").[^8] [^9] At one point, they were roommates in [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles"), California.[^9]

After dating for four years,[^9] Wilson married [Peter Gruner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Gruner "Peter Gruner"), known on-screen as Billy Kidman, on July 11, 2003.[^4] They lived in [Tampa, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tampa,_Florida "Tampa, Florida") when not traveling.[^7] [^8] The couple divorced in 2008.[^78]

In September 2007, Wilson launched her clothing line, "Officially Jaded", alongside [Nick Mitchell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Mitchell "Nick Mitchell"), a former WWE wrestler who was known as "Mitch" of the [Spirit Squad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spirit_Squad "Spirit Squad"). The couple began dating in mid-2006 while she was still married to Billy Kidman.[^79] She opened a store in [The Woodlands, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Woodlands,_Texas "The Woodlands, Texas"), at the upscale Market Street district.[^80] On September 14, 2008, Torrie made an appearance at the inaugural Wrestler's Rescue event held in [Piscataway, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piscataway,_New_Jersey "Piscataway, New Jersey"), an event to create awareness and to help raise money to support the health care needs of retired professional wrestlers.[^81]

Wilson dated [New York Yankees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Yankees "New York Yankees") third baseman [Alex Rodriguez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Rodriguez "Alex Rodriguez") from 2011 to 2014.[^82] [^83] Wilson married Justin Tupper on September 19, 2019.[^84] The couple announced the birth of their daughter, Poppi Wilson Tupper, in November 2025, born through a surrogate mother months prior.[^85]

After retiring from wrestling, she became a web-based fitness instructor and blogger.[^86]

Wilson's father, Al Wilson, died on April 4, 2019, two days before she was inducted into the [WWE Hall of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Hall_of_Fame "WWE Hall of Fame").[^87] [^88]

## Professional wrestling style and persona

Wilson's wrestling style was extremely technical, which included an arsenal of moves like [dropkicks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dropkick "Dropkick"), [clotheslines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clothesline_\(professional_wrestling\) "Clothesline (professional wrestling)"), [suplexes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suplex "Suplex"), and [pinning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pin_\(professional_wrestling\) "Pin (professional wrestling)") moves.[^3] [^89] Among her finishing and signature moves included the Nose Job [^3] which was a [sitout facebuster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sitout_facebuster "Sitout facebuster"), the [swinging neckbreaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swinging_neckbreaker "Swinging neckbreaker"),[^90] [DDT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DDT_\(professional_wrestling\) "DDT (professional wrestling)") variations,[^91] [^92] a [handspring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Handspring_\(gymnastics\) "Handspring (gymnastics)") [back elbow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Back_elbow "Back elbow") (dubbed [springboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springboard_\(professional_wrestling\) "Springboard (professional wrestling)") elbow) [^6] [^90] and the stink face.[^89]

Her gimmick was that of the all-American glamorous and sexy [girl next door](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girl_next_door "Girl next door") who sometimes carried her female dog Chloe with her during matches.[^93] She was cited by [WWE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE "WWE") as being "one of the most desired Divas during her seven years with WWE. And while she never held the [WWE Women's Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Women's_Championship "WWE Women's Championship"), the former Miss Galaxy winner can lay claim to performing in one of the most downloaded matches in WWE.com history: a Bikini Contest against [Sable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sable_\(wrestler\) "Sable (wrestler)") from [Judgment Day in 2003](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judgment_Day_\(2003\) "Judgment Day (2003)")." [^94]

In an interview with [Chris Van Vliet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Van_Vliet "Chris Van Vliet") which aired on the February 1, 2024 episode of *Insight with Chris Van Vliet*, Wilson revealed that she actually hated doing sexualized segments while in the WWE, describing the numerous bikini contests she engaged in as "mortifying." [^95] [^96] [^97] Wilson would again express her dislike of her sexualized WWE segments in an interview with [Ariel Helwani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariel_Helwani "Ariel Helwani") which aired on the September 17, 2025 episode of *The Ariel Helwani Show*, even describing her renowned 2003 WWE Judgement Day bikini contest with Sable as being "like that nightmare where you wake up in the middle of the night and you’re at school naked.” [^98] [^99] [^100]
