The **poet laureate of [[Idaho]]** or **writer in residence** is the [poet laureate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poet_laureate "Poet laureate") for the [U.S. state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._state "U.S. state") of [Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idaho "Idaho"). After 1982 the title was changed to writer in residence.[^1]

## List of poets laureate

![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3f/Irene_Welch_Grissom.jpg)

Irene Welch Grissom was the first poet laureate of Idaho

## List of writers in residence

- Ron McFarland (1984–1985) [^1] [^2] [^3]
- Robert Wrigley (1986–1987) [^1] [^2] [^4]
- Eberle Umbach (1988–1989) [^1] [^2]
- Neidy Messer (1990–1991) [^1] [^2]
- Daryl Jones (1992–1993) [^1] [^2]
- Clay Morgan (1994–1995) [^1] [^2]
- [Lance Olsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lance_Olsen "Lance Olsen") (1996–1998) [^1] [^2]
- Bill Johnson (1999–2000) [^1] [^2]
- Jim Irons (2001–2004) [^1] [^2]
- [Kim Barnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Barnes "Kim Barnes") (2004–2006) [^1] [^2]
- [Anthony Doerr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Doerr "Anthony Doerr") (2007–2010) [^1] [^2] [^5] [^6]
- [Brady Udall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brady_Udall "Brady Udall") (2010–2013) [^1] [^2]
- [Diane Raptosh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diane_Raptosh "Diane Raptosh") (2013–2016) [^1] [^2] [^7]
- Christian Winn (2016–2019) [^1] [^2]
- Malia Collins (2019–2021) [^1] [^2] [^8]
- CMarie Fuhrman (2021–2023) [^1] [^2] [^9]
- [Kerri Webster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerri_Webster "Kerri Webster") (2023– ) [^10]
