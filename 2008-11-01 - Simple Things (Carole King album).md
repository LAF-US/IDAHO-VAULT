***Simple Things*** is the 8th album by [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States "United States") singer-songwriter [Carole King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carole_King "Carole King"), released in 1977. It is her first album on the Avatar / [Capitol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Records "Capitol Records") label.

*[Cash Box](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cash_Box "Cash Box")* said of the title song that it "lives up to the philosophy of its title and lyric" and "has a deceptively simple melody that draws the listener in after a few short seconds." [^4]
