BOISE, Idaho — Meridian Police is asking for the public’s help identifying two people believed to be involved in a battery at a Walmart parking lot on Ten Mile Road last Thursday.

The incident happened just before 7 p.m. on June 11, when one person allegedly punched the victim in the face during a confrontation, the Meridian Police Department said.

Anyone who recognizes the individuals in the photo is asked to call Ada County Dispatch’s non-emergency line at 208-377-6790 and reference Meridian Police report being 26-3475.
