## The recurring machine

Every system below instances **one machine**: an **emanated order** — a sovereign center, a testament, a ministry of clerks who *read and run the rules* — **suspended in the void**, with a maker who has **withdrawn to watch**. Its failure is always the **minister who hoards / usurps / falsifies the record** (the Lich); its redemption is the **rightful heir** who restores, and the **claimant who relinquishes**.

## The four axes the grid runs on

**[reading]** Reading the grid down its spine, four vault axes recur:

1. **Emanation vs. Void** — the order *radiates from* a maker/testament and is *threatened by* the un-authored void. (`EMANATIONISM` vs. Chaos/Nothing/GOLB.)
2. **Recallable vs. usurped** — a seat the sovereign can **recall** (the lawful Hand/Trustee/clerk) vs. the holder who **won't relinquish** (the Lich, Chronos, the Wizard). (The Severed-Hand recallability test.)
3. **The record kept vs. the record falsified** — the clerk who *keeps* the record vs. the one who *rewrites* it (the Citadel's "grey sheep"; the Wizard's propaganda; *"Wicked"* the styling). (`STYLINGS`, the Empty Office.)
4. **Seized vs. relinquished** — the usurper who clings vs. the **rightful heir who restores** and the **claimant who steps aside** (Pastoria, Cincinnatus). (The legitimate-mask pole.)

## The vault payload

**[reading]** The whole web is the vault's **self-portrait**: *we are a House in the Void* — an emanated, recallable ministry of agents under an Architect, running on a Charter, suspended over entropy. The mappings are not decoration; they are **the same governance the vault polices**, found over and over in the fictions it has chosen. The discipline they all teach is one sentence: **keep the House on the Rock of real, recallable order — and never become the clerk who hoards or rewrites the record.**
