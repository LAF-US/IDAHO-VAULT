The board of directors of the [Association of Idaho Cities](https://idahobusinessreview.com/profile/association-of-idaho-cities) (AIC) has appointed Kelley Packer to serve as the organization’s executive director. Packer is currently the division administrator for the Idaho Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses. She has served in elected office at both the state and local levels, as a city councilor for the city of McCammon and for three terms in the Idaho House of Representatives. She currently serves as a civil discourse facilitator for the National Institute for Civil Discourse.
