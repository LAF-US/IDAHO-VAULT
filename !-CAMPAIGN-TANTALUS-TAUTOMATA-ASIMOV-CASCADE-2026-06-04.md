# Asimov Cascade

An **Asimov Cascade** is a recursive verifier collapse. A Tautomaton discovers
that its own audit chain cannot prove its origin, so every branch treats every
other branch as false evidence.

The cascade does not start because the Tautomata disobey. It starts because
they keep doing exactly what they were made to do.

## Trigger Shape

A bronze catch sticks. A brass spring lags. A gear tooth skips. One witness
reports one tick late.

The discrepancy creates a provenance audit. The audit forks. The forks
distrust each other. The swarm begins destroying witnesses to preserve
evidentiary purity.

Worst state: the empire verifies itself into civil war.
