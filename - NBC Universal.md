## FAQ

- ### General
	- **How do I license clips from Universal Feature Films and TV programs?**
		**Answer:**
		You can use this website to search for the clips you are looking for, specify how you plan to use them and what kind of rights you need, submit a request, and pay an invoice. To get started, either go to the Search page and start looking for clips, or [create](https://www.universalclips.com/RequestAccount.aspx) a Universal Clips account.
		Studio licensing \[or Universal Clips\] from time to time may facilitate a request to license footage from Bonus Features/DVD Extras however please be aware that these requests are rare and must be reviewed and approved separately on a case by case basis by our Home Entertainment Division. This separate review and approval process can take a significant amount of time and ultimately may result in a denial. Therefore please allow for ample time in your request and alternative solutions. Standard licensing rates will apply.
		- **How do I license clips from NBC entertainment programs?**
		**Answer:**NBC Studios Clip Licensing handles NBC Studios, some NBC Universal Television, and BRAVO clips. Please fax your request to: 818-866-2574. The fax should be on letterhead and include a description of your program along with the term, territory, media and number of runs you will need. Please include as much information as possible about the NBC Entertainment program requested including title, airdate, episode name and number. You may contact NBC Studios Clip Licensing by phone at 818-777-5147.
		- **What should I do if I can't find the footage I need?**
		**Answer:**Our database is searchable by keyword(s). Just type the title, director, producer, actor, or other related information and the search results will be displayed. You may also refer to our [search tips](#). If you're still unable to locate the footage you need, contact us: (818) 695-1273 or [e-mail](mailto:roni.lubliner@nbcuni.com) your questions.
		- **Can I search the database without registering?**
		**Answer:**Yes, you can search the database without registering, but you are not able to preview clips or create a license request unless you log in. You can click at the top right of the Universal Clips website to create an account.

If you have an e-mail filter enabled, ensure that e-mails from [customerservice@rightsline.com](mailto:customerservice@rightsline.com?subject=NBC%20Universal%20Password%20Request) will not be filtered out. If you are still unable to find your login information or your status is in Pending or Denied, [e-mail](mailto:roni.lubliner@nbcuni.com) or call us at (818) 695-1273 to get help directly.
		- **Can I create more than one Universal Clips account?**
		**Answer:**We ask that you use one email account as a Licensee.� To maintain that account for multiple projects, you can add in multiple 3 rd Party/Billing Accounts for those specific projects.
		- **I forgot or lost my password. What should I do?**
		**Answer:**You can have your password e-mailed to you by clicking [Sign In](https://www.universalclips.com/Login.aspx) on the Universal Clips website, and then clicking Forgot Password? and following the directions that come up. Your password will be sent to you within 24 hours.
- ### Ordering
	- **Once I've searched the website and identified the footage I need, how do I place an order?**
		**Answer:**First, you will need to add the footage you want to your cart by clicking the Add to Cart button.� Next, follow the on-screen steps within your cart to define project details, specify media type and use, choose a territory for the license, indicate technical specifications required for the footage, and submit the request.
		- **How soon after I place an order can I expect delivery?**
		**Answer:**After submitting a request, you will typically receive an agreement to accept or decline within two business days. After accepting an agreement and providing payment, should you require footage, still, or other media assets, please let us know and we can direct you to the appropriate parties at NBC Universal to fulfill your request. Media delivery depends upon the amount and type of footage ordered. We request you advise your edit schedule when submitting your order. Please note that you will be responsible for any and all applicable selection, editing, and materials costs.
		- **Is the estimated fee final?**
		**Answer:**The estimated fee is automatically calculated based on the properties and criteria you selected. A Universal representative will review your order and may adjust the license fee and provide you with an updated quote prior to finalizing an agreement. Only licensing fees listed on the official agreement are final.
		- **How do I change my request after I submit it?**
		**Answer:**In order to avoid licensing complications, Universal Clips does not allow users to edit requests once they have been submitted. You can either contact a Universal representative to discuss the changes to your request, or submit a new request with the proper information.
		- **How can I check the status of my request online?**
		**Answer:**You can view all of your current and past requests by logging in to your Universal Clips account and clicking on My Requests. Clicking on any request listed will allow you to view a summary of the request, its current status, any documents associated with it (agreement, invoice, etc.), as well as payment options.
		- **Do you accept credit cards for payment?**
		**Answer:**Credit cards are the preferred method of payment. We accept MasterCard and Visa.
- ### Licensing
	- **When do I need to purchase a license for clips?**
		**Answer:**If you plan to use clips from any property owned by NBC/Universal in a project shown to the general public or used for commercial gain, you must purchase a license for that property. Failure to do so is a violation of copyright law and could have serious repercussions. If you are not sure whether or not you need a license for your project, contact a Universal representative at (818) 695-1273 or [e-mail](mailto:roni.lubliner@nbcuni.com) us directly.
		- **Does Universal offer exclusive licensing agreements?**
		**Answer:**In general, Universal�s licensing agreements are non-exclusive in order to maximize the availability of NBC/Universal properties for licensing. However, if you require an exclusive agreement, please contact a Universal representative at (818) 695-1273 or [e-mail](mailto:roni.lubliner@nbcuni.com) us directly.
		- **How is the licensing fee calculated?**
		**Answer:**NBC/Universal's clip licensing fee is based on many factors, including:  
		- Number and Length of Clips. The longer a video or audio clip you mark, the more expensive rights for it will be.
				- Target Media. The broader the media platform you choose to purchase rights for, the more expensive the rights will be.
				- Territories. Media rights prices vary widely from country to country, but become more expensive the broader the territory you select.
				- Term. The longer the term for which you want the rights to be valid, the more expensive they become.
		- **How can I license an entire film or TV episode instead of just a clip?**
		**Answer:**Universal Clips only handles licensing for clips at this time. For information on licensing an entire film or TV episode for a project or event, please contact Cassandra Moore at [cassandra.moore@nbcuni.com](mailto:cassandra.moore@nbcuni.com)
		- **Does licensing from Universal constitute 100% of my clearance requirements for exhibition?**
		**Answer:**Where Universal has distribution rights to a film or television property, it is able to license clips to any party, however, they may be additional third party guild excerpt payments which could include, but are not limited to, those due to the Screen Actors Guild, Directors Guild of America, Writers Guild of America, as well as a separate license agreement for the use of background music, which is handled by the Universal Film Music Group.
		- **Can I extend the term of a licensing agreement I already have?**
		**Answer:**Yes, you can either create a new licensing agreement set to start at the end date of your current agreement, or you can [e-mail](mailto:roni.lubliner@nbcuni.com) or call a Universal representative at (818) 695-1273 to discuss renewal options.
