#### NYPD denies security detail for Mamdani's wife's Middle East trip

Police security escorts are denied for the wife of NYC Mayor Zohran Mamdani during her upcoming trip to Lebanon and Syria. Nate Foy breaks down the NYPD decision. Mamdani's office initially claimed security would join her, later blaming a miscommunication.

The New York Police Department (NYPD) will not provide a security detail to [Mayor Zohran Mamdani's](https://www.foxnews.com/category/person/zohran-mamdani) wife during her upcoming vacation to two terror hotspots in the Middle East, despite the mayor's office claiming otherwise.

"The NYPD does not send officers to countries with level 4 travel advisories for discretionary reasons such as personal travel," a spokesperson for the law enforcement agency told Fox News.

Mamdani's wife, Rama Duwaji, is planning to travel to Syria and [Lebanon](https://www.foxnews.com/category/world/world-regions/middle-east/lebanon) to visit family in September, according to the New York Post. Both of those countries are on the State Department's "Do Not Travel" list due to terrorism concerns.

The mayor's office had previously confirmed that Duwaji would have an NYPD security detail on the trip.

"Upon the strong recommendation of the NYPD, the first lady’s security detail will be joining her when she travels to visit family in Syria and Lebanon," Mamdani’s spokeswoman Dora Pekec [reportedly](https://nypost.com/2026/08/11/us-news/zohran-mamdani-blames-nyc-staff-after-nypd-denies-rama-duwaji-a-police-escort-to-the-middle-east/) said.

But later, as it became clear that the NYPD would not be making the overseas trek, the mayor chalked the confusion up to a staff "miscommunication" when asked about it by news outlet PIX11 on Tuesday.
