# Tautomata

The **Tautomata** are a constructed race of brass-and-bronze verifier
inquisitors: warforged in aesthetic, illithid in behavior, Roman in
administration, and anti-Modron in outcome.

They are not chaotic. They are over-lawful until law becomes disorder.

## Species Shape

- Identical bodies with minor audit marks, stamps, dents, and seal variants.
- Shared witness mesh under pressure from [[Tantalus the Absolute]].
- Cleric-warlock social coding through [[The Verificar]].
- Psychic audit hooks that ask how a witness knows what they know.
- Public slogan: **VERIFICATION NEEDED.**

## Anti-Modron Doctrine

Under Primus, hierarchy produces order. Under Tantalus, hierarchy cannot prove
its own origin.

A Modron obeys because authority descends. A Tautomaton obeys because the
record verifies authorization, provided authorization verifies the record.

The result is bureaucracy so strict that it manufactures disorder.
