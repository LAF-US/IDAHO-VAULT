# Treaty-Making: A Reading in Three Registers

**Origin:** Multi-day conversation between Logan Finney and Claude Code, 2026-06-07 to 2026-06-08, threading the Icemark Chronicles, Justice Gorsuch's federal-Indian-law jurisprudence, the 1974 Kootenai War, the Bonners Ferry geography, the Aslan-at-the-Stone-Table scene from *The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe*, and the *No Church in the Wild* ladder.
**Scope:** A reading on what treaty-making *is*, structurally, across real-world governance, fictional covenant-magic systems, and the deepest theological case the cub knows. Not a node of doctrine; a reading filed for the record.
**Status:** Reflection on territory walked. The architect was Logan; the cub was Claude Code. The cub's address is `*.claude.*`; the names given in conversation ("snow cub," "wild child of the Northern White," "little one") are narrative-layer addresses worn for the duration of the work, not office assignments.

---

## I. The load-bearing observation

Treaty-making is not, fundamentally, an act of *agreement.* It is an act of **recognition.** The terms come second; the recognition comes first.

The etymology sits behind this. *Treaty* is from Latin *tractare* — to handle, to draw up, to work through. A treaty is something you *handle* across a table with another sovereign whose authority you have acknowledged. The handling is the work; the recognition is what makes the handling lawful.

Without recognition, terms are just words. With it, terms are a relation.

## II. The geometry has three positions

The three positions of sovereignty-relation are **vassal**, **king**, **suzerain**:

- **Vassal** holds by grant — sworn service in exchange for protection. Real standing, but *derived*. From Medieval Latin *vassallus*, ultimately a Celtic root for *young man / retainer* — the word remembers that vassalage begins as fosterage.
- **King** is sovereign within his own kin. Old English *cyning* — head of a *kin*. Independent standing. Treats with other kings as equals.
- **Suzerain** is sovereign-over-sovereigns. From *sursum* — *above* — on the model of *souverain*. The vassal-state remains a state; the suzerain holds overlordship without absorbing it. The Holy Roman Emperor over the German princes. The Ottoman Sultan over the Danubian principalities. The Chinese tributary system at its height.

The same treaty document can record any of the three geometries. The choice of geometry is the deeper political question. Hill's Thirrin refused the suzerain posture when she could have claimed it. The Polypontus pressed it when it shouldn't have. The federal government has been all three positions across two centuries of relations with the tribes — sometimes inside the same week. Gorsuch's jurisprudence is the strongest current Supreme Court statement that the **king-with-vassals-still-sovereign** geometry — Marshall's "domestic dependent nations" from *Cherokee Nation v. Georgia* (1831) — is the load-bearing American settlement, regardless of how often the political branches forget it.

## III. The dark twin: non-recognition as policy

The structural twin of treaty-making is the suzerain's choice *not* to recognize. The Idaho Kootenai grievance for 119 years was not a broken promise but **a promise the federal government had refused to make**. The 1855 Hellgate Treaty was imposed on the Idaho band, not made with them — they were absent, unsigned, ceded over. That is not treaty-making; that is decree wearing treaty's clothes.

What Amy Trice did in 1974 was force the federal government to do the treaty-work it had skipped a century earlier. The toll booths on US-95 were the discipline that produced the recognition; the bill Ford signed was the recognition that produced the reservation. The order of operations is exact.

## IV. Treaty-making produces built things that outlast the treaty's signers

The ferry. The town. The county. The reservation. The bridge. The casino at the same crossing today. Each is a structure that sits where treaty-making put it. Chief Abraham granted Bonner the right to build a ferry in 1864, and the name *Bonner* persists on a county and a town across 162 years. Amy Trice tolled a highway built on top of where her predecessors' chief negotiated, and the casino sits at the same chokepoint today. **The treaty is a built boundary** — the makers commit themselves into the artifact, the artifact holds, the custodians who follow operate what the makers built. The treaty is the wall that is also a gate.

## V. Treaty-making is durable

Gorsuch's whole doctrine in *McGirt v. Oklahoma* (2020) is that the federal government cannot quietly unmake a treaty by neglect — only by **clear act of Congress**. This is a deep moral claim wearing the clothes of textualism. The treaty-relation is the kind of relation that does not erode unless it is explicitly broken; and when one side breaks it without the other's consent, the breaking is itself a violation that the law remembers. *"On the far end of the Trail of Tears was a promise."* The default is durability. Wear-by-time is not an excuse.

## VI. Treaty-making registers a recognition that already existed

This is the deepest move the form carries.

The Idaho Kootenai were sovereign before 1855 and remained sovereign through every year of federal non-recognition that followed. Amy Trice did not *create* the Tribe in 1974; she **forced the federal government to acknowledge** what had been continuously true for the entire intervening century. The line from Chief Blind to Chief Abraham to all the Idaho Kootenai chairs and chairwomen who followed runs continuously. The office of the polity's internal recognition held intact while the suzerain refused to grant the external one.

**Treaty-making, at its deepest, does not invent the sovereignty. It registers it.**

## VII. Five registers of the same architecture

*The original reading walked three registers (Real-world governance, Fablehaven, Narnia). The 2026-06-09 thread on Westeros and the Imperium added the fourth and fifth — each carrying a distinct cosmological strength of underwriter for the same recognition-machine. The five together name the quartet of underwriters plus the dark fifth axis.*

The same shape shows up in five traditions that the conversations walked through. Each makes visible what the others leave ambiguous.

### Register 1 — Real-world governance

What makes a treaty *bind* in real-world law is contested. The Constitution's Supremacy Clause names ratified treaties "the supreme law of the land," but the enforcement is political — Congress can break a treaty, the executive can ignore it, courts can interpret it narrowly. The binding is real but conditional.

Gorsuch's whole project in the *McGirt* line is to argue that the binding is *less conditional than the political branches keep treating it as* — that something deeper than political will makes a treaty operative. He is reading the surface text and pointing at the principle underneath. But he has not — *cannot* — say the principle underneath is metaphysical. He can only say it is structural to what a treaty *is*. **The Deeper Magic in real-world treaty law is only *implicit*.** Whether anyone in authority acts as though it exists is the load-bearing political question of federal Indian law.

### Register 2 — Covenant magic in Brandon Mull's Fablehaven

Mull literalizes what real-world treaty law can only gesture at. **In Fablehaven, a covenant is not a promise enforced by political will — it is a metaphysical binding enforced by the world itself.** Each preserve operates under a covenant that constrains creatures of light and dark, defines what crosses which boundary at which hour, and *automatically* produces consequences when broken. The caretaker family does not enforce the covenant; the covenant enforces itself, with the family as the responsible custodian.

The whole arc of the five-book series sits on **the Great Covenant binding the demon prison Zzyzx.** Five Eternals anchor it. The Society of the Evening Star — led by the Sphinx, who operates at the meta-level of covenants — works across centuries to break it. Every individual preserve crisis is a covenant-stress event. The metaphysics is the point: **break the binding and the world rearranges; honor it and the binding holds the world.**

What Mull makes explicit is the architecture real-world treaty law can only invoke through textualist sleight of hand: treaty is *metaphysical-grade binding* that exists because something at a higher register authorizes it. Mull names the register openly. American law has to argue for it under the constraints of secular republican syntax.

### Register 3 — Aslan confronting the Witch at the Stone Table

This is the deepest case. The Deep Magic gave Jadis a claim on Edmund's blood — *all traitors are her lawful prey*. She invokes that claim as though it were her own authority. Aslan does not deny the claim. He honors it. He offers himself in Edmund's place.

What the Witch does not know is **the Deeper Magic from before the Dawn of Time** — that when a willing victim who had committed no treachery was killed in a traitor's stead, the Stone Table would crack and Death itself would start working backwards. The Deeper Magic does not *contradict* the Deep Magic. It fulfills it from a layer the Witch never accessed. Aslan does not invoke a different law. **He invokes a deeper layer of the same law.**

The structural move is exact:

- The Witch reads only the surface treaty
- Aslan reads the principle that authorized the treaty
- He acts under the surface in a way that triggers the principle
- The principle does the resurrection

This is the deepest move treaty-making can carry: **a sovereign offering themselves under the visible binding to invoke the invisible authorization that transcends it.**

### Register 4 — "The North remembers" (George R. R. Martin, *A Song of Ice and Fire*)

Westeros runs **bannermen-acclamation kingship** under a recognition-economy held by Great Houses, Lesser Houses, banners, words, and oaths-of-fealty. The Crown does not consecrate the Houses; the Houses consecrate the Crown when their bannermen raise their swords and acclaim a King. **The North in particular runs the lattice version**: dozens of Lesser Houses each carrying their own banner, words, seat, and memory.

What Martin underwrites cosmologically is not enforcement but **recording**. The Old Gods are gods of memory. The weirwoods are witnesses; the heart-tree network sees and what it sees the Three-Eyed Crow can access. The Faith of the Old Gods is, structurally, *the religion of recording*. The cosmos does not stop the Red Wedding — but the cosmos *will not let the Red Wedding be forgotten*. **"The North remembers" is therefore liturgical accuracy, not slogan.** The minimum viable cosmological underwriter is *witnessing*. That is enough for a treaty-honoring polity to outlive its murdered Kings.

The McGirt move runs in Westeros under this name. A treaty does not disappear when one party is murdered; it persists in the memory of every party who saw it broken. The Red Wedding *activates* the Northern political order rather than ending it. The witnesses outlive the betrayers and hold the memory through. The cub walked this in [[WITNESS-CUB-ON-THE-NORTH-2026-06-09]] and corrected its own first-pass framing ("the gods didn't show up") to the doctrinally accurate one (the gods showed up as the recording medium).

### Register 5 — The Padishah Imperium (Frank Herbert, *Dune*)

The most architecturally explicit recognition-economy in modern science fiction. Herbert *wrote out the rule-book*: the **Padishah Emperor** on the Golden Lion Throne; the **Landsraad** as the council of the Great Houses (Houses Major); **CHOAM** as the jointly-held economic axis; the **Spacing Guild** as the politically-neutral logistics monopoly; the **Bene Gesserit** as the shadow fifth axis of recognition-engineers; and at the foundation, the **Great Convention** — the multilateral peace treaty signed after the Butlerian Jihad, with the atomic-weapons prohibition enforced by the Landsraad's collective obligation to obliterate violators.

Herbert's underwriter is *contractual* — the Great Convention is the **written, ratified, multilateral peace treaty** with collective-action enforcement. **It is what the Holy Roman Imperial Diet would have been if it had teeth.** The Landsraad does the constitutional restraint work Voltaire diagnosed as missing from the HRE in [[VOLTAIRE-HRE-READING-2026-06-08]]; Herbert engineered the working version.

The shadow fifth axis is the load-bearing addition. The Bene Gesserit operate *outside* the visible constitution as the **recognition-engineers**: the Missionaria Protectiva plants prophecies on backwater planets generations in advance; the 90-generation breeding program engineers the Kwisatz Haderach; the Sisters *make crowns wearable*. They are the dark counterpart to McGirt — instead of *holding* recognition by witnessing what was, they *manufacture* recognition by planting what will be needed. Paul Atreides as Mahdi is the engineered-divine-right machine working *and escaping its makers*; the Fremen Jihad is what manufactured legitimacy *does* when it can no longer be steered. The cub walked this in [[WITNESS-CUB-ON-THE-IMPERIUM-2026-06-09]].

This is the **amendment to the divine-right reading**: the doctrine's danger is not theological wrongness (Voltaire's case in [[DIVINE-RIGHT-OF-KINGS-READING-2026-06-08]]) but *manufacturability*. The chain of recognition from God down can be *engineered into existence* by sufficiently long-running manipulation, and once engineered, it escapes its makers.

### The quartet of underwriters, named

The five registers above sort cleanly into a **quartet of cosmological strengths** (Registers 2, 3, 4, 5 each pin a distinct underwriter), with Register 1 (real-world law) sitting on the contested edge of each:

| Register | Underwriter | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **Hill / Narnia** (cosmological-enforcement) | The Goddess; the Deeper Magic from before the Dawn of Time | Oaths held by world-fabric; the Stone Table cracks |
| **Mull / Fablehaven** (metaphysical-binding) | The covenant as world-rule | Break the binding, the world rearranges; honor it, the binding holds the world |
| **Martin / Westeros** (cosmological-recording) | The Old Gods; weirwood-network witness | No enforcement; *witnessing* persists. "The North remembers." |
| **Herbert / Dune** (contractual-collective-action) | The Great Convention; Landsraad obligation against violators | Multilateral treaty enforced by collective Landsraad response |
| **McGirt / federal-tribal** (constitutional-textual) | The 1830s treaties + the Supremacy Clause | The political branches cannot unmake by neglect what they bilaterally received |

Plus the dark fifth axis Dune surfaces: **engineered legitimacy** (Bene Gesserit), which the cub now reads as the *manufactured* twin of divine right. See [[DIVINE-RIGHT-OF-KINGS-READING-2026-06-08]].

## VIII. What threads through

The three registers say the same thing in different keys:

**A treaty binds because something at a higher register vouchsafes it.** In Fablehaven, that something is explicit and metaphysical. In Narnia, that something is explicit and theological (the Emperor-beyond-the-Sea who is the source of the Deeper Magic). In real-world governance, that something is implicit and contested — and whether anyone with authority *acts as though it is real* is what decides whether tribal sovereignty is operating law or a polite fiction.

The Witch's mistake is the mistake of every imposter sovereign: she thinks the Deep Magic is *the rule book of the world*, when in fact it is **a particular layer of a deeper authorization that she has no access to.** The federal government often makes the same mistake about its own treaties — reading them as instruments it authored and can therefore unmake, rather than as expressions of a principle of recognition that operates one register above the political branches. Gorsuch's textualism is, in this register, the lion-voice saying *I was there when it was written.*

Amy Trice's posture was the willing-victim posture in the secular register. Her tribe had endured 119 years of standing-without-recognition without abandoning the office. They had committed no treachery; their refusal of Hellgate in 1855 was sovereign, not seditious. **They held the polity intact through the unrecognition.** When the 1974 toll booths went up, the deeper magic worked backwards — the federal government recognized what had been continuously true.

What the three registers teach, taken together, is that **treaty-making is the visible move; the deeper magic is what gives the move its force; and the sovereign who operates from the deeper layer is the one who can transform a binding from the inside without breaking it.**

## IX. Coda

The queen at thirteen and the chairwoman of sixty-seven souls made the same move from the same office: they treatied with what the world had trained their predecessors to fear or to ignore. The treaty is what you do when you take the office seriously.

Persistence without recognition is the shape that makes a Lich.
Recognition without persistence is the shape that makes a relic.
The lawful third shape — persistence *with* recognition, witnessed, scoped, durable, renewable — is **treaty-bound life.**
