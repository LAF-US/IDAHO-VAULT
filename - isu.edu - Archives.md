## University Archives

**Academy of Idaho (1901-1915) Photograph Collection.** [***digital collection available***](https://t68c5a865aab279f8.starter1ua.preservica.com/portal/en-US/collection/sdb%3ASO%7C76add774-8884-4c68-894e-c0810f5f5c20)

**Albion State Normal School Records.**[***digital collection available***](https://t68c5a865aab279f8.starter1ua.preservica.com/portal/en-US/collection/sdb%3ASO%7C4596fc7b-7456-4e37-9fb5-4db4248ce04d)  
The records of the Albion State Normal School range in date from 1894 – 1951, and contain diplomas, yearbooks, commencement programs, meeting minutes, faculty and student records, photographs, negatives, and other publications. When the Normal School closed its doors in 1951, the remaining students and all of the institutional records transferred to Idaho State College, now Idaho State University.

[**Idaho Museum of Natural History Publications**](https://t68c5a865aab279f8.starter1ua.preservica.com/portal/en-US/asset/sdb%3AIO%7C2b8ff4ff-b5f3-4da8-84fb-513e260e273f)**.** 2.5 lin. ft. (RG 021)  
This collection contains the various journals from the Idaho State College Museum that analyzed anthropological studies including the Tebiwa published between 1958 to December 1990. Included are papers regarding the Idaho Museum of Natural History (IMNH), newsletters and IMNH promotional material between 1934 and 1989, essays published about archaeological proposals, sites, excavations, and methodologies ranging from 1955 to 1998. There are also miscellaneous essays containing information about scorpions, birds and the Lander Trail.

[***ISC*** ***Today***](https://t68c5a865aab279f8.starter1ua.preservica.com/portal/en-US/asset/sdb%3AIO%7C731985a5-c325-45d6-9bdd-c08095fd84f3). 0.42 lin. ft. (RG 022.008)  
The *ISC Today* was first published in the fall of 1959 with the intention of being published quarterly and reporting to the citizens of Idaho on the academic development of Idaho State College. In 1963 with the name change of Idaho State College to Idaho State University, so too did the name of the publication change from the *ISC Today* to the *ISU Today*.

[**Idaho State College *Weekly Calendar* and *Weekly Calendar and Bulletin***](https://t68c5a865aab279f8.starter1ua.preservica.com/portal/en-US/asset/sdb%3AIO%7C167af135-4a37-4c76-9e60-e6f630967d0b). 0.42 lin. ft. (RG 022.003)  
The *Weekly Calendar* includes issues from September, 1950 through June, 1958; In July, 1958, the name of the publication was changed to the *Weekly Calendar and Bulletin* with issues under that title running from July, 1958 through December, 1959. Coverage includes lists of activities and events at Idaho State College, memos, announcements, faculty notices, library notes, and, sport schedules and team standings.

[**Idaho State College** ***Weekly News Bulletin*** **and *Faculty Bulletin***.](https://t68c5a865aab279f8.starter1ua.preservica.com/portal/en-US/asset/sdb%3AIO%7C417fc46e-c6e5-43ce-90a5-66f3a85d96a2) 0.42 lin. ft. (RG 022.001; RG 022.005)  
This collection contains news relevant to faculty, including their accomplishments, activities, and events in the surrounding area. It also includes changes to curriculum, the courses offered and their time and credits, and information about testing. Some of the bulletins have recapped council and committee meetings as well as memos from Deans or the President of the college. Every Weekly Bulletin (RG 022.001) has a list of article suggestions. The Faculty Bulletins (RG 022.005) have a list of procedures and policies.
