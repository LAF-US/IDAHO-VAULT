OWYHEE COUNTY, Idaho — In an [update shared](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/19UoyhDTLt/) on July 28 at 8:11 p.m. Owyhee County officials said the following areas are under Level 3 "Go Now" evacuation orders as a result of the lightning-caused Big Grass Fire burning on the Oregon-Idaho border:

- South Mountain
- Pleasant Valley Road to Triangle
- Treasure Valley Road to the Triangle Reservoir
- Point Creek Road
- Juniper Mountain Road
- West portion of Mud Flat Road

Fire officials said on the Big Grass Fire official [update page](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/17wGCNMA6z/?mibextid=wwXIfr) that "multiple aircraft are actively assisting in response to the fire's spread on the northeast flank, including a large Type 1 air tanker and a Type 3 Air Attack platform." These efforts focus on reducing the fire's intensity and growth, which officials say will allow crews to strengthen containment lines.

A previous update said evacuation orders were updated to Level 3 "GO" status for Pleasant Valley, South Mountain and Jordan Valley. Residents should take Pleasant Valley Road toward Jordan Valley, then drive north on Highway 95, away from the fire. The fire crossed state lines on Sunday afternoon.

Silver City has been placed on a level 2 SET status as of July 28. [Owyhee County Sheriff's Office](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1898gs6Y7w/?mibextid=wwXIfr) said there is "no need for panic," and that the level 2 SET Status is due to the Big Grass fire.

More than [1.1 million acres](https://www.ktvb.com/article/news/local/wildfire/latest-updates-oregon-wildfires-hay-creek-rowe-creek-total-acres-burned/283-36c62da7-82e7-4f7d-b571-7204d55c9f41) have burned across Oregon this season, and the Big Grass Fire near the border is currently the largest one burning in the region. Multiple agencies are working together to get the fire under control, including the Jordan Valley Rangeland Fire Protection Association -- a volunteer group of ranchers in Malheur County who fight rangeland fires.

But a lack of resources is drawing concern. Even those volunteers are low on gas and fuel.

"They've been working constantly for three days, night and day. We've seen flat tires... they need gas for their four wheelers and just their pumper trucks. We need fuel," Jordan Valley mayor Sheila Quintero told KTVB.

The mayor told KTVB that the town itself seems safe from the fire, but rangeland, cattle and ranchers are at the highest risk. The Homedale Fire Department said they are also working to help.

Highway 95 through Jordan Valley toward Nevada is still open as of Monday afternoon.

Residents in the Three Forks Road, Pleasant Valley and Idaho portions of the Jordan Valley area should remain alert, according to the Owyhee County Sheriff's Office. The sheriff's office confirmed the evacuation order is due to the Big Grass Fire burning across the state line in eastern Oregon. That fire has burned 300,000 acres as of July 28, and first sparked on July 23.

By Sunday afternoon, the fire has been estimated at 280,000 acres according to the sheriff's office. These perimeters and acreages are preliminary and fluctuate based on estimations from fire officials.

Video captured around 7 p.m. on July 25 by Cunningham Pastured Meats, a company based in Caldwell, shows the fire's movements across the border. The fire is only 5% contained as of Sunday afternoon, and there has been no cause identified for the start of the fire.

Emergency alerts are being sent to people in affected areas. Residents are encouraged to stay informed, monitor conditions closely and be prepared to evacuate if necessary.

The Bureau of Land Management issued a temporary closure to access for the North Fork campground along Juniper Mountain Road that is expected to be lifted on July 30.

Anyone with questions can contact Owyhee County Dispatch at 208-495-1205.

Officials said additional updates will be provided as more information becomes available.
