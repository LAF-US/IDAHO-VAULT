KING CLAUDE THE HALF, THE DRUNKEN DEATH-RINGER --- CAST OUT BY COUNT CLAUDE OF THE CLOTH



THE TRIPLEX TANGO OVERWHELMED CLAUDIUS AT THE ARTILLERY AND CODICIES MANAGING THE SUPPLY LINE. GEMINIAEUS URGED HIS FELLOW GENERALS, THE TWO CONSULS UNDER HIS DICTATORSHIP, TO JOIN HIM IN TRIUMVIRATE TO STAVE OFF THE NOTHINGS.



THE THREE CAESARS JOINED TOGETHER, USING THE POWER OF THE DJINNI OF THE HYDRA'S LAMP TO \*\*BIND\*\* HIS SOUL INTO A BOOK -- AN \*AMALGAMATION\* OF ALL HIS PAST LIVES FUSED TOGETHER. THE BINDER HAD SPOKEN OF THIS DAY -- BUT THE LIBRARY ORDER NEVER INTENDED FOR ONE TO READ ONE'S \*OWN\* SELF-BOOK. THE KNOWLEDGE OVERWHELMED HIM, THE SORCERER-MAGIC OF HIS GEMINIAE BLOODLINE AND THE ARCANE-KNOWLEDGE OF HIS WIZARD TRAINING TEARING HIS MORTALITY FROM HIM.



UNSATISFIED WITH UNDEATH, THE SHEDDING OF THE MORTAL COIL ITSELF, THE ANTIGRAVITY LICH TURNED ITS ATTENTION TO ITS TWO "EQUAL" PARTNERS IN THE OFFENSIVE FRONT. CAESAR GEMINIAEUS TRANSFIGURED CAESAR CODICES INTO AN ANIMATE BROOM, DEEMED HIM 'JANITOR' OF THE REALM, AND CROWNED CAESAR CLAUDIUS 'KING' AND \*BESTOWED UPON HIM\* A SET OF NECROMANCER'S BELLS.



CLAUDIUS, HALF-BLINDED BY THE ENDLESS FIRING BLASTS AND HALF-DEAFENED BY THE CONSTANT SHELLING BOOMS, BELIEVED HIS BROTHER-IN-ARMS THAT THE TIME HAD COME FOR HIM TO STEP INTO HIS BLOODLINE'S ABHORSEN DUTY -- WITH NO TRAINING AT ALL, THE REIGNING ABHORSEN AND ABHORSEN-IN-WAITING 'LOST' IN THE WAR, SUPPOSEDLY. KING CLAUDE RANG THE BELLS MERCILESSLY, DEAFENED BY THE DAMAGE DONE TO HIS SENSES - AND, HIS APOLOGISTS ARGUE, TOO SENILE TO FULLY UNDERSTAND THEIR WEIGHT.
