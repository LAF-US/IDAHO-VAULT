The [[Idaho]] Transportation Department wanted to shake things up on Eagle Rd. in hopes of decreasing accidents during rush hour.

[As BoiseDev reported,](https://boisedev.com/news/2024/06/04/eagle-road-speed-limit-meridian/) the department has been working on introducing variable speed limits. The changes would take the speed limit from 55 mph to 45 mph during peak rush hour times. The current speed limit on the road is 55 MPH.

The new variable-speed-limit signs were installed last fall and then sat, not varying the speed limit at all.

![Senior Edge Legal - Jun 2026 - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/8bc5656b-95db-4242-8441-fb6cbfe40e72.png)

Senior Edge Legal - Jun 2026 - 2:1

BoiseDev readers wanted to know what happened to the plans for the speed limit changes. According to ITD, everything is still in the works.

ITD Public Information Officer Jill Youmans said the department had to obtain two easements to connect the last outstanding signs to power.

This process ultimately held up the implementation of variable speed limits. The outstanding signs were located at The Village at Meridian and the southwest corner of Eagle Rd. and Ustick Rd.

![PCW_ P1_2_1.png](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/1aceac3f-8150-43bf-a95a-064ccc77fe38.png)

PCW\_ P1\_2\_1.png

Youmans said ITD has now gotten the easements taken care of and is getting to work on testing the signs. If all goes well with testing, she said all the signs will be on “shortly,” within a matter of weeks.

So, as you’re driving Eagle Rd. make sure to keep an eye out, the speed limit could soon be varying.
