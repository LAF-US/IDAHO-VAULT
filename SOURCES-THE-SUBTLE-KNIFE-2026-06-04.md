## Term Definitions

### Dust

In Pullman's canon, Dust is the charged matter of consciousness, knowledge,
maturity, attention, love, curiosity, and human meaning. The Magisterium frames
Dust as sin; the trilogy's moral turn rejects that reduction and treats Dust as
something worth preserving.

Operational Vault definition: **Dust is meaningful residue.** It is what leaks
when a threshold is opened carelessly, what proves that action has consequence,
and what makes a record more than inert surface.

### Windows

Windows are openings between worlds. The subtle knife can cut them; a trained
bearer can also close them. A window permits transit, but an unclosed window is
not neutral infrastructure. It remains an open threshold and becomes a leakage
point.

Operational Vault definition: **a window is any opened interface between
bounded systems.** Examples include branch-to-main transfer, cloud/local sync,
plugin authorization, notebook execution, issue automation, remote credential
access, and cross-agent handoff.

### Seams

Seams are Vault vocabulary, not a cited Pullman technical term in the sources
above. In this note, a seam means the edge of the cut: the boundary where a
window has been opened, must be held, and must be closed correctly.

Operational Vault definition: **a seam is the closure obligation created by an
opened window.** A properly closed seam leaves provenance and no live leak. A
bad seam leaves a hidden opening: stale state, dangling access, untracked
payload, orphaned branches, unclosed sync routes, or undocumented authority.

### Spectres / Specters

Spectres are the consequence class associated with knife-made windows. They are
not merely "leaked Dust." They are entities that prey on adult consciousness and
are tied to the damage caused by opening windows between worlds.

Operational Vault definition: **a Spectre is a second-order hazard created by
threshold cutting.** It is the thing released by the act of opening, even if the
operator later notices the leak. In Vault terms, Spectres include duplicate
automation, stale signers, unmanaged PR loops, invisible state dependencies,
and tool behavior that feeds on attention because a crossing was opened without
a closure plan.

## Working Notes

- The subtle knife is a threshold instrument, not merely a weapon.
- The knife's key narrative danger is not only that it cuts, but that it opens.
- An opened window creates transit, leakage, responsibility, and a closure
  problem.
- Canon check: Logan's seam formulation holds. If the cut/window is not closed,
  the opening remains a leakage point. Separate but adjacent: the act of opening
  knife-made windows is also tied to Spectre creation.
- The Vault already maps this to authorized crossing: the bearer must have a
  fit relationship to the instrument, not merely possession of the instrument.
- In digital-sovereignty terms, every opened interface is a window. If it cannot
  be closed, audited, or attributed, it is not a safe crossing.
- In Arborscape terms, the knife is the cherrypick instrument: it can make a
  precise cut across worlds/branches, but precision does not remove the duty to
  close the cut and preserve provenance.
