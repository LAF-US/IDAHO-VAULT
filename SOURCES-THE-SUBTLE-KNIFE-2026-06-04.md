## Working Notes

- The subtle knife is a threshold instrument, not merely a weapon.
- The knife's key narrative danger is not only that it cuts, but that it opens.
- An opened window creates transit, leakage, responsibility, and a closure
  problem.
- The Vault already maps this to authorized crossing: the bearer must have a
  fit relationship to the instrument, not merely possession of the instrument.
- In digital-sovereignty terms, every opened interface is a window. If it cannot
  be closed, audited, or attributed, it is not a safe crossing.
- In Arborscape terms, the knife is the cherrypick instrument: it can make a
  precise cut across worlds/branches, but precision does not remove the duty to
  close the cut and preserve provenance.
