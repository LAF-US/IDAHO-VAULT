# Table Hooks

## Entry Experience

The party crosses into the Fatherland and every person they meet says:

**You are here to see the Wizard.**

Denial confirms the itinerary. Every road routes toward [[The Centre]].

## Encounter Seeds

- A child asks why in a public queue and every adult goes silent.
- A bridge collapse becomes legal truth only after prevention became illegal.
- A [[The Verificar]] offers to correct one family record in exchange for a
  neighbor's premature truth.
- An [[Asimov Cascade]] begins after a single brass timing catch slips.
- Two Churches denounce the same apostate for saying: "The gate should have
  opened."
- A Tautomaton oracle gives a correct instruction with a missing referent.
- The party must travel without becoming guilty of roaming.
- A rebel cell speaks only in factual past tense to avoid [[Grammar Heresy]].

## Campaign Question

Can the party act on the probable before the empire turns probability into
heresy and then into proof?
