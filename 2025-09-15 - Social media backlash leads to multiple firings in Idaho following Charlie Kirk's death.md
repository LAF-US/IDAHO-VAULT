Charlie Kirk's death continues to reverberate around the country, and its effects can be seen right here in Idaho.

Kirk, who was a political activist and speaker, was assassinated on Wednesday, September 10th, while speaking at Utah Valley University.

The suspected shooter, [22-year-old Tyler Robinson](https://idahonews.com/news/nation-world/tyler-robinsons-shocking-discord-admission-it-was-me-at-uvu-yesterday), is set to appear in court on Tuesday. Robinson is facing multiple felony charges in Utah, including aggravated murder, though it is not yet clear whether or not he will be charged federally as well.

On the evening of Kirk's assassination, an impromptu candlelight vigil was held at the Idaho State Capitol. One man, first identified by the Idaho Statesman as Terry Wilson, can be seen in a now-viral video riding a scooter through the crowd of mourners, yelling "F\*\*\* Charlie Kirk." Multiple people can be seen fighting with Wilson, though, as of publication, only one man, Alfonso Ayala, has been arrested along with Wilson.

Wilson, who is a Boise Black Lives Matter organizer and former BSU adjunct professor, was also found in possession of a vape pen and a loaded handgun.

Since that day, multiple people have also been fired from various positions after posting to social media mocking Kirk's death and his supporters.

A [West Ada School District employee was fired](https://idahonews.com/news/local/west-ada-school-district-fires-employee-after-she-posts-video-gloating-over-kirks-death) from her role with the Meridian High School cheerleading program after posting a video mocking Kirk and apparently condoning his murder.

An Idaho Department of Labor employee who worked with the Human Rights Commission was also fired after posting that he thought Kirk was in hell on social media and implying on an Instagram story that Kirk's death was a "net positive" for society.

A Radio Boise volunteer was removed from programming after posting "inflammatory comments" on social media. The station said in a post that the volunteer acted without authorization and in direct violation of Radio Boise policies by making those comments on social media.

A student-teacher at Lakevue Elementary School in the Vallivue School District also resigned after making inflammatory comments regarding Kirk's death.

Nationally, calls for unity appear to be falling flat. [Marjorie Taylor Green has called for a "national divorce"](https://idahonews.com/news/nation-world/marjorie-taylor-greens-calls-for-national-divorce-from-the-left) from the left in the wake of Kirk's death, and close friend [Vice President Vance says Unity will only be possible once we reach the mountain top of truth.](https://idahonews.com/news/nation-world/vice-president-jd-vance-to-host-special-edition-of-the-charlie-kirk-show-in-tribute-to-slain-friend-utah-valley-university-suspect-tyler-robinson-trump-administration-widow-erika-turning-point-usa) That truth being that one side wants the other dead, and celebrates the death of Charlie Kirk, making unity impossible, according to VP Vance.
