BOISE, Idaho — Seven people were transported for medical care after heat-related incidents at the [Banana Ball event](https://www.ktvb.com/article/news/local/banana-ball-arrives-blue-what-you-need-to-know/277-6ece5952-1976-476b-b949-f02d421a3332) in Boise on Friday evening, authorities confirmed.

A spokesperson for the Boise Police Department told KTVB it was assisting a "large number" of patients around 8:15 p.m. on July 31 at Albertsons Stadium alongside Ada County Paramedics and the Boise Fire Department.

Ada County Paramedics later confirmed seven patients were transported as a result of heat-related issues. The agency, which led the response, also reported a "large number" of patients earlier Friday evening.

Twenty Ada County Paramedics personnel were originally working the event, but eight were brought in as patient contacts increased, an agency representative told KTVB. An additional 12 personnel from Boise Fire and two responders from Acute Rescue, a private ambulance service, were also brought in to assist.

Credit: KTVB

Boise State Athletics shortly before 9 p.m. Friday said the above agencies are "operating under a unified command" with the Boise State Department of Public Safety.

"We recommend people who plan to attend (Saturday's) Banana Ball game take extra caution and be prepared for the heat," a Boise State spokesperson said.

St. Luke's Health System confirmed the emergency department at its Boise hospital had a "small number" of patients with heat-related illness from the game.

KTVB also reached out to Saint Alphonsus for additional information.

Banana Ball is a baseball and entertainment event that travels the country and draws thousands of fans. The Boise area saw triple-digit temperatures after gates opened at 4:30 p.m. Friday at Boise State.
