HELEN HOFF AUPPERLE: ARTISTIC LICENSE (Page 68)  
If there were an “[[Idaho]] Falls School” of painting, Helen Hoff Aupperle would be its founder. An Idaho Falls native, Aupperle was the youngest student accepted to the Chouinard Art Institute. She returned to Idaho Falls in 1940 and taught art for nearly 30 years at Idaho Falls High School. Along with Fred Ochi, Ina Schwartz Oyler, and Suzanne Fonnesbeck, Aupperle helped found The Idaho Falls Art Guild in 1948. “The real measure of her ability as a teacher is that none of her students paint like her,” said Roy Reynolds, a student from the late 1950s.
