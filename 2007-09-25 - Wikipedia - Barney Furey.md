**Charles Manford** " **Barney** " **Furey** [^1] (September 7, 1886 – January 18, 1938) was an American actor of the [silent era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silent_film "Silent film").[^2] He appeared in more than 100 films between 1912 and 1937. He was born in [Boise, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise,_Idaho "Boise, Idaho") and died in [Hollywood, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood,_California "Hollywood, California").[^3]
