## Church’s United States West Area presidency shares gratitude for emergency responders, invite all to join in prayer, service

![A street sign shows sign of fire damage as a person walks their dog after a wildfire hit the northern part of Spokane, Washington, Monday, Aug. 3, 2026.](https://www.thechurchnews.com/resizer/v2/V7IAGQAGT5BB5CMSX7XXTLLZXQ.jpg?auth=fd8f33271deccea97966aa40545695af51e36423465a74a3419fa40f3ae7b06a&focal=1150%2C710&width=800&height=533)

As three different fires in eastern Washington state have burned hundreds of buildings and caused thousands of residents to evacuate near Spokane, The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and its members have been serving and providing support to those affected.

The Church is also sending three semitrucks of relief supplies to Spokane, one carrying water and two carrying food, reported [ChurchofJesusChrist.org on Aug. 6.](https://newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org/article/church-jesus-christ-spokane-wildfires) Local leaders and members are coordinating temporary housing, meals, pastoral care and other assistance as needs arise.

“Local Church leaders are working closely together with Church headquarters to identify needs and provide both temporal and spiritual support,” the Church’s United States West Area presidency said in a [statement on Aug. 6.](https://newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org/article/church-jesus-christ-spokane-wildfires) “The Church has designated emergency response funds, and throughout the Inland Northwest, stakes are supporting trusted community organizations and nonprofits that are providing shelter, food and essential supplies.”

The Church’s United States West Area presidency is composed of [Elder Takashi Wada](https://www.thechurchnews.com/2018/6/20/23221460/elder-takashi-wada-general-authority-seventy-2018/), area president, and counselors [Elder Peter M. Johnson](https://www.thechurchnews.com/leaders-and-ministry/2019-06-10/general-authority-seventy-peter-m-johnson-general-conference-1120/), [Elder Hugo E. Martínez](https://www.thechurchnews.com/2014/5/17/23223355/the-caribbeans-first-general-authority-elder-hugo-martinez/) and [Elder Hutch U. Fale](https://www.thechurchnews.com/leaders/2026/04/12/find-out-more-8-new-general-authority-seventies-sustained-april-2026-general-conference/) — all General Authority Seventies.

There are three fires in eastern Washington, near the Spokane area. The Old Trails fire is the biggest one, and the other two are called the Autumn Lane and Fairview fires. The fires — collectively the [Spokane Wildfire Complex](https://www.spokanecommunityresources.org/) — have destroyed at least 850 buildings, and more than 67,000 people have been evacuated since the fires started last weekend, according to [Associated Press reports.](https://apnews.com/article/wildfire-arson-spokane-evacuation-arrest-3738a2f795ca9cc0cca93eae76fb7ff0) Earlier this week, officials reported that firefighters had established containment lines around all three fires and that they were focused on reinforcing those lines.

“We are grateful for the courageous efforts of first responders, volunteers and community leaders,” added the United States West Area Presidency. “We invite everyone to join us in prayer and in service.”

Among those who have evacuated are Latter-day Saints living in Spokane Washington North Stake, which is in an evacuation zone, and in the neighboring Spokane Washington Mount Spokane Stake, reported [ChurchofJesusChrist.org.](https://newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org/article/church-jesus-christ-spokane-wildfires) Many Latter-day Saints have opened their homes to displaced families and have been assisting with evacuation and relief efforts throughout the region.

“We continue to pray for all those whose lives have been disrupted by these devastating fires,” said President Ronald D. Hardy, president of the Spokane North stake. “We are especially grateful for neighboring stake presidents and members who immediately opened their meetinghouses and communities to shelter displaced members and provide Christlike care during this difficult time.”

Church leaders encourage members to follow evacuation orders, remain informed through local emergency officials and continue seeking opportunities to minister to those in need. Also, the Church continues to coordinate with local emergency management and community officials as response and recovery efforts continue.

Service opportunities are available through [JustServe.org](https://www.justserve.org/), according to the report on [ChurchofJesusChrist.org.](http://churchofjesuschrist.org/) The [JustServe](https://justserve.org/) platform is a free website and app where community organizations list their service needs and where volunteers can find service opportunities around them.

Those wishing to make donations are encouraged to work through one of these established organizations: [city of Spokane](https://www.facebook.com/spokanecity/posts/pfbid02yzwrJJhprvkdfWjo17ijhb6HVKErQZHWV2PQr2H4ytit6XDEzbSebaX32PDQJ6TKl?rdid=DokxJfDoPclaaeav), [Spokane Community Resources](https://www.spokanecommunityresources.org/), [The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints’ humanitarian fund](https://philanthropies.churchofjesuschrist.org/humanitarian-services/funds/), [American Red Cross](https://www.redcross.org/donate/donation.html/) and [the Salvation Army](https://www.salvationarmyusa.org/).

## Groundbreaking date set for Spain’s second house of the Lord

## The Barcelona Spain Temple will enter its construction phase this September

![](https://www.thechurchnews.com/resizer/v2/AO2NQNJWGQLCWXPCEVSMI6YIGA.jpg?auth=c9512fc55b235c370f447100f1705d3332ded1aeff0d9ede9fe3802521e9e9f6&focal=1131%2C886&width=800&height=600)

Exterior rendering of the Barcelona Spain Temple. The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints

Ground will be broken for the [Barcelona Spain Temple](https://www.thechurchnews.com/almanac/temples/barcelona-spain/) on Saturday, Sept. 19, announced the First Presidency of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

[Elder James W. McConkie III](https://www.thechurchnews.com/2022/4/30/23217903/elder-james-w-mcconkie-iii-general-authority-seventy-power-of-the-gospel-of-jesus-christ-to-change-l/) — a General Authority Seventy and counselor in the Europe Central Area presidency — will preside over the ceremony.

After it’s constructed, this will be the second Latter-day Saint temple in Spain and the first dedicated in the 21st century.

Information about the September groundbreaking was [published Monday, Aug. 10](https://newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org/article/updates-for-temples-in-texas-and-spain "https://newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org/article/updates-for-temples-in-texas-and-spain"), on [ChurchofJesusChrist.org](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/).

This year marks the 50th anniversary since the Spain Barcelona Mission was organized — back on May 8, 1976.

## About the Barcelona temple, the Church in Spain

A few years ago, on April 3, 2022, then-Church [President Russell M. Nelson](https://www.thechurchnews.com/leaders/2025/09/28/president-russell-m-nelson-dies-101/) [announced](https://www.thechurchnews.com/2022/4/3/23217529/17-new-temples-announced-by-president-nelson-totaling-100-during-his-ministry/) a temple for Barcelona, Spain. He identified 17 new houses of the Lord in [April 2022 general conference](https://www.thechurchnews.com/2022/4/1/23217651/lds-general-conference-april-2022-talks-photos-summaries/), bringing the number of temples he had announced to that point to 100.

The Barcelona temple, as [previously announced](https://www.thechurchnews.com/temples/2023/1/9/23546044/barcelona-spain-temple-site-location/), is planned as a single-story building of approximately 27,500 square feet. Also planned for the grounds are patron housing, an arrival center and a distribution center.

This temple will be built on a 5.4-acre site northeast of the intersection of Avinguda de la Vía Augusta and Avinguda de la Clota in Sant Cugat de Vallès, a municipality north of Barcelona.

Spain currently has only one operating house of the Lord — the [Madrid Spain Temple](https://www.thechurchnews.com/almanac/temples/madrid-spain/), dedicated in 1999. The Barcelona temple is the only other temple currently announced for the country.

Around 72,000 Latter-day Saints live in Spain, meeting in about 150 wards and branches.

The Church’s first converts in Spain were baptized during the 1950s and 1960s. In October 1968, the Church became legally recognized in Spain. On June 6, 1969, four missionaries arrived from the North Argentine Mission to begin missionary work in Madrid.

The Spain Mission was officially organized on July 1, 1970, and later renamed the Spain Madrid Mission in 1974. The first Spain meetinghouse built by the Church was dedicated on July 10, 1977, in Madrid, and the country’s first stake was also created in Madrid on March 14, 1982.
