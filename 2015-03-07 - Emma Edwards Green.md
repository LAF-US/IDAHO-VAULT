**Emma Edwards Green**, born **Emma Sarah Etine Edwards** (1856–1942), later known as **Mrs. Emma Green**, was an American painter and designer. She designed the original [State Seal of Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_and_seal_of_Idaho "Flag and seal of Idaho") that was used from 1891 to 1957, a modified version of which is also used on the [flag of Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Idaho "Flag of Idaho"). She is the only woman to have designed a state seal.[^1] [^2]

## Biography

![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/76/Seal_of_Idaho_%281891%E2%80%931957%29.png/250px-Seal_of_Idaho_%281891%E2%80%931957%29.png)

The original state seal of Idaho as designed by Green and adopted in 1891; it was used until 1957.

Emma Edwards Green was born Emma Sarah Etine Edwards in 1856 [^3] to Emma Jeanne Catherine Richard and former Missouri Governor [John Cummins Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Cummins_Edwards "John Cummins Edwards") in [Stockton, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockton,_California "Stockton, California"). In 1890, Emma Edwards Green stopped in [Boise, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise,_Idaho "Boise, Idaho"), to visit friends on her way home from the [Arts Students League of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arts_Students_League_of_New_York?action=edit&redlink=1 "Arts Students League of New York (page does not exist)").[^3] [^4] She decided to stay in Boise and began teaching art classes. In 1891, Edwards was invited to submit a design for the State Seal competition sponsored by the First Legislature for the State of Idaho.[^5] On May 5, 1891, Edwards was awarded $100 by Governor [N. B. Willey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N._B._Willey "N. B. Willey") for her design of the [state seal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_and_seal_of_Idaho "Flag and seal of Idaho"), which depicts a miner, a woman and various natural resources of Idaho.[^4] [^5] The original seal painting is now held at the [Idaho State Historical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idaho_State_Historical_Society "Idaho State Historical Society").[^6]

A few years after designing the State Seal of Idaho, Edwards married James G. Green. She had no children, but assisted in raising her [nephews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nephew "Nephew"). Emma Edwards Green died in Boise, Idaho, on January 6, 1942, and was buried with her husband in [Oakland, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakland,_California "Oakland, California").
