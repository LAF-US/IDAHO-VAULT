- Title:
- Date Created/Published: c1896-c1937.
- Medium: 24 photoprints: 23 gelatin silver and 1 albumen; 20 x 13 cm. or smaller.
- Summary: Studio and outdoor portraits of women, infants, and men of several Plateau groups including Yakima, Spokan, Cayuse, Nez Percé, Kootenai, and Salish (Flathead). Includes images of babies in cradleboards, a mother breast feeding a baby outdoors, and family groups. Sitters include Annie Kash Kash, Curly Jim, Minnie Krailey, Antoine, and Joseph Cregg.
