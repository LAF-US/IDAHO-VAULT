This week, I had the opportunity to join over 30 other Idaho state legislators at the **White House State Leadership Conference** in Washington, D.C. The conference was hosted by the White House Office of Intergovernmental Affairs and brought together Idaho lawmakers to hear directly from senior officials in the Trump Administration.

Throughout the day, we received briefings from executive branch employees representing the Departments of State, Interior, Homeland Security, Agriculture, Health and Human Services, the Domestic Policy Council, the Office of Political Affairs, the Office of the U.S. Trade Representative, and the White House Chief Technology Officer. These discussions provided an overview of the Administration’s priorities and the direction it is moving. It was encouraging to hear that many of these policies would be beneficial to our state and local communities and actually reduce the size and scope of the federal government.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!NBlg!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F1551301e-58a4-454e-9259-4043385aa877_328x799.png)

The purpose of the conference was to strengthen communication between the federal government and Idaho legislators and to personally connect us with federal contacts to help us deal with questions and concerns with federal policies. This gathering provided an opportunity to better understand the Administration’s agenda, ask questions, and learn what federal policy initiatives may impact Idaho and other states.

We had the opportunity to see some of our own Idahoans who had been recruited to help the Trump Administration, including Alex Adams and former Representative Wendy Horman. Senator Risch hosted a social event the first evening which was a great way to welcome us to Washington D.C.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!yneL!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8a862e50-26a0-467c-bc0b-f5a3ff14c2a1_1060x580.jpeg)

The White House Office of Intergovernmental Affairs serves as President Trump’s primary liaison to state, local, tribal, and territorial governments, helping coordinate efforts across all levels of government.

As one of your representatives, I believe it is important to stay informed about federal initiatives that could affect Idaho. Hearing directly from federal executive branch officials allows me to better understand national priorities while continuing to advocate for the interests and sovereignty of our state.

I spent the afternoon before I flew back home visiting some of the historic monuments and sights in the big city. I was happy to finally see the WW2 Memorial, in honor of my grandfathers who both fought in the war. It’s amazing to think that George Washington was in the same area with his troops over 200 years ago!

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!EtDd!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Faf79add0-55db-4689-9657-7800110eb6e7_1536x2048.jpeg)

I appreciated the opportunity to represent Idaho at this event and will continue working to ensure Idaho’s voice is heard in federal conversations that shape our nation’s future.
