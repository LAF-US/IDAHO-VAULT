A grocery store is finally confirmed for a growing corridor in Caldwell.

Albertsons announced plans Monday to build a 60,000 sq. ft. store at the northwest corner of Karcher Rd. and Lake Ave. The store is slated to open in the fall of 2027, according to a news release from the company.

“Caldwell is a vibrant and growing community, and we’ve been eager to expand our investment in the area to serve more families here in our backyard,” Brad Street, Albertsons’s executive vice president for its western region, said in a news release. “As a company founded in Idaho, we take great pride in serving communities across our home state. This new store reflects our commitment to delivering the value, convenience and fresh products that matter most to our customers and making shopping easy for local families. We look forward to earning more customers for life and being a strong community partner for generations to come.”

BoiseDev reported [the likelihood of an Albertsons store at that location in May](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/05/06/you-asked-which-new-grocery-stores-are-coming-to-karcher-rd-in-caldwell/). At the time, a company representative said she could not confirm or deny the company’s involvement at the site.

The news release said that the store will include offerings familiar to Albertsons patrons at other locations, including a Starbucks, meat and seafood, deli offerings, fresh produce, a floral department, and a pharmacy with a drive-thru option.

The grocery store would be part of a commercial development called The Charles Marketplace. In addition to the Albertsons, a site plan for the project showed additional potential businesses, including banks, an auto shop, a gas station and convenience store, and small businesses like dentists and hair salons.

The announcement comes as Albertsons shared plans earlier this year to add [two to three new stores in Idaho](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/03/20/albertsons-susan-morris-idaho/). The company also owns land [at the northeast corner of Cherry Ln. and Franklin Rd. in Nampa](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/06/09/you-asked-is-albertsons-planning-a-store-at-cherry-and-franklin-in-nampa/), but did not respond to BoiseDev’s inquiry earlier this summer about potential plans at that site.

Meanwhile, to the west, another grocery store is also in the works at Karcher Rd. and Indiana Ave. Colliers Associate Logan Wheeler told BoiseDev that plans are still moving along there with an unnamed grocer. While contract signing could come in the next month, the exact timing on that is not set in stone, he said.
