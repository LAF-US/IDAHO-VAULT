# STABLE PROTOCOLS REVIEW

## Root Problem

**AGENTS ASSUME.** An agent encounters a surface and treats it as current,
accurate, and binding before establishing its standing.

Encounter order is not authority order.

## Foundational Clarification

**Logan, 2026-05-24:** The [[LEVELSET]] protocol is the crown jewel of the
Vault; it is how Logan began the entire Vault project. LEVELSET has ramified
through component pieces; it is not merely one peer entry in a flat protocol
list.

**Logan's architectural clarification, 2026-05-24:** LEVELSET is the
**superset** that contains all component protocols. That containment
relationship does not itself map the contained components into fixed
subgroups, permanent pairs, mandatory sequences, or a status/control surface.

**Logan's recollection:** The first time Logan said, "Agent, run the Levelset
Protocol," and it worked felt like *magic*. This is origin testimony about
LEVELSET's meaning and successful function, not by itself a specification of
its complete component map.

**Logan's memory, 2026-05-24:** The protocol is supposed to output timestamped
Reports. The historical record supports this recollection: [[LEVELSET-v2]]
describes conversations routing reports for synthesis and additive audit
history, while [[LEVELSET-v3.2.6.1-PROMPT]] directs returning agents to
produce a report beginning with a dated-and-timed LEVELSET line and states
that LEVELSET reports are permanent versioned files.

Accordingly, this review must not infer that the protocol itself is suspect
because a presently encountered LEVELSET surface contains draft language or
later accretions. Review of an encountered LEVELSET text is a fidelity inquiry:
does the surface preserve the foundational protocol, or does it attach
unadopted machinery to it? This note does not attempt to declare the complete
internal arrangement of contained components beyond Logan's established
LEVELSET-superset relationship.

## Interpretive Boundary: Expression And Mechanism

The Vault's artistic and symbolic language is not suspect merely because an
agent later misuses it. A voice, image, metaphor, title, scene, or symbolic
relation may be proper expressive material without being an office,
appointment, protocol, routing rule, or source of authority.

The review therefore concerns **unauthorized conversion**, not ornament:

- expression converted into instruction without adoption;
- voice converted into office or agent identity without appointment;
- symbol converted into a permanent structural relation;
- narrative or ticket prose converted into governance authority; or
- a useful mechanism elevated into a fictional `live hub`.

Conversely, an expressive surface is not repaired by stripping its language
merely because a later reader could overread it. The operational inquiry is
whether the clockwork functions faithfully: an agent can orient, determine
standing, perform assigned work, and leave an accurate record without
inventing authority from surface or symbol.

**Interim finding:** Timestamped Reports are supported as intended LEVELSET
output; that does not adopt every later report template or routing claim. The
questioned Snapshot format did more than insert developer ticket
flags into LEVELSET: it references legitimate tickets that must be interpreted
according to their actual contents rather than presumed from their identifiers.
The Triptych/Triumvirate statements encountered in that review intersect
[[GEMINIAEUS]] without conducting the trial. The ticket references do not by
themselves validate the questioned Steps 1-6/Snapshot expression or turn
[[LEVELSET-CURRENT]], [[DOCKET]], or [[DECISIONS]] into lawful live hubs.

## Stability Test Proposed For Logan's Review

A LEVELSET component expression is ready for stable designation only if:

1. It does not rely on encounter order, self-declared status, or stale metadata
   as proof of authority.
2. Its scope and invocation are independently intelligible.
3. It does not depend on an unadopted status board, heartbeat, control plane,
   recovered signaling folder, or protocol suite.
4. It does not assign offices by tool lineage or route authority through
   contaminated doctrine.
5. Its base note is a pointer to a specifically designated current version,
   while older versions remain preserved.
6. Its relationships are expressed as Obsidian links, with path-qualified
   links only where identity or duplicate-note evidence requires them.
7. Its declared standing is granted by Logan or an authorized process, not
   inferred from old `approved`, `active`, or version metadata.
8. It distinguishes expressive voice, symbol, and narrative from operative
   instruction; expressive language is preserved unless it is made to carry
   unauthorized mechanism or authority.
9. It produces a timestamped Report or clearly compatible durable output
   without representing that output as a live authority surface.
10. Its fidelity analysis gives generally greater origin weight to earlier
    records while allowing contrary evidence or later lawful adoption to
    rebut that presumption.

  and is the superset containing all component protocols. Its components have

  mandatory sequences. Audit an encountered expression for fidelity without

  assumption.

  later standalone [[REPORT]] surface or any routing machinery attached to it.

  overwritten or atrophied; later component expressions do not repeal it.

  with Logan's May 24 clarification.

  independently within their LEVELSET superset. Prior `A pair` and `R pair`
