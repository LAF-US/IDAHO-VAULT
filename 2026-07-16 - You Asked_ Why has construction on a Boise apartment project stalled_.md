Earlier this spring, crews arrived on a long-vacant piece of land along ParkCenter Blvd., near Apple St., to start work on a new apartment building.

The property, at 787 E. ParkCenter Blvd., [is set to include](https://boisedev.com/news/2024/11/04/787-parkcenter-boise-kimball/) 30 apartment units targeted at residents over the age of 55, parking, and a small retail space in a three-story building next to an ICCU branch.

But after some wood framing and steel beams went up, construction workers have not been spotted on the site for weeks, leading scores of BoiseDev readers to ask about the status of the project.

Rendering of the 787 ParkCenter project in SE Boise. Rendering: Courtesy Studio H Architecture

Beth Gregg, who is working on the project with her sister Jenni Storti and dad Scott Kimball said in a statement they hope to restart work soon.

“Construction is temporarily paused while we work through an issue that was identified during the inspection process,” they wrote. “We have come to a solution with the city, and are awaiting formal approval. We anticipate construction to begin shortly.”

Plans submitted to the city late last year showed minor changes from BoiseDev’s first report, including changing the cafe space to a small retail space and adding an additional apartment to the plan.

![BoiseDev House - OTT - Commercial](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/8b916803-64e1-4279-a378-7e47d28a2871.png)

One reader thought the project was listed for sale due to a sign posted on the property next door, which is currently being used to store construction materials for the apartments. But the apartment project itself is not for sale.

Kimball, who developed the Aspen Lofts in Downtown Boise nearly 20 years ago, [told BoiseDev in 2024](https://boisedev.com/news/2024/11/04/787-parkcenter-boise-kimball/) that he plans to live on the property and keep the project long-term.
