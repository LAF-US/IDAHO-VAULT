An Eagle nursery specializing in locally grown perennials that thrive in the Treasure Valley is now open at its new location.

[Old Valley Nursery,](https://www.oldvalleynurseryidaho.com/) which has been at its original location for over 30 years, moved down the road to 4278 W. Old Valley Rd.

“It’s a gorgeous property with a 100+ year-old house on it! We’re open now, but over the next couple of years, we’ll be updating the space with plans to add a gift shop and coffee shop,” owner Greg Merideth said.

![PCW - P3_ 2_1.png](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/c4722f34-be9c-413f-844a-ffd39cbbecde.png)

PCW - P3\_ 2\_1.png

Old Valley Nursery was founded more than 30 years ago by Kathy Ellensohn and Tim Fease on their family farm, where a passion for hand-nurtured perennials took root. In 2023, Merideth and his family assumed ownership of the retail nursery, carrying on that legacy.  
  
“Today, we blend tradition with community-first initiatives — supporting veterans, funding youth scholarships, and donating to local cancer hospice care,” Merideth said. “Because at Old Valley Nursery, it’s in the roots.”

In addition to locally grown perennials, the nursery also has an array of seeds, flowers, and decor to bring your garden to life. Old Valley Nursery also offers landscape design services.

The new location is open seasonally from April through October, Monday through Saturday from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.
