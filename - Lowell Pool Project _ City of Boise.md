## Lowell Pool Project

![Lowell Pool Entrance](https://www.cityofboise.org/media/14181/lowell-pool-entrance-2.jpg?width=1200&mode=max&upscale=false)

Lowell Pool Entrance

The City of Boise has selected [CORE Construction](https://coreconstruction.com/ "https://coreconstruction.com/") to complete the design work for Lowell Pool. A contract was approved by the Boise City Council on Tuesday, July 14, 2026. The timeline for on-site construction has not yet been determined, but the Boise Parks and Recreation Department will keep the public informed as the project progresses. Please be assured that the City of Boise remains fully committed to this project and is dedicated to seeing it through to completion.

*Project completion estimates are subject to change due to volatility in the construction industry. Current challenges include material availability, rising costs, supply chain disruptions, staffing shortages and more. Boise Parks and Recreation will update project timelines as needed if delays occur.*
