As the Treasure Valley starts to develop to the east, a large Utah developer is at work on a plan that could add thousands of homes along the Ada/Elmore county line.

Third Cadence has taken on the role of developer for 1,400 acres in a 5,000-acre development planned for Elmore County called Mayfield Townsite. The company has experience in master planning large communities like 4,100-acre Daybreak in South Jordan, Utah, and 634-acre, 2,817-home Terraine in West Jordan, Utah.

Daybreak was worked on by Third Cadence and later sold to Larry H. Miller Real Estate. According to [Building Design + Construction,](https://www.bdcnetwork.com/building-sector-reports/mixed-use/news/55165838/a-surging-master-planned-community-in-utah-gets-its-own-entertainment-district) the Utah project includes more than 9,300 homes, with another 10,000 planned.

[BoiseDev last reported](https://boisedev.com/news/2021/08/05/mayfield-townsite-elmore-boise/) on Mayfield Townsite in 2021 when the property owners and county were caught in a back-and-forth over whether the 2011 development agreement was still valid. Property owner Teresa McCallum and her late husband, John, were seeking approval of a preliminary plat for the property at the time.

Mayfield Townsite is one of at least four large-scale projects in the works in the area, including [the Mayfield Springs project](https://boisedev.com/news/2022/10/11/mayfield-springs-idaho/), which has construction currently underway, mixed-use commercial and [industrial development Peregrine](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/05/27/with-treasure-valley-expanding-east-large-industrial-commercial-project-planned-next-to-possible-casino/), and a [possible casino](https://boisedev.com/project/peregrine-shopai-casino/) spearheaded by the Coeur d’Alene and ShoPai tribes.

## Preliminary plat

Since then, Third Cadence Senior Vice President of Community Development David Crowder said a preliminary plat has been approved by the county, and a new ownership group, Mayfield Ranch Estates LLC, bought up 1,400 acres of McCallum’s property — with the option to buy more in the future.

![Western States - 2026 Q2 - 4:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/77f3a89a-125c-4f85-be07-1598966cc675.jpg)

“We’re looking at that as phase one, so future phases would come along later, assuming that we’re successful in everything,” Crowder said.

This 1,400-acre property, known as Settler Springs Village at Mayfield Ranch on the preliminary plat, has entitlements for 3,000 homes, according to Crowder. The preliminary plat for the property shows just over 1,400 homes planned.

[As BoiseDev reported,](https://boisedev.com/news/2021/08/05/mayfield-townsite-elmore-boise/) 15,000 homes are possible for the entire Mayfield Townsite. A 2023 preliminary plat BoiseDev obtained from Elmore County shows the acreage divided into 14 different planning areas.

Crowder said the exact phasing of Settler Springs is still being worked out and that right now, Third Cadence is in the early planning stages for the property.

## What’s next

He said his team is meeting with local entities and service providers to determine how to deliver various services and infrastructure to the site.

He said the developers will be required to provide their own wastewater treatment facility, much like the requirement for Mayfield Springs. Crowder said in addition to wastewater, Mayfield Townsite is required to have its own water system. He said his team is still figuring out the details of this. Crowder said long-term plans for schools are still in the works.

![Spitfire Tacos - 2:1 - July](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/54ed1bde-1f1b-4a28-b2f9-6a9a0f55a1c5.jpg)

Spitfire Tacos - 2:1 - July

Crowder said he expects all the engineering, planning, and infrastructure coordination to take approximately a year before the developer can move into construction.

“Timing will ultimately depend on further analysis and coordination with public agencies,” Crowder said in an email.

When construction does start, Crowder said his team will initially be focused primarily on housing. While there could be minor commercial uses mixed in, he said large places like a grocery store would likely set up shop closer to I-84 at first, but Mayfield Ranch LLC doesn’t own the land over there.

![A1 Heating - 2:1 - Beat the Heat](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/89752a40-7ebf-454b-9e1f-898264293d49.jpg)

In addition to multiple neighborhoods being broken up over 14 planning areas, the 2023 preliminary plat shows pathways for equestrian and ATV access, water storage ponds, open space, and walking and bike paths.

Crowder said more detailed site plans will be finalized in the coming months.
