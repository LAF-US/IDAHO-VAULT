**English:** Photographs taken by — the official photographer with the Ferdinand V. Hayden Geological Surveys of the Territories, 1869-1878.

- In pre-statehood [Idaho Territory](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Idaho_Territory "Category:Idaho Territory"), [Montana Territory](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Montana_Territory "Category:Montana Territory"), and [Wyoming Territory](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Wyoming_Territory "Category:Wyoming Territory").
- Including the **[Hayden Geological Survey of 1871](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayden_Geological_Survey_of_1871 "w:Hayden Geological Survey of 1871")**.
