# DISAMBIGUATION - ALETHIOMETER

**Filed:** 2026-07-09. This note fixes the scope of `alethiometer` as a specific Philip Pullman / *His Dark Materials* reference. It is a disambiguation surface only; it does not adopt new VAULT doctrine.

## 1. Source Reference

An **alethiometer** is a fictional instrument in Philip Pullman's *His Dark Materials* and related Pullman-world materials.

It is commonly described as a compass-like or watch-like device with symbolic markings around its face. A reader sets hands to symbols to form a question, then interprets a moving indicator's response through layered symbol meanings. In the fiction, the device is associated with truth-reading and with communication through the deeper metaphysics of Pullman's world.

The term is generally glossed from Greek roots:

- `aletheia` - truth
- `metron` - measure

This etymology supports the source-world sense of a truth-measuring or truth-reading device, but the device remains a specific Pullman reference.

## 2. Core Lore Function

The alethiometer is not just an ornament or generic magic item. Its function depends on:

- a fixed symbolic interface
- layered meanings for each symbol
- a reader capable of holding the question and meanings correctly
- interpretation rather than simple mechanical output
- source-world linkage to Dust / Shadows and truth-access

The important feature is disciplined symbolic reading inside Pullman's fictional system.

## 3. Distinguish From

### The subtle knife

The VAULT already contains Pullman-related doctrine around the **subtle knife**, especially in `THRESHOLD-DOCTRINE-v1-2026-05-21.md`, where the knife is used as an intrinsic-fitness / authorized-crossing model.

The alethiometer is not the subtle knife. The knife cuts thresholds between worlds. The alethiometer reads truth through symbols.

### Dust

Dust is part of the metaphysical field in Pullman's work and is related to how alethiometers can communicate truth. Dust is not itself the alethiometer.

### Daemons

Daemons are Pullman-world manifestations of a person's inner being or soul. They are not alethiometers.

### Generic divination

The alethiometer may resemble a divinatory instrument, but the term should not be flattened into a generic name for any oracle, sensor, dashboard, intuition, reading practice, or interpretive framework.
