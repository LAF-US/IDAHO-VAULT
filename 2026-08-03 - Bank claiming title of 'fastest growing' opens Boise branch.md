A new bank that says it is growing faster than any other in the country set up shop in Boise.

Dubuque, Iowa-based Capra Bank has added its fourth branch location in the former Wells Fargo branch location at 1400 S. Vista Ave. in Boise. The bank also has locations in Lubbock, Texas, and Albuquerque, New Mexico.

“Like our other markets, Boise has lost many of their great local financial institutions to acquisitions that just did not pan out for the clients, the community, or the employees,” Capra CEO Tut Fuller said. “It doesn’t have to be that way, and Capra can fill that void.”

![Valley Lookout house - generic](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/82689d5d-b8b7-4c7f-9d4c-b24100ffd632.gif)

Valley Lookout house - generic

Capra was founded as Peoples Savings Bank in Montezuma, Iowa. The Fuller family purchased Peoples Savings, and after rebranding the Montezuma location, the branch was acquired, and customers and employees [were moved to another bank.](https://www.montejournal.com/home/first-state-bank-announces-the-acquisition-of-capra-banks-montezuma-location)

Capra’s Boise branch is led by former First Interstate Bank market president Jeff Huhn.

“I’m excited to build the leading team of bankers in the Treasure Valley,” Huhn said. “Capra gives us a unique platform to deliver local banking and local decision-making to the multi-decade relationships we value so much.”

![Valley Lookout house - Drew](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/12c5b6ba-e344-4f74-a83b-fe4af9ea9df5.gif)

Valley Lookout house - Drew

Capra [claims the title](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/capra-bank-recognized-fastest-growing-204000418.html) of fastest-growing bank in the country, though the rankings looked at just 30 banks across the country for the classification under $3 billion in assets. When Capra crossed $1 billion in assets, it gave all employees at the time a $10,000 bonus, [according to the Dubuque Telegraph-Herald.](https://www.telegraphherald.com/news/public_announcements/article_9fd6260d-1bfb-4b4f-af4c-9c0499e71ae8.html)
