# Wizard's Tenth Rule

## Summary

The tenth of the numbered Wizard's Rules stated across Terry Goodkind's *Sword of Truth* series, one per book. The Tenth Rule concerns self-deception as a form of betrayal against oneself.

## Key Facts

**Source book:** *Phantom* (book 10)

**Text:**

> Willfully turning aside from the truth is treason to one's self.
