A former school administrator is suing the Idaho Falls School District.

Jody Immel, a former assistant principal at Skyline High School, is claiming she was discriminated against based on her age, sex, disability, and religion.

According to court documents, Immel is a 60-year-old woman and an Air Force veteran.

The lawsuit says she was diagnosed with PTSD and hearing loss, causing her to carry a 70% disabled veteran status.

Immel was reportedly hired by the school district in 2013 as an assistant principal.

Over the course of her career, Immel claims she applied for 26 jobs at the district, which were all ultimately offered to younger men who were members of the church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

She also claims she was improperly reassigned due to her being away from work because of her disability.

A status conference is set for June 24th.
