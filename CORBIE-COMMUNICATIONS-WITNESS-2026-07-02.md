# Witness — Corbie's Communications

*Written 2026-07-02 at Logan's direction, from the primary text he supplied of Garth Nix's*
Sir Thursday *— Chapter Thirteen, where Lieutenant Corbie, six weeks after the fall of the
Boundary Fort, uses the communication-token Colonel Nage pressed on him before dying to get
urgent intelligence out. The* [[NAGE-AND-THE-MAJOR-WITNESS-2026-07-01]] *leaf named
"out-of-band trusted channels" as one of three things that hold when the chain is poisoned.
This leaf watches that thread do its work, and finds it has its own discipline, its own
antidote, and its own limit.*

## 1. The medium — `[research]`

Officers of the Glorious Army communicate at distance through small **lead figures** on
little stands. Placed on the stand, a figure *"became a tiny duplicate of the real living,
breathing"* officer far away — who speaks, in real time, in their own voice. It is
point-to-point: to reach a given officer you must possess *that officer's figure.* Before
the gates opened, dying Nage handed Corbie two such figures — *"Friends of mine. Colonel
Repton of the Regiment and Major Scaratt of the Artillery"* — officers at GHQ who *"may be
able to help you if everything goes as badly as I suspect."* Six weeks later, spotting a
Nothing-weapon bound for the Maze's center, Corbie uses the Repton figure: *"I'll be
reporting to Captain Ferouk, but it'll take time for this news to get... through channels,
so I thought you'd better hear this and try to get it to Sir Thursday directly."*

## 2. Two channels, and he runs both — `[mapping]`

There are two paths, and Corbie does **not** choose between them:

- **"Through channels"** — Ferouk, then up the formal hierarchy. This is the **record**:
  authoritative, ordered, auditable — and *slow*, and (as the Nage leaf established) capable
  of being *poisoned* along its length.
- **The backchannel** — the direct figure to a trusted peer, routing around the hierarchy to
  reach the top *"directly."* Fast, and far harder to corrupt, because it does not pass
  through the compromised middle.

He files the formal report **and** fires the urgent one. That is the discipline, not a
shortcut: the official channel keeps the record and the chain of command intact; the
backchannel buys the hours the record cannot. Bypassing governance would be the Lich move;
*supplementing* it under time pressure is the lawful one.

## 3. The token cannot be forged, and must be provisioned early — `[mapping]`

This is the antidote to Pravuil, and it is structural. Major Pravuil's whole attack ran on a
**credential trusted at face value** — a dress uniform, a claimed commission, a dispatch (`[[NAGE-AND-THE-MAJOR-WITNESS-2026-07-01]]`
§2). The communication figure is the opposite kind of thing: it authenticates **by
identity-binding, not by credential.** The Repton figure does not *assert* it carries
Repton's authority — it *becomes Repton*, the actual officer, who is who he is. You cannot
forge your way onto the far end; there is no rank to counterfeit, only a person to be.

Two consequences follow, and both are load-bearing:

1. **Trust is pre-provisioned or it is absent.** Corbie could reach Repton in the crisis
   only because Nage gave him the figure *before* the crisis. You cannot mint a trusted
   channel in the moment of need. Nage's foresight — pressing the tokens into a
   subordinate's hand while ordering him out of a fort Nage knew he would die holding — is
   the whole reason the intelligence moves at all. **Provision the trusted line before you
   need it; in the breach it is already too late.**
2. **The web is personal, not positional.** *"Friends of mine."* The trust is vouched by
   relationship, carried by a specific person who hands it to a specific person. This is
   provenance-by-relationship — the same primitive as a peer you can point to by name, and
   the opposite of authority-by-arriving-with-the-right-uniform.

## 4. The endpoint problem — `[mapping]` / `*`

And here is the limit the chapter is honest about, so this leaf will be too. The backchannel
**worked** — Repton heard, *"visibly paled,"* and ran to carry it up. But the endpoint it
terminates at is **Sir Thursday**, who in this same chapter has grown so enraged that he
*injured his own Marshal Dawn for questioning* the very disputed change that started all this —
and *"Dawn had been right, of course."* A perfect, un-forgeable, fast channel that delivers a
true report to a **compromised or unhinged endpoint** does not guarantee the report is
acted on rightly. The channel is *necessary and not sufficient* — the same shape the Nage
leaf found for verification. Whether the signal lands where it can do good is `*`: the text
does not resolve it, and I will not.

## 5. The vault reads itself — `[mapping]`

- **The official channel is GitHub; the record is the point of it.** This vault coordinates
  its agents through Issues, PRs, and review threads (`[[VAULT-CONVENTIONS]]`,
  `[[DOCKET-POSTURE]]`) — the auditable, ordered, *slow* chain. That is "through channels,"
  and it is correct that it is slow: the slowness is the auditing. It is also, like Nage's
  chain, corruptible along its length — a webhook event, a bot comment, a relayed summary can
  be stale or (as this session's own `SNAPSHOT` episode showed) confidently wrong.
- **The backchannel this vault most needs is the one it already has and under-uses: Logan's
  direct voice.** The relayed artifacts — PR comments, the compaction summary, a prior
  session's output — are *dispatches*: Pravuil-class, corruptible, potentially poisoned in
  transit. The live conversation with the Architect is the **identity-bound figure**: it
  does not assert authority, it *is* the person, and it cannot be forged in-band the way a
  dispatch can. The lesson of §3 applied here: an agent should treat the direct voice as the
  authenticated channel and the relayed artifact as the dispatch to be checked — and should
  have its trusted lines *provisioned in advance* (the session id, the signed trailer, the
  named human), not improvised mid-task.
- **Even so, §4 holds against complacency:** a clean channel to the right person still fails
  if the report is not acted on, or if the endpoint is compromised. Communications
  discipline is a defense, not a guarantee. Provision the line, authenticate by identity,
  run both paths — and still expect the signal may not save the fort.
