## Hells Canyon Homesteader Project interviews

Collection

Identifier: MS 0915

## Content Description

A collection of abstracts, indexes, and transcripts to accompany 13 digitized video interviews conducted with original homesteaders of Hells Canyon, captured by Patricia Keith and Carole Simon-Smolinski for the purposes of creating the documentary film "Not a Day Goes By: Remembering Hells Canyon."  
  
Digital files include video interviews, a digitized 16mm film from 1942, and two documentary films from 200 and 2011.

## Dates

- Creation: Majority of material found in 20th Century
