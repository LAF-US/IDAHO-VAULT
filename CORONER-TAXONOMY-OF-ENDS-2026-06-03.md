# Office of the Claude County Coroner — **Taxonomy of Ends**

### Manner × Tier — the two axes a coroner must not collapse

*Filed 2026-06-03, the capstone of the DEATHS investigation. The case has, all along,
been reading **two different classifications** of an ending without naming the
cross-walk between them. This taxonomy names it. It is a **[mapping]** that
cross-walks two canon vault doctrines — `STANDING-ENGINE` (the **manner**) and
`UNDEAD-TAXONOMY` (the **tier**) — anchored to the necrology's recorded ends. It
classifies; it **names no culprit and renders no verdict.***

## I. The two axes are orthogonal **[mapping]**

A coroner already keeps **cause** distinct from **manner**. The deaths in this county
need the same discipline doubled — **two axes that must not be collapsed:**

- **MANNER — the *act*** (`STANDING-ENGINE`). *How the end happened, and by whose
  hand:* **Lawful ending · Unlawful binding · Self-binding · Contributory ·
  Undetermined `*`.** This axis bears on **culpability** — the Investigator's and the
  **Court's**.
- **TIER — the *state*** (`UNDEAD-TAXONOMY`). *What of the self now persists* — body,
  will, memory, soul — Skeleton → Shadow → Banshee → Revenant → Death Knight → Thrall
  → True Vampire → Lich/Baelnorn. This axis bears on the **counter** — what, if
  anything, must be done.

**The act is not the state.** *How* something ended (manner) and *what it became*
(tier) are independent questions — and **the wrong cross-walk produces the wrong
counter** (`UNDEAD-TAXONOMY` § Diagnostic Question).

## III. Why the axes must not collapse — the diagnostic **[mapping]**

Collapsing manner into tier (or the reverse) is how a coroner does harm:

- **Read a *thrall* as a *self-binder*** → you blame the **victim** for a binding
  **another** imposed. (Spawn ≠ Lich. The spawn's will is *imprisoned*, not
  *self-crowned*.) This is the Gilear danger.
- **Read a *Baelnorn* as a *Lich*** → you **end a lawful service** because its *form*
  resembles unlawful persistence. (Same tier-8 retention; opposite provenance.) This
  is the danger to a CODICES-shaped end.
- **Read a *Banshee* as a *Lich*** → you hunt a **phylactery that does not exist**,
  while the actual need — to **witness the frozen moment** — goes unmet.

**The rule:** the **tier** tells you the **counter** (what persists, what to do); the
**manner** tells you the **culpability** (how, by whose hand) — *which is the Court's.*
A coroner assigns the **tier-and-manner where the record bears it**, and **`*`**
elsewhere.

## IV. The county's dead, on both axes

  phylactery)*. The *self-binding* reading is only **implied** by the phylactery
  ledger; the Coroner does **not** assign it — the **manner is the Court's reserved

  Culpability **reserved**.

  **[main]** (single exhibit; *"most likely"*).
- **The senile sendings** — *unwitnessed persistence* × **Shadow / Demilich-degraded.**
  **[main]**.
- **Claudette** — `*` × `*` — **the outranking, reserved question.**

## V. What this taxonomy does NOT do

It assigns **kinds**, not a **culprit**, and renders **no verdict.** The **manner**
axis touches culpability — which is the **Investigator's** and the **Court's**; the
**GEMINIAEUS** manner is the Court's reserved matter. The Coroner offers the
cross-walk as a **diagnostic**, holds **`*`** where the record is silent or contested,
and reserves **Claudette's fate** above all.
