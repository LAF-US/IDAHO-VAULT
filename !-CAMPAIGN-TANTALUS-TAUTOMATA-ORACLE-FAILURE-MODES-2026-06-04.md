# Oracle Failure Modes

The Tautomata are often right. That is the problem.

They produce accurate non-help: true statements that fail at timing, context,
social uptake, or actionability.

## Modes

- Cassandra failure: true warning, socially rejected.
- Agnes failure: true prediction, context-starved until too late.
- Padparadscha failure: true observation, temporally delayed.
- Brazen Head failure: true announcement, action window closed.
- Croesus failure: true prophecy, fatally ambiguous referent.

Identical Tautomata bodies make the failure mode unknowable until after the
consequence.

Doctrine line: truth without timing is indistinguishable from mockery.
