FāVS News is expanding

FāVS News is expanding its coverage across Washington state, and we’re inviting clergy, faith leaders, nonprofits and community members to help shape the conversation.

Since our founding in 2012, FāVS News has been America’s only digital newsroom devoted exclusively to local religion news. As a nonprofit, we’ve built a [regional and national wire](https://favs.news/use-our-content-religion/) serving 15 media partners — including The Spokesman-Review, Northwest Public Broadcasting and Spokane Public Radio.

As we expand statewide, our goal is to amplify diverse voices and cover how faith impacts communities across Washington — from Spokane to Seattle, from rural towns to urban neighborhoods.

## What we cover

- Faith communities and religious life
- Social justice and community service
- Interfaith dialogue
- Ethics and public life
- Religion and politics
- Education and faith
- Local and statewide community issues

Our mission is to foster thoughtful conversation, highlight community work and elevate diverse voices across Washington.

## Communities we’re looking to connect with

As FāVS News expands, we’re especially interested in hearing from communities in Seattle, Tacoma, Olympia, Vancouver, Yakima, the Tri-Cities, Bellingham, Wenatchee and rural communities across Washington.

## How to get involved

**Pitch a story idea**  
Know of an issue affecting your faith community? A unique program or a compelling personal story? We welcome tips and ideas from across the state. Email FāVS editor Tracy Simmons at [tracysimmons@favs.news](mailto:tracysimmons@favs.news).

**Write a column**  
We publish perspectives from clergy, faith leaders, scholars and community members representing a wide range of traditions and viewpoints. [FāVS columnists](https://favs.news/columnists/) write on a recurring basis — all perspectives are welcome. Email [tracysimmons@favs.news](mailto:tracysimmons@favs.news) to pitch your column idea.

**Freelance for us**  
We’re looking for freelance reporters based across Washington state to help expand our coverage. Religion beat experience is helpful but not required — curiosity, fairness and comfort with AP Style matter most. Email [tracysimmons@favs.news](mailto:tracysimmons@favs.news) with a brief introduction and any clips.

**Share your community’s work**  
Faith-based and community nonprofits are encouraged to share initiatives, events and community impact stories. We’re especially interested in how organizations are serving their communities across Washington.

**Subscribe to our newsletter**  
Not ready to contribute but want to stay connected? [Subscribe to the FāVS News newsletter](https://www.claudeusercontent.com/?domain=claude.ai&parentOrigin=https%3A%2F%2Fclaude.ai&errorReportingMode=parent&formattedSpreadsheets=true#) to receive our coverage directly in your inbox.

## Partner with us

FāVS News currently syndicates to 15 regional and national media partners, and we’re looking to grow that network. If your publication — secular or religious — is interested in receiving our coverage, email [tracysimmons@favs.news](mailto:tracysimmons@favs.news).

## Support our work

FāVS News is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Our journalism is made possible by readers and community supporters who believe local religion news matters. If your organization is interested in underwriting our statewide coverage, we’d love to talk. Email [meganguido@favs.news](mailto:tracysimmons@favs.news) to learn about sponsorship and underwriting opportunities.

You can also support FāVS News with a tax-deductible donation at [https://favs-news.fundjournalism.org/supportfavs/](https://favs-news.fundjournalism.org/supportfavs/).
