**Anne Rearick** (born 1960) [^1] is an American photographer.[^2] [^3] [^4] In 2003 she was awarded a [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship "Guggenheim Fellowship") and in 2014 the [Prix Roger-Pic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prix_Roger-Pic?action=edit&redlink=1 "Prix Roger-Pic (page does not exist)"). Her work is held in the collection of the [San Francisco Museum of Modern Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_Museum_of_Modern_Art "San Francisco Museum of Modern Art").

## Early life and education

Rearick was born in [Caldwell, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caldwell,_Idaho "Caldwell, Idaho").[^1] She received a Master of Fine Arts degree from the [Massachusetts College of Art and Design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_College_of_Art_and_Design "Massachusetts College of Art and Design").[^5]
