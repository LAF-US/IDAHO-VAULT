# Deltora — The Seven Tribes and Their Lands

> A standalone reference on **Emily Rodda's *Deltora Quest*** — the land of **Deltora**, its seven ancient **tribes**, their seven talisman **gems**, and the **Belt** that binds them. ## The land

- **[canon]** **Deltora** is a roughly **pentagonal** land, bordered by the **Silver Sea** (west), the **Sea of Serpents** (east), the **Ocean of the South** (south), and the **Barrier Mountains** to the **north**. Beyond those northern mountains lies the **Shadowlands** — a barren, desolate plain ruled by the **Shadow Lord**, Deltora's enemy.
- **[canon]** **Del** is the capital and royal seat — where the blacksmith **Adin** forged the Belt. Three rivers thread the land: the **River Del** (from the **Os-Mine Hills** past Del to the sea), the **River Tor** (rising near **Dread Mountain**, running through the **Emerald** and **Amethyst** territories), and the **River Broad**.
- **[canon]** The land is divided into the **seven ancient tribal territories**, each named for the gem of the tribe that holds it — so the map of Deltora *is* the map of the seven tribes.

## The seven tribes and their gems

**[canon]** Each tribe revered one **talisman gem**. In the order they sit on the Belt, the seven gems' initials spell **DELTORA**:

| Gem | Tribe | Symbol of | Power (per series) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **D**iamond | the **Jalis** | strength, innocence, purity | gives courage and strength; wards pestilence; aids true love |
| **E**merald | the **Dread Gnomes** | honour | dulls in the presence of evil **or a broken vow**; remedy for sores, antidote to poison |
| **L**apis Lazuli | the **Mere** | good fortune | the "heavenly stone" — brings luck; affiliated with the opal |
| **T**opaz | the **Del tribe** | faithfulness | opens doors to the **spirit world**; clears the mind; wards the terrors of the night |
| **O**pal | the **Plains tribe** | hope | grants **glimpses of the future**; aids weak sight |
| **R**uby | the **Ralad** | happiness | **pales** near evil or threatening misfortune; wards evil spirits; antidote to snake venom |
| **A**methyst | the **Toran** (of **Tora**) | truth | changes colour near illness; **pales near poison**; guides to sincerity and peace |

**[per series, ⚠️ light]** Tribal characters as the series gives them: the **Jalis** warriors; the **Dread Gnomes** of Dread Mountain; the water-dwelling **Mere**; the **Del** smiths (Adin's people); the **Plains** folk; the **Ralad** master-builders (who raised the palace at Del); and the **Toran** sorcerers, a people **bound by a solemn vow** — which is why their kin-gem the **emerald** dulls when a vow is broken.

## The Belt of Deltora

- **[canon]** **Adin**, a blacksmith of Del, dreamed of a **belt of seven steel medallions** joined by fine chains. He forged it, then travelled to **every tribe** to persuade each to give up its talisman gem. So armed, he **united the seven tribes**, drove out the **Shadow Lord**, and became Deltora's first king.
- **[canon]** While worn by the **true heir of Adin's line**, the assembled Belt is an artefact of **immense protective power** that keeps the Shadow Lord beyond the mountains. Its strength **depends on the unity of all seven gems** — and on the legitimacy of the wearer.

## The breaking — and the quest

- **[canon]** The Shadow Lord conquered Deltora not by force but by **deceit**: the Belt was set aside / broken, its **seven gems scattered** and hidden across the land in **seven guarded perils**, each a deadly place — the **Forests of Silence**, the **Lake of Tears**, the **City of the Rats**, the **Shifting Sands**, **Dread Mountain**, the **Maze of the Beast**, and the **Valley of the Lost**.
- **[canon]** Only the **rightful heir** (Lief, of Adin's line) and his companions can win the gems back from their guardians and **reassemble the Belt** — the scattered tokens of legitimacy made whole again to restore the kingdom and cast out the Shadow Lord.
