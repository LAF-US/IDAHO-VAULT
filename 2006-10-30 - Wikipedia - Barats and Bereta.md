**Barats and Bereta** was the web-based [sketch comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sketch_comedy "Sketch comedy") duo of **Luke Barats** and **Joseph Bereta**. Their [viral videos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viral_video "Viral video") have also earned them the description of an Internet phenomenon. At its peak, their YouTube channel had over 420,000 subscribers.[^1] Both have portrayed characters on the YouTube web series *[Annoying Orange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annoying_Orange "Annoying Orange")*.

## History

Formed in 2003, the duo originally made videos to amuse friends, often combining real and fictional acts. They met when both were students of [Gonzaga University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gonzaga_University "Gonzaga University").[^2] [^3] These videos eventually made it onto their website and onto [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube "YouTube").

[NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC "NBC") signed them for a one-year deal in 2006.[^3] "This is Culdesac", the sketch comedy pilot they produced, directed, wrote, edited, and acted in under contract, was not picked up by NBC. Their next television project was a more traditional [sitcom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sitcom "Sitcom").[^4] The pilot did not result in the series airing.

*[The Huffington Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Huffington_Post "The Huffington Post")* has covered videos from the duo in multiple [online publications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_publication "Online publication").[^5] [^6]

In 2012, Bereta became a host on the news and current events web series, *[SourceFed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SourceFed "SourceFed")*, and would remain as a host on the channel until December 31, 2014.

In 2014, Barats and Bereta was listed on [NewMediaRockstars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NewMediaRockstars "NewMediaRockstars") Top 100 Channels, ranked at 50.[^7]

In 2017, both Barats and Bereta became members of the [Smosh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smosh "Smosh") team; Barats as a writer for Smosh sketches, while Bereta worked as a content producer.[^8]

As of April 2018, Bereta is no longer working for Smosh and is working full-time on [The Valleyfolk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Valleyfolk "The Valleyfolk") YouTube channel and company with his friends and fellow founding SourceFed hosts [Elliott Morgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliott_Morgan "Elliott Morgan") and [Steve Zaragoza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Zaragoza "Steve Zaragoza"). Barats later replaced his position.[^9] In 2019, the Valleyfolk competed on the NBC comedy competition show *[Bring the Funny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bring_the_Funny "Bring the Funny")*, winning the show's first season.

From 2022 to 2025, Joe Bereta worked as an executive producer and host of the YouTube channel REACT.[^10]
