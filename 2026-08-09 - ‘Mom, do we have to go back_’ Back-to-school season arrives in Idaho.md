August is back-to-school month across Idaho – for most students.

Two schools started last week. Friday, Aug. 7 was Day 1 for Sage International students in Boise and Middleton.

Idaho’s two largest districts, Boise and West Ada, return Wednesday, Aug. 12.

To the excitement – or chagrin – of some students, another month remains before the latest first day of school in Idaho: Sept. 9 at the Hollister Charter School.

The first days of school in Idaho’s 198 school districts and charters fall on a 33-day spectrum. Earlier this year, [a failed bill](https://www.idahoednews.org/legislative-roundups/statehouse-roundup-2-16-26-bills-updating-school-facilities-funds-head-to-house/#ww--1) in the Statehouse would have consolidated that spectrum into one day: the Tuesday after Labor Day, which falls on Sept. 8 this year. Currently, around 16 districts or charters start on that day.

The bill’s sponsor, Rep. John Vander Woude, a Nampa Republican, said the tourist industry “would like to keep their employees through Labor Day.” The House Education Committee killed the bill.

As school buses return to Idaho roads, drivers can refresh their memory on the rules around the big yellow buses with [a guide from the Ada County Highway District.](https://www.achdidaho.org/my-commute/let-s-get-there-safely/winter-driving-copy)
