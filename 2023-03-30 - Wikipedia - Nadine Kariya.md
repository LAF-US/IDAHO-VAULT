**Nadine Kariya** (born 1947, Boise, Idaho) is an American [jewelry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewellery "Jewellery") artist. She attended the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington "University of Washington").[^1]

In 2017 the [Bainbridge Island Museum of Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bainbridge_Island_Museum_of_Art "Bainbridge Island Museum of Art") held a retrospective of her work entitled *Nadine Kariya: The Hammer and the Peony*.[^2] Her necklace, *Kingfisher Caught Between Man's God and Mother Nature,* was acquired by the [Smithsonian American Art Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smithsonian_American_Art_Museum "Smithsonian American Art Museum") as part of the [Renwick Gallery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renwick_Gallery "Renwick Gallery") 's 50th Anniversary Campaign.[^3]
