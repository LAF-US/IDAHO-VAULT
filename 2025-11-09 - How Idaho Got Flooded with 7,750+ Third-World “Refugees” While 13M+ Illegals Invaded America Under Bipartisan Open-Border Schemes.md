## Follow the Money

The entire anti-American replacement system depends on one vulnerable point: taxpayer funding that can be cut off completely.

The Office of Refugee Resettlement received [more than $7 billion in FY 2024](https://www.cfr.org/backgrounder/how-does-us-refugee-system-work-trump-biden-afghanistan) under Biden. Every foreign placement generated $12,750 in immediate federal grants, but the real theft happened through state matching requirements that multiplied costs exponentially. Yet as I write this, you’llbe pleased to know that Trump has slashed the entire operation. His administration [suspended refugee resettlement](https://www.rescue.org/article/how-have-trump-policies-impacted-refugees), [froze millions in federal funding](https://www.npr.org/2025/02/12/nx-s1-5288819/refugee-agencies-federal-funds-layoffs), and [proposed eliminating](https://news.bgov.com/bloomberg-government-news/refugee-money-slashed-migrant-child-fund-launched-in-trump-plan) the $307 million Refugee Support Services program and the $564 million Transitional and Medical Services program entirely.

In Idaho, orgs like the [International Rescue Committee](https://www.rescue.org/united-states/idaho) and [Agency for New Americans](https://www.jannus.org/programs/agency-for-new-americans/) (aka Jannus) have siphoned millions from American taxpayers, processing thousands of foreign placements while American veterans sleep homeless and American families struggle with medical debt.
