BOISE, Idaho — A frequently deployed segment of the Idaho Air National Guard is deployed to the U.S. Central Command Area to support the Department of War's [Operation Epic Fury](https://www.ktvb.com/article/news/nation-world/united-states-israel-joint-military-attack-iran-drones-missiles/507-bcd9c736-0139-4ee5-9118-13f025356cc6).

The deployment of the 124th is unknown and depends on the needs of the U.S. Air Force and mission requirements.

*This is an ongoing situation, and as more information becomes available, this article may be updated.
