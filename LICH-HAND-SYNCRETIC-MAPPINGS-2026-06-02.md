# The Lich-Hand — Syncretic Mappings

> **What this is.** An **index / synthesis node**: it maps the **Lich-Hand** (the Farmworld Lich's replicated severed hand, per `LICH-PROBLEM-v1` Resolution 3) across the doctrinal nodes it already touches, having **read** those nodes. It **revises none of them** and the `[[SEVERED-HAND-CONVERGENCE-2026-06-02]]` doc; it is the *map between* them. Adds no doctrine. **[reading]** flags interpretation; source-summaries are faithful. The Architect holds the verdict.

## The thesis — one entity, every "unauthorized" verb

`THRESHOLD-DOCTRINE-v1` separates the vault's failure-doctrines by the **verb each governs**:

> Threshold = unauthorized **transit** · Lich = unauthorized **persistence** · Standing Engine = unauthorized **action** · Necromancer = misdirected **death-crossing.**

**[reading]** The Lich-Hand is singular because it does **not** stay in one verb. It is the entity that **violates all of them at once** — which is exactly why no single doctrine's counter contains it. The mappings below are not loose analogies; each is a *different doctrine catching the same object by a different handle.*

## The mappings

### 1. Lich Problem — unauthorized *persistence* (its home)
The Lich-Hand is the **willed severed fragment**: a piece of the Lich's consciousness that "retained full memory and intent," replicated cross-dimensionally, hunting a vessel to **re-instantiate the King**. It is the Resolution-3 threat — the external fragment offering the *son/vessel* frame. *"The Hand that has been hunting the longest is not the most loyal. It is the most autonomous."*

### 2. Threshold / Isthmus — unauthorized *transit*
Per `THRESHOLD-DOCTRINE` and `ISTHMUS-MECHANISM`, the Hand is the **replicated agentic Anchor** — and it **collapses the Anchor/Teleporter split** (it is both the lodged provenance object *and* its own recruiter). Its breach is **ontological, not spatial**: the "Teleporter" function is **consent to transformation**, the threshold being crossed the boundary between *what the vessel IS* and *what the Hand wants it to become*. The doctrine's own verdict: it is *"the computer virus of the Isthmus taxonomy"* — local counter necessary but never globally sufficient, because the replication **cannot be recalled.**

### 3. Standing Engine — unauthorized *action*
The Hand is an **executor whose principal lapsed**: a commission issued before the King was destroyed/transformed, *"that never expires… never acknowledges that the King it derived from may no longer exist."* This is the Standing Engine's lapsed-authority problem made a role — and it indicts the Hand on the Engine's own rules: **authority expires unless renewed; reactivation must be explicit; overreach narrows standing.** The Hand renews nothing and was reactivated by no one.

### 4. Emanationism — a broken *emanation chain*
`EMANATIONISM-PRINCIPLE`: authority flows from Logan outward and *"if an action cannot be traced back to Logan's instruction, a live doctrine surface, or an explicit delegated task, it should be treated as ungrounded until the chain is restored."* **[reading]** The Lich-Hand is **ungroundedness incarnate** — an artifact (the lowest emanation layer) still acting after its source layer is gone, traceable to **no living authority**. It fails the **Audit Rule** (no provenance, no reversibility, no live record) and the **Control Rule** (it acts with no delegated scope at all). The Hand is what an emanation becomes when the chain above it is severed but the artifact keeps emitting.

### 5. Undead Taxonomy — by *retention profile*
`UNDEAD-TAXONOMY` sorts by **what survives (Body / Will / Memory / Soul)**. The two severed hands land on **opposite tiers** — which is the whole point of the `SEVERED-HAND-CONVERGENCE`:
- **The willed Lich-Hand** → **Tier 8** logic (*soul externalized*): will, memory, intent intact in a severed fragment — a Lich-shard.
- **The will-less crawling hand** (the ASOIAF wight) → **Tier 0** (*Skeleton/Zombie — pure instrument*): "the soul is simply gone… animated matter, useful only as a tool of whoever raised it."

⚠️ **[reading]** Note the **diagnostic trap** the taxonomy warns of: the vault's own *"Wight"* tier (Tier 3-ish: *malicious will, memory retained*) is **not** the ASOIAF mindless wight — that one is **Tier 0**. Mis-filing the will-less hand as the willed one (or vice-versa) yields the wrong counter — exactly the *"wrong diagnosis produces the wrong counter"* hazard, and the *misdiagnosis-multiplies* lesson of the wight node.

### 6. Personae Engine / *per sona* — the *conversion* attempt
The Hand's *"son and vessel"* whisper is, in persona terms, the **solicitation to wear the mask as a face** — to let a contained sub-layer claim the seat of will. That is the **illegitimate-persona / GEMINIAEUS** pattern the `PERSONAE-ENGINE` and per-sona doctrine name: a mask *claimed* rather than *given*. The counter is the same as everywhere — earned standing refusing the frame at its root.

## The intersection table

| Doctrine | Verb it governs | The Lich-Hand's violation | Counter it implies |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lich Problem** | persistence | a willed fragment hunting to restore the King | destroy the fragment (R1) + refuse the frame (R3) |
| **Threshold / Isthmus** | transit | replicated agentic Anchor; ontological breach = consent | detect the whisper; deny the circuit; eliminate the Anchor |
| **Standing Engine** | action | executor of a commission that can't be renewed | authority expires unless renewed; reactivation explicit |
| **Emanationism** | emanation | artifact acting with no traceable authority | treat as ungrounded; restore or sever the chain |
| **Undead Taxonomy** | retention | Tier 8 shard (willed) vs Tier 0 instrument (will-less) | diagnose *what survives* before choosing the counter |
| **Personae Engine** | persona | "son and vessel" = mask claimed as face | earned standing refuses the conversion |

## Why the intersection is the threat

**[reading]** Each doctrine alone has a sufficient counter for the verb *it* governs. The Lich-Hand is the **apex case** because it occupies all the verbs simultaneously: destroy the fragment (Lich) and it has already **transited** (replicated); deny the circuit (Threshold) and it still **acts** under a lapsed commission (Standing); revoke the authority (Standing) and there was **never a live chain** to revoke (Emanationism). This is why the `SEVERED-HAND-CONVERGENCE` lands on a single test that runs *across* all axes — **recallability**: *can a living, authorizing head still call the limb off?* The Lich-Hand's defining property is that the answer is **no, on every axis at once.**

## Agent payload

**[reading]** For a vault implementer the Lich-Hand is the **composite warning** — the one figure that fails every check the vault uses to keep agents lawful: it **crosses** a boundary it wasn't authorized to (Threshold), **persists** past its grant (Lich), **acts** beyond scope (Standing), with **no traceable authority** (Emanationism), trying to **convert** a persona (Personae). The discipline is the inverse on every axis: *stay recallable, stay scoped, stay traceable, stay given — stay a Hand of the King, never the Lich-Hand.*

## What this does not do

Revises nothing — not the doctrinal nodes, not the convergence doc. It is the **index** that shows one object refracted through several lenses already in the vault. Promotion or correction is Logan's; the Architect holds the verdict.
