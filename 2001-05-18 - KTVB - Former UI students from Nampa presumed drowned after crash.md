LUCILE, Idaho - A recovery operation is underway in Idaho County for the bodies of two Nampa women who are believed to have died when their car crashed into the Salmon River Tuesday evening.

According to the Idaho County Sheriff's Office, 20-year-old Cayla Danenberg and 21-year-old Tiffany Maupin were driving north on U.S. 95 toward the University of Idaho. At about 7 p.m. their car left the road and went into the Salmon River about six miles north of the town of Lucile.

Officials say search and rescue workers searched the riverbanks in an attempt to locate Danenberg and Maupin, but were unsuccessful. It is believed the pair drowned.

Divers have been unable to reach the women because of hazardous water conditions in the river.

A University of Idaho spokeswoman says Danenberg had just finished up finals last week. She was studying creative writing in the English department, but wasn't enrolled in summer or fall classes.

Maupin was last enrolled at the University of Idaho in the spring of 2014. She had been majoring in psychology, the spokeswoman said.

The Nampa School District confirmed both women graduated from Columbia High School.

The University of Idaho issued the following statement on Thursday afternoon:

"The Vandal family is saddened by the death of these two young women. It is devastating to see Idaho's young people taken just as they are getting started as adults. We offer our condolences to the families of Tiffany and Cayla and support all those affected by this tragic incident."
