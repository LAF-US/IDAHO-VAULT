Fire activity continued to build across the United States as firefighters worked to contain dozens of large blazes and respond to new starts reported over the past day.

As of Aug. 9, 2026, 183 new fires were reported nationwide since yesterday, including eight new large fires. Firefighters were working to contain 94 large fires across the country.

A total of 30,419 personnel were assigned to incidents nationwide, including 645 crews, 1,870 engines and 226 helicopters. In addition, 27 complex incident management teams and one national incident management organization were committed.

With extreme fire behavior reported across multiple geographic areas and fires reaching hundreds of thousands of acres in size, fire officials emphasized the importance of quickly stopping new ignitions before they grow. The vast majority of wildfires are contained within 24 hours of their discovery during the initial attack stage, meaning they do not become large enough to appear on the incident management summary report.

Officials said strategically prepositioning firefighters and firefighting resources for initial attack — especially in areas where predictive services have identified the potential for large fire growth — remains a critical consideration during the busiest part of the fire year, while also balancing the heavy demand for resources on large fires.
