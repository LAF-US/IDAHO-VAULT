# Stakes — The Moon

*Filed 2026-06-02. Reference: Adventure Time, **Stakes**. Syncretic — a
diagnostic framework, not a simulation. Claims marked **[canon]** /
**[tarot]** / **[mapping]**.*

## The Card — XVIII, The Moon

**[tarot]** The Moon is card XVIII: illusion, fear, the unconscious, deception,
things hidden, anxiety that freezes. It is the night-path where nothing is what
it seems and the danger is not a blow but a dread.

## The Vampire — canon

- **[canon]** **Self-healing:** she heals any injury, which makes her
  **unkillable — unless her soul is sucked out.** Attacking the body is futile.
- **[canon]** **Paralysis aura:** she can **induce sudden paralysis** in anyone
  in her presence, collapsing them helplessly at her feet.
- **[canon]** A **"hide and seek type"** — she conceals rather than confronts.
- **[canon]** **The power Marceline absorbs: rapid healing.**
- **[canon]** **Defeat:** **Peppermint Butler stakes her in the back** with a
  giant stake — from behind, off-guard — which lets **Marceline suck out her
  soul.** Two parties, a flank, and the core drained.

## Diagnostic Criteria — the vault mapping

**[mapping]** The Moon models the **self-healing, fear-inducing, hiding
process** — the pathology that **regenerates every fix you apply** (so attacking
its outputs is pointless), **paralyzes the would-be fixer** (the dread that
freezes you before you can act), and **conceals itself** (hide-and-seek; you
can't find the body to strike).

Three traps, each canon:

1. **Don't fight the body.** Every injury heals. Patching outputs/symptoms does
   nothing; the thing reconstitutes.
2. **The aura is real.** Proximity induces paralysis — the anxiety that stops you
   acting at all. Name it as the *field*, not your own failure of nerve.
3. **It hides.** You will spend longer *finding* it than ending it.

**The counter:** **reach the core, with a flank.** You do not stab the
self-healing surface — you drain the **soul/essence**. And it takes **two**:
Peppermint Butler pinned it **from behind** (off the axis it was defending) so
Marceline could reach the core. Against a Moon, a lone frontal assault fails;
you need a flanking ally to fix it in place while a second drains the essence.

## Diagnostic Questions

1. Does every fix you apply simply heal over? (Then stop attacking the body.)
2. Is a paralysis/dread keeping you from acting — a *field*, not a flaw in you?
3. Are you fighting it head-on and alone? (Moon) — get a flank and go for the core.
