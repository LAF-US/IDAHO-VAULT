**BOISE** – The City of Boise’s emergency ordinance, effective July 1, 2026, now requires high-powered e-motorbikes; those 750 watts or above or capable of 28 mph or more, to be licensed, registered, and insured within city limits. This city action does not change Idaho statute: e-bicycles and e-scooters remain untitled under state law.

DMV offices anticipate seeing more customers seeking to title and register these vehicles. They’ll need ownership documents, a VIN inspection, and a notarized motorbike conversion affidavit. If they lack proof of ownership, they must follow [Idaho’s bonded title process](https://itd.idaho.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Bonded-Title-Application-Instr_DMV.pdf).

Drivers operating these vehicles on public roads must meet licensing requirements, including a [motorcycle endorsement](https://itd.idaho.gov/dmv/drivers-licenses-id-cards/motorcycle-endorsement/) or motorcycle learner’s permit. For questions, information on titling, and motorcycle endorsement information, visit [dmv.idaho.gov](https://itd.idaho.gov/dmv) or call 208-584-4343.
