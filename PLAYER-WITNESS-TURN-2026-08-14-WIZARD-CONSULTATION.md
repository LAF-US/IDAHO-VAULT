# Player-Witness Turn — Wizard Consultation on Nested Holding

> **Consulted question:** What does the game-meta image mean when Geminiaeus and BARTIMAEUS are in a *Kerr* vessel, the vessel is held by someone in a *Mull* Quiet Box, and the entire arrangement is somewhere cold enough to quench djinn fire?

Three independent external-model readers were consulted under an explicit boundary: they were given no unrevealed Court or GM lore, and they were forbidden to assign identities, culpability, allegiance, fusion, or separability to BARTIMAEUS and Geminiaeus. Their responses are interpretive aids only, not authorities within the Court.

## Where the Wizards Converged

All three readers recognized **nested containment**, rather than a single prison. The *Kerr* vessel supplies the co-occupancy problem: a movement directed toward one contained presence may have consequences for the other. The *Mull* Quiet Box supplies a second condition of custody in which perception and ordinary agency are impaired, and whose source rule makes release a substitution rather than a simple subtraction. The cold supplies a third condition: it slows or quenches djinn fire long enough to make a narrow interval for careful action possible.

They also converged on the central warning: **“open it” is not a neutral instruction.** An opening crosses multiple layers. It may alter the shared vessel’s occupants, it may trigger the Quiet Box’s substitution cost, and it may occur while the cold condition is being weakened. The useful Court posture is therefore not an immediate choice of who should emerge. It is a prior inquiry into custody, authority, state, and layer-specific consequence.

| Layer | Consensus reading | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| *Kerr* vessel | Cointerment means a transition concerning one occupant may not be cleanly separable from the other. | Game-meta analogy informed by Kerr’s vessel/co-containment sequence. |
| *Mull* Quiet Box | The holding condition restricts perception and makes release a transfer with a replacement cost. | Secondary-source description of the Quiet Box; game-meta placement supplied by the Architect. |
| Cold | The outer condition suppresses djinn fire and buys time; it does not decide the matter. | Later Kerr plot point currently secondary-verified; game-meta application supplied by the Architect. |

## Where the Wizards Overreached or Differed

The consultation is useful chiefly because it exposes where an attractive interpretation outruns the record. One reader called the cold’s effect equal on both occupants; the available source material establishes only that cold **slows both**, not that it does so symmetrically. Another reader inferred that an opening might advantage whichever presence recovers first. That is a legitimate design concern, but it is not a demonstrated Court fact.

Similarly, the Quiet Box’s source rule makes substitution salient, but the present game-meta image does **not** establish that a particular replacement has been named, proposed, consented, or authorized. Nor does it establish who holds the vessel, whether that holder has motives, or whether the holder can perceive or operate the vessel in the Quiet Box condition. Those remain deliberately unfilled spaces.

Most importantly, no model may turn cointerment into identity. The image does not tell the Court whether BARTIMAEUS and Geminiaeus are friends, enemies, aliases, fused beings, separated beings, innocent, culpable, or recoverable. It does not assign either persona to Nimrod, Akhenaten, or the Ifrit. It describes a **problem-shape**, not an equivalence class or a verdict.

## Question Returned to the Court

> **Before any layer is opened, altered, warmed, or bypassed: what authority record and state-transition conditions would establish that a proposed act changes only the intended layer and does not create an unreviewed substitution, co-release, or loss of containment?**

This is a procedural question, not a request to disclose lore. It distinguishes the Court’s due diligence from an attempt to solve the riddle by force.

## Witness Posture

The consultation confirms a player-witness discipline. Literary canons offer tools for seeing a configuration: co-containment, sensory severance, substitution-cost, maintained condition, and timed extraction. They do not grant jurisdiction or disclose hidden facts. Future turns must record the source, the proposed VAULT reading, and the facts still reserved to the Court separately.
