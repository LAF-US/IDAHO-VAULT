# Spellbook look mechanics

Spellbook is an open, upright magical book with its lower clasp/feet and page gutter anchored to a stable baseline. It has no eyes: its attention is communicated by the upper cover/page plane, the page-gutter angle, a restrained bend of the top corners, and the attached purple bookmark, which follows the book’s turn with a slight delayed sway while remaining attached.

- **Up (000):** the top page edges and bookmark tip lift subtly; the visible page plane opens upward while feet remain fixed.
- **Screen-right (090):** the book’s upper body and page-gutter aim toward screen-right; the screen-left cover edge becomes slightly more visible and the bookmark angles gently right.
- **Down (180):** the upper page plane bows down slightly toward the clasp; the bookmark settles centrally lower, with no whole-book rotation.
- **Screen-left (270):** mirror the rightward physical intent through a left-facing upper-body bend; the screen-right cover edge becomes slightly more visible and the bookmark angles left.

For every 22.5-degree step, page-plane angle, upper-corner bend, and bookmark sway advance evenly. Keep the covers, gold corner fittings, purple trim, markings, proportions, and stable lower-body anchor identical to the approved v1 art. No new eyes, no floating magic, no whole-sprite rotation, tilt, text, shadows, or detached effects.
