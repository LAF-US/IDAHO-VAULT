And if you think it’s just Idaho, you’re not paying attention because this is playing out across the country.

Caldwell just rubber-stamped nearly 1,000 new homes on 280 acres near Lake Lowell. Three hundred apartments with retail mixed in.

<video src="blob:https://brianlenney.substack.com/309334ee-b199-46e2-a431-5f7e81ffbf88" controls=""></video>

In Boise they’re putting up a massive high-rise affordable housing facility so refugees and California’s homeless population get a walkable neighborhood while the people who’ve been here for decades get to watch their property values and their community change overnight.

Or, what about this massive project south of Boise?

<video src="blob:https://brianlenney.substack.com/313bcaa8-07a6-4182-b502-02c6539139a9" controls=""></video>

***They needed cheap labor so they imported a new population. That’s the whole story.***

But here’s where it gets really ugly. Your tax dollars are funding relocation through NGOs that nobody audits. Just a few months ago I pushed a bill to force transparency on where that money goes and audit the orgs who do this. And Idaho legislative leadership (R) killed it. They looked at a bill that just said “show us the receipts” and buried it.

**Ask yourself why a Republican leadership team would fight to keep NGO funding in the dark. Who benefits from that?**

You know what happened to Virginia?

It was solid red for years before the developments went in, the demographics shifted, and fifteen years later it’s deep blue. Conservatives there still walk around confused about it (like it’s some mystery nobody could’ve predicted). Every person with a calculator saw it coming and nobody in leadership cared because the construction money was too good.

Many Idaho Republicans are running the same play right now by partnering with Democrats on “affordable housing bills” that let developers flood the state (while Idaho families who can’t get into a home are left holding the bag).

And the people moving in?

Well... they hate you.

***They’re never pulling the lever for your values.***

***They don’t even like you.***

That’s just the math of importing an electorate that has zero connection to what Idaho was built on. Assimilation isn’t what happens. What happens is the voter rolls change and suddenly your state doesn’t belong to you anymore.

Calvin Coolidge [saw all of this](https://reader.library.cornell.edu/docviewer/digital?id=hearth6417403_1366_002#page/15/mode/1up) a hundred years ago:

> **“We want no such additions to our population as those who prey upon our institutions or our property.”**

Prey upon. He called it what it was.

And then he went further:

> **“The retroactive immigrant is a danger in our midst. His discontent gives him no time to seize a healthy opportunity to improve himself. His purpose is to tear down. There is no room for him here.”**

A sitting Vice President published that in a national magazine and nobody flinched because everyone understood it was just the truth. Now you say that in Boise and your own Republican leadership acts like you’re the problem.

Coolidge’s solution?

> **“He needs to be deported, not as a substitute for, but as a part of his punishment.”**

Meanwhile Idaho Republicans can’t even pass a simple audit bill for the NGOs relocating these people into your neighborhoods.

This is happening all over the country.

Virginia already fell. So did Colorado. Conservative voters had zero clue what they were signing off on. Your own guys sold you out and called it “economic development.” By the time the voter rolls shift in Idaho (and they will), the Republicans who made it happen will be retired somewhere cashing consulting checks from the companies that paid for all of it.

***Your own party needs to stop telling you that demographic replacement is economic growth (because you know it isn’t and so do they).***

Wake up, Idaho.

And then do something about it.
