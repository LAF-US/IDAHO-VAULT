**Kate Harrington** (December 8, 1902 – November 23, 1978) was an American television and movie actress.

Born and raised in [Boise, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise,_Idaho "Boise, Idaho"), Harrington studied dramatics at the Bush Conservatory in Chicago.[^1] Three years later she was given her first professional engagement; the lead in a J.C. Nugent Company production of *Kempy*.[^2]

Harrington spent twelve years in Hollywood, during which time she made numerous movies including *Rhapsody in Blue*, the *Scattergood Baines* comedy series starring Guy Kibbee, as well as a number of Tim Holt westerns.[^3]

Harrington made her Broadway debut in 1943's *Slightly Married*, in which she co-starred Leon Ames.[^4] Later Broadway appearances included *Buy Me Blue Ribbons* in 1951, *The Happiest Millionaire* from 1956 to 1957, playing opposite [Walter Pidgeon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Pidgeon "Walter Pidgeon") in the role of Emma, which she originated (Harrington was also in the cast of the show's national tour) and *Minor Miracle* in 1965.[^5] Other off-Broadway roles included *Buy Me Blue Ribbons*, *Morning's at Seven*, *The Plough and the Stars*, *The Days and Nights of Beebee Fenstermaker*, *Not a Way of Life* and *Stephen D,* a play adapted from [James Joyce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Joyce "James Joyce") 's novel *[A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Portrait_of_the_Artist_as_a_Young_Man "A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man")*.[^6]

Occasional film roles included those in *[Madigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madigan "Madigan")* and *[Rachel, Rachel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel,_Rachel "Rachel, Rachel")* in 1968, *[Love Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_Story_\(1970_film\) "Love Story (1970 film)")* in 1970, *[The Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hospital "The Hospital")* in 1971, *[Child's Play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child's_Play_\(1972_film\) "Child's Play (1972 film)")* in 1972 and *[The Sentinel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sentinel_\(1977_film\) "The Sentinel (1977 film)")* in 1977.

Beginning in 1956, Harrington made sporadic television appearances, including *[NBC Experiment in Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC_Experiment_in_Television "NBC Experiment in Television")* and *[Great Performances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Performances "Great Performances")*.[^7] On daytime television, Harrington was the second actress to play [Katherine 'Kate' Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Martin_\(All_My_Children\) "Kate Martin (All My Children)") on *[All My Children](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_My_Children "All My Children")*, a role she played from early February until August 1970. Then Harrington moved on to the role of Marion Conway (#2) on *[Guiding Light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guiding_Light "Guiding Light")* from 1971 to 1972.[^8]

Harrington died in New York City from complications of a [stroke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stroke "Stroke") suffered the previous year.[^9]
