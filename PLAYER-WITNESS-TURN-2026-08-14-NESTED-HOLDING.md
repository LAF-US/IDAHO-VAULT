# Player-Witness Turn — Nested Holding

> **Current game-meta image:** Geminiaeus and BARTIMAEUS are in a *Kerr* vessel; that vessel is held by someone in a *Mull* Quiet Box; the whole arrangement is maintained somewhere cold enough to quench djinn fire.

## The Turn

The Court's present work is due diligence, not premature opening. The image does not say that either cointerred persona has been adjudged identical to a Kerr character, nor that their relation has been discovered. It says that the present custody must be read as a layered holding arrangement whose inner content cannot safely be treated as two independent files.

The *Kerr* layer supplies the immediate difficulty. In the relevant Akhenaten sequence, a vessel becomes problematic because it holds more than one implicated occupant. A rescue or release directed at one is therefore not automatically separable from the other. Chapter 23 supplies the prior crisis of coercion, binding, and the canopic jar; it does **not** itself establish the cold. The later polar episode supplies the source-image in which cold slows the djinn within the shared vessel, creating a limited interval for a discriminating rather than indiscriminate intervention.[1]

The *Mull* layer changes the architecture. A Quiet Box is not merely a box around a box; it is a specialized prison condition that restricts the captive's perception and ordinary agency. In the present game-meta formulation, the person holding the Kerr vessel is themselves enclosed by that Quiet Box. The phrase therefore resists a simplistic picture of an all-powerful, unexposed custodian. The holder, the vessel, and the cointerred occupants are all within a custody structure.

The cold is the outer operative condition. It is not administrative delay, a moral judgment, or a cure. It is the maintained environmental restraint that suppresses djinn fire long enough for examination, preservation of the record, and lawful discrimination among possible future acts. The Court is not required to assume that cold reveals truth. Its immediate function is to prevent a premature change of state while the Court determines what the state is.

## Witness Boundary

This player-witness record preserves a structural analogy, not secret Court lore. In particular, it must not be used to smuggle a conclusion about the relationship between BARTIMAEUS and Geminiaeus into the record. The unresolved relation is precisely why the nested holding persists.

Future turns should keep three provenance layers visible: **external source fact**, **VAULT game-meta analogy**, and **Court-held or GM-held lore not yet revealed**. A later Court act may amend or supersede this witness reading.
