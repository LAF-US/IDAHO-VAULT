## Want to weigh in on Prop 1, the Idaho abortion ballot initiative? Here’s how.

![Volunteers pass boxes of signatures supporting a ballot initiative to end Idaho's ban on abortion outside the state capitol.](https://idahocapitalsun.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/IMG_4021.png)

Volunteers pass boxes of signatures supporting a ballot initiative to end [[Idaho]]'s ban on abortion outside the state capitol.

Volunteers pass boxes of signatures supporting a ballot initiative to end Idaho’s strict ban on abortion on July 2, 2026, outside the state Capitol in Boise. (Photo by Abigail Gerstein/Idaho Capital Sun)

Idaho state election officials are asking the public to weigh in on a proposed ballot initiative to [end the state’s strict abortion ban](https://idahocapitalsun.com/2026/07/13/initiative-to-end-idahos-strict-abortion-ban-qualifies-for-novembers-general-election-ballot/).

The Idaho Secretary of State’s Office will feature some arguments in favor and against the ballot initiative on a voter pamphlet that it mails to all Idaho households ahead of the Nov. 3 general election.

Arguments related to the Reproductive Freedom and Privacy Act are due by 11:59 p.m. Mountain time on Friday, July 31, the Secretary of State’s Office announced in a news release. Submit arguments [online](https://forms.sos.idaho.gov/pro-con/) at [https://forms.sos.idaho.gov/pro-con/](https://forms.sos.idaho.gov/pro-con/). Learn more about Idaho’s ballot initiative process [online](https://voteidaho.gov/initiatives-amendments/) at [voteidaho.gov/initiatives-amendments/](https://voteidaho.gov/initiatives-amendments/).

This year’s voter pamphlet will include information on two constitutional amendments, proposed by the Legislature. Here’s a primer on those:

- One proposed amendment, through [House Joint Resolution 4](https://legislature.idaho.gov/sessioninfo/2025/legislation/HJR004/), would allow only the Idaho Legislature – and not Idaho voters – to have the authority to legalize medical cannabis, recreational marijuana or any other drug. A proposed ballot initiative to legalize medical marijuana [failed to qualify for the ballot](https://idahocapitalsun.com/2026/07/14/medical-cannabis-initiative-fails-to-qualify-for-idahos-general-election-ballot/) this year, election officials announced last week.
- Another proposed amendment, through [House Joint Resolution 6](https://legislature.idaho.gov/sessioninfo/2025/legislation/HJR006/), would [declare English as the state’s official language](https://idahocapitalsun.com/2025/03/25/english-could-become-idahos-official-language-under-constitutional-amendment/).

A committee of legislative leaders last month [approved language to describe](https://idahocapitalsun.com/2026/06/04/idaho-legislators-approve-language-for-ballot-measures-on-marijuana-english-as-official-language/) those two proposed constitutional amendments on ballots.

The voter pamphlet will also include information on an advisory question for voters to designate a state gun for Idaho. Lawmakers approved the advisory question this year through [House Bill 932](https://legislature.idaho.gov/sessioninfo/2026/legislation/H0932/).
