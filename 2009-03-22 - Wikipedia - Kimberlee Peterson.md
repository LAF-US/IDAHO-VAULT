**Kimberlee Michelle Peterson** (born May 8, 1980) is an American television and film [actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actress "Actress").

## Background

Peterson was born in [Boise, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise,_Idaho "Boise, Idaho"), but raised in [Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado "Colorado"). At the age of four, Peterson began her career as a child actress in episodes of *[Perry Mason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perry_Mason_\(TV_film_series\) "Perry Mason (TV film series)")*. At the age of 15, she co-starred in *[Homecoming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homecoming_\(1996_film\) "Homecoming (1996 film)")* opposite [Anne Bancroft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne_Bancroft "Anne Bancroft").[^1]

Throughout the 1990s and 2000s, Peterson appeared in numerous television episodes. Peterson is the owner of Lotta Photography, a headshot photography business based in the [San Fernando Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Fernando_Valley "San Fernando Valley").[^2] [^3]
