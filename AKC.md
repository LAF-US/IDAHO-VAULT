# AKC - American Kennel Club

**AKC** = American Kennel Club

The American Kennel Club (AKC) is the primary purebred dog registry in the United States. Founded in 1884, it maintains breed standards, sanctions dog shows, and provides pedigree documentation for purebred dogs.

## Key Functions

- **Breed Recognition:** AKC recognizes and maintains standards for 200+ purebred dog breeds
- **Pedigree Registry:** Official family trees for purebred dogs
- **Dog Shows:** Sanctions conformation, agility, obedience, and other competitive events
- **Canine Health:** Promotes health testing and responsible breeding practices
