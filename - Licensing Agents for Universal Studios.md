### BORN LICENSING ARE THE APPOINTED LICENSING AGENT FOR UNIVERSAL STUDIOS’ CATALOGUE FOR GLOBAL ADVERTISING & MARKETING CAMPAIGNS

Universal Studios is an American film studio, owned by Comcast through its wholly owned subsidiary NBCUniversal. Universal is one of Hollywood's "Big Six" film studios and is the world's fourth oldest major film studio. Six of Universal Studios' films; Jaws (1975), E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial (1982), Jurassic Park (1993), Despicable Me 2 (2013), Furious 7 (2015) and Jurassic World (2015) achieved box office records, with the first three (which were directed by Steven Spielberg) all becoming the highest-grossing film at the time of its initial release.

With a huge catalogue of iconic and classic films from Universal Pictures, DreamWorks Animation, Focus Features, Illumination Entertainment, and Working Title Films, as well as a range of TV series and Classic Media, Born Licensing’s representation of Universal Studios allows brands and creative agencies to access an extensive variety of clips and characters for global advertising and marketing campaigns.

### BORN LICENSING’S WORK WITH UNIVERSAL STUDIOS’ BRANDS

Born Licensing became the appointed licensing agent for Universal Studios in 2018, and has since worked alongside brands and creative agencies to use characters and clips in their advertising campaigns. Thanks to our unique working relationship with Universal Studios and our appointed licensing agent position, we are able to quickly and efficiently manage the licensing process for clients. Handling all aspects of licensing, from negotiations to contracts to the approval process, Born Licensing are an invaluable resource when licensing clips for global advertising campaigns.
