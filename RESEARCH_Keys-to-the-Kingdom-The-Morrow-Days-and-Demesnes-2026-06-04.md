# The House — The Morrow Days and Their Demesnes

> A standalone reference on **Garth Nix's *Keys to the Kingdom*** — the **House**, its seven **Trustees** (the *Morrow Days*), and the seven **demesnes** they rule. Companion to the ASOIAF `[[RESEARCH_A-Song-of-Ice-and-Fire-The-Small-Council-2026-06-03]]`; the **syncretic crosswalk** between the two systems lives **only** in `[[THE-SEVENFOLD-BODY-SEATS-TRUSTEES-GEMS-2026-06-03]]`. ## The House — the cosmology

- **[canon]** The **House** is the **centre of the Universe** — a vast, hierarchical, bureaucratic cosmos from which the mortal worlds (the **Secondary Realms**) radiate. It was raised by the **Architect** out of **Nothing**.
- **[canon]** The **Architect** (the maker) **vanished**, leaving behind a **Will** (her testament) and **Seven Keys**, and dividing the House among **seven Trustees** — one per day of the week, each entrusted with one Key, one **demesne**, and a fragment of the Will they were charged to **execute**.
- **[canon]** Instead, each Trustee **broke the trust** — hoarding its Will-fragment to keep its power, each corrupted by **one of the seven deadly sins.** The imprisoned Will, once reassembled, names a **Rightful Heir** — the mortal boy **Arthur Penhaligon** — to reclaim the Keys and restore the House.
- **[canon]** **Nothing** is the void the House is made *from* and floats *in* — origin and threat at once; it can be **shaped** into matter (the Far Reaches) but also **erodes and reclaims** the House where it breaches. The **Old One** — a chained, incarcerated divine being, in fact **a part of the Architect herself** — is bound to the House and freed only when the Will rejoins; the Architect "was bored of living forever but could not die without destroying all creation."

## The Morrow Days — the seven Trustees

**[canon]** *"All of the Trustees are afflicted with one of the Seven deadly sins: Monday with sloth, Tuesday with greed, Wednesday with gluttony, Thursday with wrath, Friday with lust, Saturday with envy and Sunday with pride."*

| Trustee | Key | Demesne | Sin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Mister Monday** | First | the **Lower House** | Sloth |
| **Grim Tuesday** | Second | the **Far Reaches** | Greed |
| **Drowned Wednesday** | Third | the **Border Sea** | Gluttony |
| **Sir Thursday** | Fourth | the **Great Maze** | Wrath |
| **Lady Friday** | Fifth | the **Middle House** | Lust |
| **Superior Saturday** | Sixth | the **Upper House** | Envy |
| **Lord Sunday** | Seventh | the **Incomparable Gardens** | Pride |

## The seven demesnes — in detail

**The Lower House — Monday · Sloth.** The House's administrative ground floor and point of entry — the bureaucratic heart. The **Atrium**, Monday's Antechamber and **Dayroom**, the **Coal Cellars**, and tens of thousands of floors of offices and residences. Runs on **records-administration**; Monday's **Sloth** made it "impossibly inefficient." Its Coal Cellar **borders the Void**. *(Holds Part One of the Will.)*

**The Far Reaches — Tuesday · Greed.** Originally a **Grand Cavern** with a spring of **Nothing**, which Tuesday shaped (via the Second Key) into raw materials *and* finished goods — teapots, nibs, cabling, statues, automatons (Sentinels, Commissionaires, Not-Horses) — **sold to the other demesnes.** The House's **manufactory.** His **Greed** drove him to deepen the spring into the vast **Pit** that swallowed most of the demesne (plus the **Treasure Tower** in a Glass Pyramid and the workshops); **indentured miners** dig Nothing on a four-month descent. The Pit cuts so deep it nearly **breaches the Void**.

**The Border Sea — Wednesday · Gluttony.** An ocean **connected to every sea, river, and navigable water across the millions of Secondary Realms**, walled off by a **line of storms** — the House's **gateway to the mortal worlds.** Wednesday's **Gluttony** cost her shape-control: she became a **126-mile Leviathan** (a white whale), flooded nine-tenths of the demesne, and **ate her own Noon and Dusk.** Submerged buildings became **ships**; her fleet numbered **49**; the **Raised Rats** (mortal rats brought by the Piper) pilot steamboats as trackers. *(Part Three of the Will lay in a worldlet inside her stomach.)*

**The Great Maze — Thursday · Wrath.** A maze of **1,000,000 one-mile tiles**, each its own habitat, that **teleport/reshuffle every sundown** — near-impossible to navigate. Home of the **Glorious Army of the Architect**, the House's main military power; **every House inhabitant must serve 100 years** in it. Fixed strongpoints: Fort Transformation, the Cannon Arsenal, the Citadel. Thursday's **Wrath** commands it.

**The Middle House — Friday · Lust.** A **thrice-terraced mountain** (Top Shelf, Middle of Middle, the Flat) under simulated skies. Its function is **records *restoration*** — the **Guild of Binding and Restoration**, and **Gilding** — the conservation tier. Friday's **Scriptorium** crowns the pinnacle *(Part Five of the Will, chained in the lair of the Winged Servants).* Her **Lust** is to "**experience**" mortals — draining their beings, emotions, and memories into the Key; she **abdicated** her rule as a trap.

**The Upper House — Saturday · Envy.** A single **tower of ~2,000 floors** built of rearranging "**Office Cubes**" — offices that physically **move up or down as workers are promoted or demoted.** Perpetually raining *(the rain is the Sixth Part of the Will).* Where the House's **Sorcerers are trained**; Saturday, "**Superior Sorcerer of the House**," the **oldest Denizen and most powerful sorcerer**, has spent **~10,000 years building her tower ever-higher** to reach and usurp the Gardens. Her **Envy** points straight up.

**The Incomparable Gardens — Sunday · Pride.** Canon twist: **not merely another ordinary demesne** — the Gardens are the **true epicentre** of the House/universe and the **very first thing the Architect created from Nothing** — before the Secondary Realms, before the other demesnes. The highest, oldest, **best domain**, given to **Sunday** (the Architect's eldest son). His **Pride**; the **source of Saturday's envy.**

## The sub-structure — Dawn, Noon, Dusk

**[canon]** Beneath each Trustee stand three chief officers — a **Dawn, a Noon, and a Dusk** — its triadic lieutenants (a 7×3 sub-hierarchy). The verified exemplar is **Monday's**: his **Dawn, Noon, and Dusk** command, respectively, the **Corps of Inspectors**, the **Metal Commissionaires** and **Denizen Commissionaire Sergeants**, and the **Midnight Visitors**. Each demesne also fields its own force — Thursday's **Glorious Army**, Wednesday's **fleet of 49** (and the Raised Rats), Tuesday's **indentured miners**, Saturday's **Office-Cube sorcerers**.

## The vertical order — and an honest ambiguity

**[canon, ⚠️ contested]** The House is stratified, but its exact bottom-to-top order is **not consistent across sources.** **House Precedence** lists a floor sequence; the **Far Reaches** wiki calls *it* "physically the lowest" (its Pit descends deepest toward the Void); yet the **Lower House**'s Coal Cellar *also* borders Nothing. Treat "the lowest demesne" as **genuinely unsettled** in canon — and note that **precedence (rank)** and **physical height** are two different orderings. *(An earlier vault draft over-asserted a single answer; corrected here.)*

## The drama — the betrayed Will

**[reading]** The engine is always the same: an **absent maker**, a **testament** (the Will) that delegated, **scoped, recallable** authority to seven holders — and seven holders who **clung** to their fragments rather than execute the trust. The redemptive pole is the **Rightful Heir** who reclaims the Keys and reunites the Will. *(How this rhymes with the vault's own doctrine and with the ASOIAF Small Council is treated only in the crosswalk node, not here.)*
