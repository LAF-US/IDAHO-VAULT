Featuring works by Marina Zavalova, Ovanes Berberian, and Sergei Bongart, this exhibit explores the Russian Impressionism movement in Idaho. Defined by bold impasto brushwork and vibrant color harmonies, these artists prioritize mood, light, and the raw spirit of the West over meticulous detail, capturing the essence of local landscapes and the natural world through energetic, spontaneous application.
