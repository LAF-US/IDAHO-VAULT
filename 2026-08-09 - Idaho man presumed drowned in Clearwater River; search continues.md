Search and recovery crews continued working Saturday for a man who went missing in the Clearwater River near Lenore and is presumed drowned.

The Nez Perce County Sheriff’s Office said Joseph Wren of Peck, Idaho, was reported missing after entering the water and failing to resurface. Dispatch received the report at about 2:51 p.m. Aug. 8, 2026.

Nez Perce County deputies, Nez Perce Tribal Police and Clearwater County deputies, along with additional search-and-rescue resources, responded to the area and began searching immediately, the sheriff’s office said.

Since the initial report, responders have used shoreline searches, aerial resources and other methods. The Nez Perce County Dive Team has also been deployed as part of the operation. The sheriff’s office said sonar equipment and other specialized recovery tools will be used as conditions allow and as resources become available.

Wren had not been located as of the latest update. Based on witness statements and the circumstances surrounding the incident, authorities said they believe he may have drowned, though he remains missing and search and recovery operations are ongoing.

Search operations are being evaluated daily based on environmental conditions, available resources and information developed during the investigation.

Anyone who witnessed the incident or has information is asked to contact the Nez Perce County Sheriff’s Office Dispatch Center at 208-799-3131. Additional updates are expected as more information becomes available.
