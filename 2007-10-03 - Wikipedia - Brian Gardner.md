**Brian Knapp Gardner**, also known as **Brian** " **Big Bass** " **Gardner**,[^1] is an American [mastering engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mastering_engineer "Mastering engineer"). He has worked on a number of recordings since the mid-1960s, including [classic rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classic_rock "Classic rock"), [funk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funk "Funk"), [disco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disco "Disco"), [alternative rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_rock "Alternative rock"), [R&B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R&B "R&B"), [hip hop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hip_hop "Hip hop"), [pop punk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_punk "Pop punk") and [dance-pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dance-pop "Dance-pop").[^2] He is known for his work on [hip hop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hip_hop "Hip hop") albums, including collaborations with [Dr. Dre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._Dre "Dr. Dre"), who gave him the nickname "Big Bass".[^3]

He was last employed at Bernie Grundman Mastering, a mastering house founded and run by [Bernie Grundman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernie_Grundman "Bernie Grundman"), and is now independent.

## Approach

Gardner's style is to [compress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamic_range_compression "Dynamic range compression") music to the point of [distortion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clipping_\(audio\) "Clipping (audio)"), in order to make it sound [louder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loudness_wars "Loudness wars").[^4]

He has been nominated eight times for the [Grammy Award for Album of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Album_of_the_Year "Grammy Award for Album of the Year") award, winning it once for the [Outkast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outkast "Outkast") album *[Speakerboxxx/The Love Below](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speakerboxxx/The_Love_Below "Speakerboxxx/The Love Below")*.[^5] [^6]
