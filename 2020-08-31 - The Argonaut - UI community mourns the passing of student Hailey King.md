In an email to students, faculty and staff Aug. 31, University of Idaho Dean of Students Blaine Eckles shared that Hailey King, a senior majoring in animal and veterinary science, passed away on Friday after sustaining injuries in a car accident Aug. 22.

King was driving north on U.S. Highway 95 when she drifted off the right side of the road, over-corrected twice and rolled into the northbound lane, according to [reporting from KTVB](https://www.ktvb.com/article/news/local/single-vehicle-crash-near-midvale-and-cambridge-leaves-one-dead/277-5baa6119-f3be-4075-a4c9-0bf1e5395821). Idaho State Police is still investigating the crash.

King, a Kuna native, led both in the classroom and in her sorority, Delta Gamma.
