Snake River up from Taylors Bridge. Idaho. 1871.


Descriptive Catalog of the Photographs of the United States Geological Survey of the Territories, W. H. Jackson, Photographer, Second Edition, Illustrated, 1871 Series, page 23, No. 178:  Snake River. Looking up from Taylor's Bridge, 26 miles west from Fort Hall. The river here rushes through a narrow channel of dark, nearly black basalt. Our view was taken in the spring, when the channel was full. In the fall, when the river is low, it reveals deep canyon-like walls about 100 feet below the surface.
