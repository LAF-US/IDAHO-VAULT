On Wednesday, two Idaho Republican lawmakers issued a letter demanding the Idaho Department of Health and Welfare stopped childcare welfare payments. The duo, Rep. Josh Tanner and Sen. Brian Lenney, wants to make sure the payments are legitimate and service providers are delivering service.

The letter comes after a social media journalist exposed tens of millions in taxpayer funds to Minnesota Somali childcare providers that don’t appear to provide childcare.

The strongly-worded letter is an interesting response to the Minnesota scandal, and it’s sure to generate a ton of X impressions.

Cool.

Yet, I’m left with a different response and question about this whole episode.

Why in the world are our Republican lawmakers voting to fund a childcare welfare handout like this?

Some Idaho Republicans likely find these dollars less than objectionable because they are funded by the federal government. In short, they don’t affect Idaho’s state budget all that much.

Yet, these payments are little more than the redistribution of wealth likely dressed up as an aide to working parents. It really is that simple.

So why then did the co-chair of the Idaho Freedom Caucus, Sen. Tammy Nichols, vote for this welfare handout? Why did Freedom Caucus member Rob Beiswenger vote for it?
