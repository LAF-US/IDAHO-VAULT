Final Tribute Paid To O. E. Kirkpatrick SALMON CITY (AP) - Orion E. Kirkpatrick, 76, author of "Leesburg Pioneers," and considered one of the few authorities on the famous Idaho mining camp of Leesburg, buried Thursday. 
He was recently honored by the State Historical society as the Lemhi county historian.
Kirkpatrick served one term in the Idaho legislature and was an active Mason.
He was buried under a stall shaft which he erected several months ago in the Masonic cemetery.
