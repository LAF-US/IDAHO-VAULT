To get the RSS feed of any substack newsletter, you can use the following URL:

[https://{newsletter-name}.substack.com/feed](https://{newsletter-name}.substack.com/feed).

**For example:**

1\. If we want to get the RSS feed for this newsletter, we will be replacing the link from [https://roiss.substack.com/?utm\_source=discover\_search](https://roiss.substack.com/?utm_source=discover_search) to [https://roiss.substack.com/feed](https://roiss.substack.com/feed).

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5abc898d2c7d3a0e9366d6c3/images/6243dae9ab585b230a8a6585/file-ycAchZ9uW1.png) 2. Once we have the RSS link we can search for it in the “Discover websites” search bar and follow it.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5abc898d2c7d3a0e9366d6c3/images/6243db9b2ce7ed0fb091b266/file-W5EPn2LgGu.png) ![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5abc898d2c7d3a0e9366d6c3/images/6243db7d42ba434a7afe1260/file-upGVRBMGc3.png)

3\. Finally, add it to any of your feeds, and from now on, it will update with all new entries.

If for some reason the RSS does not work, you can still use your personal email address to subscribe to the Newsletter and apply a forwarding rule to get them in Feedly, see **[Can I automatically forward my email newsletters from my inbox to the Feedly email?](https://feedly.helpscoutdocs.com/article/380-can-i-automatically-forward-my-email-newsletters-from-my-inbox-to-the-feedly-email)** doc for more instructions.
