The Boise Co-op’s second Meridian location just took a step forward.

[As BoiseDev reported,](https://boisedev.com/news/2025/03/26/new-boise-co-op-location-could-be-in-the-works-for-meridian/) the local grocery brand has been eyeing a new location on Waverton Dr. near Chinden Blvd. and Black Cat Rd. in North Meridian. Now, the process is moving forward as the Co-op has submitted an official application to the City of Meridian.

Application documents show a store with 27,467 square feet of retail floor space.

![Freeform - Invested in Boise - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/8d57412c-01ee-47ef-b704-9f71ec57d3ba.png)

Freeform - Invested in Boise - 2:1

BoiseDev reported that two other buildings were planned for the property. Site plans submitted to the city currently only show the one Co-op store and a parking lot, but these plans account for just over half of the five-acre property.

This could be the Boise Co-op’s fifth shop in the Treasure Valley and the second one in Meridian. The store has full Co-op Market locations on Fort St. in Boise’s North End and at The Village at Meridian. The Co-Op also has separate wine and pet supply stores in Boise.

The Boise Co-op was founded in 1973 and gives regular discounts to its members. Anyone can get a Co-op membership for a fee. The store sells a variety of groceries, many of them coming from local brands, and also has a kitchen that serves freshly-made fare like sandwiches, pizza, burritos, and smoothies. Patrons can also sign up to take wine and baking classes.
