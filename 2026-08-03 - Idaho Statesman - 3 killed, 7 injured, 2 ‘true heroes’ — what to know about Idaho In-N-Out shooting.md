A gunman opened fire Saturday afternoon, Aug. 1, at an In-N-Out Burger in Twin Falls. Three people were killed and at least seven were wounded in the attack, according to a Twin Falls police media briefing. In addition, the gunman died of a self-inflicted gunshot wound. An off-duty police officer and an armed civilian returned fire and helped drive the shooter away from the restaurant.

Here are the key details known about the shooting so far:

- Twin Falls Police Chief Matthew Hicks confirmed [multiple fatalities at the In-N-Out on Blue Lakes Boulevard North](https://www.idahostatesman.com/news/northwest/idaho/article316738578.html#storylink=var-wtk) on Saturday afternoon, describing the incident as one of the most chaotic scenes local law enforcement had faced with hundreds of people in the area at the time.
- The attacker, [24-year-old Chad Williams, died of a self-inflicted gunshot wound](https://www.idahostatesman.com/news/local/crime/article316743345.html#storylink=var-wtk), and police said they are confident he acted alone, though his motive remains unknown.
- Of the seven wounded, [two have been treated and released, three were in stable condition and two were critically injured](https://www.idahostatesman.com/news/local/crime/article316743345.html#storylink=var-wtk), Hicks said at a Sunday press conference at Twin Falls City Hall.
- In-N-Out President Lynsi Snyder confirmed in a statement that [a restaurant employee was among those killed](https://www.idahostatesman.com/news/local/crime/article316743345.html#storylink=var-wtk), saying the associate was “taking care of our most important asset — our customers.” She was [identified](https://www.idahostatesman.com/news/local/crime/article316754956.html) by family and friends Monday as Ashley Garibay, 23, of Stockton, California. Garibay was in Twin Falls training employees of the newly opened restaurant. “We are heartbroken and devastated by the loss of Ashley — a devoted daughter, sister, auntie, cousin and friend — whose life was taken from us far too soon,” her family wrote in a statement provided to the Idaho Statesman.
- Friends and family members [began to identify](https://www.idahostatesman.com/news/local/crime/article316756042.html) additional victims Monday, describing them as an “ace,” a “beautiful friend,” and someone who made a “courageous decision.”
- Jordan Salinas, a 35-year-old healthcare worker from Kimberly, drew an FN Five-Seven semi-automatic pistol and [fired at the shooter after arriving for lunch](https://www.idahostatesman.com/news/local/crime/article316743551.html#storylink=var-wtk) around 2:30 p.m., following a four-step tactical framework he had drilled for years after the 2021 Boise Towne Square mall shooting.
- Twin Falls County Sheriff Jack Johnson called the off-duty officer and armed civilian “true heroes,” saying their [return fire drove Williams from the drive-through](https://www.idahostatesman.com/news/local/crime/article316743551.html#storylink=var-wtk) toward the city’s visitor center parking lot.
- FBI Special Agent in Charge Robert Bohls promised a thorough investigation and [set up a tip website at fbi.gov/twinfallsshooting](https://www.idahostatesman.com/news/local/crime/article316743345.html#storylink=var-wtk) to gather media files and information from witnesses.
- Officers from Meridian, Caldwell, Nampa and the Idaho State Police were [sworn in to increase patrols around Twin Falls](https://www.idahostatesman.com/news/local/crime/article316743345.html#storylink=var-wtk) as a precaution.
- The attack occurred about a week after the In-N-Out’s [July 24 grand opening](https://www.kmvt.com/2026/07/25/in-n-out-opens-its-doors-twin-falls-drawing-overnight-crowd/).
