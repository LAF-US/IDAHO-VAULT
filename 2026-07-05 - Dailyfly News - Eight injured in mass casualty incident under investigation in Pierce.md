PIERCE, ID — A mass casualty incident in Pierce early Sunday morning left approximately eight people injured, several with life-threatening burns, according to the Clearwater County Sheriff’s Office.

Emergency responders were dispatched at about 2:56 a.m. Sunday, July 5, to the incident in Pierce. Authorities said multiple emergency medical service agencies responded, and several victims were transported to regional hospitals for treatment.

The incident remains under investigation by the Idaho State Fire Marshal’s Office and the Clearwater County Sheriff’s Office, with assistance from the Orofino Police Department. Investigators are working to determine the cause and circumstances surrounding what happened.

In a press release, the sheriff’s office thanked firefighters, emergency medical personnel, law enforcement officers, dispatchers and assisting agencies for their coordinated response during the initial stages of the incident.

Officials said the investigation is ongoing and additional information will be released when it becomes available and can be shared without compromising the case.
