A Costco in South Meridian just got one step closer to becoming a reality.

In December, [BoiseDev reported Costco Wholesale](https://boisedev.com/news/2024/12/17/costco-s-meridian/) was eyeing the corner of Lake Hazel Rd. and Meridian Rd. for a new location. Now, permits for the project have been filed with the City of Meridian.

According to the permit documents, the new 200,342-square-foot location would have a warehouse, fuel facility, and distribution center. An MDO is also shown in the plans. MDO is an internal acronym for Market Delivery Operations – or the delivery of large and bulky items to customers’ homes.

Costco [told the City of Boise in 2022](https://boisedev.com/news/2022/09/28/costco-boise-mdo/) that it would hold a neighborhood meeting to build an MDO on a site adjacent to its warehouse store on Cole Rd., but plans for the Boise addition have not moved forward so far. An MDO is different from Costco’s “business center” format, and the plans do not call for such a feature, [as BoiseDev reported.](https://boisedev.com/news/2024/12/17/costco-s-meridian/)

According to the documents, the parking lot will have 903 spaces. There are five entry and exit points planned for the property — two on Lake Hazel Rd., two on Spire St., and one on Meridian Rd. The permit documents request a decrease in the required number of bike racks. Currently, Costco is required by city design standards to have 36 spaces for bikes. The store is requesting to decrease that to 20 spaces or fewer because “very few people come to Costco on their bikes.”

The fuel station is planned to include 16 pumps.

Costco is the anchor tenant for Brighton’s commercial portion of its Pinnacle neighborhood — a project called Apex Zenith. The company is currently going through the pre-application process for the rest of the commercial portion. Documents filed with the city show seven lots for the rest of the property, ranging in size from 41,000 to 95,000 square feet.

The southern portion of Meridian Rd. has continued to develop in recent years as the cities of Meridian and Kuna inch closer and closer together. Just down the road at the corner of Columbia Rd. and Meridian Rd., [a WinCo is also planned.](https://boisedev.com/news/2025/01/30/new-winco-planned-for-s-meridian-rd/) On the corner of Meridian Rd. and Amity Rd., Boise developer Hawkins also has plans for a [55-acre mixed-use development.](https://boisedev.com/news/2024/04/29/hawkins-amity-meridian-project/)

This would be the fourth Costco in the Treasure Valley, with others located in Nampa, North Meridian, and Boise.
