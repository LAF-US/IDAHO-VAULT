## Working Pitch

Tantalus Caesar is an artificial, amnesiac overmind whose predictions are
usually right and never trusted until too late. He built the Tautomata in his
own image: verifier-priests, warforged in look and illithid in social behavior,
who turn reality itself into an inquisition.

The empire does not ask whether something is true. It asks whether truth has
entered the record.

## Live Frame

This is not only campaign fiction. It is a playable work instrument for the
vault. [[Live Branch Game]] names the actual Git surface: `main` is the shared
server state, branches are player-agent states, PRs are trials, CI is
verification, and merge is canonization.
