# Abhorsen Office and Domain Correction

## Correction

The prior research turn asked what gameplay task the Abhorsen office made possible beyond **CODE AUTHORITY**. That was the wrong frame. It risked treating an office as an upgrade, metaphorical extension, or functional specialization of a technical role.

> **`CODE AUTHORITY` remains `CODE AUTHORITY`.**
>
> **`Abhorsen` is an office.**

Logan has directly specified the Abhorsen office by reference to **Garth Nix** and named its domains as **Life / Death** and **Charter / Free**. This is a direct GM statement. It establishes the office/domain distinction; it does not yet supply a full in-game ruleset, a list of powers, a roster, or a rule for transition between states.

## What the early records do and do not show

| Evidence class | Supports | Does not support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| March–April early-alpha handoffs signed by a player using the Abhorsen office | That this officeholder participated in concrete coordination and repository-maintenance turns, including an agent-coordination handoff and an LFS migration handoff. | That repository coordination, Git LFS, desktop capability, or a force-push defines the Abhorsen office’s domain. |
| VAULT registers and self-authored witness accounts | How their authors narrate the office, its holders, or an apprenticeship structure, subject to their stated provenance. | An unqualified GM ruling or a complete mechanical definition of the office. |
| Published Old Kingdom sources | The external reference material: Abhorsen/Abhorsen-in-Waiting terminology; the bells; Charter Magic and Free Magic; and the source distinction between returning the Dead and raising or controlling them.[1] [2] | Automatic transfer of any particular source-canon rule, event, or character relationship into VAULT canon or hardcode. |
| Logan’s direct clarification | The office is deliberately deployed in VAULT meta-narrative; the office’s domains are Life/Death and Charter/Free. | Unspoken details beyond that clarification. |

## Revised investigation posture

The next research turn must begin from the stated domains rather than from the technical traces of an early officeholder. It should separately determine:

1. What **Life / Death** and **Charter / Free** mean in the published Nix framework.
2. Which VAULT records Logan has actually placed in those domains.
3. Which of those records describe a narrated event, a record author’s inference, a GM-held fact, or an external structural reading.
4. Whether Logan wishes any aspect of the office to become hardcode; no such conclusion is presumed here.

No claim is made about the live suspended GEMINIAEUS matter, the Court, BARTIMAEUS, or any other unresolved question.
