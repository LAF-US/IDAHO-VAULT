The United States recently celebrated its 250th year with a time capsule that has now been sealed and buried at Independence National Historical Park in Philadelphia, and won’t be opened until 2276. The contents include pieces highlighting the State of Idaho’s tribal heritage, agricultural roots, and growing role in advanced manufacturing and energy innovation, [as BoiseDev reported.](https://boisedev.com/am-story/from-spuddy-buddy-to-a-micron-chip-heres-what-idaho-placed-in-americas-250-year-time-capsule/)

Word of this time capsule had one BoiseDev reader reach out, asking about a previous time capsule given to the state back in 1976 to celebrate the U.S. Bicentennial, saying the time capsule is missing and that the Idaho Historical Society does not know where it is.

## Reynolds 1976 Time Capsules

![](https://boisedev.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/you-asked-where-is-the-1976-bicentennial-time-capsule-for-the-state-of-idaho-National-Archives-1953x2048.jpg)

The Reynolds 1976 Bicentennial Time Capsule, one of the 56. Photo: Courtesy the U.S. National Archives

The Reynolds 1976 Bicentennial Time Capsule was a gift given to all 50 states in 1976 from Reynolds Metals. They constructed 56 seventeen-inch by seventeen-inch cubes containing waterproof time capsule drums and given to the governors of all 50 states, the National Archives, four offshore possessions, and Queen Elizabeth II.

![Corwin Ford Nampa - July 2026 - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/02b5ccc2-e925-42c6-9e39-de8bd0223814.jpg)

Corwin Ford Nampa - July 2026 - 2:1

States were encouraged to fill the time capsules with state maps, aerial photos, forecasts about life in 2076, photos of the state’s legislatures, and more, to be buried and opened in the year 2075.

## Idaho’s Time Capsule Selection

![](https://boisedev.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/you-asked-where-is-the-1976-bicentennial-time-capsule-for-the-state-of-idaho-image.png)

Copy of the Idaho Statesman from July 4, 1976. Photo: Courtesy Idaho Statesman

As the Idaho Statesman reported in 1976, the Idaho Historical Society and then-Governor Cecil D. Andrus selected a silver dollar, a copy of an Idaho income tax return, a state roadmap, postcards, a “blue book” list of state government officers and election data, a copy of a July 4, 1976 edition of the Idaho Statesman, an ashtray with the state seal, letters to the people of 2076 from Andrus and the Idaho congressional delegation, a centennial medal from 1876, copies of several Idaho magazines, and historical newsletters from several county historical societies.

The capsule was set to be buried in the cornerstone of the planned addition to the Idaho Historical Society and Idaho State Library building at 3rd and State St. to be opened in the year 2075.

![Wright Brothers - 40 years - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/90eb1cf7-f7f6-4c60-a70a-79506345fabf.png)

Wright Brothers - 40 years - 2:1

The Idaho State Archives also has copies of the documents included in the 1976 Bicentennial Time Capsule, [which can be viewed here.](https://idahostatearchives.libraryhost.com/repositories/2/resources/2563)

In 2006, the name and mission of the State Library changed when it became the Idaho Commission for Libraries (ICfL), the state’s library development agency, which is still located in that same building along with the Secretary of State’s Office at 325 W. State St.

## So where is the time capsule?

BoiseDev reached out to the Idaho Historical Society with a public records request on the whereabouts of the time capsule.

![Sundance Company](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/a95b6100-2267-485a-87f4-0eb633a38c0e.png)

Sundance Company

Angie Davis, the collection and outreach archivist at the Idaho Historical Society, confirmed that, “The time capsule was buried beneath the plaque listing the contributors to the new wing of the State Library at 325 W. State (now the Secretary of State’s Office) at the dedication of the new wing on July 15, 1980. Presumably, it is still there?”

![](https://boisedev.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/you-asked-where-is-the-1976-bicentennial-time-capsule-for-the-state-of-idaho-IMG-2946-Large.jpeg)

Plaque listing the contributors to the new wing of the State Library at 325 W. State, where the Reynold’s 1976 Time Capsule is buried. Photo: Don Day/BoiseDev

While no one can be 100% sure the capsule is still buried in the cornerstone beneath the marker above, Idahoans have another 50 years until it is officially unearthed.

BoiseDev followed up with the Idaho Historical Society to inquire how the organization plans to unearth the time capsule in 2076. As of the publication of this article, the historical society has not responded.
