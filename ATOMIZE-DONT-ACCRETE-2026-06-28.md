An append-only notebook is a **drift surface** — the DOCKET / standing-ledger anti-pattern. *A context doc too big to read is the opposite of useful.*

Fix: **atomic notes** — one idea, titled, linked, short. **Read** the record before producing; re-deriving a "catch" already written is navel-gaze.
