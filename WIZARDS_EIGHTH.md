# Wizard's Eighth Rule

## Summary

The eighth of the numbered Wizard's Rules stated across Terry Goodkind's *Sword of Truth* series, one per book. Given in the fictional High D'Haran language, "Talga Vassternich."

## Key Facts

**Source book:** *Naked Empire* (book 8)

**Text:**

> Talga Vassternich. (Deserve Victory.)
