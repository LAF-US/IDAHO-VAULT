Two University of Idaho students from Nampa are presumed to be dead after driving off the road on Highway 95 into the Salmon River Tuesday night.

The Idaho County Sheriff's Office says Cayla Danenberg, 20, and Tiffany Maupin, 21, were heading north to the University of Idaho when at about 7 p.m. their 2004 Nissan Maxima veered off the roadway and went into the river.

The sheriff's office believes both have drowned and deputies are searching for their bodies.

The search and rescue team has had unsuccessful attempts at finding the students. Police say divers cannot help because of the hazardous water conditions and location of the accident, which occurred just before milepost 211.

Crews are still looking and family members have been notified.

The University of Idaho posted this statement to its Facebook page: "The Vandal family is saddened by the passing of Cayla Danenberg and Tiffany Maupin, both who have been UI students, in a tragic car wreck. It is devastating to see Idaho’s young people taken just as they are getting started as adults. We offer our condolences to the families of Tiffany and Cayla and support all those affected by this tragic incident."
