**Kristine Sutherland** (born **Kristine Young**; April 17, 1955) is an American actress best known for her starring role as [Buffy Summers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffy_Summers "Buffy Summers") ' mother [Joyce Summers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joyce_Summers "Joyce Summers") on the television series *[Buffy the Vampire Slayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffy_the_Vampire_Slayer "Buffy the Vampire Slayer")*, where she appeared in every season (1997–2003), and her role as Mae Thompson in *[Honey, I Shrunk the Kids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honey,_I_Shrunk_the_Kids "Honey, I Shrunk the Kids")* (1989).

## Early life

She was born Kristine Young in [Boise, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise,_Idaho "Boise, Idaho"), and changed her name because she was often confused with another similarly named actress. The last name "Sutherland" was after her cat, whom she named "Donald" after [Donald Sutherland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Sutherland "Donald Sutherland"), who, coincidentally, had a role in the original *[Buffy the Vampire Slayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffy_the_Vampire_Slayer_\(film\) "Buffy the Vampire Slayer (film)")* film, [Joss Whedon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joss_Whedon "Joss Whedon") 's first attempt to bring Buffy to audiences.[^1] She is not related to the [Sutherland Acting Family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Sutherland "Donald Sutherland").[^2]

She attended high school in [Lexington, Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lexington,_Kentucky "Lexington, Kentucky"), where she participated in the Tates Creek Drama program. After graduating, she enrolled in the [University of Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kentucky "University of Kentucky").

## Career

Sutherland's first film role was as a secretary in *[Legal Eagles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legal_Eagles "Legal Eagles")* (1986). She appeared as Mae Thompson in the 1989 film *[Honey, I Shrunk the Kids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honey,_I_Shrunk_the_Kids "Honey, I Shrunk the Kids")*. Her career received a big boost when she played Buffy's mother in the action-horror television series *[Buffy the Vampire Slayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffy_the_Vampire_Slayer "Buffy the Vampire Slayer")*, from 1997 to 2002.[^3] Creator Joss Whedon later stated that one of the reasons he chose Sutherland for the role was that she exuded a warm demeanor, as well as what he believed to be a close resemblance to actress [Sarah Michelle Gellar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Michelle_Gellar "Sarah Michelle Gellar"), who was to portray her daughter in the series. Sutherland appeared regularly on the show for the first five seasons, with only one appearance in season 6 and two in season 7.

After leaving *Buffy*, Sutherland attended a photography course at the [Santa Monica College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Monica_College "Santa Monica College") and subsequently set up a studio specializing in portraits. In 2008, she appeared in the television miniseries *[Comanche Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comanche_Moon_\(miniseries\) "Comanche Moon (miniseries)")* and had a guest role in an episode of *[New Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Amsterdam_\(2008_TV_series\) "New Amsterdam (2008 TV series)")*.[^3]

She voiced Haru's Mother in the English dub of *[The Cat Returns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Cat_Returns "The Cat Returns")*.

## Personal life

Sutherland is married to actor [John Pankow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Pankow "John Pankow"). They have one daughter.
