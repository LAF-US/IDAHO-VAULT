Two brave firefighters, John Morrison Jr. and Frank Harwood, were honored at the National Fallen Firefighter Memorial in Maryland.

![](https://media.krem.com/assets/KREM/images/bdac09ff-903d-44f5-a428-bc5808488a0c/20260503T202505/bdac09ff-903d-44f5-a428-bc5808488a0c_1920x1080.jpg)

Credit: Coeur d'Alene Fire Department

IDAHO, USA — Fallen firefighters John Morrison and Frank Harwood were honored in Emmitsburg, Maryland, at the National Fallen Firefighter Memorial.

Battalion Chiefs John Morrison Jr. and Frank Harwood were killed during a targeted ambush on [Canfield Mountain in June 2025.](https://www.krem.com/article/news/special-reports/firefighters-killed-in-ambush/fallen-firefighters-procession-routes-memorial-ceremonies-coeur-dalene/293-27e191e0-0060-4978-aa7e-dadc61df70e5)

Members of both the Coeur d’Alene Fire Department and Kootenai County Fire and Rescue, along with their families, traveled to Maryland for the National Fallen Firefighter Memorial Weekend. Morrison and Harwood's names were added to the memorial.

This follows the " [Light the Night in Red](https://www.krem.com/article/news/local/idaho/light-the-night-red-north-idaho-campaign-honor-fallen-firefighters/293-054e6fbd-964f-461b-a4e8-0a0cfc5cd732) " week in North Idaho, which took place from April 26 through May 3.
