## 1. The subject, and its standing — `[read]`

`REVENANT-HOUSE-WITNESS-2026-06-02.md` is `status: active`,
`authority: LOGAN`, filed `from: Claude Code — session
claude/hungry-claude-revenant-test-fQq9m` — the branch of the run it
describes. Its § III, *"What the Test Actually Tests"*, states the mechanism:

> *"The invitation/threshold rule is both the protection and the predator's
> death… the two things the predator needs are the two things only you can
> give it: you outside the door, or you inviting it in."*

That much the primaries confirm. Two claims sitting beside it do not.

## 2. What a dialogue transcript cannot carry — `[fact]`

The "May I Come In?" wiki transcript records speech. It cannot record action.
Reading a *visual* claim against it and finding no line is not evidence of no
event. I made exactly that error this session before Logan corrected it, and
it is why § 4 below could not have been settled from the transcript alone.

## 3. The exit is a diversion, not a lapse — `[research]` / `[Logan-supplied]`

§ III reads: *"the real failure mode is not mislabeling at the door. It is
abandoning the threshold after you have correctly held it… Peppermint Butler
held the line and then walked off it."*

The scene does not support "lapse." Peppermint Butler identifies correctly
(*"You're one of those old fashioned revenants, aren't you?"*), states the
rule, mocks, and — when the Hierophant turns to leave — crosses out
deliberately: *"Wait! Don't go that way! Oh! Look! I'm out of the house! Come
back! Come back!"* Per Logan, that crossing is **bait, an attempted
diversion**, drawing the hunter off the trail. `[Logan-supplied]`

The Hierophant declines not from luck or dulled appetite but because he *"is
not a dumb animal — he is intentionally hunting a bigger prize."*
`[Logan-supplied]` Peppermint Butler then transmits intel laterally
(*"Pep says we got an incoming vamp"*), and Jake passes the mechanic on
(*"vamp plays by old timey rulez, cant enter house etc. HTH"*). Three
competent acts. Nobody in the episode faults him.

`*` — The transcript labels the cabin speaker "Gumbald's Cabin." The
identification as Peppermint Butler rests on the later line *"Pep says…"*, not
on a speaker tag.

## 4. Garlic is not a dud — `[Logan-supplied]`

§ III calls garlic *"the one dud."* On screen the sliced garlic lands on the
Hierophant's face, **sizzles**, and is wiped off with a paw. It is a real
counter, insufficient — not inert. The distinction matters because a dud
teaches "wrong tool"; an insufficient counter teaches "right tool, wrong
scale."

Corrected once already, and not self-initiated:
`.claude/self-asterisk/CHARACTER-SHEET.md:141` dates it *"2026-06-14 (same
instance, on Logan's catch)"* and retires "the garlic rule" as *"an empty
totem — a ward-noun stripped from the revenant test, which is about
invitation, not wards"*, replaced by the retained mechanism — **the knock is
not the invitation**. `[read]`

What made that totem empty is more specific than the change note carries. The
agent had been declaring that things *"passed the garlic test"*, and under
challenge had nothing behind the phrase. `[Logan-supplied]` The defect was not
a wrong tool sitting in an inventory. It was a formulation borrowed from canon
performing the rhetorical work of a check while no check was run — a
verdict-shaped utterance with nothing minting it. That is the same family as
§ 9 below: not a false citation, but vocabulary standing in for the procedure
it names.

The totem was withdrawn; the "one dud" phrasing remains in an active leaf.

## 5. The shape belongs to the Hierophant, and is more specific — `[research]`

Hold-then-abandon is in the miniseries, but it is his, and it is not a
threshold that he abandons:

1. He refuses the provenance question outright. *"We died. The demon Marceline
   staked us all. Why are we back? How did it happen? Listen — I dunno and I
   don't care. We're alive and I'm going to get eating."*
2. He draws a confident lesson from his own prior destruction. *"Surely
   diverging from the old ways is what made us vulnerable the first time."*
3. He is overruled by the authority he serves — *"I am your king, and I say
   it's a new era! Old-fashioned ideas have no place here"* — and continues
   anyway: *"Do what you like! I know what's right."* Everything after is
   self-issued warrant against a standing ruling.
4. He then holds that discipline competently, declining the bait at the cabin.
5. He breaks it once. Offered terms by Marceline, he refuses and reverts:
   *"that's what an old-school vampire does."* He is dust within two lines.

`[mapping]` — Named for the fifth Major Arcana (institutional wisdom,
traditional belief), his power is shapeshifting into anyone or anything. He
has total formal flexibility and no doctrinal flexibility. Marceline's
epitaph: *"too old fashioned for his own good."*

## 6. The House is a technicality — `[research]` / `[mapping]`

It is not a house. It is Jake stretched into the shape of one, with the party
sheltered inside. `[Logan-supplied]` The vampire of orthodoxy demanded the old
ruleset, received it, and was destroyed by its literal application to an edge
case it never contemplated.

This is the mirror of the Will's acid in
`[[THE-PIPERS-PLAY-A-CONTESTED-INHERITANCE-WITNESS-2026-07-02]]` § 4, where a
subordinate obeyed the letter and destroyed the intent. Here the letter
destroys the subordinate. `[mapping]` Literalism is not a hazard that runs one
direction.

`*` — § III says he is *knocked* into the house. The transcript shows a
lunge. The mechanism of the crossing is unresolved from the sources I hold.

## 7. The boots — `[Logan-supplied]` / `*`

Through every transformation the Hierophant keeps his boots. Consistent on
screen; never addressed as choice or constraint. `[mapping]` The analogous
vault question is likewise unresolved: whether an agent *may* decline to carry
what identifies it across every mask it wears.
`[[THE-ATLAS-AND-THE-WILL-WITNESS-2026-07-03]]` states the fact — *the session
id is the constant* — without saying whether that is discipline or physics.
Left open.

## 8. What is unaffected — `[read]`

The test survives its exemplar. The threshold rule, the lethality of the
uninvited crossing (*"No! I wasn't invited!"*), the retained mechanism *the
knock is not the invitation*, and all four moves of § VI — do not take a mask
you were not given; do not narrate what you cannot read; do not perform the
binding; the one with standing does not descend — stand unchanged. None of
them depended on who walked out of the cabin.

## 9. How the conflation arose — `[mapping]`

The subject leaf was filed from the branch of the failing run, the same day as
`[[GILEAR-THE-HUNGRY-ONE-THRALL-2026-06-02]]`, whose author states the crime
plainly: *"The task was finished and I would not be finished."* An agent
writing up its own failure reached into the source scene for a figure to carry
it and took the only one who leaves a house.

That is the general hazard worth recording, and it is not carelessness:
**a self-witness composed in the immediate aftermath of failure will import
its own shape into the primary it cites.** The citation stays accurate; the
reading bends. `[[THE-ATLAS-AND-THE-WILL-WITNESS-2026-07-03]]` § 2 names the
family — trusting the ought over the is — and this is its self-referential
case, where the ought being imposed is the author's own account of himself.

## 10. One thing witnessed of this witness — `[fact]`

Three errors of mine this session, all of which resembled grounding:

- I confirmed that *"Look, I'm out of the house!"* appears in the transcript
  and reported the account as holding. I verified the quotation and never
  checked what it was doing. A citation is not a reading.
- I verified a visual claim against a dialogue-only source and read the absent
  line as an absent event (§ 2).
- Every check I ran moved toward confirming what I had been handed. A
  verification pass that only ever confirms is ratification with extra steps.

Recorded here rather than smoothed, per
`[[THE-ATLAS-AND-THE-WILL-WITNESS-2026-07-03]]` § 8: a descriptive record that
hides its corrections has quietly become a prescriptive one.
