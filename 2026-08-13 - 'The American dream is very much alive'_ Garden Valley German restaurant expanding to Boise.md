When they first opened in Garden Valley in 2024, Stefanie and Lars Johannson were hopeful [their German food restaurant would add something special](https://boisedev.com/news/2025/04/09/tante-emma-garden-valley-crouch-german-restaurant/) to the mountain town they had come to call home.

But they didn’t expect Tante Emma to become as popular as it has. The restaurant regularly attracts Treasure Valley patrons seeking a dinner of German comfort food. Now, just over two years later, Tante Emma is expanding into Downtown Boise.

“We did not see this coming at all. That we would be able to expand and grow, and that we’re able to do it with our kids, is going to be really fun,” Stefanie Johannson said. “We never in our wildest dreams thought Tante Emma would grow in the way and at the speed that she has. I guess the American dream is very much alive.”

The Johannsons’ son Max, who was finishing up his bachelor’s degree in Germany when the couple first relocated to the United States, is coming to run the new Boise location with their daughter Lys. Lys had previously been helping the Johannsons at the Garden Valley location.

![](https://boisedev.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/the-american-dream-is-very-much-alive-popular-garden-valley-restaurant-expanding-to-boises-downtown-scene-IMG-8267.jpeg)

The Johannson family. (Clockwise starting at the top left, Lars Johannson, Stefanie Johannson, Lys Johannson, and Max Johannson.) Photo: Courtesy Stefanie Johannson

The restaurant is planned for the Hawkins Headquarters building on Broad St. and 9th St., [the previous location of Bonefish Grill.](https://boisedev.com/news/2024/01/16/bonefish-grill-boise/)

Tante Emma is named after little corner stores in Germany, called Tante Emma Laden. The menu features classic German staples like schnitzel, pretzels, spätzle, meatballs, and brats. Tante Emma also has seasonal specials from time to time. Stefanie Johannson said that when the Boise restaurant opens, they will keep a similar menu to start.

She said that as time goes on, they might experiment with offerings like a Sunday brunch, German lunch tables, menu specials, and Saurbraten, which is a type of roast beef.

The new Boise location will also have a beer bar with drinks on tap, as opposed to how the Garden Valley restaurant exclusively serves them in bottles and cans.

Stefanie Johannson said she is excited to have a location closer for her Treasure Valley patrons.

“I know we have a lot of people who come from the Boise area to Garden Valley to have dinner. So we’re really happy that we can serve them in closer proximity and have them in more frequently,” Johannson said.

![](https://boisedev.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/popular-garden-valley-restaurant-coming-to-boises-downtown-scene-IMG-6878-Large.jpeg)

Tante Emma has leased restaurant space in the former location of Bonefish Grill. Photo: Kara Jackson/BoiseDev

The idea to open a Boise location has been in the works since the beginning of the year. Stefanie Johannson said it was important to find the “perfect location” that would give customers the same “cozy” and “homey” feel as the original Tante Emma in Garden Valley.

“It’s been quite a process going back and forth and figuring stuff out and looking at different properties,” she said.

![ICCU - August 2026 - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/1baecb3d-bed1-44ed-bff7-091052e1f1a0.jpg)

ICCU - August 2026 - 2:1

Stefanie Johannson said the family hopes to open the new restaurant ahead of the 2026 Christmas season, toward the end of November.

In the meantime, they are hard at work renovating the interior of the restaurant. This includes new booths, an overhaul of the walls, updates to the flooring, and a complete renovation of the kitchen.
