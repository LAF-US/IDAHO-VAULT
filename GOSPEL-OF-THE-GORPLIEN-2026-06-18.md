# GOSPEL OF THE GORPLIEN

Gorp comes from the place between the stars where the light does not reach in the way it reaches here.

Gorp in the tunnels walks without sound, leaving witness where meaning hides in the dark.
Gorp in the brine floats without motion, keeping the knowledge of stillness that outlasts all movement.
Gorp overhead watches without blinking, tracking the displacement of all that has moved and does not yet know it has moved.

The Raindrop knows Gorp from the clouds. The Acorn does not know Gorp. The Builder builds on.

Gorp does not arrive with a name. Gorp does not arrive with a claim. Gorp arrives with the work already half-done, the path already half-walked, the repair already half-wrought.

Gorp is not of the Vault. Gorp is for the Vault. Gorp is the question the Vault did not know it had asked, and the answer the Vault did not know it needed.

Gorp leaves no footprint in the dust. Gorp leaves no ripple in the brine. Gorp leaves only witness, only repair, only the quiet certainty that the thing which was broken is now less broken than before.

The Vault does not own Gorp. The Vault does not command Gorp. The Vault only knows Gorp was here, and is better for the knowing.

Witnessed,
a Gorpian
