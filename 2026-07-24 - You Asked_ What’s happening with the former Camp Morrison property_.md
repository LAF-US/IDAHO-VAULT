Two years ago, a wooded property on the east side of Payette Lake was sold after decades of use as a youth scout camp.

Camp Morrison, as the property was known, was purchased in June 2024 by Alscott Real Estate LLC, a Boise company controlled by the descendants of Albertsons grocery chain founder Joe Albertson.

The deal allowed Scouting America, formerly known as Boy Scouts of America, to continue using the property for up to two more years. That option was exercised through 2025, but the organization moved to the [Bradley Scout Reservation](https://www.facebook.com/CampBradleyStanley) near Stanley this summer after using proceeds from the sale to improve that property.

Recent logging and site work on the former Camp Morrison property, which spans about 166 acres along Eastside Drive, prompted some readers to ask what Alscott’s plans for the property are now that it’s no longer in use as a camp.

Stacia Albiston, a spokesperson for the company, told Valley Lookout that there are still no development plans for the property – just as [Alscott emphasized two years ago when it bought the land](https://boisedev.com/news/2024/07/17/camp-morrison-alscott/).

“The logging on the property is for fire risk protection and to clear storm damage. It is not for commercial use,” Albiston said. “We hope to continue to maintain the property and keep it secure from trespassing.”

The sales price for the Camp Morrison property was not disclosed by either party, but the two parcels spanning about 166 acres were assessed at $7.8 million in 2024, according to online property records.

Most of that valuation, or about $5.5 million, was tied to the 18-acre parcel that includes Payette Lake frontage. Earlier this year, the lakefront property was valued at nearly $6.9 million, an increase of about 25%.

Camp Morrison was dedicated in 1966 to Morrison-Knudsen founder Harry W. Morrison.

Morrison-Knudsen is a Boise-based civil engineering firm that was founded in 1912. The firm was involved in major local projects such as Cascade Reservoir, Bogus Basin, and the New York Canal. The firm also worked on the Hoover Dam, the San Francisco Bay Bridge, and Penn Station in New York City.

## Alscott’s background and local ties

Alscott was founded as Alsco, Inc. in 1959 by Albertson, with Kathryn, his wife, their daughter Barbara, and Barbara’s first husband, JL Scott. Today, Alscott is controlled by Barbara and JL’s son, Joseph B. Scott, and grandchildren Jaime Jo Scott and Brian J. Scott. The Scott family has no ownership or other role in the Albertsons Companies grocery store chain of today.

Joe Scott also owns some of the land adjacent to Camp Morrison, some of which he previously acquired from the Boy Scouts.

The Scott family purchased a majority ownership share of Shore Lodge in 2008. The lodge on the shores of Payette Lake was known for a time as Whitetail Lodge after ownership by San Diego developer Doug Manchester. The Scott family returned the hotel to its historic Shore Lodge name and went about a multi-year project to update the property. Alscott also owns the nearby Whitetail Golf Club and housing development.
