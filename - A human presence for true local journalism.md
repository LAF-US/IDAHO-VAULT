## In good company

Over 75 local reporters are already using Local Journalist Accounts on Nextdoor, representing publications like those shown here. Join them!

Simply fill out [this form](https://nextdoor.com/create_journalist_account) and wait to hear back from our team.

You determine where your posts appear. Each post requires a geo-tag. You can target a metro/city, county, or a specific neighborhood. Match the tag to your story's scope, and choose a central location to maximize reach to the neighbors the story is actually about.

If you have any questions or issues with your account, [contact us here](https://help.nextdoor.com/s/contactus?language=en_US&audience=News%20Account) or at newshelp@nextdoor.com.
