Sen. Dan Foreman is a garbage Republican.

And no, you don’t have to vote for him just because he’s the “lesser of two evils.”

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!i7Y6!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F0cb8c70f-a296-43b1-921c-6b9218ce5932_646x497.png)

Foreman was once a rock-solid conservative. Then he turned his back on the very voters who put him in office—running cover for the woke teachers union this legislative session.

This is a guy who used to sit near the top of the Idaho Freedom Index. But ever since he took the gavel of the Senate Commerce and Human Resources Committee from Sen. Kelly Anthon, he’s been a complete letdown.

He holds a D rating from the Idaho Freedom Foundation and a D+ on the Idaho GOP Platform scorecard.

Disappointing.

Yes, Lori McCann is worse. Much worse. She’s not a serious alternative for conservatives.

What’s a conservative to do, then?

If you’re part of the new breed of Moyle-loving, donut-eating Republicans like Brian Almon, you probably shrug, hold your nose, and vote for the lesser evil. “You have to win to govern,” right?

But what message does that send?

Voting for Foreman tells every Republican in Idaho that they can betray conservatives, defend the teachers union, and still count on your vote.

Why would they ever change?

How many times have we watched politicians campaign like conservatives, then govern like something else entirely?

If you’re sick of that, then act like it.

If I were a District 6 voter, I’d take my GOP primary ballot and leave the Senate race blank. (And probably one of the House seats because Colton Bennett would keep Medicaid expansion in place.)

The Idaho Senate is already run by RINO leadership like Kelly Anthon, who blocked key conservative immigration bills this year. Foreman’s marginally-better-than-McCann vote won’t meaningfully change the balance next session.

But holding your own side accountable? That matters.

Drawing a line matters.

District 6, send the message.

Knife Foreman. Then we will recruit a real conservative to replace McCann in 2028.
