## Findings

### ARB-F1 - Address-Space Leaves Were Exposed To A False Disposal Signal

The May 9 local splinter census counts `19,501` zero-byte root Markdown leaves
and places tiny or zero-byte stubs at the bottom of its salvage order unless
they carry known wiki-index value. The earlier [[!/!REPORT-TO-JANITOR-2026-04-09]]
already reported an address-space experiment made of stub notes, counted about
`39,022` zero-byte Markdown leaves, and directed that the stub lattice be
treated as intentional unless Logan said otherwise.

The May 9 leaf therefore cannot be safely used as a disposal rubric for small
or empty Markdown leaves without an address-space exception. This is not a
finding that every stub must be kept forever. It is a finding that byte size
does not establish expendability in this vault.

**Proposed disposition:** marginalia on
[[LOCAL-ARBORSCAPE-IDAHO-VAULT-SPLINTERS-2026-05-09]] preserving the census
while directing readers to [[!/!REPORT-TO-JANITOR-2026-04-09]],
[[!/ADDRESS-GRAMMAR-v1-2026-05-22]], and [[VAULTED-SYNTAX]] before pruning
stub leaves.

### ARB-F2 - Scope Ramified Without A Stable Boundary Finding

The April records concern branch gardening. The May 9 census says it applies
existing doctrine to local worktrees and payload salvage. The May 17 completion
report then states that ARBORSCAPE doctrine applies to branches, not living
worktrees. The May 22 draft proposes extension into PR triage while expressly
leaving metadata, local-state grooming, and doctrinal attribution outside the
protocol.

These records demonstrate useful investigations conducted under a shared
metaphor. They do not yet establish one adopted ARBORSCAPING scope after the
original branch-focused protocol.

**Question reserved for Logan:** does ARBORSCAPING remain branch gardening with
separately commissioned adjacent investigations, or has any specific
ramification into PR management been adopted?

### ARB-F3 - Draft PR-Expansion Proposals Are Referenced By Executable Workflows

[[!/ARBORSCAPE-PR-EXPANSION-2026-05-22]] is marked `status: draft` and says
promotion to active is reserved to Logan. Nevertheless:

- [[.github/workflows/agent-review-gate.yml]] identifies its
  `action_required` sweep as implementation of `IF 8` from that draft.
- [[.github/workflows/review-feedback-loop.yml]] identifies its verification
  job as implementation of `IF 7` from that draft.
- [[.github/workflows/metadata-survey.yml]] cites Phase `1d` of the draft even
  though the draft itself identifies metadata/frontmatter work as outside
  ARBORSCAPE proper.

The presence of working code does not itself promote the cited draft to
doctrine. The concurrent metadata survey is noted as activity, not treated as
cause for alarm or alteration in this investigation.

**Proposed disposition:** obtain a ruling distinguishing accepted workflow
implementations from adoption, rejection, or limitation of the PR-expansion
doctrinal framing.

### ARB-F4 - The Draft's Dependabot Finding Was Inaccurate In Its Own Lineage

The May 22 draft states in `IF 12` that
[[.github/workflows/dependabot-rhythm.yml]] and
[[.github/workflows/dependabot-reaper.yml]] both used `gh pr merge --auto`.
Git inspection of the draft commit's parent tree (`612ba76e^`) shows both
workflows already using direct `gh pr merge --squash --delete-branch` commands.

The direct-squash posture therefore was not merely a later correction on the
present branch; it was already the document's immediate inherited evidence
when the contrary finding was filed.

**Proposed disposition:** marginalia on
[[!/ARBORSCAPE-PR-EXPANSION-2026-05-22]] limiting `IF 12` as an inaccurate
workflow observation, without disturbing any separately valid question about
branch protections or merge authority.

### ARB-F5 - Control-Plane Language Is Adjacent Residue, Not Arborscaping Standing

[[BRIEF-LAF-25-2026-04-04]] requests that Arborscaping be recorded as an
"active control-plane governance need." [[!/REPO-SLIMMING-PROTOCOL]] repeatedly
describes the root repo as a "control plane." Logan has directly clarified that
there is no currently adopted control plane, heartbeat, or status board.

The legitimate branch-gardening subject is separable from this terminology.
The control-plane phrasing should not be used as evidence of adopted
coordination architecture.

**Proposed disposition:** refer the two affected leaves for correction or
marginalia on their own terms, without converting ARBORSCAPING itself into a
heresy matter.

### ARB-F6 - Completion Language Records A Dated Act, Not Present Standing

[[!/ARBORSCAPE-COMPLETION-REPORT-2026-05-17]] records that the schedule in
`vault-ingest.yml` was silenced while manual dispatch was preserved. Git
history corroborates a May 17 workflow modification and shows that
`.github/workflows/vault-ingest.yml` was deleted on May 20.

The May 17 language is useful as a dated record of action. It is not a current
workflow reference and should not be read as one.

### ARB-F7 - The May 9 Trunk-Return Commit Requires Artifact-Level Review

The commit titled `Arborscape trunk-return salvage batch` (`a6be16ad`) added
the May 9 census, branch workflows, and many other files in a 114-file change.
This demonstrates that the name ARBORSCAPE was used for a substantial return of
artifacts to the trunk, not only for a census or branch deletion pass.

No impropriety is inferred from a large salvage commit. Its breadth does mean
that the label alone cannot prove every returning artifact inherited approved
doctrinal standing or appropriate preservation treatment.

**Proposed disposition:** review artifacts by class when questions arise,
rather than accepting or rejecting the batch from its commit title.
