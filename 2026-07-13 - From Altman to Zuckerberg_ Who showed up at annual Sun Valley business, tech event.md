For a few days each summer, the Sun Valley swans move down the list of the resort’s most famous residents.

Each summer, Sun Valley becomes home to the highest concentration of wealth and power on earth for a few days. New York investment bank Allen and Co. brings its annual media and technology summit to the Sun Valley Resort each year after the Fourth of July.

The event wrapped up on Saturday, and hundreds of people were spotted by photographers and reporters roaming the grounds of the central Idaho resort.

Ahead of the event, Hollywood trade publication Variety puts out a leaked list of some of the invited attendees, but the list of people who show up is far larger. BoiseDev scoured published reports and photographs to put together a fuller picture of who actually attended the event, which has picked up the media-fueled nickname “summer camp for billionaires.”

Scores of Fortune 500 companies are represented, from Apple to OpenAI to Meta to General Motors and more. Gov. Brad Little was in attendance, spotted by photographers in a red-branded [Allen and Co. windbreaker.](https://www.gettyimages.com/detail/news-photo/brad-little-governor-of-idaho-during-the-allen-co-media-and-news-photo/2284899715?adppopup=true)

Several administration officials, including U.S. Secretary of the Interior Doug Burgum and the new Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh, were present, as well as President Donald Trump’s daughter Ivanka Trump and her husband Jared Kushner. Kushner’s brother Josh Kushner and wife, model Karlie Kloss, followed up their weekend at Taylor Swift’s wedding with one in Sun Valley.

The events, which are closed to the media, featured panels including Yellowstone creator Taylor Sheridan, Democratic Senator John Fetterman, former Israeli prime minister Naftali Bennett, and others, [including Puck reporter Dylan Byers.](https://x.com/DylanByers?lang=en)

Though the panels are closed to the media for reporting purposes, top personalities, including Anderson Cooper, Gayle King, CNBC anchor Becky Quick, and others, appeared and often moderated events.

The commissioners of the NBA, NHL, NFL, and Major League Baseball were each in attendance.
