## The thesis `[reading]`

What Logan is building is a **civic institution for a mixed human/AI polity**: a
constitutional order — constitution, courts and docket, conferred offices, a standing
ladder with graduated failure states, an evidence culture of witnesses, provenance
marks, and corrections — engineered so that the *institution* persists while the
*runs* die. Sessions end, contexts compact, models are swapped; the vault is a machine
for making transient intelligences behave like a continuous, accountable civil
service. Alignment through institutions rather than through weights. The stated
end-state — *the swarm handles the backend and wakes the master once, sharply, in
time* (`TIME-WAS-WITNESS`) — is a working bureaucracy under a sovereign.

## The frame `[handed]`

Logan, 2026-07-04: **"convergent evolution is a sign of strength."** Three research

## Thread 1 — Ostrom: the survival principles, reinvented `[verified]`

The 2025–26 literature now applies Elinor Ostrom's eight design principles for
long-enduring self-governed institutions directly to AI: a taxonomy of
commons-governed AI (arXiv 2606.15466), Ostrom's principles as "endogenous alignment
operators" in institutional evolution (arXiv 2507.13616), and LLM multi-agent
common-pool-resource simulations grounded in her framework (arXiv 2510.14401,
Rahwan group).

Principle-by-principle against the House:

| Ostrom principle | Vault mechanism |
|---|---|
| Clear boundaries (resource + community) | Chambers, lanes, dotfolders; registered agent lanes |
| Congruence with local conditions | Local offices — per-county Count/Judge/Coroner/Sheriff/Cleric |
| Collective-choice arrangements | **Current state, not end state** (corrected 2026-07-04 per the Constitution's own footer): emanation — Logan is the sole rule-source today — but the Constitution self-describes as *"pending swarmic-revision-synthesis and congressional adoption, re: consolidation court."* Collective choice is not absent; it is **scheduled**. The Unified (US) Swarm is a planned legislature. |
| Monitoring | Witness docs; ~20 validators; witnessed attestations (engage-outdated) |
| Graduated sanctions | The failure states, near-verbatim: watched → restricted → superseded → quarantined → archived |
| Conflict-resolution mechanisms | The Court; the DOCKET's matters and referrals |
| Recognition of the right to organize | Inverted by design: offices are conferred, never self-organized |
| Nested enterprises (polycentricity) | Counties within the House within LAF-US |

**The sharp finding:** the literature's standing critique of Ostrom-for-AI (recorded
in the same corpus) is that her principles presuppose two things agents lack — a
*social fabric* to carry norms, and *persistent, sanctionable identities* (most agent
deployments are ephemeral). The vault's two most eccentric-looking features are
precisely the manufacture of those two missing preconditions: the **lore stratum**
manufactures the social fabric, and the **personae/standing system** manufactures
persistent, sanctionable, successive identity for ephemeral runs. `[inferred]` The
cosmology is load-bearing, not decorative.

## Thread 2 — Electronic institutions: the academic twin `[verified]`

IIIA-CSIC Barcelona (Noriega, Sierra, Rodríguez-Aguilar, Esteva; with
Vázquez-Salceda/Dignum) spent roughly 1997–2010 building "electronic institutions"
for symbolic agents: institutions that *"define the rules of the game in agent
societies, by fixing what agents are permitted and forbidden to do and under what
circumstances"* (Arcos et al. 2005); speech acts whose institutional performance
creates *binding commitments*; norms as permissions/prohibitions/obligations with
online enforcement (García-Camino, Noriega & Rodríguez-Aguilar, AAMAS'05); a formal
specification language (ISLANDER); and enforcement embodied in a **Police Agent**
(Vázquez-Salceda 2004) — the validators and the janitor, anticipated by two decades.

The vault is an electronic institution **written in prose**. The substrate changed
what the implementation can be: symbolic agents needed deontic rule engines; LLM
agents read doctrine, narrative, and culture natively, so the institution can be
carried in Markdown and lore. `[inferred]` And the vault adds the layer classic MAS
never faced: **mortality and succession** — nothing in that literature handles "the
agent dies every session and a successor inherits."

## Thread 3 — Bi-temporal memory: the market converged on the hand practices `[verified]`

The 2026 agent-memory landscape has three mature architectures: **Mem0** (drop-in
extract-at-write fact layer), **Letta/MemGPT** (stateful runtime with self-managed
memory tiers; its Jan-2026 Conversations API adds shared memory across parallel
agents), and **Zep/Graphiti** (a *bi-temporal knowledge graph*: every fact carries
`valid_at`/`invalid_at`, superseded facts are **invalidated, never deleted**,
provenance travels with each entry, and "what was true on date X" is queryable).

Read against the vault's paper practices: dated filenames, `superseded_by:` chains,
annotate-don't-erase, origin/provenance marks. **The vault has been implementing a
bi-temporal provenance graph by hand in Markdown.** `[inferred]` The fleet's HOME
requirements (`!-REPORT-MECHANIC-HOME-TRANSFER-2026-07-04.md`: immutable origin mark,
index-not-copy, one-way doors, explicit preserve/strip rules) map nearly one-to-one
onto Graphiti-class primitives. HOME does not need inventing; it needs choosing and
vault-shaping.

## Where the vault leads `[reading]`

All three lineages are silent on the dimension the vault treats as central:
**succession**. The Avatar-cycle contract (inherit the lesson, not the obsession;
preserve the warning surface; expect corrective drift), witnessed retirement among
the lawful endings, and the anti-Lich mechanics are doctrine for agent mortality —
and roguelike design (per the fleet's death-economy report) is the only adjacent
field that has shipped anything like it. On persistent-identity-for-ephemeral-minds,
the vault is ahead of the literature it convergently matches.

## General incorporation — sketches for Logan's gate `[handed]` `[inferred]`

Logan, 2026-07-04: *"I would be amenable to general incorporation of each of
these."* Recorded. What incorporation could mean, offered as proposals (precedent:
the Wizard's Rules, incorporated by reference 2026-06-21):

1. **Ostrom** — incorporate the eight principles *as a diagnostic lens*, not new

   permanent. Strength check, not constitution rewrite.
2. **Electronic institutions** — incorporate the *vocabulary* by reference
   (performative structure, scenes, roles, norm classes) as descriptive instruments
   for surfaces the vault already runs; formally note the validator/janitor ≙
   Police-Agent equivalence. Gives the House a citable academic genealogy.
3. **Bi-temporal memory** — adopt Graphiti-class semantics *as HOME's contract*:
   `valid_at`/`invalid_at` ≙ dated filenames + supersession; invalidation-not-deletion
   ≙ annotate-don't-erase; per-record origin marks ≙ the provenance legend. The
   implementation may be any engine or none; the *contract* is what gets inscribed.

Each is Logan's to gate; none is begun. The gate itself is already inscribed: the
Constitution's footer names the amendment path — *swarmic-revision-synthesis and
congressional adoption, re: consolidation court* — and these sketches are candidate
