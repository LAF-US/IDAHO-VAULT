**By FāVS News Staff**

FāVS News reporter [Morgen White](https://favs.news/?s=morgen+white) has been selected for the 2026 European Journalism Institute’s [Professional Journalism Program](https://www.tfasinternational.org/program/european-journalism-institute/), a competitive international program focused on the importance of independent, fact-based journalism as a foundation of liberty and free societies.

Hosted by The Fund for American Studies in partnership with The Media Project and Anglo-American University, EJI will run July 18–26 in Prague and challenge participants to engage with industry professionals to expand their understanding of religion in public life, explore fundamental issues related to journalism ethics and boost reporting skills.

White, a TikTok and YouTube producer for FāVS News, was chosen from a highly competitive pool of applicants.

She graduated from Murrow College at WSU, where she studied religion reporting with FāVS Executive Director Tracy Simmons.

“Morgen is exactly the kind of journalist the field needs right now — curious, ethical and deeply committed to telling stories that matter. I’m incredibly proud of her,” said Simmons.”She excelled at WSU, and I have no doubt she’ll bring that same dedication and rigor to Prague. This recognition is well-deserved.”

For White, who also works at KUOW, the opportunity represents a significant step in her journalism career, combining international reporting perspectives with deeper study of the political and cultural forces shaping global media.

“I have seen firsthand how conversations around faith can either deepen understanding or entrench division. I believe that telling honest fact-based stories plays a significant role in bringing forth understanding. I look forward to strengthening my international awareness, ethical clarity and cultural literacy as I sharpen my journalistic skills,” White said.

The institute’s academic program is designed to engage students in a rigorous and philosophical examination of government, economics, society, culture and conflict management.

In addition to classroom learning, the program emphasizes hands-on journalism training.

Students will earn academic credit upon completion, including European Credit Transfer System hours from Charles University. Founded in 1993, TFAS’s Prague program is part of a broader mission to prepare future leaders through cross-cultural dialogue and academic excellence.
