**Johnny Sequoyah Friedenberg** (born October 25, 2002) [^1] is an American actress, best known for her roles as Bo Adams on the [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC "NBC") television series *[Believe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Believe_\(TV_series\) "Believe (TV series)")* and Audrey on the *[Dexter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dexter_\(TV_series\) "Dexter (TV series)")* revival miniseries *[Dexter: New Blood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dexter:_New_Blood "Dexter: New Blood")*.

## Early life

Sequoyah was born in [Boise, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise,_Idaho "Boise, Idaho"), and spent her first eight years there.

## Career

She began acting at age eight in independent films. In 2013 and 2014, she appeared in *[Ass Backwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ass_Backwards "Ass Backwards")* and *[I Believe in Unicorns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Believe_in_Unicorns "I Believe in Unicorns")*. She starred in *[Among Ravens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Among_Ravens "Among Ravens")*, directed by her father, Russell Friedenberg, and produced by her mother, [Heather Rae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heather_Rae "Heather Rae").[^2]

In 2014, she appeared in the thriller film *Wind Walkers*, also directed by her father.[^3] Her most notable role was Bo Adams in the [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC "NBC") television series *[Believe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Believe_\(TV_series\) "Believe (TV series)")*.[^4] In 2016, she portrayed Taylor Otto in the pilot for the ABC series *[American Housewife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Housewife "American Housewife")*, but was replaced by [Meg Donnelly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Donnelly "Meg Donnelly") before the series was picked up by the network.[^5]

In August 2019, it was announced that Sequoyah had joined the cast of *[Love, Victor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love,_Victor "Love, Victor")*, the [Hulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hulu "Hulu") sequel series to the 2018 film *[Love, Simon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love,_Simon "Love, Simon")*, set to play the role of Mia.[^6] However, later that month, the role was recast with [Rachel Hilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Hilson "Rachel Hilson") due to a decision to take the character in a new creative direction.[^7] In 2021, Sequoyah played Audrey on the *[Dexter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dexter_\(TV_series\) "Dexter (TV series)")* revival miniseries *[Dexter: New Blood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dexter:_New_Blood "Dexter: New Blood")*.
