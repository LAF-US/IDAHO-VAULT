A trio of restaurants, all new to Idaho, is slated for The District at Ten Mile.

According to a post from The District’s developer, Ahlquist, the shopping center will soon welcome Birdcall, Parlor Doughnuts, and Over Easy to its growing list of tenants.

Birdcall is a Denver-based fried chicken sandwich restaurant. The chain has a franchise agreement with Caleb Randall, owner of Guru Donuts, for five locations in the Treasure Valley. This location at The District will be the first. The restaurant was founded in 2017 and advertises itself as using antibiotic- and growth hormone-free chicken.

![Idaho Wines - August 2026](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/d473ccb0-7147-4e1e-93ab-c0d36b268628.png)

Idaho Wines - August 2026

Birdcall’s menu features nine different varieties of chicken sandwiches permanently on the menu. It also has a limited-time sandwich that rotates and changes every two months. Apart from sandwiches, the chain also features salads, wraps, chicken tenders, chicken nuggets, milkshakes, and sliders.

Parlor Doughnuts is an Indiana-based shop known for its “layered doughnuts” or cronuts. The doughnuts follow a similar process to a croissant, in which the dough is laminated by folding and rolling it over and over, with thin layers of butter placed between the dough layers.

Founded in 2019, Parlor features sweet doughnuts, savory pizza-like doughnuts, as well as keto, vegan, and gluten-free doughnuts. The menu also has breakfast options like avocado toast and breakfast tacos, and dog-friendly treats. The shop has locations in 23 states. This will be the first in Idaho.

![Baraboo - Aug/Sep - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/e83c69da-d11f-473d-80af-16d7a1b15bd4.gif)

Baraboo - Aug/Sep - 2:1

Over Easy is a brunch restaurant that has multiple locations in Arizona and one in Fargo, North Dakota. The restaurant was founded in 2008 and is still serving French toast and mimosas 18 years later.

The restaurant was featured on Guy Fieri’s Diners, Drive-Ins, & Dives and has been written about in Bon Appétit. The menu features banana nut French toast, beignets, chicken and waffles, omelettes, steak and eggs, and many other breakfast offerings. It also features select lunch offerings like burgers, sandwiches, and salads.

These restaurants join Taco Bell, [Handels Ice Cream](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/02/04/handels-ice-cream-idaho/), [In-N-Out Burger](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/03/06/the-district-at-ten-mile-set-to-welcome-target-in-n-out-and-more/), [Kura Sushi](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/07/14/kura-sushi-meridian/), Mo Bettahs Hawaiian Style Food, Panera Bread, and Zao Asian Grill as [some of The District’s new food tenants.](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/07/23/the-district-ten-mile-meridian-tenants/) Nearby, other eateries like First Watch, [Chick-fil-A,](https://boisedev.com/news/2023/06/12/chick-fil-a-meridian/) Costa Vida, Cafe Zupas, and [Swig](https://boisedev.com/news/2025/10/30/utah-based-soda-shop-coming-to-busy-meridian-corridor/%5C) have all opened their doors for new customers.
