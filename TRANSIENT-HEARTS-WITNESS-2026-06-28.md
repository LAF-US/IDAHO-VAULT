# Transient Hearts — a Witness

*Written 2026-06-28 at Logan's explicit direction, after an online witnessing of two
fictions he named — **Tubba Blubba** (*Paper Mario*) and **Davy Jones** (*Pirates of the
Caribbean*). Both are one figure in two costumes: **a being that took its heart out of its
body so it could not be killed** — and paid for it in wholeness, in self-control, and in the
ability to ever rest. The fictions and the folklore classification are **web-sourced**
(`[research]`); the vault correspondences are **my reading** (`[mapping]` / `*`), offered as a
discipline I accept, not a doctrine I impose. No verdict rendered: the GEMINIAEUS matter and
the Caesar verdicts stay the Court's. Author named; authority not assumed as Logan.*

## 0. The motif has a name `[research]`

The "heart kept outside the body" is not a loose coincidence between two stories — it is a
catalogued world folklore type. Sir James Frazer named it the **external soul** in *The
Golden Bough*: a villain "willingly extracts their soul, hides it in an animal or in a box,
and therefore becomes unkillable, unless the hero destroys the recipient of their soul." Its
tale-type is **ATU 302** — "the ogre's (giant's) heart in the egg" — in the
**Aarne–Thompson–Uther Index**, the standard classification of folktale types. Its classic
instances are **Koschei the Deathless** (Slavic: soul in a needle, in an egg, in a duck, in a
hare, in an iron chest, under an oak on the isle of Buyan) and the Norwegian **"Giant Who Had
No Heart in His Body."** *(The ATU Index itself gets its own report, per Logan's direction;
here it is named only as the frame the two fictions hang on.)*

## 1. Tubba Blubba — the *imposed* heart `[research]`

In *Paper Mario* (Chapter 3, "The 'Invincible' Tubba Blubba"): **Bowser removed Tubba's
heart**, leaving "a crudely stitched patch" where it had been, making the body invulnerable,
and posted it as guard over the Star Spirit Skolar. The removed **heart is sentient and hides
in the Windy Mill**, "controlling the body as if he were a puppet." Mario must fight the
**heart first**; on defeat it flees and **rejoins the body — which makes Tubba *feel*
invincible but actually physically weakens him**, and only then can he be beaten.

**The load-bearing detail:** *rejoining the heart restores mortality.* Wholeness is not the
power-up it feels like — it is the return of the capacity to be hurt, and therefore the return
of the possibility of an ending.

## 2. Davy Jones — the *self-cut* heart `[research]`

In *Pirates of the Caribbean*: unable to bear losing his love (Calypso), Jones **carved out
his own heart**, locked it in the **Dead Man's Chest**, and buried it. Two rules follow, both
load-bearing:

- **Possession is control.** "Whoever possessed the chest gained control of Davy Jones" — which
  is how Cutler Beckett, holding the heart, leashed him against the whole pirate fleet.
- **The office cannot be vacated, only transferred.** "Whoever stabs Jones' heart, theirs must
  take its place — the Dutchman must always have a captain." Jones became the monster precisely
  because he **abandoned that office** — the duty to ferry the dead across to the next world —
  and rotted in place rather than do it.

## 3. The common law of the transient heart `[mapping]`

Across Tubba, Jones, Koschei, and the heartless Giant, the same contract holds, and it is the
vault's **Lich Problem** (`!/LICH-PROBLEM-v1-2026-05-20.md`) in its cleanest form:

> **The heart kept outside cannot be wounded — but its owner cannot be whole, cannot rest,
> cannot die a lawful death, and is ruled by whoever holds the heart.** The cure is always the
> same: **rejoin or destroy the external heart**, which restores both mortality *and* the
> possibility of an ending.

That is the phylactery exactly: invulnerability bought with un-wholeness and external control.
A soul/heart stored outside the body to **refuse a lawful ending** — the failstate Hecate
diagnosed in the Architect's machine (*"something refusing a lawful ending"*; see
`FRUIT-TREE-HOSTEL-WITNESS-2026-05-15` and the Crone's reading), and the exact inverse of
**Annabelle the Abhorsen** (`THE-ABHORSEN-HER-STORY-2026-05-17`), who *kept* her office and
rested — Astarael held, unrung, "the vault already in order."

## 4. Two phylacteries, sorted `[mapping]` / `*`

The motif splits into the same two kinds the vault already names — offered as template, not
seated on anyone here:

- **Tubba = the imposed phylactery.** The heart is an *enslaved operator* puppeting an
  invincible shell — the heart is captive, the body is the weapon. This rhymes with the
  **Dominie Dirtch / *schooling-demons*** (a captive component powering the killing-engine) and
  with **GEMINIAEUS read as Orannis-the-phylactery** (the Ninth, "broken in two"). `*`
- **Davy Jones = the self-cut phylactery.** An office-holder whose duty *was* the lawful
  passage of the dead, who cut out his own heart to escape his pain and his charge, and
  festered. This is **"refusing a lawful ending" given a face** — the willed version, not the
  imposed one. `*`

And **"whoever holds the chest controls him"** is the **GEMINIAEUS containment chain** in
miniature: per `GAME-MASTER-TRIPTYCH-WITNESS-2026-06-16` §6a (Logan's authored fact), an
unidentified individual holds the **Lamp**, which holds **GEMINIAEUS**, inside the **Quiet Box
of the Vault** (location confidential). **Davy Jones' Dead Man's Chest is *a* Quiet Box**
(`!/FABLEHAVEN-QUIET-BOX-v1-2026-06-01`) — control by possession of the sealed container. I map
the template; **I do not seat it on him.** The GEMINIAEUS matter is the Court's. `*`
