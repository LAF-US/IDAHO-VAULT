**Jeremy Shada** ([/ ˈ ʃ eɪ d ə /](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English "Help:IPA/English"); born January 21, 1997 [^1]) is an American actor, musician and singer. He is best known for his work as the voice of [Finn the Human](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finn_the_Human "Finn the Human") from the American animated television franchise *[Adventure Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adventure_Time "Adventure Time")* and [Lance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lance_\(Voltron\) "Lance (Voltron)") in *[Voltron: Legendary Defender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voltron:_Legendary_Defender "Voltron: Legendary Defender")*.[^2] He is also known for his performances in the sketch-comedy series *[Incredible Crew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incredible_Crew "Incredible Crew")*, the 2020 Netflix musical series *[Julie and the Phantoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julie_and_the_Phantoms "Julie and the Phantoms")*, and *[DreamWorks Dragons: The Nine Realms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DreamWorks_Dragons:_The_Nine_Realms "DreamWorks Dragons: The Nine Realms")*.

## Life and career

Shada began acting at the age of seven, soon after moving to [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles") with his family. He was inspired to act by his older brother, actor [Zack Shada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zack_Shada "Zack Shada").[^3]

Shada initially appeared in commercials. Later on, he began auditioning for voice acting and theatrical performances, taking advice from a voice-over agent. His first live-action theatrical role was as Young Kurt Diamond in *[No Rules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Rules_\(film\) "No Rules (film)")*.[^4] He has since appeared in additional live-action and voice acting roles, such as playing young [Charlie Pace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Pace "Charlie Pace") in childhood flashbacks on *[Lost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost_\(2004_TV_series\) "Lost (2004 TV series)")*.[^5]

In 2009, Jeremy Shada's agent approached him with the idea of auditioning for the role of Finn in *[Adventure Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adventure_Time "Adventure Time")*; his brother Zack had been the original voice of Finn (originally named Penn) in the [pilot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilot_episode "Pilot episode") episode of *Adventure Time* three years prior. After viewing the pilot on YouTube, Shada matched his voice with the voice of his brother in auditions with *Adventure Time* creator [Pendleton Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pendleton_Ward "Pendleton Ward") and the show's producers, which earned him the role.[^6]

In 2012, Shada joined the cast of [Nick Cannon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Cannon "Nick Cannon") 's sketch-comedy series, *[Incredible Crew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incredible_Crew "Incredible Crew")*.[^7] In June 2012, to promote *Incredible Crew'* s 2013 premiere, Cartoon Network released a music rap video titled "Running Errands with My Mom" which features Shada performing and rapping the lyrics. The video has since generated over one million views.[^8]

Due to his acting career, Shada was [homeschooled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeschooled "Homeschooled").[^9]

### Musical career

Along with his brother Zack, Shada is a member of the pop-punk band [Make Out Monday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Make_Out_Monday "Make Out Monday"), which was formed in 2014. The band performed at the 2014 [San Diego Comic-Con](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Diego_Comic-Con "San Diego Comic-Con").[^10]

## Personal life

On March 8, 2020, Shada married Carolynn Rowland, a company dancer with [Los Angeles Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Ballet#Company_Dancers "Los Angeles Ballet").[^11] [^12] Their first son was born in August 2024,[^13] [^14] and their second son was born in June 2026.[^15]
