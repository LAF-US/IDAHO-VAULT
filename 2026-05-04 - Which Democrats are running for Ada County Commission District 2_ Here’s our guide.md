Two Democrats are vying to face off against the Republican incumbent in November for the Ada County Commission District 2 seat.

Otto Rene Gramajo and Michael Fitzgerald are running against each other in the May 19 Democratic primary. Whoever wins moves on to the general election, where they’ll go against Rod Beck, who was first elected in 2020.

The Idaho Statesman sent surveys to each of the candidates, and their responses are presented below, unedited.
