The exclusive annual Allen & Co. conference held at Sun Valley Resort is set to begin today, welcoming top media and technology executives as well as prominent journalists.

The invite-only conference, sometimes called a “summer camp for billionaires,” provides its attendees with opportunities to explore the Sun Valley area through outdoor recreation while networking.

Friedman Memorial Airport Director Tim Burke confirmed that the official fly-in day for the event will be Tuesday, with most attendees departing the Wood River Valley on Friday, July 10, and Saturday, July 11.

![Senior Edge Legal - Jun 2026 - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/8bc5656b-95db-4242-8441-fb6cbfe40e72.png)

Senior Edge Legal - Jun 2026 - 2:1

The event, which has been occurring for decades, typically packs Friedman Memorial Airport with private jets.

While security around the event is tight, agendas are confidential and media access is slim, the conference has served as a major incubator for some of the country’s most prominent deals, including Disney’s merger with ABC in 1995, Jeff Bezos’ acquisition of the Washington Post in 2013 and Verizon’s purchase of AOL in 2014.

Though it’s difficult to pin down the event’s exact economic impact on the Wood River Valley, county economic and tourism experts have said the conference creates more daily economic impact than any other week of the year and drums up media coverage portraying the area as an idyllic retreat, providing free tourism marketing.

## Who to expect around Sun Valley and Ketchum

![Sun Valley Allen & Co.](https://boisedev.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/IMG_2893-2048x1536.jpeg)

The front of the Sun Valley Lodge during the Allen & Co. event in 2019. Photo: Don Day/BoiseDev

While the conference’s guest list is difficult to come by, Variety Magazine reported that it obtained a copy.

Among the tech executives expected to attend the conference are incoming Apple CEO John Ternus and his predecessor, Tim Cook; Amazon Founder Jeff Bezos; Meta CEO and Founder Mark Zuckerberg; Alphabet and Google CEO Sundar Pichai; Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei; OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and President Greg Brockman; Palantir CEO Alex Karp; Microsoft CEO Bill Gates, and more [according to a June 9 article by Variety.](https://boisedev.com/news/2024/05/31/sun-valley-allen-2026-2-2/)

Additionally, media moguls who have been invited to attend the conference include Paramount Skydance Chairman and CEO David Ellison; Disney CEO Josh D’Amaro; Netflix CEOs Ted Sarandos and Greg Peters; YouTube CEO Neal Mohan; and Warner Bros. Discovery President and CEO David Zaslav, who has been in talks to sell his company to Ellison’s Paramount.

![Visit Boise - 2:1 - Easier to brag 1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/7c2cf378-0990-43b2-949c-d6e349b008ba.png)

Visit Boise - 2:1 - Easier to brag 1

Among the invited guests are prominent journalists from across the nation, including CBS Editor-in-Chief Bari Weiss and journalist Gayle King. Variety also reported that CNN anchor Anderson Cooper, Fox News anchor Bret Baier, and New York Times Columnist/Editor and CNBC anchor Andrew Ross Sorkin have been invited to attend the event.

People missing from the guest list shared by Variety who have historically attended the conference include former Berkshire Hathaway CEO Warren Buffett; former Paramount Global President Shari Redstone; former adviser to President Donald Trump and Tesla/SpaceX CEO Elon Musk; Oprah Winfrey; and members of the Trump family, including Eric, Ivanka and Donald Trump Jr.

Last year, Gov. Brad Little was not on the [list leaked to Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/david-ellison-bari-weiss-josh-damaro-sun-valley-2026-1236770724/), but Little did attend the event, BoiseDev confirmed with sources familiar with his schedule. Little alluded to his attendance during another event at the resort this spring. Little again does not appear on Variety’s list — but the list includes names of those who may not attend and leaves out names of those who ultimately do show up.

## Protests scheduled

![Sun Valley Allen 2022](https://boisedev.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/tim-cook.png)

Apple CEO Tim Cook attends the Allen & Company Sun Valley Conference on July 08, 2021 in Sun Valley, Idaho. Photo:Kevin Dietsch/Getty Images

Conference guests are again set to be greeted by protests organized by area residents.

Hailey resident and activist Katja Burmester in a Monday email shared information about rallies scheduled in the area she said are “focused on economic fairness, corporate accountability, and the growing gap between extraordinary wealth and the financial challenges facing many working families.”

![BoiseDev House - OTT - Commercial](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/8b916803-64e1-4279-a378-7e47d28a2871.png)

BoiseDev House - OTT - Commercial

Hailey resident Cliff Cunha has organized protests on Sunday, July 5, and Monday, July 6. Attendees will gather at the intersection of McKercher Boulevard and Main Street in Hailey on both days from 7:30 a.m. to 9 a.m. and from 4:30 p.m. to 6 p.m.

A flyer shared with the Express by Cunha referred to conference attendees as “oligarchs” and stated, “Billionaires shouldn’t exist. Democracy should.”

“Protesters will demonstrate to residents and workers that that there is a strong and viable resistance to those who threaten our rights, security and existence,” the flyer stated.

![BoiseDev House - OTT - Commercial](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/8b916803-64e1-4279-a378-7e47d28a2871.png)

BoiseDev House - OTT - Commercial

The Sun Valley chapter of Democratic Socialists of America has organized a protest Saturday, July 11, in Ketchum Town Square.

“Our message is that billionaires and oligarchs are the enemy, that the Wood River Valley is class-conscious, and that, when we organize, unionize, and can effectively fight oligarchy in this country, we have power,” the organization stated in an event description.

Speakers will address the crowd in Town Square starting at 10 a.m. before a march.
