**Billie Bird Sellen** (February 28, 1908 – November 27, 2002), better known professionally as **Billie Bird**, was an American character actress and [comedian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comedian "Comedian").[^1] She played Margie in *[Dear John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dear_John_\(American_TV_series\) "Dear John (American TV series)")* (1988–1992).

## Early life

Born in [Pocatello, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pocatello,_Idaho "Pocatello, Idaho"),[^2] [^3] Bird was discovered at the age of eight while living at an orphanage. As a child, she worked in [vaudeville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaudeville "Vaudeville"), including an act with the [King Sisters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Sisters "King Sisters"),[^4] and later in [theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theater "Theater") / [cabaret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabaret "Cabaret") before moving on to [television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television "Television") and [films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film "Film").[^5]

## Career

She is credited with an appearance in a 1921 film *Grass Widowers*, but it is not clear if this is accurate. Otherwise, she broke into films in 1950, later making a brief, uncredited appearance in *[The Odd Couple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Odd_Couple_\(film\) "The Odd Couple (film)")* as a [chambermaid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maid "Maid"). Her only line was "Goodnight", which was said to Felix Ungar, who responded, "Goodbye." Bird often was cast by director [John Hughes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hughes_\(filmmaker\) "John Hughes (filmmaker)") and appeared in many of his 1980 and 1990s films, such as *[Sixteen Candles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sixteen_Candles "Sixteen Candles")* (1984), *[Home Alone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_Alone "Home Alone")* (1990), and *[Dennis the Menace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dennis_the_Menace_\(1993_film\) "Dennis the Menace (1993 film)")* (1993), the latter two of which both paired her with veteran Hughes actor [Bill Erwin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Erwin "Bill Erwin") playing her husband. She also starred as Mrs. Lois Feldman in *[Police Academy 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_Academy_4:_Citizens_on_Patrol "Police Academy 4: Citizens on Patrol")* (1987) and Mrs. Stanwyck in *[Police Academy 6: City Under Siege](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_Academy_6:_City_Under_Siege "Police Academy 6: City Under Siege")* (1989). Her last film appearance was in 1995's *[Jury Duty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jury_Duty_\(film\) "Jury Duty (film)")*, with [Pauly Shore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pauly_Shore "Pauly Shore").

In addition to films, Bird made numerous appearances on various television series, including roles on *[The Waltons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Waltons "The Waltons")*, *[Eight Is Enough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eight_Is_Enough "Eight Is Enough")*, *[Happy Days](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Happy_Days "Happy Days")*, *[Silver Spoons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_Spoons "Silver Spoons")*, *[Ironside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ironside_\(1967_TV_series\) "Ironside (1967 TV series)")*, *[The Facts of Life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Facts_of_Life_\(TV_series\) "The Facts of Life (TV series)")*, *[Who's the Boss?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Who's_the_Boss%3F "Who's the Boss?")*, *[Knots Landing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knots_Landing "Knots Landing")*, and *[The Wonder Years](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wonder_Years "The Wonder Years")*. She was also a regular cast member on the sitcoms *[It Takes Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/It_Takes_Two_\(U.S._TV_series\) "It Takes Two (U.S. TV series)")* in 1982, *[Benson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benson_\(TV_series\) "Benson (TV series)")* from 1984 to 1986, and in 1988 *[Dear John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dear_John_\(U.S._TV_series\) "Dear John (U.S. TV series)")* starring [Judd Hirsch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judd_Hirsch "Judd Hirsch"). Her last acting role was a brief appearance in 1997 on an episode of the sitcom *[George and Leo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_and_Leo "George and Leo")*, which starred [Bob Newhart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Newhart "Bob Newhart") and Bird's former co-star, Hirsch.

Bird entertained troops in Vietnam with her own variety act, "Flying High With Billie Bird." For her efforts, she was one of the few women to ever be made an honorary member of the Green Berets.[^4]

## Death

Bird died on November 27, 2002, in [Granada Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granada_Hills,_Los_Angeles "Granada Hills, Los Angeles"), [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California "California"), at the age of 94 after several years of [Alzheimer's disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alzheimer's_disease "Alzheimer's disease").[^5]
