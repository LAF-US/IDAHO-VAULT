**Drew Jacoby** (born September 2, 1984) is an American contemporary ballet dancer. As of 2020, she is a principal dancer of [Royal Ballet of Flanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Ballet_of_Flanders "Royal Ballet of Flanders").[^1]

## Early life

Jacoby was born in [Boise, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise,_Idaho "Boise, Idaho") in 1984. She trained at the [School of American Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_of_American_Ballet "School of American Ballet"), the [San Francisco Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_Ballet "San Francisco Ballet") and [Pacific Northwest Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Northwest_Ballet "Pacific Northwest Ballet").[^1]

## Career

In 2002, at age 17, Jacoby joined [Alonzo King LINES Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alonzo_King_LINES_Ballet "Alonzo King LINES Ballet") in San Francisco, at the invitation of [Alonzo King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alonzo_King "Alonzo King").[^2] While at the Lines Ballet, Jacoby became a principal dancer and had numerous original works created on her by King.[^3] In 2005, she joined [Sylvie Guillem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvie_Guillem "Sylvie Guillem") 's Japan tour, which she danced with dancers from [The Royal Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Royal_Ballet "The Royal Ballet") and [Paris Opera Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Opera_Ballet "Paris Opera Ballet").[^4]

In 2007 she moved to New York City to begin her freelance career,[^5] and market herself independently from a ballet company.[^6] In 2008, she formed an independent dance duo Jacoby & Pronk with [Complexions Contemporary Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complexions_Contemporary_Ballet "Complexions Contemporary Ballet") dancer [Rubinald Pronk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rubinald_Pronk "Rubinald Pronk"). The dual has danced with [Dutch National Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_National_Ballet "Dutch National Ballet") and [Morphoses/The Wheeldon Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morphoses/The_Wheeldon_Company "Morphoses/The Wheeldon Company").[^4] [^7] In 2010, Jacoby performed alongside [David Hallberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Hallberg "David Hallberg"), premiering Lauri Stalling's *Both*.[^8]

Jacoby danced with [Nederlands Dans Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nederlands_Dans_Theater "Nederlands Dans Theater") from 2012 to 2015.[^4] Her partnership with Pronk therefore ended.[^9] She joined the [Royal Ballet of Flanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Ballet_of_Flanders "Royal Ballet of Flanders") as a principal dancer in 2015, where she danced works by [Pina Bausch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pina_Bausch "Pina Bausch"), [William Forsythe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Forsythe_\(choreographer\) "William Forsythe (choreographer)") and [Maurice Béjart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_B%C3%A9jart "Maurice Béjart"), and worked with choreographers such as [Sidi Larbi Cherkaoui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidi_Larbi_Cherkaoui "Sidi Larbi Cherkaoui") and [Jonah Bokaer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonah_Bokaer "Jonah Bokaer").[^1] [^4] In 2019, Jacoby returned to the US to perform at [Maria Kochetkova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Kochetkova "Maria Kochetkova") 's solo program, *Catch Her If You Can* at the [Joyce Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joyce_Theater "Joyce Theater"). The program also featured works choreographed by Jacoby, danced by herself and Kochetkova.[^10]

## Selected repertory

Jacoby's repertory with the Royal Ballet of Flanders includes:[^1]

- *Café Müller* (Pina Bausch)
- *Approximate Sonata* (William Forsythe)
- *The Sleeping Beauty* (Marcia Haydée after Marius Petipa): Lilac Fairy
- *Boléro* (Maurice Béjart): Melody

### Created roles

- *Requiem* (Sidi Larbi Cherkaoui)
- *The Nutcracker* (Demis Volpi): Cake Aunt
- *Shahrazad* (Jonah Bokaer)
- *Exhibition* (Larbi Cherkaoui): Lady in the Long Dress
- *Ma Mère l’Oye* (Jeroen Verbruggen): Alma
- *Fall* (Sidi Larbi Cherkaoui)

## Film

Jacoby has appeared in several films and videos, including the short film *I Will Fall For You*, directed by [Sidi Larbi Cherkaoui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidi_Larbi_Cherkaoui "Sidi Larbi Cherkaoui") and [Woodkid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoann_Lemoine "Yoann Lemoine").[^11] [^12] Jacoby portrayed American dancer and theatrical pioneer [Loie Fuller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loie_Fuller "Loie Fuller") in the 2019 film *[Radioactive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radioactive_\(film\) "Radioactive (film)")*, starring [Rosamund Pike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosamund_Pike "Rosamund Pike") and [Sam Riley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Riley "Sam Riley").[^13] [^14]
