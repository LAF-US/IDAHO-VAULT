BOISE, Idaho — Dorothy Moon, who has led the Idaho Republican Party for four years, on Monday announced her bid for a third term in pursuit of re-election.

Moon, who served three terms as a member of the Idaho House of Representatives, has been the chairwoman since 2022.

"Together, we have worked to keep Idaho the conservative leader of the nation, defend election integrity, support our county parties and grassroots volunteers, and elect Republicans who stand firmly for the values of faith, family, freedom, and limited government," Moon said in the June 1 announcement.

Under Moon's leadership, Idaho's GOP in the summer of 2022 adopted [a new party platform](https://www.ktvb.com/article/news/local/208/gop-committee-says-idaho-representative-cannot-run-as-republican/277-b01dc5dc-fd00-4a47-b414-ceef5a245748), describing it as a return to conservative principles. They also approved their state party rules, which have undergone several amendments since then.

[Article XX](https://idgop.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2025/06/2025-June-State-Party-Rules-final.pdf) of the Idaho Republican Party Rules has been a staple since the summer of 2023. The article allows the county, central and legislative district committees to censure a lawmaker for not following the platform closely enough.

The change in the party platform didn't come without pushback – Rep. Stephanie Mickelsen, who sat down with KTVB's Brian Holmes in 2024, said she had been censured. They have not only censured a sitting lawmaker, but also voted to remove party support [from Mickelsen](https://www.ktvb.com/article/news/local/208/gop-committee-says-idaho-representative-cannot-run-as-republican/277-b01dc5dc-fd00-4a47-b414-ceef5a245748) and prohibit her from running as a Republican.

**RELATED: [GOP committee votes, says Idaho Representative cannot run as Republican](https://www.ktvb.com/article/news/local/208/gop-committee-says-idaho-representative-cannot-run-as-republican/277-b01dc5dc-fd00-4a47-b414-ceef5a245748)**

"I think we just need to make sure that the party reflects the people instead of a faction of the party, I think that's been kind of the problem," Mickelsen said in response to the news in 2024.

Moon's candidacy announcement comes ahead of the 2026 Idaho GOP State Convention, scheduled for [June 18–20](https://idgop.org/event/2026-idaho-gop-state-convention/) in Meridian. During the convention, Republican delegates will be able to submit two proposals for changes in its rules, regulations and the platform.

Moon said she is seeking another term to continue building the party and supporting Republican candidates across Idaho.

She stated that her leadership contributed to higher volunteer engagement, improved county party coordination, backing Republican victories and the effort to kill the [ranked-choice voting initiative in 2024](https://www.ktvb.com/article/news/politics/elections/election-day-idaho-prop-1-open-primaries-ranked-choice-voting/277-497f9442-0c3e-4492-8966-32a5789ef6d1).

Moon said party unity will be especially important heading into the 2026 election cycle.

It's unclear if she faces any opposition.
