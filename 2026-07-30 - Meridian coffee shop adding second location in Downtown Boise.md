Just months after opening its doors, Mindful Coffee is already preparing to expand.

The Meridian-based coffee shop, which celebrated its grand opening in March 2026, is planning to open a second location in Downtown Boise at 1202 W. Front Street in the Saratoga building.

Mindful Coffee specializes in gluten-free food and beverages made with organic ingredients and bakes its pastries in-house. Its menu features a variety of grab-and-go bites, signature beverages, classic and specialty coffee drinks, teas, matcha, kombucha, cold brew, and more.

![SERVPRO Boise - Jan25](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/5b2a85ae-933e-4c98-82a5-e207d65304e9.png)

SERVPRO Boise - Jan25

The original Mindful Coffee location debuted in March 2026, [as BoiseDev reported.](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/02/12/the-treasure-valley-is-gaining-another-coffee-shop-very-soon/)

“The goal was to be able to bring Mindful all over the Treasure Valley,” owner Brooke Ochojski said. “But we obviously had to start somewhere, which is why we opened our first location in Meridian.”

![](https://boisedev.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/meridian-coffee-shop-is-gaining-a-second-location-in-downtown-boise-504A8163-1-Large.jpeg)

Mindful Coffee’s first location in Meridian. Photo: Courtesy Brooke Ochojski/ Mindful Coffee

Ochojski said she had no intention of opening a second location this quickly, but because of how well it was received, the shop did well from the very start, and that’s what led to adding the second location.

![Regence - 2:1 - Business Grow](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/6f251bf1-00e6-48d7-8365-570820fe299b.gif)

Regence - 2:1 - Business Grow

The new location is going to have its own vibe, Ochojski said, but still be inspired by the original location.

“Each location I want to do something a little bit different aesthetically,” Ochojski said. “Just to give each area its own vibe, but it will definitely have a similar feel to it.”

Ochojski said the menu will stay consistent with the Meridian location.

![ICCU - May 2026 - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/fb80406e-2f0f-4291-9c09-5bc7bec024d5.png)

ICCU - May 2026 - 2:1

The new location of Mindful Coffee will be open Monday to Friday from 7 a.m. to 4 p.m., and Saturday and Sunday from 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. Ochojski said she will adjust the hours accordingly based on supply and demand in the area.

No exact opening date is set for the shop; however, Ochojski said she expects to be open and operating in late Fall 2026.

According to the Downtown Boise Association’s listing, there are more than 22 coffee and tea shops in the downtown area.
