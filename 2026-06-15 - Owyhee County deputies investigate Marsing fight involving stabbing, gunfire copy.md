The Owyhee County Sheriff’s Office is investigating a fight in Marsing that left people with stab wounds and involved gunfire.

Deputies responded to reports of a physical altercation on the night of Saturday, June 13. When they arrived, they determined that a fight had occurred. During the altercation, deputies say individuals suffered stab wounds, and a person involved in the dispute fired shots.

Preliminary findings indicate the incident is gang-related. The Sheriff’s Office said investigators believe the incident was a targeted, isolated event and does not pose an ongoing safety risk to the general public.

Investigators continue to actively investigate the case. The Sheriff’s Office is asking the community for help gathering more information about what led up to the violence.

Anyone who witnessed the incident or has video footage or other relevant information is encouraged to contact the Owyhee County Sheriff’s Office non-emergency line at 208-495-1154.
