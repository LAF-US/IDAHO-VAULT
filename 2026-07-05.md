 > Gemini: A **Swainson's hawk** (*Buteo swainsoni*) seems highly consistent with a Boise, Idaho location on July 5. This species is a common summer breeding resident in open country and agricultural areas throughout southern Idaho during this time of year. 119642_2.jpg and 119640_2.jpg show the key structural detail of the wingtips reaching the very tip of the tail. This long-winged silhouette is characteristic of a perched Swainson's hawk, whereas a Red-tailed hawk's wings typically fall noticeably short of the tail tip. The dark plumage across the head and breast suggests an intermediate or dark-morph individual. 119639.jpg and 119641.jpg shows the raptor's size relative to the utility infrastructure. The presence of the smaller birds on the wire nearby is a common occurrence, as local passerines often monitor perched predators. The structural proportions and the specific summer timing in Idaho strongly support the identification of a Swainson's hawk.*
