## Working Notes

- Each book form solves three linked problems: writing surface, sequence, and
  transport/storage.
- Clay tablets solve inscription and durability, not portability.
- Scrolls solve sequence and portability, but make random access difficult.
- The codex solves random access, two-sided use, and multi-text aggregation.
- Binding evolves from sewing support to industrial casing and adhesive
  binding.
- Digital books move "binding" into packaging, metadata, manifest, spine, and
  rendering rules.
