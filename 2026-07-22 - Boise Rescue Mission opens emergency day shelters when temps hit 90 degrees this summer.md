BOISE, [[Idaho]] (CBS2) — Boise Rescue Mission Ministries is providing emergency day shelters on extremely hot days throughout the summer.

In a recent news release, the Mission states:

> As temperatures continue to stretch into the triple digits and air quality conditions grow worse throughout the Treasure Valley, those experiencing homelessness in our community are at severe risk. Many of them have no reliable space to stay cool, rested, and hydrated. These high temperatures can often lead to serious heat-related illnesses, including heat exhaustion, dehydration, and heat stroke. For young children, elderly people, or those with health conditions, this heat can be dangerous.

> Our homeless neighbors need a place to be safe, and Boise Rescue Mission Ministries is here to serve that need.

The Mission encourages anyone, even those who are not homeless, to come inside for a drink of water, a meal and a safe, cool place to rest.

Everyone is encouraged to come inside for a drink of water, a nutritious meal and a safe, cool place to rest.

**Boise**

[River of Life Men's Shelter](https://boiserm.org/facilities/river-of-life/?bblinkid=290029195&bbemailid=62907621&bbejrid=-875402457): 575 S. 13th St. - (208)389-9840

- Men, women and children are welcome in the shelter throughout the day when temperatures reach 90 degrees.
- Cool water and sunscreen are available.

[City Light Home for Women & Children](https://boiserm.org/facilities/city-light/?bblinkid=290029194&bbemailid=62907621&bbejrid=-875402457): 1404 W. Jefferson St. - (208)368-9901

- Women and children are welcome when temperatures reach 90 degrees by noon. Check-in times are 12 p.m., 1 p.m., and 4 p.m.
- Cold water, sack lunches, and sunscreen are available.

**Nampa**

[Valley Women and Children's Shelter](https://boiserm.org/facilities/valley/?bblinkid=290029196&bbemailid=62907621&bbejrid=-875402457): 869 W. Corporate Ln. - (208)475-0725

- Women and children are welcome when temperatures reach 90 degrees by noon. Check-in times are 12 p.m., 1 p.m., and 4 p.m.
- Cold water, sack lunches and sunscreen are available to guests after check-in.
