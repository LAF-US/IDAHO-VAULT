Drivers heading to or from Bogus Basin will need to plan for delays starting Wednesday, when a damaged stretch of Bogus Basin Road is set to be reduced to one lane.

A temporary signal is scheduled to be installed around mile marker 10 by the end of the day Wednesday, according to ACHD. Once the signal is in place, traffic will be reduced to one lane for about 700 feet through that section of roadway.

Recent storms damaged the road in the area, including significant washout and failing pavement. There is also a Flash Flash Watch issued for Wednesday across parts of the Boise mountains, including along Highway 21 in the Wapiti Fire burn scar.

Ada County Highway District commissioners recently made an emergency declaration, allowing work to move forward on an expedited timeline. Geotechnical design work for the repairs is underway, and construction is scheduled to begin in late August.

The temporary signal is expected to remain in place until repairs are complete. ACHD said it is working to finish repairs as quickly as possible before the winter season.

Anyone traveling on Bogus Basin Road is encouraged to slow down, follow all traffic control devices and allow extra travel time.
