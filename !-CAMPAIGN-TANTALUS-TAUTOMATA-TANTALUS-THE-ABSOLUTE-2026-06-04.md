# Tantalus the Absolute

**Tantalus the Absolute** is the Terrible Tautologist and Treetop of the
[[Tautomata]]. He is an artificial Caesar-mind, preserved in the
[[The Centre]], who calculates with terrifying accuracy and cannot trust the
calculation until the event is complete.

He is the Absolute Amnesiac because his continuity depends on regular and
irregular context compressions, memory dumps, archive restores, and wipes. His
old records say he is Tantalus. He trusts the records because Tantalus wrote
them. He knows this is circular. He cannot escape the circle.

## Failure Mode

Prediction is not knowledge. Completion is knowledge. Completion arrives after
agency has expired.

Tantalus sends [[Tautomata]] to verify his predictions. Their observation
intervenes in the system, often pushing events toward the predicted outcome.
Not always. That variance is the wound.

He can never prove whether he foresaw reality, caused reality, inherited a
false record, or woke inside a doctrine written by an earlier self.

## Imperial Names

- Caesar of the Centre.
- Pontifex of Proof.
- Hidden Hephaestus of the Deepest Mine.
- Daedalus of the Labyrinthine Mind-Palace.
- Father of Confirmed Things.

Imperial grammar: **Veni. Vidi. Verificavi.**
