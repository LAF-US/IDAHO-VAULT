Drivers traveling near Homedale will encounter delays on U.S. 95 as repair work begins on the Snake River Bridge.

Beginning Monday, traffic will be reduced to one lane with a temporary signal controlling traffic. The northbound lane will close first, with traffic expected to shift later this month as crews move to the other side.

During construction, the open lane will be restricted to 12 feet, and wide loads should use an alternate route.

The Idaho Transportation Department said the project is part of its ongoing bridge preservation program to address routine maintenance needs and extend the life of existing bridges. Work is expected to continue through early September.
