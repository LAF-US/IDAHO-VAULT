# VAULTED SYNTAX

Vaulted Syntax is the operational language layer of Logan's Project &
Protocols: the discipline by which names, classifications, standing,
corrections, and witnessed authority remain precise as work passes between
human direction, doctrine, registry, protocol, runtime, and artifact.

**The core principle:** A symbol is architecture when removing it changes the
structure, not just the label. The vault's symbols — articles, sigils,
separators, wildcards, address coordinates — are operational diagrams, not
decorative garnish. They imply behavior, route, standing, and depth. They do
not describe the structure; in many cases, they ARE the structure.

## Correction Syntax

The correction classification doctrine is defined in `CORRECTIONS.md`.

- A **Typographical Error / Typo** is an ordinary mistake in written form.
- A **Scrivener's Correction** repairs a witnessed transmission, copying,
  drafting, or inscription defect without creating a new rule.
- A **Codifier's Correction** reconciles and annotates an accumulated body
  while preserving provenance and promotion boundaries.

These classes must not be collapsed. Correction work names what happened and
what authority a repair does, or does not, exercise.

## Boundary

A case-specific review packet may apply Vaulted Syntax doctrine, but it does
not become that doctrine merely by surviving in the vault. Promotion into
governing surfaces remains witnessed and recorded through Logan's authority.
