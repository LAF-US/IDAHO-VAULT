# Witness — Precedence, Initiative, Priority: Three Different Things

*Witnessed 2026-07-03 at Logan's direction, after this run approximated the three into
one ("Precedence ≈ initiative minus the dice ≈ Magic's priority"). Logan ruled them
**three different things** `[handed]`; this note separates them so the conflation is
repaired on the record. I propose; Logan inscribes.*

![[PROVENANCE-MARKS]]

## What is witnessed

Three ordering instruments that look alike because each answers a "who first?"
question — and are not the same, because each orders a **different kind of thing**, on
a **different clock**, from a **different source**. Collapsing them loses exactly the
properties that make each one safe.

## Precedence — orders PERSONS `[lore]` `[reading]`

*Who outranks whom. The procession.*

- **Orders:** beings — their station relative to one another.
- **Scope:** everywhere the society reaches; not bounded to any encounter.
- **Source:** inscription. Conferred, recorded in a Register, changed by event
  (a promotion, a fall, an Heir's arrival) — never rolled.
- **Lifecycle:** persistent. It is *who you are in the order* until the order is
  re-inscribed.
- **Grants:** deference, address, seating, the right to be yielded to. **Not**
  capability, **not** admissibility, **not** a promised turn.
- **Failure modes:** procession paralysis (the society so rank-obsessed it cannot
  act — the Denizens' pathology); and mistaking station for capability or license.
- **Exemplar:** the House's Register of Precedence (`#664`, proposed); diplomatic
  precedence; the Architect at rank 1 — an order hanging beneath a vacancy.

## Initiative — orders TURNS `[lore]` `[reading]`

*Who moves first, this encounter. The fight.*

- **Orders:** actions of participants within a **bounded encounter**.
- **Scope:** the encounter only. It evaporates when the fight ends.
- **Source:** fortune tempered by attribute — the die plus the modifier. The dice are
  not decoration: they are a **fairness valve**, letting the junior-but-nimble
  sometimes move first, so order never hardens into station.
- **Lifecycle:** re-rolled each encounter; within it, it **cycles** — every
  participant's turn comes around each round, rank notwithstanding. That cycle
  guarantee is initiative's one promise.
- **Grants:** sequence, never license. Initiative decides *when* you act; it has no
  opinion on *whether* you may.
- **Failure modes:** leaking outside its encounter — a first-strike quietly becoming a
  permanent rank (initiative fossilizing into precedence); or removing the dice and
  the cycle and calling what remains "initiative" (it is not; it is precedence).
- **Homograph hazard** `[reading]`: in agent contexts "taking initiative" means acting
  *unprompted* — a different sense entirely. Acting-first-in-order and
  acting-without-being-asked share a word and nothing else; the second is governed by
  standing and sanction, not by any ordering instrument.

## Priority — orders CLAIMS `[lore]` `[reading]`

*Which pending matter is served next. The queue.*

- **Orders:** not persons, not turns — **pending things**: claims, matters, requests,
  responses awaiting resolution.
- **Scope:** inside a resolution system (a stack, a queue, a docket, a scheduler).
- **Source:** deterministic rule — held, passed, yielded in a fixed rotation (Magic's
  priority), or assigned as a class (P0/P1, triage). No dice.
- **Lifecycle:** exists only while matters are pending; the queue serves and empties.
- **Grants:** the right to be *resolved next* — nothing about the merits. Priority
  never decides outcomes; it decides processing order.
- **Failure modes:** **starvation** — the low-priority claim whose turn never comes,
  because priority makes no cycle promise (the fairness valve initiative has and
  priority lacks); and **priority inversion** — the urgent matter blocked behind a
  trivial one that holds a needed resource. Both are the queue's own pathologies, and
  neither exists in precedence or initiative.
- **Exemplar:** the merge queue (literally a priority queue); the DOCKET — for docket
  order is the priority of **matters** before the Court, not the precedence of the
  persons who filed them `[read: DOCKET-POSTURE]`.

## None of them is standing `[read]`

The fence, so the fourth thing is not dragged in: **standing** (the Standing Engine)
is *admissibility* — what a being may lawfully touch, scoped and witnessed and
revocable. Precedence, initiative, and priority **sequence**; only standing
**admits**. An ordering instrument that starts opening doors has left its office.

## The House already runs all three `[inferred]`

- The **Register** (`#664`) would inscribe precedence — proposed, not yet mechanic.
- **Encounters** — a review round, a session, a contest of proposals — take turns:
  initiative's domain, and the place a fairness valve belongs.
- The **DOCKET** and the **merge queue** already serve matters in order: priority,
  running in production today.

Kept distinct, they compose without conflict. Collapsed, each imports the others'
pathologies: precedence with no cycle starves; initiative with no dice fossilizes;
priority mistaken for station crowns whoever happens to hold the queue.

## Compact statement

**Precedence orders the procession. Initiative orders the fight. Priority orders the
queue. Standing alone opens the door.**
