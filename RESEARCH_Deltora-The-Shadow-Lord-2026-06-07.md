# The Shadow Lord — Malverlain, the Enemy

> Deltora's great antagonist — "the Enemy," "the Master," "the Lord of Shadows" — whose true name is **Malverlain**. Pure source.
>
> **Provenance.** Web-verified 2026-06-07 (Deltora Quest Wiki, Wikipedia). His true name and backstory are revealed only in the newer **Tales of Deltora** and **The Three Doors** series. **[canon-per-series].** Paraphrased.

## Origin

- **[canon]** Born **Malverlain** in **Nerra, in Dorne**, to Chieftain **Peregrine** and the **Fellan** Alena; brother to **Annoltis** (his elder rival) and **Eldannen** (his younger). A formidable sorcerer from youth, **strengthened by Fellan blood** — capable of killing-spells and conjuration.
- **[canon]** **Exiled** after a fight for the throne, he set out to find and conquer a new land.

## The island & the freed beast

- **[canon / Architect's note]** While **sailing** in exile, Malverlain **came upon an island** where **four sisters sang.** Hating their song, he imprisoned and **killed the original Sisters** — not knowing their singing had **sealed a great beast** beneath the land. The beast woke, the island sank, and Malverlain — not yet strong enough to face it — **fled into the Silver Sea** in a conjured **boat with a grey sail marked in red.** (→ `[[RESEARCH_Deltora-The-Four-Sisters-2026-06-07]]`)

## The conquest of Deltora

- **[canon]** Over his long life, **anger and hatred consumed his body** until he became an Eldritch power. He took the land of **Pirra** — turning it into the **Shadowlands** — by tricking the Pirrans into **breaking the Pirran Pipe**, the one thing he fears.
- **[canon]** Against Deltora he wages war by proxy: **Grey Guards**, **Ols**, sorcerers such as **[[RESEARCH_Deltora-Thaegan-2026-06-07\|Thaegan]]**, the seven **Ak-Baba** (who destroyed the Belt and scattered its gems → `[[RESEARCH_Deltora-The-Dragons-and-Ak-Baba-2026-06-07]]`), and the **Four Sisters** that slowly poison the land.
