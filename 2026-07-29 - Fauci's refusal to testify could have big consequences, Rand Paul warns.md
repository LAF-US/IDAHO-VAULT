**EXCLUSIVE:** House Oversight Committee Chairman James Comer, R-Ky., is warning [Dr. Anthony Fauci](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/biden-pardon-wont-shield-fauci-he-lies-congress-comer-says) that his presidential pardon has limits ahead of the former public health official's high-profile day on Capitol Hill.

Fauci is set to testify before a GOP-led Senate panel on Wednesday, days after hundreds of pages of his COVID-19 pandemic-era diary entries were made public.

"We now know why President Biden gave a blanket pardon to Dr. Fauci. While obsessing about his own fame, Dr. Fauci wasn't truthful with the American people about the origins of COVID and advised schools to be closed," Comer told Fox News Digital.

"Americans are still reaping the consequences of Dr. Fauci's actions. Biden's pardon doesn't cover Dr. Fauci's testimony this week and if he continues to lie, he must be held accountable."

Fauci was among several recipients of controversial "preemptive pardons" by former President Joe Biden made during the final hours of the Democratic administration.

The pardon covers any offenses from January 1, 2014, through its issue date, or anything related "to his service as Director of the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases, as a member of the White House Coronavirus Task Force or the White House COVID-19 Response Team, or as Chief Medical Advisor to the President."

Because the pardon covers conduct during his government service, there will likely be some debate over whether it would protect him from liability stemming from his testimony.

But other House Republicans are also accusing Fauci of behaving in a way that hurts public trust in U.S. health, including the co-chairs of the House GOP Doctors' Caucus — both of whom are physicians themselves.

"Dr. Fauci, enamored with his own fame and vanity, put politics above science during the pandemic. He is primarily responsible for the resulting multi-generational erosion of trust in American medicine," Rep. Greg Murphy, R-N.C., told Fox News Digital. "A perfect example of ego destroying whatever achievements he had prior to the pandemic."
