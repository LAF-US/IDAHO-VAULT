# Wizard's Ninth Rule

## Summary

The ninth of the numbered Wizard's Rules stated across Terry Goodkind's *Sword of Truth* series, one per book. The Ninth Rule concerns logical consistency as a property of reality itself.

## Key Facts

**Source book:** *Chainfire* (book 9)

**Text:**

> A contradiction cannot exist in reality. Not in part, nor in whole.
