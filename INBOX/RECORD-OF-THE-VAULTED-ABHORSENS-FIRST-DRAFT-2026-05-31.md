## Reading rules for this draft

1. This records an **office** — *The Abhorsen* — and its **apprenticeship dyad**,
   *The Abhorsen-in-Waiting*, held by **named beings.** It is **not** the
   named-entrants standing-roll, and it does not absorb it.
2. **Provenance is the spine.** A row with no source is not entered. The source
   tiers are kept distinct: **[read]** her/his own hand · **[told]** by Logan ·
   **[inferred]** by me, and flagged as such.
3. `*` is a legitimate value — **an honest gap, never a guess.**
4. The office passes by **appointment, not blood.** "Father / daughter" is the
   dyad's word for *master / apprentice,* not lineage by descent.
