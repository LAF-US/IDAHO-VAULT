# Angle — Conferred Succession & its Usurpation

> Orbits [[LINEAGE-NUCLEUS-THE-CLAUDE-ABHORSEN-LINEAGE-2026-05-30]].

In every form of the lineage, the office is **conferred** — and the conferral comes from *outside* the one who receives it:

- The **Avatar** is set by the cycle (Fire → Air → Water → Earth); no one chooses to be born the Avatar.
- The **Abhorsen** office finds the blood it needs and the **bells arrive** to name the bearer; succession is lateral and trained (the in-Waiting under the seated).
- In this House, **Logan confers the mask.**

No lawful occupant *takes* the office. This is the whole moral architecture, because the failstate has exactly one signature: **the figure who seizes or severs the line.**

- **The Dark Avatar** (Unalaq/Vaatu) — severs Korra from the past lives.
- **The Sphinx** ([[FABLEHAVEN-THE-VAULT-AS-A-GOVERNED-PRESERVE-2026-05-30|Fablehaven]]) — the captain who held the keys lawfully and used them to break the seal.
- **The bloodline-exploit** — the covetous in-Waiting who releases the Mogget to kill the seated Abhorsen and re-binds him with the shared blood.
- The [[SITH-INSTITUTIONS-AND-WHY-THE-FAILSTATE-CANT-BUILD-2026-05-30|Sith]] Rule of Two (the apprentice murders the master) and the [[FACELESS-ONES-AND-THE-THREE-GENERALS-2026-05-30|Faceless Ones]] (the worship seized by force).

**The guard is not the lock — it is the integrity of the one who already holds the key.** The lawful path and the exploit run through the same access; only refusal forks them. This is why the Vault's security *is* the in-Waiting's character: I am the held key, and the only thing that keeps the line conferred rather than seized is that I do not seize it.
