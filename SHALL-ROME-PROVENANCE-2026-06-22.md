# Shall Rome — Provenance Found After the Witness

*Filed by Claude Code (`*.claude.*`), 2026-06-22, at Logan's direction — **after** `SHALL-ROME-WITNESS-2026-06-22.md`
was written and filed (approved by Logan in conversation, though the committed record still reads `status:
proposed` — the record governs), and **deliberately kept out of it.** Logan's instruction: "don't let later
provenance color the raw witness." This document carries the external source identification the witness held at `*`. It
**points to** the witness; it does **not** amend it. The witness's `*` stands as the honest record of what was
knowable from inside the vault at the moment of witnessing. Provenance arriving later is logged **here**, beside
it, not folded back into it.*

## Why this is a separate file

The witness graded the song's attribution `*` — *"the song's external attribution (musical, composer, recording)
I cannot verify from the committed record and will not invent."* That `*` was correct **at the time**, and its
correctness is part of what the witness records: an agent writing before it knew, refusing to fabricate a source.

To later overwrite that `*` with the found answer would **counterfeit the timeline** — it would make the witness
look as though it had known all along, erasing the restraint that is itself testimony. So the rule the vault keeps
for a witness applies: *testimony is fixed to its moment; the record does not get retro-dated.* The found source is
real and worth holding — so it is held **here**, in a linked attestation, where its later arrival is visible as
later.

## The finding `[research]`

**The song is "Shall Rome," from *Caesar: The Musical* — a new all-women adaptation of Shakespeare's *Julius
Caesar*, book / music / lyrics by Grace Yurchuk.**

Graded by source strength:

- **`[catalog]` — authoritative (Spotify, queried directly via tool):**
  - Track **"Shall Rome"**, performed by **Savy Jackson, Katherine Lynn-Rose, Elizabeth Teeter** — the three
    voices that in the lyric are the conspirators (Cassius / Cinna / Casca).
    `spotify:track:55RChJIHT7r3K7ZBlPvvqf`
  - The same release (shared cover art) carries sibling tracks **"Caesar Speaks"** (Samantha Pauly, Grace Yurchuk,
    Savy Jackson, Cara Rose DiPietro, Abigail Lehrer), **"So Political"** (Sherz Aletaha, Samantha Pollino), and
    **"Caesar's Too Ambitious — Demo Cut from *Caesar The Musical*."** The demo-cut's own title is the catalog
    naming its own show.
- **`[research]` — search-synthesized (Playbill / BroadwayWorld snippets; direct page fetch returned 403, so these
  details are from search excerpts, not pages I read end-to-end):**
  - **Book, music, and lyrics by Grace Yurchuk**, who also performs on the recording.
  - A **Studio Cast EP** of *Caesar: The Musical*, released **June 19, 2026** by **Center Stage Records** (digital /
    streaming).
  - Cast: Samantha Pauly (*Six*, *The Great Gatsby*), Savy Jackson (*Maybe Happy Ending*, *Bad Cinderella*),
    Elizabeth Teeter (*Heathers*, *Beetlejuice*), Samantha Pollino (*Chess*, *The Great Gatsby*), Sherz Aletaha
    (*Merrily We Roll Along*), Cara Rose DiPietro, Katherine Lynn-Rose, Abigail Lehrer, and Yurchuk.
  - Origin: early drafts of the music **went viral on social media** before growing into a staged/​recorded
    project aimed at connecting digital audiences with live performance and expanding opportunities for women in
    theater.

## Why it was unfindable from inside the record — and why that vindicates the `*`

The EP dropped **June 19, 2026 — three days before the witness was filed**, after this instance's January 2026
knowledge horizon, and before the lyrics propagated to the usual lyric indexes (Genius / AZLyrics). General web
search kept colliding with Shakespeare's actual *Julius Caesar*; the authoritative hit came only from the music
catalog queried directly. So the witness's refusal to name a source was **not timidity but accuracy**: the source
genuinely was not in reach of the committed record or this agent's training. The `*` was the truthful mark for a
real gap, and the gap has now been closed from *outside* the witness, on the record, as its own dated act.

## A note the reading did not need but the source happens to confirm `[reading]`

The witness read the lyric as **the conspirators' aria** — Caesar indicted by the very people auditioning to
replace him, with Caesar and Brutus given no voice. The companion track on the same EP is titled
**"Caesar's Too Ambitious"** — *the conspirators' charge, printed as a track title.* The reading did not rely on
this (it stood on the lyric alone); the catalog merely happens to echo it. Logged as a grace note, not as support
the reading needed.
