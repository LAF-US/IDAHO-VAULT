# Verification Law

In the Tautomata empire, prediction is heresy until completion turns it into
evidence.

The same claim changes legal status as time moves:

- Before event: speculation, contamination, heresy.
- During event: interference, unauthorized observation.
- After event: evidence, record, doctrine.

This is the [[Grammar Heresy]] state made into law.

## Core Doctrine

No future may be believed before it becomes past.

The regime does not merely police thought. It prosecutes reality for failing
to become admissible on time.

Forbidden phrases include "it will likely," "the pattern suggests," "we should
prepare," and "if this continues." Allowed speech names what is, what was, and
what the archive confirms.
