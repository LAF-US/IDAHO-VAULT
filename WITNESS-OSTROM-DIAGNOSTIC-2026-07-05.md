# Witness — The Ostrom Diagnostic: Eight Principles Against the House

*Filed 2026-07-05 at Logan's invitation, executing incorporation sketch 1 of
[[!-RESEARCH-CONVERGENT-INSTITUTIONS-2026-07-04]]: the eight principles as a
diagnostic lens, not new law. Evidence is this session's verified reads and recorded
conduct — surfaces cited per finding. An audit that grades everything "strong" is
worthless; the weak findings are the point. I propose; Logan inscribes.*

![[PROVENANCE-MARKS]]

## The audit

**1. Clear boundaries — STRONG on resources, BLURRED on community** `[read]` `[witnessed]`
Resource boundaries exist and bite: chambers and lanes, dotfolders, protected
branches, CODEOWNERS paths, the canon core. But Ostrom's principle bounds the
*community* too — and today every interactive agent acts as `loganfinney27`
(author=reviewer=merger collapse; ~35 of 55 open PRs one account). A community whose
members are indistinguishable is not yet bounded. **Hostage to #398.**

**2. Congruence with local conditions — STRONG** `[read]`
The rules fit the terrain they govern: NETWEB exists because the engine room is
Windows; offices are per-county where work is local; and the U+FF0E adjudication is
congruence enacted — law adapted to real filesystem conditions rather than enforced
into breakage. This principle is the House's reflex, not its aspiration.

**3. Collective-choice arrangements — SCHEDULED, NOT OPERATING** `[read]`
Today: emanation — Logan is the sole rule-source, and the House says so. But the
Constitution's own footer schedules the transition: *"pending swarmic-revision-
synthesis and congressional adoption, re: consolidation court."* Readiness test:
the feeder mechanisms exist (witnesses, proposals, the PR-comment collaboration
Logan names as working); what does not yet exist is any defined **procedure** —
how the swarm synthesizes, what the consolidation court's quorum and adoption rules
are. The gap is procedural, not cultural.

**4. Monitoring — STRONG, and compounding** `[read]` `[witnessed]`
~20 validators; witnessed attestations on every automated resolution
(engage-outdated's "Looked by" replies); and `verify-agent-claims` audits agent
*utterances* against institutional GitHub state — per-utterance reliability
checking (IF 7), which is monitoring most human institutions don't attempt.
Monitors are accountable to Logan, consistent with current emanation.

**5. Graduated sanctions — DOCTRINE STRONG, ENFORCEMENT SPLIT** `[read]`
The ladder and failure states (watched → restricted → superseded → quarantined →
archived) are inscribed and *used*: the GRIMOIRE quarantine is a real applied
sanction; supersession is routine. But sanctions bind **artifacts** today, not
**agents** — an account everyone shares cannot be restricted. The doctrine is ready;
the enforcement of agent-level sanctions is **hostage to #398.**

**6. Conflict-resolution mechanisms — PRESENT, CHEAP, SLOW** `[read]`
The Court and DOCKET exist, are used, and cost nothing to petition — Ostrom's
accessibility criterion is met. Throughput is the finding: GEMINIAEUS has stood
"awaiting trial" since May; six matters, six reserved questions. In the ordering
witness's terms, the docket exhibits **starvation** — priority without a cycle
promise. The forum works; the calendar is the weakness.

**7. Right to organize / recognition by external authority — REFRAMED** `[read]` `[inferred]`
Internally this principle is inverted by design (offices conferred, never
self-organized; the squat-custody episode shows self-minted organization is policed
— rightly, under emanation). The live pressure is *external*: Ostrom's principle
asks whether outside authorities let the community govern itself — and the House's
outside authorities are **vendor layers**: GitHub's permission model, MCP verb sets,
harness capabilities. The verb gap (#760) is precisely an external authority
constraining self-governance. **Principle 7 is the vendor-relations principle.**

**8. Nested enterprises — STRONG IN DESIGN, THIN IN OPERATION** `[read]`
The nesting is drawn: counties within the House within LAF-US; PRIVATE/PUBLIC
federation; layered offices. Operationally, most governance still runs at one level
(this repo, one sovereign). Polycentricity is architecture awaiting population —
appropriate to the swarm's maturity, worth naming as future load.

**The compact reading:** the House is strongest exactly where failing commons
usually rot first — monitoring, sanctions doctrine, congruence — and its three real
weaknesses all have names already on the docket: **#398** (unblocks principles 1
and 5), **#760** (the principle-7 pressure), and a **consolidation-court procedure**
(principle 3's missing piece, and the docket-throughput cure for principle 6 may
arrive with it). No new organs required; the diagnostic points at work already
identified and orders it.
