For many of us, our first glimpse of the future of transportation was in the classic TV show ***The Jetsons***. George Jetson commuted in a flying car, the family traveled through the skies, and futuristic transportation seemed like pure science fiction. More than 60 years later, that vision is becoming reality.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!iCt9!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9e6c2173-7667-44ad-bdc1-439532869075_2000x1333.jpeg)

[Joby Aviation](https://www.jobyaviation.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) is a California-based company that has developed an all-electric air taxi capable of vertical takeoff and landing (eVTOL), allowing it to operate without traditional runways. The aircraft can carry a pilot and four passengers while traveling at speeds of up to 200 miles per hour.

Joby recently announced plans to begin early U.S. operations through a White House-backed air taxi program. The company has been selected to participate in projects spanning multiple states, including Idaho, and is working with airports, transportation agencies, and local governments to develop routes for passenger transportation, cargo delivery, and emergency response services.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!XVgl!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F966fd353-6232-44d7-9834-7bf508fd89ed_1444x706.jpeg)

The company has already completed demonstration flights in New York City and the San Francisco Bay Area, showing that electric air taxis can safely operate in busy urban environments. What once looked like a cartoon is now being tested in real-world conditions.

Imagine reducing our one-hour drive to a ten-minute flight, bypassing traffic congestion entirely! While there are still regulatory and infrastructure hurdles ahead, advances in battery technology, aviation systems, and electric propulsion are bringing airborne commuting closer to everyday reality in Idaho.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mbZ7!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F70221f5d-ba34-4bb4-bff3-7acccfa504aa_552x168.jpeg)

For those who study Bible prophecy, these developments are also interesting. In Daniel 12:4, we are told that in the “time of the end,” *“many shall run to and fro, and knowledge shall increase.”*

Historically, many scholars understood this passage to describe people searching the Scriptures for understanding. Others view it as a broader picture of a future age marked by rapid travel and an explosion of knowledge. Today, humanity can cross oceans in hours, access information instantly, and may soon travel between cities in electric air taxis that resemble something from a 1960s cartoon.

One thing is for sure, we are living in extraordinary times! It is amazing to watch technologies once confined to science fiction become reality before our eyes. The Jetsons imagined a world of flying vehicles. The engineers of today are working to make it happen.
