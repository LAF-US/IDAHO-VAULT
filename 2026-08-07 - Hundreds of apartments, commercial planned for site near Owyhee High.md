Housing, commercial, storage units, and more could be coming to Ustick Rd. and McDermott Rd., near Highway 16 and Owyhee High School.

Boise developer Hawkins has submitted early planning documents to the City of Meridian for a development featuring 252 apartments on ten acres of land. Early concept design also shows plans for a pool, a sports court, a dog park, and a play area.

The project is located at the intersection of Ustick Rd. and McDermott Rd., on the east side of Highway 16.

![Weather with Sarah - Idaho Power](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/54fe6a7b-4e48-4c07-b9ad-1f79c34fb57e.gif)

Weather with Sarah - Idaho Power

The housing complex is currently planned to have 126 one-bedroom units, 84 two-bedroom units, and 42 three-bedroom units.

The apartments are housed in thirteen separate buildings. Twelve of the buildings are depicted as 18-plexes and one as a 36-plex, although the exact layout of the buildings and the development’s amenities vary between the different application materials submitted.

![](https://boisedev.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/hundreds-of-apartments-commercial-planned-for-site-near-owyhee-high-Screenshot-2026-08-0-1.jpeg)

Preliminary site plans for a Hawkins development on McDermott Rd. Map: Courtesy Marla Carson

The surrounding properties are planned as two acres of self-storage, over 12 acres of office space, four acres of “flex space”, and four acres of commercial.

![SERVPRO Boise - Jan25](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/5b2a85ae-933e-4c98-82a5-e207d65304e9.png)

SERVPRO Boise - Jan25

The four-acre commercial space also has early planning documents submitted to the city under a different case number and by a different entity. Charlie Hoff, of Hoff Companies, submitted early plans for the parcel. Concept plans show three buildings with sizes of 3,728 square feet, 4,210 square feet, and 5,235 square feet.

![](https://boisedev.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/hundreds-of-apartments-commercial-planned-for-site-near-owyhee-high-Screenshot-2026-08-0-2.jpeg)

Concept plans for a commercial development on McDermott Rd. Map: Courtesy KM Engineering

The area around Owyhee High School and Highway 16 has seen significant development in recent years. The large, [480-acre “The Fields” project](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/07/16/meridian-city-council-approves-plans-for-480-acre-development-near-owyhee-high-school/) was approved by Meridian City Council last month. Last year, Toll Brothers proposed sister developments of Springday and [Dayspring,](https://boisedev.com/news/2025/07/16/dayspring-toll-brothers-meridian/) which ultimately got approval after the developer and city [came to an agreement that would allow sewer to be extended to the area faster.](https://boisedev.com/news/2025/07/10/meridian-city-council-approves-one-tables-another-toll-brothers-development-with-hundreds-of-homes/) A bit further north near Chinden Blvd., developments like [Windsor Commons](https://boisedev.com/news/2025/10/20/about-427-more-homes-proposed-for-north-meridian/) and [Basque Ranch](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/04/17/basque-ranch-meridian/) are also planned.

The project is still in the early phases. Hawkins and Hoff would still need to submit formal permit applications with the city before anything could officially move forward.
