# The Vaulted Office — Case Evidence Ledger

*Compiled 2026-06-03 by Claude Code (the clerk, not a detective), at Logan's
direction, after reading the full `game-discussion` corpus. This is an
**examination of the bodies of evidence** — both senses — laid out for whoever is
eventually appointed to the case.*

> [!important] What this is, and what it refuses to be
> **There is no detective on this case**, and this clerk is not one. This ledger
> **gathers, sorts, and sources**; it **names no culprit and reaches no verdict.**
> In this matter that restraint is not modesty — *naming the solution by confident
> narrative is the charged offence itself* (`!/GEMINIAEUS.md`). So the verdict, the
> culprit, and the merits are **reserved to Logan / the Court.** The clerk lays out
> the table; someone with standing works it.
>
> **Provenance tiers** (every item is tagged):
> **[RULED]** Logan/Court has ruled it · **[RECORD]** grounded, multiple committed
> sources agree · **[TENDERED]** on the record as evidence but *contested /
> quarantined*, not established · **[THRALL]** sourced to an enthralled/compromised
> hand — uncertifiable on its own authority · **`*`** open.
>
> **Location caveat:** most documentary evidence lives on the **unmerged
> `game-discussion` branch (PR #400)**, not on `main`. Cited honestly; not yet
> auditable from the vault.
