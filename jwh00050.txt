Camp near the head of Cache Valley, looking south, 75 miles north of Ogden. The valley is about 60 miles in length and 7 miles width. Is well settled with six or eight Mormon villages, and a total population of some 10,000. Elevation 4,624 feet. Cache County, Utah. 1871.
