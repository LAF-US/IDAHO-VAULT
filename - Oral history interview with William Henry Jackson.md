Description

Description of content: Interview of the famous photographer William Henry Jackson, who served as official photographer for the United States Geological Survey. Interviewed on the eve of his 98th birthday.  
  
Recording length: 38 minutes  
  
Physical Description: Originally on two 10 inch discs. Available in digital format on SoundCloud.  
  
Project description: Thanks to the generosity of the John W. Emery Family, in 2018 History Colorado hired an Assistant Curator of Oral History to manage all aspects of History Colorado’s oral history collections. In October of 2018, an initial survey of the 700 + recordings identified as oral histories began. The goal of the project includes adding the material to the Argus database and increasing its exposure and access.

Related Materials

History Colorado owns many materials related to William Henry Jackson, including records of his studio and many of his original images.  
Slide of William Henry Jackson on this record taken at age 98 by Fred Payne Clatworthy in 1941. Accession # 96.174.5605
