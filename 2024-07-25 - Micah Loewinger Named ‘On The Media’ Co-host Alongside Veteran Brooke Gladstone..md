On The Media hosts

New York Public Radio is giving Brooke Gladstone, the host of its podcast and radio show “ [On The Media](https://www.iheart.com/podcast/19-on-the-media-23568271/) ” for more than two decades, a new co-host. Producer and reporter Micah Loewinger has been promoted to the role after serving on the show’s staff for more than eight years — first as a producer, then as the show’s first-ever reporter, and, most recently, as a regular guest host. He will now officially co-host alongside Gladstone.

New York Public Radio says Loewinger’s work has largely focused on digital media’s influence on politics and political movements. That included an award-winning investigation into the walkie-talkie app Zello. which revealed the way right-wing militias were using it to recruit new members, and his sustained tracking of these groups on the app led him to uncover their ties to local police departments and discover one group’s plan to storm the Capitol.

Loewinger has also covered stories on QAnon, teenage fascists who had been deradicalized by left-wing YouTubers, why preppers weren’t prepping for the pandemic, how restorative justice might help rehabilitate trolls on Reddit, how a video game about war helped a family survive a actual war, and why a conservative election director became a pariah in her deep-red county.

“As someone who grew up with the internet –– watching more YouTube than cable news –– I’m excited to help the show to rethink how we define media,” Loewinger said. “I hope to incorporate the voices of more independent and social media-based journalists on our show to better reflect how and where Americans get news and information. The media industry is struggling, and there’s plenty of disagreement about how to transform and save it, but it’s truly an honor to be a part of a team committed to helping our listeners contend with what’s happening in real-time.”

Gladstone in a statement credits Loewinger for his “investigative skills, his deep engagement, and his distinctive voice,” and says he has helped steer the series into new areas of media coverage. Gladstone has been hosting the show solo since 2021 when [longtime cohost Bob Garfield was fired](https://www.insideradio.com/free/on-the-media-co-host-bob-garfield-fired-for-violating-anti-bullying-policy/article_91e436c0-b7cf-11eb-9fa5-23e788e88525.html) for what New York Public Radio said was “a pattern of behavior that violated NYPR’s anti-bullying policy.”

The Peabody award-winning “On The Media” airs on more than 400 public radio stations across the country, and has a biweekly podcast. In addition to Gladstone and Loewinger, the series is executive produced by Katya Rogers.

“Twenty-plus years into its run, ‘On The Media’ remains one of the most relevant, essential guides to understanding the way media shapes our public perception and democracy,” said Kenya Young, Senior VP of WNYC Programming and WNYC Studios. “How people consume news and information is changing at an ever-faster clip, and Micah’s appointment heralds an exciting moment for the show, as he brings his digital native perspective and investigative chops to an era of rampant mis- and disinformation.”
