BOISE, Idaho — [***LATEST:*** ***Man accused of Boise Greenbelt murder makes first court appearance***](https://www.ktvb.com/article/news/crime/man-accused-greenbelt-murder-makes-first-court-appearance/277-8a3592d8-d257-4c89-9319-074b4efd37fa)

Police arrested a Boise man Thursday in connection with a murder along the Boise River Greenbelt near 11th Street earlier this week.

Ross Wardlaw, 41, was taken into custody about 3:30 p.m. and booked into the Ada County Jail on a first-degree murder charge, according to the Boise Police Department. Investigators accuse Wardlaw of stabbing Jordan Harbst, saying he stabbed Harbst multiple times.

In court records obtained by KTVB, Wardlaw is accused of stabbing Harbst "multiple times with a fixed blade knife."

Investigators said the attack happened shortly after midnight Monday as Harbst was traveling along the Greenbelt near 11th Street.

Police said Wardlaw and Harbst did not know each other and there was no known fight before the stabbing. Harbst died from his injuries.

Boise Police Chief Chris Dennison said investigators and staff had been working "around the clock" to find answers and locate the person responsible. He said the crime is rare in Boise and acknowledged the fear a violent incident can cause in the community.

As previously reported, BPD said that at 7:30 a.m. on July 6, officers responded to a report of an unresponsive man on the Greenbelt between Pioneer Pathway and 11th Street. Police said when officers arrived, they found a deceased man and quickly shut down the area for an investigation.

Detectives from Boise Police's Violent Crimes Unit responded to the scene and began their death investigation on July 6. Detectives said at the time they believed Harbst died between midnight and 7:30 a.m. that day.

The coroner's office identified Harbst as the deceased man and stated he was pronounced dead behind West River Street on the Boise Greenbelt at 8:41 a.m. on Monday, July 6. His cause of death has not been released by the coroner's office, and police said this was to "protect the integrity of the investigation." Thursday's news release shows his death was due to a stabbing.

The family of Harbst is holding a candlelight vigil in his honor just before midnight on Friday, July 10 at the Boise Greenbelt between 11th Street and Pioneer Street.
