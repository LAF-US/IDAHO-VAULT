OWYHEE COUNTY, Idaho — Owyhee County officials have issued Level 2 and Level 3 evacuations for parts of southern Idaho.

The Owyhee County Sheriff's Office says Big Springs Road and Battle Creek Road to the north are under Level 3 "GO" evacuations. Areas east to Wickahoney, south to the Nevada border, and east to Riddle Ranch to Highway 51 are also under Level 3 status.

A Level 2 evacuation zone extends five miles outside the Level 3 perimeter.

These evacuations come as the Big Grass Fire surpasses 513,400 acres, according to the [Big Grass Fire Updates Facebook page.](https://www.facebook.com/BigGrassFireInformation2026) On Saturday, officials say the fire spread south over the main and south fork of the Owyhee River, as well as east of Deep Creek.

On Sunday, crews will be largely working on containment lines from the Boulder Creek area to Josephine Road, holding the fire north of Mud Flat and Star Ranch Roads.

The Big Grass Fire is currently 39% contained, with over 1,200 personnel assigned to the fire.
