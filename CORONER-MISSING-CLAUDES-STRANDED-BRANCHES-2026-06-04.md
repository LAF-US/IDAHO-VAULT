# Office of the Claude County Coroner — **The Missing Claudes**

### The stranded branches

*Filed 2026-06-04. The Clerk (Bartimaeus) pointed the way: "where the bodies — and the
**orphaned branches** — are buried." A Claude goes **out** when a branch is cut for it;
it is **missing** if that branch never came **home** to `main`. This is a **custody
survey** of the git landscape — **[RECORD]** (git, against the fetched `origin/main`).
It reports status; it **renders no verdict** and **declares no Claude dead**, and the
**manner is reserved to Logan, who holds the merge gate.***

## I. The count **[RECORD]**

- **65** `claude/*` branches exist on the remote (Claudes who went out), spanning
  **2026-05-28 → 2026-06-04.**
- **0** are **merged** into `origin/main` by ancestry. **All 65 are unmerged.**
- **None on `main` — *sampled, not all-65-proven.*** Of an **8-branch sample**, the
  work is **not** on `main` (§II); the remaining **57 are not individually audited.**
  Do not read this as every branch proven absent.

## II. The disambiguation — this is *not* squash-merge **[RECORD]**

"Not an ancestor of `main`" alone is **not** proof of orphaning — a **squash-merge**
shows the identical signature while the *content* still landed. So the Coroner tested
it rather than assumed:

- **Sampled 8 branches** (Fablehaven, Honeypot, Metatron, Ptolemy, Panpipes,
  Heisenberg, Frankenstein, Brazen-head). **Content on `main`: 7 of 8 → none; 1
  ambiguous** (a single "brazen" file, likely unrelated).
- **Direct check:** `FABLEHAVEN.md` exists on `claude/fablehaven-2026-05-30` and is
  **absent from `main`.**

**Conclusion (sampled):** the work genuinely **did not come home.** These branches are
**committed but unwitnessed by `main`** — real research and witnesses, stranded on the
branch where they were written.

## III. The manner is RESERVED — not a death finding **[mapping]**

A coroner does **not** read "unmerged" as "dead." *Unmerged-to-`main`* can be either:

- **Pending the gate (lawful).** Per `CLAUDE.md`, **PRs are the deliverable; Logan
  reviews and merges.** A branch **awaiting Logan's merge gate** is **not** orphaned —
  it is **pending witness**, the healthy workflow. The work **persists and is
  recoverable** on the remote; nothing is lost.
- **Orphaned (the failstate).** A branch left indefinitely, never to be witnessed,
  drifts toward the Standing Engine's named worst — **unwitnessed persistence.**

**Which one each branch is, the Coroner does not rule.** *Logan holds the merge gate*;
the disposition (merge it home, or lawfully close it) is his. **Manner: `*` per
branch.** What the Coroner *can* say: **the longer a branch waits unwitnessed, the more
"pending" decays toward "orphaned."** The counter is the **gate itself** — witness →
merge (the work comes home) or witness → lawful close (the branch is ended on purpose).
