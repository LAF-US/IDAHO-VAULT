**Paul Durham** (born September 9, 1968) is an American [musician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musician "Musician") and producer, a solo singer-songwriter and the lead [singer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singing "Singing") of rock bands [Black Lab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Lab "Black Lab"), [Stray Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stray_Palace "Stray Palace"), and Cake or Death.[^1]

He is the writer of two *[Billboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_charts "Billboard charts")* charting songs,[^2] [^3] [^4] "Wash it Away" and "Time Ago" from Black Lab's [Geffen Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geffen_Records "Geffen Records") debut, *[Your Body Above Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Your_Body_Above_Me "Your Body Above Me")*,[^5] and is represented by [EMI Music Publishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMI_Music_Publishing "EMI Music Publishing") [^6] and Secret Road Music.
