PBS is excited to introduce **21 new** ***App Homescreen Station Lists*** available for customization across connected TV and mobile apps — giving your station **seven times more options** than before to enhance local presence and viewing.  
  
These new *App Homescreen Station Lists* provide stations with the ability to easily hand-pick shows and videos to feature on the PBS app's main page for localized users. Each new list offers a **custom display name**, providing the freedom to **organize content by collection, partner, genre, date, or any theme** that best fits your station’s audience and programming strategy.

The *App Homescreen Station Lists* populate throughout the PBS app homescreen, holding the 5th and 10th row position before alternating with nationally programmed lists in groups of 4 down the rest of the page. These 21 new lists join the ***Station Shows*** and ***Videos and Shows Highlighted by Your Local Station*** **lists** as optional station controlled spaces on the app homescreen. [**A diagram of how lists are organized on the PBS app homescreen can be found here.**](https://docs.pbs.org/space/CB/1600651265/App+Homescreen+Station+Lists#List-Order)

With these newest rows, the PBS app blends custom local station curation, user-based content, recommendation engine-powered suggestions, and national highlights for an engaging, localized user experience.
