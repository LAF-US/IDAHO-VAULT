Payette Brewing is undergoing a rebrand.

In a news release sent to email subscribers on Thursday and posted [on their website](https://www.payettebrewing.com/payette-provisions), the company, founded in 2011, is now called [Payette Provisions.](https://www.payettebrewing.com/)

“It all started with beer,” the release said. “But somewhere along the way, Payette became so much more than that. It became a place to gather after work, celebrate life’s milestones, listen to live music, bring the kids, support local nonprofits, and experience everything that makes Idaho special. Beer will always be at the heart of who we are. But today, Payette represents something even bigger than what’s in the glass. That’s why we’re excited to introduce ourselves as Payette Provisions.”

![Idaho Wines - August 2026](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/d473ccb0-7147-4e1e-93ab-c0d36b268628.png)

Idaho Wines - August 2026

Payette Provisions co-founder and CEO Mike Francis told BoiseDev the idea started late last year, after moving their out-of-state brewing operations from Upslope Brewing Company in Boulder, Colorado, back to Idaho.

[As BoiseDev reported in 2024](https://boisedev.com/news/2024/08/15/boise-brewery-partners-with-colorado-craft-beer-company-assures-no-sale-on-tap/), Payette Brewing “joined forces” with Upslope to share resources. In November 2025, the [Denver Post reported](https://www.denverpost.com/2025/11/13/upslope-brewing-sold-wildling-colorado/) that Upslope Brewing was sold to Wilding Brands.

Francis said this allowed them to bring all their operations back to Idaho and begin rebranding to encapsulate all their products, which include beer and canned cocktails.

![BoiseDev House - OTT - Commercial](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/8b916803-64e1-4279-a378-7e47d28a2871.png)

BoiseDev House - OTT - Commercial

“Provisions encapsulates who we are and where we’re going,” he said.

Customers can expect a new logo, colors, and other elements. Francis said they worked with Boise-based Against on their new branding.

“While our name is changing, the things that matter most to us are not,” the release said.

![Freeform - Invested in Boise - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/8d57412c-01ee-47ef-b704-9f71ec57d3ba.png)

Freeform - Invested in Boise - 2:1

Since its inception, Payette Brewing has had a nonprofit arm called [Payette Forward](https://www.payettebrewing.com/payette-forward-program), which donates to and volunteers with local nonprofits. They were recently featured as a [BoiseDev ‘Invested in Idaho’ company](https://boisedev.com/invested-in-idaho/just-seems-natural-giving-back-is-part-of-payette-brewings-history/) for their commitment to the community through nonprofit work and fundraising.

”We’ve always been an Idaho company,” the release said. “While production spent a brief chapter outside the state, our commitment to Idaho never changed. Today, we’re proud that every Payette product is once again brewed and crafted right here in Idaho. We’re still the same team. The same values. The same love for this place we call home. Born. Brewed. Crafted. Canned. We’re all Idaho.”

Francis said there is already one beer available for purchase with the new branding, and that customers can expect to see all products with the new logo roll out over the next few weeks and months.
