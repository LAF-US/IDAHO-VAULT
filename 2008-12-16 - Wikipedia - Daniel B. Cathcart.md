**Daniel B. Cathcart** (December 8, 1906 – January 23, 1959) was an American [art director](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_director "Art director"). He was nominated for two [Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Awards "Academy Awards") in the category [Best Art Direction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Art_Direction "Academy Award for Best Art Direction"). He was born in [Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idaho "Idaho") and died in [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles"), [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California "California").

## Selected filmography

Cathcart was nominated for two Academy Awards for Best Art Direction:

- *[Thousands Cheer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thousands_Cheer "Thousands Cheer")* (1943) [^1]
- *[Kismet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kismet_\(1944_film\) "Kismet (1944 film)")* (1944) [^2]
