# Source Constellation

The campaign is syncretic. Sources are used as structural inspirations, not as
one-to-one imports.

## Myth and Literature

- Tantalus: punitive nearness and unreachable completion.
- Brazen Head: "Time is. Time was. Time is past."
- Croesus at Delphi: true prophecy with fatal context gap.
- Cassandra: true warning without social uptake.
- Daedalus and Hephaestus: maker, forge, labyrinth.

## Game and Speculative Fiction

- Modrons and Primus: lawful hierarchy and periodic march.
- Anti-Modron inversion: procedure producing disorder.
- Robert House: preserved calculator-sovereign.
- Skynet and Omnius: machine empire references.
- Cybermen and Daleks: conversion logic and repeated imperative.
- Vogons: bureaucracy as cruelty.
- Oz and the Nome King: road-to-Wizard structure and subterranean tyranny.
- Clockwork and brass-head traditions: artificial oracle failure.
