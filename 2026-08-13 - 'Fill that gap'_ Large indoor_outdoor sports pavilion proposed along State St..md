A new indoor-outdoor sports venue could come to Highway 44 in Eagle.

PSP Holdings has proposed to build the Premier Sports Pavilion on a 6.3-acre site along State St. near Horseshoe Bend Rd., to the east of the St. Luke’s Eagle Medical Plaza.

“PSP has identified a market in the rapidly growing communities of Eagle, Star, Meridian, and Boise for a centralized, high-quality facility where athletes of all ages and skill levels can train, compete, recover, and connect under one roof,” PSP wrote in an application letter for a conditional use permit filed with the City of Eagle. “The facility, Premier Sports Pavilion, is designed to fill that gap by combining professional-grade training technology, professional recovery and physical therapy services, and outdoor multi-sport fields and courts with community-oriented food-and-beverage experiences in a single, purpose-built venue.”

![Build Idaho - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/8679b89c-de2f-4b22-948e-bd39a76c4c7f.png)

Build Idaho - 2:1

PSP Holdings is controlled by Louise and Victor Bertagnolli of Eagle, according to Idaho Secretary of State records. The Bertagnollis [founded JST Manufacturing](https://commerce.idaho.gov/press/meridian-manufacturer-announces-expansion/), a semiconductor company with operations in Meridian.

The plan includes a large 20,000-square-foot building along State St. with a full-service fitness center, fitness studios, and a physical therapy clinic. The building would also include cold plunge spas, locker rooms, a sauna, and showers. Plans also call for four baseball training bays, two boxing simulation bays, and six golf simulators. The golf simulators would feature seating, display screens, and lounge features. The plan also calls for a podcast and broadcast studio on site.

A cafe would feature espresso, smoothies, acai, and a “light food menu.” Indoor seating for 40 to 50 and outdoor seating for 40 on a covered patio is also proposed.

![Build Idaho - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/8679b89c-de2f-4b22-948e-bd39a76c4c7f.png)

Build Idaho - 2:1

Behind the building, PSP says it hopes to build a large covered multi-sport complex, with three synthetic turf fields and two hard-floored courts. The hard-floored courts would provide support for basketball, volleyball, futsal, and other sports. The courts would be “sheltered beneath a steel canopy structure engineered for the applicable wind, snow, and equipment suspension loads of the Eagle area.”

The plan also calls for 216 vehicle parking spaces, with “two bicycle parking positions.”

“Eagle and the surrounding Treasure Valley are among the fastest-growing communities in the United States,” PSP wrote. “That growth has brought an expanding population of youth athletes, recreational sports participants, fitness-oriented adults, and sports entertainment consumers who currently lack a comprehensive, high-quality facility in the immediate area. Premier Sports Pavilion is designed to serve that demand locally, reducing the need for residents to travel to Boise or Meridian for comparable experiences.”

The project is set to be heard by the Eagle Planning & Zoning Commission on August 17th. The project will need to win approval for a request for a conditional use permit, as well as a rezoning application.
