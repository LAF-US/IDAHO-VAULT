## Alberto Gonzalez, Idaho’s CIO and the administrator of its Office of Information Technology Services, will start a new position in July as director of the state transportation department.

![Image shows overlapping streets and/or highways with data points and lines connecting overlayed on top of the streets to represent an intersection between transporation and data/technology.](https://erepublic.brightspotcdn.com/dims4/default/b9f48cc/2147483647/strip/true/crop/5342x2785+0+107/resize/840x438!/quality/90/?url=http%3A%2F%2Ferepublic-brightspot.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fbe%2F27%2F550ad2b746959d9efff722a7dc60%2Fshutterstock-1485922235.jpg)

[Alberto Gonzalez](https://www.govtech.com/tag/alberto-gonzalez), who has been serving as Idaho’s CIO and administrator of the Office of Information Technology Services (ITS) for nearly four years, has been appointed director of the Idaho Transportation Department (ITD).  
  
He will start the new role with ITD on July 7, ITS Communications Manager Jennifer Gonzalez, no relation, said in an email. Idaho Department of Commerce Administrator Jake Reynolds will serve as interim administrator for ITS, she said.  
  
“Alberto Gonzalez has been an exceptional public servant whose leadership has modernized state government and improved services for Idahoans,” Gov. Brad Little said in a [statement](https://gov.idaho.gov/pressrelease/gov-little-announces-new-appointments-4/). “I am confident he will continue that success as he leads ITD.”  
  
Gonzalez will step in shortly after the retirement of ITD’s current director, Scott Stokes, who [announced](https://itd.idaho.gov/news/itd-director-scott-stokes-announces-retirement/) in April he would exit June 30 after a 35-year career.  
  
The incoming CIO brings with him more than two decades of service in Idaho government. Before joining ITS, he served as the administrator for ITD’s Division of Motor Vehicles, per the state. He also worked for the Idaho Department of Health and Welfare; and, earlier still, spent 14 years in leadership roles at a national communications company. Gonzalez is a graduate of the National Transportation Leadership Institute at Indiana University.  
  
Gonzalez began as state CIO and ITS administrator in [October 2022](https://www.govtech.com/workforce/alberto-gonzalez-tapped-to-lead-idaho-its-as-administrator). During his time leading the young agency — ITS was created in 2018 — he helped advance the state’s [IT consolidation goals](https://www.govtech.com/workforce/idahos-near-term-mission-consolidating-and-modernizing-state-it). The consolidation initiative [has enabled an array of technological advances](https://www.govtech.com/workforce/in-idaho-it-consolidation-supports-technological-advances), from [cybersecurity](https://www.govtech.com/security/idaho-takes-a-hands-on-approach-to-state-cybersecurity) to [AI](https://www.govtech.com/workforce/in-idaho-ai-improves-government-experience-efficiency).  
  
“AI has allowed us to make privacy sexy again,” the CIO [said in December](https://www.govtech.com/workforce/in-idaho-ai-improves-government-experience-efficiency)*.*
