Buc-ee’s made headlines this summer as foreigners flocked to the Beaver-mascotted travel centers during the World Cup to experience the American phenomenon.

Meanwhile, the Meridian lot where the chain was eyeing an Idaho location stood largely still.

[BoiseDev reported](https://boisedev.com/news/2025/07/08/buc-ees-idaho/) the travel center was planning one of its large convenience stores and fuel centers for the Hawkins Companies-owned site at Meridian Rd. and I-84 in September 2025. Since then, the travel center’s plans for the site have come to a halt due to traffic concerns.

But the possibility of a Buc-ee’s in the valley is not dead yet, according to one local developer.

## Traffic problems

Plans for a Buc-ee’s hit a roadblock before an application was even submitted, largely due to traffic concerns.

Public records obtained by BoiseDev show employees at both the Idaho Department of Transportation and the City of Meridian had concerns with the estimated 19,000 vehicles predicted to stop at the travel center each day. The area near I-84 and Meridian Rd. experiences high levels of traffic during commuting hours.

Public employees spent several meetings working with Buc-ee’s engineers. The staff took issue with some of the traffic models presented to them and whether they were accurate representations of the travel center’s impact. Meridian Long-Range Planning Supervisor Brian McClure called the traffic plans and the models that came with them “unrealistic.”

“The model was a trainwreck. It was one of the worst things I’ve seen in terms of obvious, gross negligence (more likely), or just simple unethical ‘malpractice.’ ACHD is frustrated with the amount of time invested and the developer’s consultant seemingly unable to deliver. ITD was not a fan, either,” McClure wrote in an email in January.

ITD Communications Director Jill Youmans said the plans were not feasible. She also said Buc-ee’s proposed a “flyover” structure on the interchange as a solution to some of the traffic problems. Youmans said there were problems with a proposal for this raised bridge because it wasn’t determined whether it would actually fit or who would own it, among other concerns.

![SERVPRO Boise - Jan25](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/5b2a85ae-933e-4c98-82a5-e207d65304e9.png)

Youmans told BoiseDev ITD had not heard back from Buc-ees since it raised concerns about its traffic study and proposed flyover ramp.

[The Idaho Statesman first reported](https://www.idahostatesman.com/news/local/community/west-ada/article314567521.html) on some elements of the change.

## The future of beaver nuggets in Boise

According to Boise developer Ahlquist’s Chief Leasing Officer Mark Cleverley, Buc-ee’s isn’t gone for good.

![Sundance Company](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/a95b6100-2267-485a-87f4-0eb633a38c0e.png)

Sundance Company

In fact, Cleverley said he was confident in the travel center’s future in the Treasure Valley on a recent livestream.

“Buc-ee’s is going to come to the valley. At some point, they will be here,” Cleverley said. “We’ve got a couple of sites in front of them that we’d love them to go to, but we’ll see.”

After BoiseDev broke news that Buc-ee’s was looking at Idaho last summer, the chain announced it [would add a location in Utah.](https://www.fox13now.com/news/local-news/northern-utah/buc-ees-to-utah-the-dream-is-set-to-become-reality)
