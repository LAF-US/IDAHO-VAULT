PAYETTE COUNTY, Idaho — With [multiple fires](https://www.ktvb.com/wildfires) sparking throughout Idaho before the first official day of summer has hit, some county's across Idaho are implementing their annual burn bans ahead of schedule.

[Payette County](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/18cbvt79Fk/) has implemented a burn ban, effective Monday, June 15, for all unincorporated areas of Payette County and within the City of Payette and New Plymouth.

An official for Payette Fire confirmed the annual ban comes a bit earlier than usual, with most local ordinances being implemented in early July, due to dry conditions and recent high temperatures. [Canyon County](https://www.ktvb.com/article/news/local/caldwell-implements-city-wide-burn-ban-canyon-county-dry-conditions/277-27c42849-df77-4639-88fe-c56d907aae6f) also called for a burn ban ahead of schedule due to unseasonably warm, dry and windy late-spring conditions.

The ban also comes after [Sand Hollow Fire Protection District](https://www.facebook.com/sandhollowfire/posts/just-a-reminder-that-county-burn-ban-starts-june-15th-sandhollowfirewildlandfire/1455235933073722/) shared a Facebook post in late May, calling for the Payette County Commissioners to implement a ban due to 'high fuel loads. early seasonal reduction in fuel moisture levels \[and\] persistently dry underlying conditions across our fire districts."
