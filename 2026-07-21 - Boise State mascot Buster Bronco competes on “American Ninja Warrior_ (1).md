BOISE, [[Idaho]] (CBS2) — Viewers watching Season 18 of NBC’s American Ninja Warrior may have noticed a familiar Boise face on the course: Boise State’s mascot, Buster Bronco.

Buster walked onto the set ready to take on the show’s high-speed obstacle course, but removed the mask before competing, revealing Matthew Duffy, a recent Boise State graduate (BBA finance, 2025). Duffy spent two of his four years at Boise State as Buster, according to Boise State.

Duffy said he got the call about appearing on the show about a month before filming, after a friend helped him put together an audition video. The video included clips filmed on campus of Duffy as Buster, along with footage at Bodies in Motion, a Boise gym that has a full American Ninja Warriors training course.

“I got a call later in the summer, and honestly, I thought it was one of my friends pranking me,” Duffy said. “And they were like, no, we’re going to have you on the show. We’re going fly you down, pay for your hotel and your travel.”

After the call, Duffy began training for the specific demands of American Ninja Warrior, including swinging from ropes and balancing on unstable platforms. He connected with another Boise State alum during the process: Bodies in Motion owner Duncan Richardson (BS civil engineering, 2000).

While on set, Duffy leaned into his Boise State ties. He received permission from the Boise State Spirit Squad to travel with the Buster Bronco suit and filmed promotional material for the show as the mascot. Before he ran the course, Duffy also received a surprise video message from Boise State football star Ashton Jeanty.

Duffy did not advance past the qualifying round, but said the experience was still memorable for the people he met during filming.
