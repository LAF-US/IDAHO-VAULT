**Benjamin Clapp** (born October 13, 1977) is an American musician from [Boise, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise,_Idaho "Boise, Idaho"). He has performed, composed, and recorded music with numerous artists, most notably [Erik Sanko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_Sanko "Erik Sanko") ([The Lounge Lizards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lounge_Lizards "The Lounge Lizards")) and [Skeleton Key](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skeleton_Key_\(band\) "Skeleton Key (band)"), [Tom Marshall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Marshall_\(singer\) "Tom Marshall (singer)") ([Phish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phish "Phish")) and [Amfibian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amfibian "Amfibian"), Anthony Krizan ([Spin Doctors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_Doctors "Spin Doctors")), [Jim Breuer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Breuer "Jim Breuer"), [Kronos Quartet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kronos_Quartet "Kronos Quartet"), [Jesse Blaze Snider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Blaze_Snider "Jesse Blaze Snider") and [Baptized By Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baptized_By_Fire_\(band\) "Baptized By Fire (band)"), [Dee Snider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dee_Snider "Dee Snider") ([Twisted Sister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twisted_Sister "Twisted Sister")), [Billy Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Martin_\(percussionist\) "Billy Martin (percussionist)") ([Medeski Martin and Wood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medeski_Martin_and_Wood "Medeski Martin and Wood")), [David Peel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Peel_\(musician\) "David Peel (musician)") ([The Lower East Side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lower_East_Side_Band "The Lower East Side Band")), and [White Trash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Trash_\(metal_band\) "White Trash (metal band)").[^1] [^2] [^3] [^4]

## Early life and education

At the age of eleven, Clapp began studying [trombone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trombone "Trombone") in Idaho public schools. He studied privately under bass trombonist Mark Sellman for a number of years, and was awarded the [Louis Armstrong Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Armstrong_award "Louis Armstrong award") in 1995, as well as performance awards at [Boise State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise_State_University "Boise State University") and the [Lionel Hampton International Jazz Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionel_Hampton_Jazz_Festival "Lionel Hampton Jazz Festival"). Meanwhile, he was self-learning to play drums; influenced early on by drummers [Stewart Copeland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stewart_Copeland "Stewart Copeland"), [Lars Ulrich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lars_Ulrich "Lars Ulrich"), and [Dale Crover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Crover "Dale Crover").[^5]

## Career

### 1993-2000

In 1993, Clapp joined Boise punk band, Haggis, following ex- [Septic Death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Septic_Death "Septic Death") drummer [Paul Birnbaum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Birnbaum "Paul Birnbaum") 's departure from the band. The band went on to tour the West Coast, and release a number of singles and compilation tracks internationally, including the vinyl only 1995 release *Step Into It*, on German record label, Bilharziose.[^6] Through the next several years, Clapp was involved with a number of independent music projects in [Boise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise "Boise"), and went on to collaborate again with members of Haggis - this time with inclusion of [Birnbaum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Birnbaum "Paul Birnbaum"). Clapp was a founding member of the [Adversives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adversives?action=edit&redlink=1 "Adversives (page does not exist)") and the [Mosquitones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosquitones?action=edit&redlink=1 "Mosquitones (page does not exist)"), and performed on all of their recorded releases and national tours.

Clapp formed [independent record label](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_record_label "Independent record label") Spudtown Records in 1995, and released the vinyl only compilation *12 Songs You Won't Like By 12 Bands You've Never Heard of From Boise, Idaho*.[^7] The label continued working with regional bands to release their EPs and albums under the moniker STMC (Spudtown Music Collective) until Clapp relocated to the East Coast.

### 2001-present

In early 2001, Clapp moved to [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City"), and within months was hired on as drummer for a [Warsaw Poland Brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_Poland_Brothers?action=edit&redlink=1 "Warsaw Poland Brothers (page does not exist)") US tour, performing for the first time with the band in [New Orleans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans "New Orleans") with no audition or rehearsal. Later the same year he moved to [New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jersey "New Jersey") and became active in the music scene fostered by legendary promoter/performer Jack Monahan, at the [Brighton Bar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brighton_Bar "Brighton Bar"). He was invited to join the Do Dads, who would then go on to 3 [Asbury Park Music Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asbury_Park_Music_Awards "Asbury Park Music Awards") nominations.[^8]

By recommendation from rock photographer [Mark Weiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Weiss "Mark Weiss") to [Twisted Sister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twisted_Sister "Twisted Sister") frontman [Dee Snider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dee_Snider "Dee Snider"), Clapp was introduced to Snider's eldest son [Jesse Blaze Snider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Blaze_Snider "Jesse Blaze Snider") in 2004, and they went on to perform together as Blazed, and from 2006 to 2008 as [Baptized By Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baptized_By_Fire_\(band\) "Baptized By Fire (band)").[^9] Clapp also joined [Erik Sanko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_Sanko "Erik Sanko") 's band [Skeleton Key](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skeleton_Key_\(band\) "Skeleton Key (band)") in 2004 as their "junk percussionist", and became further recognized for his use of non-traditional percussion: "...the real dirty work's done by brutish showman Benjamin Clapp. He chucks broken cymbals up into the air and slugs them on their way down. He has the worlds \[sic\] most dangerous tambourine: A rectangular frame, I'm guessing 36" x 24", with CIRCULAR SAW BLADES strung on it." [^10] Skeleton Key continued to tour the United States and Canada with Clapp in the band, and recorded songs which would eventually go on to limited release as *The Lyons Quintette* EP.

Among a number of studio projects and touring artists that hire Clapp to perform on stage and in the studio, [Phish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phish "Phish") lyricist [Tom Marshall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Marshall_\(singer\) "Tom Marshall (singer)") and his band [Amfibian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amfibian "Amfibian") feature Clapp's trombone playing on their studio albums *[From the Ether](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/From_the_Ether "From the Ether")* and *[Skip the Goodbyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skip_the_Goodbyes "Skip the Goodbyes")*. Clapp's trombone playing is also a fixture in the current line-up for [Elektra Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elektra_Records "Elektra Records") recording artists: [White Trash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Trash_\(metal_band\) "White Trash (metal band)"), and is heard on their 2009 release *3-D Monkeys In Space*.
