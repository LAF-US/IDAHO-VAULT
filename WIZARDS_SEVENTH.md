# Wizard's Seventh Rule

## Summary

The seventh of the numbered Wizard's Rules stated across Terry Goodkind's *Sword of Truth* series, one per book. The Seventh Rule concerns orienting toward the future rather than the past.

## Key Facts

**Source book:** *The Pillars of Creation* (book 7)

**Text:**

> Life is the future, not the past.
