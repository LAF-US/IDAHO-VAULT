**Charles Fay Harris** (June 22, 1946 – May 23, 1995) was an American [professional wrestler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestling "Professional wrestling") [^3] who worked as **Chris Colt** for a variety of different wrestling promotions in the United States such as [Big Time Wrestling (Detroit)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Time_Wrestling_\(Detroit\) "Big Time Wrestling (Detroit)"), [NWA All-Star Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NWA_All-Star_Wrestling "NWA All-Star Wrestling"), [American Wrestling Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Wrestling_Association "American Wrestling Association"), and [Pacific Northwest Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Northwest_Wrestling "Pacific Northwest Wrestling"). He also competed as **Paul Dupree**, **Jim Dillinger**, **Chuck Dupree** and **Chris Von Colt**.

## Professional wrestling career

Colt made his professional wrestling debut in 1964 in Massachusetts.[^4] In 1966, he formed a tag team with Ron Dupree as the Hell's Angels, a biker-themed tag team, in Phoenix, Arizona. Sometimes they were known as the California Hell's Angels or Comancheros. In 1968, the duo burned the American flag ([kayfabe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayfabe "Kayfabe")) in Phoenix on TV which the promotion shut down due to the incident. They would win many tag team titles in Phoenix, and Detroit until breaking up in 1971.[^5]

In 1969, he formed a tag team with [Jack Dillinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Fargo "Don Fargo") as the Chain Gang. Colt became Jim Dillinger. They won the [WWA World Tag Team Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWA_World_Tag_Team_Championship "WWA World Tag Team Championship") in Indianapolis from 1969 to 1970.

In 1974, Colt reunited with Ron Dupree as the Hells Angels in the Gulf Coast, winning the [NWA Gulf Coast Tag Team Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NWA_Gulf_Coast_Tag_Team_Championship "NWA Gulf Coast Tag Team Championship") twice. The team suddenly ended on October 17, 1975, when Ron Dupree died from a heart attack while ring announcing.[^6] Afterwards, Colt would focus more on his singles career and changed his gimmick to that of a rocker.[^7]

Colt won his first singles title the [NWA Mississippi Heavyweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NWA_Mississippi_Heavyweight_Championship_\(Gulf_Coast_version\) "NWA Mississippi Heavyweight Championship (Gulf Coast version)") defeating [Cowboy Bob Kelly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cowboy_Bob_Kelly "Cowboy Bob Kelly") in March 1975.

Superstar mainstay "Crazy" Chris Colt used [Alice Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alice_Cooper "Alice Cooper") 's " [Welcome to My Nightmare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welcome_to_My_Nightmare_\(song\) "Welcome to My Nightmare (song)") " as his entrance music. This is one of the earliest instances of a promotion [playing theme music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_in_professional_wrestling "Music in professional wrestling") for wrestlers.[^8] [^9]

In an infamous incident in Phoenix that took place in 1975, Colt was high on [LSD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LSD "LSD") during a cage match seeing spiders all around due to hallucinations. He fled the venue fighting with both the fans and arena security.[^10]

Colt made his debut in Vancouver, Canada for [NWA All-Star Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NWA_All-Star_Wrestling "NWA All-Star Wrestling") in 1979 winning the [NWA Canadian Tag Team Championship (Vancouver version)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NWA_Canadian_Tag_Team_Championship_\(Vancouver_version\) "NWA Canadian Tag Team Championship (Vancouver version)") twice with [Bobby Jaggers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobby_Jaggers "Bobby Jaggers") and [Buddy Rose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddy_Rose "Buddy Rose"). He worked in Vancouver until 1982. Also in 1979, he made his debut in Portland, Oregon for [Pacific Northwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Northwest "Pacific Northwest") where he quickly became a star.

Later in his career, he worked for [Dave McKigney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_McKigney "Dave McKigney") 's Big Bear Promotions in Ontario, Alabama, and Oregon. He changed his character in Alabama as Chris Von Colt to a [neo-nazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neo-nazi "Neo-nazi") gimmick. Colt's last known matches happened in December 1987 for [Portland Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland_Wrestling "Portland Wrestling").

## Personal life

Colt was openly homosexual and appeared in gay-themed [adult films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adult_films "Adult films") after he retired from wrestling.[^11] [^12] Colt and his tag team partner Ron Dupree were a romantic couple outside of wrestling.[^12] [^9] Ron died of a heart attack during a professional wrestling show in Washington state in 1975 with Colt present.[^12] [^9] The [ring name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_name "Ring name") "Chris Colt" derived from [Colt Studio Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colt_Studio_Group "Colt Studio Group"), a gay American pornographic group.[^9]

## Death

After years of drug and alcohol addictions, Colt died on May 23, 1995, in a [Seattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle "Seattle") homeless shelter, allegedly due to complications from [AIDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AIDS "AIDS"). His niece said he died in an alley, and a friend said that he died with a needle in his arm. It is not known if his death was caused by an accidental overdose or suicide. No autopsy was done. He was 48 years old.[^13]

Colt was featured on [Vice Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_Media "Vice Media") 's *[Dark Side of the Ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_Side_of_the_Ring "Dark Side of the Ring")* season 5 broadcast on April 9, 2024.[^14] [^15]
