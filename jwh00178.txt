(Panorama) Panoramic view of Henrys Lake, from a point on the east side, between Raynolds and Tyghee Passes, embracing a view from Raynolds Pass, on the right, past Red Rock and Henrys Lake Passes, to Tyghee on the left. In the center is Sawtells Peak, a well-defined extinct volcano. 10,600 feet in height, with a crater 1,000 feet in depth, and one and one-half miles across its longest diameter. Fremont County, Idaho. 1872.
