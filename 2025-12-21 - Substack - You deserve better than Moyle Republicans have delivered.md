A lot of people have asked me, and some friends of mine, why we won’t get in line behind Idaho Speaker Mike Moyle. These folks think Idaho is generally going in the right direction and Moyle isn’t hindering that.

The answer is simple: **Idahoans deserve better than Moyle Republicans are delivering.**

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!hZSv!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8be45f8f-5da3-46b1-9bf4-1d922974dedb_1079x956.jpeg)

Having Moyle as House Speaker would be a blessing in a purple or blue state. That fact is not lost on me.

Alas, we live in ruby-red Idaho, and Moyle and his pals should be delivering far more bang for the buck.

Now, his apologists will point out that he has cut income taxes year after year. That is correct. And that should be applauded.

Republicans are supposed to cut taxes at every turn. It’s why, among myriad other reasons, Idahoans continue to elect Republicans and not radical, tax-hungry Democrats.

Yet a quick glance at the rest of Moyle’s record should disappoint even his biggest apologists.

**Moyle has presided over an explosion in government spending.** State spending has grown more than 60% over the past six years. Lawmakers have added more than $6 billion to the annual budget and now face a massive deficit because they spent like Democrats.

**He voted to give a tax break to data centers,** a vote that drew Facebook to build a data center in Kuna.

**Moyle fought tooth and nail to keep the Idaho Transportation Department’s State Street campus.** Instead of selling the property for $40 million, the state has now dumped $60 million into renovations, and tens of millions more might be needed. That’s a $100 million swing at a time when the state needs cash.

**He continues to block grocery tax repeal when families are still grappling with high grocery prices.** Moyle has blocked the measure dozens of times, while granting hearings to far more liberal bills, including the next item on this list.

**Moyle continues to allow Launch, Idaho’s free-college scheme, to be funded.** It’s an $80 million welfare handout designed to get Idaho kids hooked on higher education. The program makes life harder for middle-class families trying to save for their kids’ college accounts. And yes, Moyle has voted against it. That’s clear. *But why hasn’t he blocked it the way he has blocked grocery tax repeal?*

**We still have Medicaid expansion, a nearly $100 million expense that gives free healthcare to single, able-bodied adults who choose not to work much.** It’s embarrassing that we continue to fund that year after year.

**We have a three-legged stool that taxes Idaho families at every turn.** I would prefer to repeal property taxes, but I ultimately want to see the three-legged stool concept dispensed with entirely. We could have already ended property taxes had Moyle and lawmakers exercised spending restraint.

Idahoans are a conservative bunch, and they expect a lot out of Republican lawmakers. They are not getting it. Performative, toothless measures like this year’s flag bill might get you X impressions and podcast appearance invites, but they don’t help Idahoans live freer, more prosperous lives.

I want a conservative Legislature that does bold, audacious things. I want a Legislature that has a vision for a freer Idaho and works tirelessly to get us there.

That’s not what we have now. It’s a tragedy.

And Idahoans deserve better.
