**Jacob Scott Sinclair** [^1] (born March 7, 1985) is an American musician, [record producer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Record_producer "Record producer"), singer, audio engineer, and songwriter. His production and songwriting credits include [Weezer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weezer "Weezer"), [Fall Out Boy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_Out_Boy "Fall Out Boy"), [Panic! at the Disco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panic!_at_the_Disco "Panic! at the Disco"), [5 Seconds of Summer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5_Seconds_of_Summer "5 Seconds of Summer"), [Pink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink_\(singer\) "Pink (singer)"), [New Politics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Politics_\(band\) "New Politics (band)"), [Andrew McMahon in the Wilderness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_McMahon_in_the_Wilderness "Andrew McMahon in the Wilderness"), [Gin Wigmore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gin_Wigmore "Gin Wigmore"), and [Train](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Train_\(band\) "Train (band)").[^2] [^3] [^4]

## Career

After writing and producing his first RIAA [Gold single](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gold_single "Gold single") with [New Politics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Politics_\(band\) "New Politics (band)") ("Harlem"), followed by his first RIAA [Platinum single](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_recording_sales_certification "Music recording sales certification") with [5 Seconds of Summer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5_Seconds_of_Summer "5 Seconds of Summer") ("She Looks So Perfect"), Sinclair moved into his own studio in [Echo Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Echo_Park,_Los_Angeles "Echo Park, Los Angeles"). There he produced Panic! At the Disco's #1 album *[Death of a Bachelor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_of_a_Bachelor "Death of a Bachelor")* and Weezer's *[White Album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weezer_\(2016_album\) "Weezer (2016 album)")*. He is performing on tour with [Beck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beck "Beck").

Sinclair co-wrote and produced Panic! at the Disco's *[Pray for the Wicked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pray_for_the_Wicked "Pray for the Wicked")* and *[Death of a Bachelor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_of_a_Bachelor "Death of a Bachelor")* albums (which debuted at number one on the [US Billboard 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Billboard_200 "US Billboard 200")) [^5] [^3] and produced Weezer's 2016 [*Weezer (White Album)*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weezer_\(2016_album\) "Weezer (2016 album)").[^3] Both were nominated for [Best Rock Album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Rock_Album "Grammy Award for Best Rock Album") at the [59th Annual Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/59th_Annual_Grammy_Awards "59th Annual Grammy Awards").[^6] He co-wrote and produced " [Uma Thurman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uma_Thurman_\(song\) "Uma Thurman (song)") " by Fall Out Boy,[^3] which debuted at number one on the U.S. iTunes Chart,[^7] reached number 22 on the [Billboard Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 "Billboard Hot 100"), and was certified 2× Platinum by the [RIAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recording_Industry_Association_of_America "Recording Industry Association of America") in December 2015.[^8] Sinclair received a Grammy nomination for [Album of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Album_of_the_Year "Grammy Award for Album of the Year") for his work as engineer and [bassist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bassist "Bassist") on [Taylor Swift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Swift "Taylor Swift") 's " [Everything Has Changed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everything_Has_Changed "Everything Has Changed") " [^3] alongside producer [Butch Walker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butch_Walker "Butch Walker").[^9] He co-wrote and produced the debut single, " [She Looks So Perfect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_Looks_So_Perfect "She Looks So Perfect") " by 5 Seconds of Summer [^3] that peaked at number one in over five countries and won *Song of the Year* at the 2014 [ARIA Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARIA_Music_Awards "ARIA Music Awards").[^10] Sinclair is the former bassist of the [indie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_rock "Indie rock") / [pop rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_rock "Pop rock") band The Films [^11] and the lead singer and producer of the indie pop duo Alohaha.[^12] [^3] In November 2018 Sinclair won the Spotify Secret Genius Award for best rock producer.[^13]

## Personal life

Sinclair is married to actress [Kate Micucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Micucci "Kate Micucci"). They had a son in January 2020.[^14] They live in Los Angeles and New York with their dog Claire.[^15]
