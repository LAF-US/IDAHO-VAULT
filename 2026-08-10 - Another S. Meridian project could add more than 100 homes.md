Another multi-acre property in South Meridian is spoken for.

Permit documents filed with the City of Meridian show plans for 127 single-family homes on just over 19 acres of land on Lake Hazel Rd., near Locust Grove Rd. The application characterizes the infill project, called Ravenna Subdivision, as “compact” and “well-connected.”

Renderings of the homes show two-story buildings with front-facing garages. Floor plans show three-, four-, and five-bedroom layouts.

![PCW - P3_ 2_1.png](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/c4722f34-be9c-413f-844a-ffd39cbbecde.png)

PCW - P3\_ 2\_1.png

The amenities include a playground, picnic space, and a 250-meter “fitness path”, and pathways along the Rawson Canal. The plans show the canal remaining open instead of being piped because “it is not feasible to pipe the canal.” The developers will need special permission from the city to do this. The canal bisects the property, and plans call for homes on either side. In addition to pathways along the canal, a road with a bridge is planned to go over it.

![](https://boisedev.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Screenshot-2026-08-1-Screenshot-2026-08-1-1.jpeg)

Plans for Ravenna Subdivision. Map: Courtesy The Land Group

The neighborhood would be built out in two phases: the first on the northern portion of the property, with 84 homes and a common area; and the second on the southern portion of the property, with 43 homes and the canal pathways.

The Lake Hazel Rd. area between Eagle Rd. and Meridian Rd. has seen a lot of development in recent years. Next door to Ravenna, [Brighton Corp.’s Pinnacle development](https://boisedev.com/project/pinnacle-meridian/) has taken shape with acres of homes, a library, [and a Costco.](https://boisedev.com/news/2025/06/26/costco-meridian-kuna/) Other developments have also cropped up in the area, like the Keep, Keep West, and Lavender Heights.
