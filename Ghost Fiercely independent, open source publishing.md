Review Ghost?

Reviewers mostly see Ghost as a clean, easy publishing CMS that works especially well for blogs, newsletters, memberships, and quick launches, with strong support and a fast back office. Several users say recent updates improved newsletter controls, comments, analytics, and day-to-day convenience. But the praise is tempered by repeated complaints about limits: email sending is tied to Mailgun, custom sender setup can be fiddly, and design flexibility is thin. One long-term user strongly criticizes lock-in, weak APIs, theming, and migration pain.
