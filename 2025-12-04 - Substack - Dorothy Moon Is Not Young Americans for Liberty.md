Idaho Republican Party Chairwoman Dorothy Moon is not Young Americans for Liberty.

She’s an Idaho resident and a longtime activist in Idaho’s policy sphere.

I’m writing this because state Rep. Heather Scott appears not to know that about Moon.

Scott also appears not to understand that the Idaho Republican Party— *her* party—fully supports repealing Idaho’s insidious grocery tax.

Let’s back up for a moment.

For nearly a decade, Heather Scott served as the conservative conscience of the Idaho House of Representatives. She stood shoulder to shoulder with principled conservatives and often went toe to toe with leadership to fight for, among other things, grocery tax repeal. There are dozens of examples of Scott battling leadership to get grocery tax repeal heard in the House. If you need a refresher, [here’s one of her epic tangles with then–House Majority Leader Mike Moyle](https://www.facebook.com/reel/10156202450269169).

It’s worth noting that almost exactly one year ago, Scott told a crowd in a forum that she and her fellow North Idaho lawmakers would vote to support grocery tax repeal. [You can see that at about the 3:30 mark in this video](https://open.substack.com/pub/dustinvseveryone/p/dorothy-moon-is-not-young-americans?r=5d9hfh&utm_campaign=post&utm_medium=web&showWelcomeOnShare=true).

Heather Scott was right then.

Moyle has wielded his power to block grocery tax repeal from getting a fair hearing while allowing all sorts of garbage into his chamber. He could have blocked, for example, the bill to give data centers a tax break. But he didn’t. He could have stopped Brad Little’s socialist college education handout. But he didn’t.

Instead, he has blocked grocery tax repeal dozens of times.

Heather Scott is no longer the principled rebel she once was—something I wrote on X a year ago. She has changed her tune and secretly hitched her wagon to the Moyle train. [Daniel Walters wrote about this shift some time ago.](https://www.investigatewest.org/secret-recording-shows-how-a-right-wing-idaho-lobbyist-tried-to-keep-a-legislator-in-lockstep/)

Folks, the transformation is complete, as evidenced by her recent comments at a North Idaho town hall about the upcoming 2026 legislative session. Here are her remarks in full:

> *I just want to add, since we’re on the topic, the grocery tax. **It’s a really easy sell for marketing, for lobby groups to say, oh, repeal the grocery tax, and it sounds great, and we think it’s great.** We as Idaho citizens get all our money back. We get a tax credit, but it forces everyone from Spokane or out of state to have to pay for groceries when they have to pay that tax. It also is the only tax that illegals are paying when they buy groceries. So keep that in mind, because it’s really been pushed on our state by an out-of-state group, and it seems like everyone wants to get rid of a tax. And I’ve heard, like, well, 98% of the people want to get rid of the grocery tax. I said, 98% of the people want to get rid of the property tax, too, and the income tax. But that tax is a tax where you get all your money back as a tax credit, but the illegals and the out-of-staters don’t get that money back. We keep that to take care of the roads and the local stuff. So just keep that in mind. Go ahead, sorry.*

This is, almost word for word, the thinking Mike Moyle has used to kill grocery tax repeal for the last two decades. And Heather has fully and completely adopted it.

What’s worse is that Heather is trying to dunk on a political foe in Young Americans for Liberty, but in doing so, she’s actually attacking the work of grassroots Idahoans who support the Idaho Republican Party’s push to put grocery tax repeal on the ballot in 2026.

[Moon wrote about the party’s efforts in a column earlier this year](https://idgop.org/2025/08/07/op-ed-its-time-to-end-the-grocery-tax/).

“Repealing the grocery tax is a common sense idea,” the chairwoman explained. She added, “Everyone needs food—rich, poor, or middle class—and it just seems wrong to tax something so essential to human life. We’re still feeling the effects of four years of Bidenflation, and working families could sure use a break at the checkout line.”

She also noted that this isn’t a top-down effort: “Local Republican volunteers will also have petitions available at your county fairs, along with signs and palm cards to share with friends and neighbors,” Moon continued.

Heather Scott’s remarks should be a slap in the face to everyone who has gathered signatures to put grocery tax repeal on the ballot. That’s saying nothing of the GOP delegates who have worked the process to position the party as supportive of repeal, despite Moyle’s efforts to block it.

Beyond that, Scott is simply wrong on the facts. Every attentive parent knows food costs are sky-high these days, and the so-called credit absolutely does not cover all the grocery tax families pay.

And it’s hardly the only tax illegal immigrants pay. They pay sales tax on all other purchases, and they pay for roads and bridges through fuel taxes.

Heather Scott is lying through her teeth to please Moyle. Idahoans deserve better.
