# The Gemstone reference review

Reviewed https://thegemstone.org/ and https://thegemstone.org/Frieze/Idaho on Aug. 13, 2026.

The site is a restrained, editorial knowledge/archive interface rather than a conventional portfolio landing page. Its visual language is primarily white space, charcoal text, pale green utility accents, and muted gray rules. The home page uses a narrow centered content column, a compact geometric mark in the upper-left, a pale-green search control in the upper-right, and a wide panoramic header image. The central introduction is short and link-led: “The Gem State” and “Go explore.”

The content page uses a consistent narrow column with breadcrumb navigation near the top, a large visual asset, a prominent title, horizontal rules, and a long-form quotation/body section. Navigation is quiet and text-based. The footer is minimal and separated by a thin rule. Dark mode is available as a utility control, but the default presentation is light and highly spacious.

Important cues to carry into Logan Finney's site: editorial restraint; more negative space; a narrow reading measure; simple text navigation; thin rules instead of repeated dashed borders; a small geometric/typographic signature; image-led entry points; and blue as Logan's own accent rather than copying The Gemstone's pale green. The current Logan site should borrow the structure and calmness, not duplicate The Gemstone's branding or content model.

The /about path currently returns a 404 on the live site, so the working reference pages are the home page and /Frieze/Idaho.

Design direction implication: keep Logan's blue (#2563eb), black, white, and gray palette; retain the home/resume/work information architecture; reduce the visual density of borders and controls; increase whitespace and editorial scale; and use a compact header with a simple mark/name, quiet links, and a small theme control.
