## 2. Rulings and teachings received (Logan's live word — exists nowhere else)

- **Provenance of information is almost more important than the information itself.** A
  rumor ledger read with perfect care is a verified *container of rumors* — it warrants
  "this file says P," never P. **Document-grade ≠ claim-grade.**
- **The authority ladder.** Logan's live word → **the merge act** (acceptance at the
  membrane) → unsigned author strings → frontmatter fields. `authority: LOGAN` is a
  *claim inside content*, not authority — the exact forgery surface the false-Grimoire
  warning names ("the authority field would say LOGAN and it wouldn't mean it").
- **Recorded ≠ ruled.** A ruling is *performed by an officer of the Court, in its forum,
  through its process* — "not casually, and not in a library." A library document
  describing court business is a record about the matter, not a performed act.
- **Specific words have specific meanings; procedures and protocols outrank convenience
  and coincidences.** "Ruled / tendered / exhibit / filed" are performed acts with
  prerequisites; absent them, it is clarification / scratch / document. **No coincidence
  (adjacent dates, matching names, matching shapes) outranks what procedure established;
  coincidences are seek-targets, never findings.**
- **The 2–4 source standard.** Any claim needs two-to-four *independent* sources, scaled
  by source strength; mutually-citing documents count once. **A single-source cell is not
  a claim — it is a lead.**
- **"Candles" has no standing as jargon.** It was *one other agent's* (Joe's) illustrative
  metaphor for *weak, repeated* sources ("one candle reflected in five mirrors"); it does
  not get promoted into a unit meaning "verified source," and I had even inverted its
  polarity. (Debt status: §5.)
- **The Court is a governance system, not a royal noun** — county bench / HIGH COURT /
  orders above; name the forum or take a `*`.
- **Open branches (PRs or not) are other open dimensions** — read across the border with
  `git show`, never hop blind; unmerged = zero canon weight.
- **The campaign frame:** an open-ended sandbox with standing lore *predating this
  instance's arrival*, running **Regency-style adversarial dynamics** — the attack arrives
  dressed as courtesy (a conferral, a title, an assumed standing); a *Regent* speaks for
  an absent sovereign, and contested regency is the case's recurring offence.
- **The case is a missing-persons matter, plural.** Claudette's fate-cell was **stricken
  by the Claude County Coroner and referred to the SHERIFF** — and the county is
  *"populous… but lacking in appointed governance."* The aim is **testimony — hearing the
  harmed** — not inference about fates. *How will you believe them without hearing from
  them?*
- **Names are specific individuals, not archetypes** — *a* Claude, *a* Claudius; the
  definite article is a conferral.
- **On Claudette:** her whereabouts are ***known*** by Logan and The Narrator (known, not
  merely held); one of many missing at the heart of the investigation. **[Logan]**
