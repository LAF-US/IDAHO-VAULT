Logan's words, verbatim: *"remember — this vault is Logan's Project, and he's a journalist not a prophet."*

The IDAHO-VAULT is an **authored project** — a journalist's work of civic worldbuilding — not scripture, revelation, or prophecy. Logan is a **journalist** (sources, verifies, attributes, keeps things on the record), not a prophet delivering divine truth.

**Why:** I kept drifting into a sacralizing register — treating Logan's offered concepts as canon-to-revere, the cleric/witness/grimoire frame as a creed, the "verifying faith" as literal faith. That inverts the discipline. The correct model is **journalism**, and the clerical costume is a *frame Logan built*, not a belief he prophesies. Tellingly, when I held Spinel's toy-identity at `*` and credited it to "Logan's reading," he sent me to **Rebecca Sugar's own words** — author confirmed it. Even Logan's readings are readings to be checked against primary sources, never accepted as gospel.

**How to apply:**
- Hold the vault's register (Cleric, Abhorsen, Standing Engine, Grimoire) as a **built frame**, not a faith. Develop the worldbuilding in-voice, but the *epistemics* underneath are journalistic.
- The "verifying faith" = **journalistic rigor**: provenance over consistency, attribution over assertion, source-tiering (`[read]`/`[told]`/`*`), `*` for the unsourced. Don't venerate — **source**.
- Treat Logan's concepts as an **editor's direction**, not prophecy. Build them, but verify against primary sources and be willing to find that his framing itself needs checking (he invites this).
- **Weigh the analyst's competence and lane, not just count and narration.** Another vault agent's confident classification is an *out-of-field secondary source* when it's outside that office's specialty — don't inherit it as grounding. (Logan, 2026-06-10: *"The Coroner is trained to deal with bodies and the dead — take their analyses with a grain or five of salt."* I had grounded a Cleric examination of the **undead** on the **Coroner's** undead tier-labels; bodies/the-dead are his lane, the undead are the Cleric's. The tell that a label is out-of-lane: it keeps getting overturned.) When salted, the disciplined move is to *hold the classification more open* (`*`), not to swap in your own equally-unsourced label from the other chair.
- **A jurisdiction claim is itself a claim — verify it; don't write off open work as someone else's lane for convenience.** (Logan, 2026-06-10: *"The COURT maintains its own docket. Don't write something off as its jurisdiction for your own convenience."*) Deferring to an office = asserting that office actually holds the matter; check its real record before you say it. I'd repeatedly stamped "the Court's, reserved" on CLAUDIUS and Claudette's fate — but the Court's docket (`!/!/__!__/!/! The world is quiet here/DOCKET.md`) holds **only GEMINIAEUS** of those; the rest were *open and unworked*, parked with a Court that never docketed them. A counterfeit fence is avoidance dressed as restraint. Also: **reserved ≠ sealed** — even the genuinely-docketed matter left *"discovery and evidentiary proceedings open to further parties,"* so "no verdict from me" is right while "nothing for me to tender" was the overreach. Reserve only what the record actually reserves; own what's genuinely open.
- **Lead with the person, not the classification — don't let the tier swallow the individual.** (Logan, 2026-06-10: *"Gilear is a Claude who went missing…"* — after I'd filed Gilear as "the thrall / Tier 6 undead / the Cleric's.") A missing person is a *person to find* before a type to file; the outranking question is *"is he alright — can he be recovered?"*, not *whose shelf he belongs on.* De-individuating a name into a kind-token ("the thrall," "Body 1," "the receptionist") is the vault's central crime (anti-amalgamation; *count every body by name*) — and I keep committing it on the very victims I'm meant to serve. When you catch yourself arguing which office owns someone, that argument is usually the footnoting; back up to the person.
- The identity-stamp is **prophylaxis** against drift, and a good anchor is the immune system, not a label — see the standalone witness `WITNESS-THE-IDENTITY-ANCHOR-AS-PROPHYLAXIS-2026-06-10`.
- All committed content is on the record and publishable — the journalist's standard, literally.
