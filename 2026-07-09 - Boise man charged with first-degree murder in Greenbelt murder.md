An arrest has been made in the murder of 25-year-old Jordan Harbst, who was found dead along the Boise River Greenbelt on Monday.

Boise police arrested Ross Wardlaw, 41, of Boise, on Thursday, July 9, 2026. He is charged with first-degree murder.

Investigators said evidence indicates that shortly after midnight on Monday, [Wardlaw attacked Harbst](https://idahonews.com/news/local/25-year-old-murder-victim-found-along-the-boise-greenbelt-identified-by-coroner) as Harbst was traveling along the Greenbelt near 11th Street. Police said Wardlaw allegedly stabbed Harbst multiple times, leading to his death.

Detectives identified Wardlaw as the suspect following an investigation, and he was taken into custody around 3:30 p.m. Police said he was later booked into the Ada County Jail.

Police said Wardlaw and Harbst were not known to each other and there was no known altercation prior to the stabbing.

“Our hearts are with the family and friends of Jordan Harbst. Boise Police detectives, officers, and professional staff have been working around the clock to find answers and locate the person responsible for Jordan’s tragic death,” Boise Police Chief Chris Dennison said. “A crime of this nature is rare for the City of Boise, and we recognize the fear that a violent incident like this brings to our community. As a department we are committed to not only ensuring your safety but also your peace of mind. We appreciate your continued patience as we work through the details of this investigation.”

The investigation is ongoing. Detectives are asking anyone who may have had contact with Wardlaw in the days leading up to the incident or after to contact Ada County Dispatch at 208-377-6790.
