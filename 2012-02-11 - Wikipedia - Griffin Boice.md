**Griffin Boice**, is an American multi-platinum [record producer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Record_producer "Record producer"), [mixer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audio_engineering "Audio engineering"), [songwriter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songwriter "Songwriter"), and [composer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Composer "Composer").

Born and raised in [Rexburg, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rexburg,_Idaho "Rexburg, Idaho"), Griffin grew up playing the double bass for 10 years in the symphony orchestra. Griffin was trained in the recording studio by Grammy nominated recording engineer Trent Walker. Moving to Los Angeles in 2008, he signed a music publishing deal with EMI/Sony as a producer / songwriter. Griffin has done software beta testing as well as co-creating sample libraries for virtual software instruments for Slate Digital.

Griffin has composed scores for feature films as well as music for advertisements, TV, and movie trailers.

Griffin Boice is the founder and creator of Puretone, a sonic meditation experience.

Griffin has worked with [Black Eyed Peas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Eyed_Peas "Black Eyed Peas"),[^1] [Wiz Khalifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiz_Khalifa "Wiz Khalifa"), [Rob Zombie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Zombie "Rob Zombie"),[^2] [Hollywood Undead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Undead "Hollywood Undead"),[^3] [^4] [^5] [John 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_5_\(guitarist\) "John 5 (guitarist)"),[^6] [^7] [The Saturdays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Saturdays "The Saturdays"),[^8] [Leighton Meester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leighton_Meester "Leighton Meester"), [Christina Milian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christina_Milian "Christina Milian"), [Gavin Rossdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gavin_Rossdale "Gavin Rossdale"), [Lucas Vidal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucas_Vidal "Lucas Vidal"), [Clever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clever_\(rapper\) "Clever (rapper)") aka Who Is Clever, [Cassadee Pope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassadee_Pope "Cassadee Pope"), [Aloe Blacc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aloe_Blacc "Aloe Blacc"), Mat Musto aka [Blackbear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackbear_\(musician\) "Blackbear (musician)"), [Krayzie Bone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krayzie_Bone "Krayzie Bone"), [Bebe Winans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bebe_Winans "Bebe Winans"), [Frankie J](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankie_J "Frankie J"), [Paradiso Girls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paradiso_Girls "Paradiso Girls") [^9]
