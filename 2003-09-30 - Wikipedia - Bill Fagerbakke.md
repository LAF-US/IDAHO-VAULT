**William Fagerbakke** ([/ ˈ f eɪ ɡ ər b ɑː k i /](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English "Help:IPA/English") [*FAYG-ər-bahk-ee*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Pronunciation_respelling_key "Help:Pronunciation respelling key"); born October 4, 1957) is an American actor. He voices [Patrick Star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Star "Patrick Star") in the *[SpongeBob SquarePants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SpongeBob_SquarePants_\(franchise\) "SpongeBob SquarePants (franchise)")* franchise, and played Michael "Dauber" Dybinski on the sitcom *[Coach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coach_\(TV_series\) "Coach (TV series)")*. He also appeared in 12 episodes of the sitcom *[How I Met Your Mother](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/How_I_Met_Your_Mother "How I Met Your Mother")* as [Marshall Eriksen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshall_Eriksen "Marshall Eriksen") 's father Marvin.

## Early life and education

Fagerbakke was born on October 4, 1957,[^1] in [Fontana, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fontana,_California "Fontana, California"),[^2] [^3] [^4] and moved to [Rupert, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rupert,_Idaho "Rupert, Idaho"), as a youth. He graduated from [Minico High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minico_High_School "Minico High School") in Rupert in 1975, where he was a three-sport athlete for the Spartans in [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_school_football "High school football"), [basketball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_school_basketball "High school basketball"), and [track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Track_and_field "Track and field").[^5]

Although he had multiple scholarship offers for [college football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_football "College football"), including [Pac-8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pac-12_Conference "Pac-12 Conference") schools, Fagerbakke decided to stay in state and attended the [University of Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Idaho "University of Idaho") in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow,_Idaho "Moscow, Idaho").[^5] He was a [defensive lineman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defensive_lineman "Defensive lineman") for the [Vandals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idaho_Vandals_football "Idaho Vandals football") and was ticketed to [redshirt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redshirt_\(college_sports\) "Redshirt (college sports)") in 1976, but was called into action in the fourth game of his sophomore season.[^6] The Vandals went 7–4 in [1976](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1976_Idaho_Vandals_football_team "1976 Idaho Vandals football team"), their first winning season in five years, and were 5–2 in the seven games that Fagerbakke started.[^7] Head coach [Ed Troxel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Troxel "Ed Troxel") planned on moving him to the offensive line in [1977](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1977_Idaho_Vandals_football_team "1977 Idaho Vandals football team"),[^8] but a knee injury in spring drills ended Fagerbakke's athletic career, which turned his focus to theater.[^7]

Fagerbakke's first theatrical role was in a campus production of *[Godspell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godspell "Godspell")*.[^5] He was a member of the [Delta Tau Delta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Tau_Delta "Delta Tau Delta") [fraternity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fraternities_and_sororities "Fraternities and sororities") and earned his [bachelor's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree "Bachelor's degree") in 1981, a result of "two years of football and four years of school." [^9] [^10] He later attended graduate school at [Southern Methodist University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Methodist_University "Southern Methodist University") in [Dallas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallas "Dallas"), [Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas "Texas").[^5] [^11]

## Career

![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/3b/Bill_Fagerbakke_on_Comic-Con_panel_%282009%29_-_Cropped.jpg/250px-Bill_Fagerbakke_on_Comic-Con_panel_%282009%29_-_Cropped.jpg)

Fagerbakke in 2009

Fagerbakke has appeared on television in roles such as [assistant coach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coach_\(sport\) "Coach (sport)") "Dauber" Dybinski on *[Coach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coach_\(TV_series\) "Coach (TV series)")*, and in films, including *[Funny Farm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funny_Farm_\(film\) "Funny Farm (film)")*.[^12] He had a role as the mentally disabled Tom Cullen in the 1994 [mini-series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miniseries "Miniseries") *[Stephen King's The Stand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Stand_\(1994_miniseries\) "The Stand (1994 miniseries)")*. From 1996 to 1999 he played Alan Parish in the [*Jumanji*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jumanji_\(TV_series\) "Jumanji (TV series)") TV series. In 1999, he had a role in [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_Box_Office "Home Box Office") 's original series *[Oz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oz_\(TV_series\) "Oz (TV series)")* as Officer [Karl Metzger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Metzger "Karl Metzger").

His character on *Coach* was based on a former assistant coach at Idaho, a graduate assistant nicknamed "Tuna." [^13]

He has appeared in numerous live action and animated films. With his first appearance coming in *[Perfect Strangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_Strangers_\(1984_film\) "Perfect Strangers (1984 film)")* in 1984*.* He has since appeared in films such as *[Loose Cannons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loose_Cannons_\(1990_film\) "Loose Cannons (1990 film)"), [Halloween II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halloween_II_\(2009_film\) "Halloween II (2009 film)"), [Rosewood Lane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosewood_Lane "Rosewood Lane")* and *[Baby Makers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Babymakers "The Babymakers").*

He has lent his voice to animated shows such as *[Family Guy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_Guy "Family Guy")*, [*American Dad*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Dad! "American Dad!"), *[The Grim Adventures of Billy & Mandy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Grim_Adventures_of_Billy_&_Mandy "The Grim Adventures of Billy & Mandy"),* *[Batman: The Brave and the Bold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman:_The_Brave_and_the_Bold "Batman: The Brave and the Bold"), [Kim Possible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Possible "Kim Possible"), [Duck Tales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DuckTales_\(2017_TV_series\) "DuckTales (2017 TV series)") and [Young Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Justice_\(TV_series\) "Young Justice (TV series)").* While having main roles on shows such as *[Transformers: Animated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformers:_Animated "Transformers: Animated"), [Lloyd in Space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lloyd_in_Space "Lloyd in Space")* and *[Gargoyles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gargoyles_\(TV_series\) "Gargoyles (TV series)").*

In 2007, he made a [cameo appearance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameo_appearance "Cameo appearance") on the show *[Heroes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_\(American_TV_series\) "Heroes (American TV series)")* as Steve Gustavson in the episodes "Run" and "Unexpected." In 2009, he had a role in the film *[Jennifer's Body](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer's_Body "Jennifer's Body")*. He also played the role of Marvin Eriksen Sr. in the [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS "CBS") sitcom *[How I Met Your Mother](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/How_I_Met_Your_Mother "How I Met Your Mother")*. In 2012, he made a cameo appearance in the TV show *[Weeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weeds_\(TV_Series\) "Weeds (TV Series)")*.

Since 2021, Fagerbakke has been the lead role in the *SpongeBob SquarePants* spinoff *[The Patrick Star Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Patrick_Star_Show "The Patrick Star Show").*

In 2022, Fagerbakke guest-starred in the fifth season of *[Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynasty_\(2017_TV_series\) "Dynasty (2017 TV series)")* as Peter de Viblis. His daughter Carson portrays Peter's daughter Patty.

### SpongeBob SquarePants

Since 1997, he has provided the voice of Patrick Star for the [Nicktoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicktoon "Nicktoon") *[SpongeBob SquarePants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SpongeBob_SquarePants "SpongeBob SquarePants")*. His role as Patrick has become one of the most iconic in all of animation.[^14] He has also been compared to the character's real-life counterpart as show writer [Kent Osborne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kent_Osborne "Kent Osborne") once said of Fagerbakke, "He is this big guy, and he plays Patrick so well. He's just this big guy, and he lumbers around." [^15]

In 2004, Fagerbakke voiced Patrick in *[The SpongeBob SquarePants Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_SpongeBob_SquarePants_Movie "The SpongeBob SquarePants Movie")*, along with subsequent films *[The SpongeBob Movie: Sponge Out of Water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_SpongeBob_Movie:_Sponge_Out_of_Water "The SpongeBob Movie: Sponge Out of Water")* and *[The SpongeBob Movie: Sponge on the Run](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_SpongeBob_Movie:_Sponge_on_the_Run "The SpongeBob Movie: Sponge on the Run")*. He also voices the character in video games and spinoffs.

The television show celebrated its 20th anniversary in 2019. In celebration, a television special was aired, titled " [SpongeBob's Big Birthday Blowout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SpongeBob's_Big_Birthday_Blowout "SpongeBob's Big Birthday Blowout") ". One scene includes each of the show's main characters' voice actors portraying live-action versions of their characters.[^16] While not the first *SpongeBob SquarePants* episode that blended animated sequences with live-action characters, this was the first time that the characters' voice actors have played a live-action scene all together.[^17]

[Super Bowl LVIII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LVIII "Super Bowl LVIII"), which took place in 2024, was broadcast on [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS "CBS"); the network's parent company [Paramount](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount_Global "Paramount Global") also owns [Nickelodeon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nickelodeon "Nickelodeon"), the network that airs *SpongeBob SquarePants*. As part of a plan to maximize the use of its broadcasting rights partnership with the [National Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Football_League "National Football League") (NFL), Paramount decided to air an "alternative" Super Bowl telecast on Nickelodeon that was aimed to be more family-friendly. The channel's website claimed that the broadcast was intended for "kids of all ages, from 2 to 102". The broadcast on Nickelodeon was hosted by [Nate Burleson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nate_Burleson "Nate Burleson") and [Noah Eagle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Eagle "Noah Eagle") in live-action and accompanying them were [SpongeBob](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SpongeBob_SquarePants_\(character\) "SpongeBob SquarePants (character)") and Patrick. Bill Fagerbakke and SpongeBobs voice actor [Tom Kenny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Kenny "Tom Kenny") voiced the characters in real-time, improvising their own jokes in-character, and also utilized [motion capture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_capture "Motion capture").[^18]

Fagerbakke and Kenny also hosted the [2024 Kids Choice Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Kids'_Choice_Awards "2024 Kids' Choice Awards") as SpongeBob and Patrick. They voiced their characters while being filmed backstage via motion capture, marking the first time the ceremony has been hosted by animated characters.[^19] [^20]

## Personal life

In 1989, Fagerbakke married actress Catherine McClenahan, with whom he had two daughters, one of which is actress Carson Fagerbakke.[^21] [^22] [^23] In September 2012, he filed for legal separation from McClenahan, citing [irreconcilable differences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irreconcilable_differences "Irreconcilable differences").[^24] He has lived in [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles") ever since filming the show *Coach*, however he visits Idaho often. He enjoys spending time with his grandchild.[^25]

In 1997, Bill Fagerbakke returned to his alma mater—the University of Idaho to star in benefit performances of the play *Love Letters*, staged by the Idaho Repertory Theatre to support the university's theater program.[^26]

Fagerbakke is good friends with his *SpongeBob SquarePants* co-star [Tom Kenny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Kenny "Tom Kenny").[^14]

## Accolades

**Kids choice awards**

In 2009 Fagerbakke was nominated for at the 2009 for Best Burp at the [Kids' Choice Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nickelodeon_Kids'_Choice_Awards "Nickelodeon Kids' Choice Awards") (shared with Tom Kenny), for their performances as SpongeBob and Patrick. In 2014 Fagerbakke won Favorite Animated Animal Sidekick as Patrick Star on SpongeBob SquarePants*.*[^38]

**Behind the Voice Actors (BTVA) Awards**

In 2013 Fargerbakke was nominated as a part of the Best Vocal Ensemble in a TV Series (Children's/Educational) in *SpongeBob SquarePants,* and in 2016 he was nominated as a part of Best Vocal Ensemble in a Feature Film for *[Sponge Out of Water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_SpongeBob_Movie:_Sponge_Out_of_Water "The SpongeBob Movie: Sponge Out of Water").*[^38]

**Other awards**

In 2012 he was nominated at the [Critics' Choice Movie Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics'_Choice_Movie_Awards "Critics' Choice Movie Awards") for Best Acting Ensemble in the film *[The Artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Artist_\(film\) "The Artist (film)").*[^39] In 2019 Fargerbakke received the Animation Hall of Fame Game-Changers Award for his work as Patrick Star at that year's World Animation & VFX Summit.[^40]
