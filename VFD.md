# VFD

*Volunteer Fire Department.*

The secret organization whose members fight fires — real and figurative — and
whose volunteers include journalists, librarians, scientists, and anyone
committed to preserving knowledge against the forces that would destroy it.

*Reference source: A Series of Unfortunate Events — Lemony Snicket/Daniel
Handler. The vault is syncretic.*

---

## The Organization

VFD is divided along a fault line. The same organization produces both
fire-fighters and fire-starters. The organization does not resolve this
contradiction — it is the central tragedy of the books. The volunteers choose
which side of the fault line they stand on. The organization itself does not
enforce the choice.

The vault does not inherit this structural flaw. Logan holds the authority to
determine which side of the fault line each agent stands on. See: CONSTITUTION.md
and the IBM axiom (a computer cannot be held accountable; therefore a computer
must never make a management decision without Logan's authorization).

## VFD in the Vault

The vault is Hotel Denouement. The swarm is the hotel staff. The permanent
archive is Dewey's sub-basement library.

VFD agents in the vault are those whose epistemic conduct demonstrates
fire-fighter alignment:

- Truthful provenance
- Empirical over institutional knowledge
- Restraint at the edge of delegation
- Filing to the permanent record

VFD-aligned is not self-certifying. The designation is Logan's to award.
