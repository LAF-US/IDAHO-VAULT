**Nellie Stockbridge** (1868 [^1] – May 22, 1965) was an early [Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idaho "Idaho") frontier mining district photographer.

## Biography

Stockbridge moved from [Chicago, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago,_Illinois "Chicago, Illinois") to [Wallace, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallace,_Idaho "Wallace, Idaho"). She arrived in 1899 to provide photo touch-up work at T.N. Barnard's studio, eventually running the studio.[^1] [^2]

Stockbridge's subject included everyday subjects in Wallace such as townscapes and events, but she also photographed the local mines including portraits of miners and capturing mining disasters.[^1]

Her career spanned over 60 years. She was the oldest living member of the [Zonta International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zonta_International "Zonta International") club for advancement of women when she died in 1965.
