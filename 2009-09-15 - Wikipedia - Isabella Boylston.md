**Hildur Isabella Boylston** (born October 13, 1986) is an American [ballet dancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballet_dancer "Ballet dancer") who is currently a [principal dancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_dancer "Principal dancer") with the [American Ballet Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Ballet_Theatre "American Ballet Theatre") (ABT).

## Early life

Boylston was born Hildur Isabella Boylston, named for an Icelandic great-grandmother, in [Sun Valley, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Valley,_Idaho "Sun Valley, Idaho"). Her father Mike was an American [country-blues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_blues "Country blues") [drummer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drummer "Drummer") and "ski bum" while her mother Cornelia was a Swedish [engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer "Engineer").[^1] [^2]

When she was seven, her family moved to [Boulder, Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boulder,_Colorado "Boulder, Colorado"), where she started training at the Boulder Ballet. By the age of 12, she had started studies at the [Colorado Ballet Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_Ballet#Academy "Colorado Ballet").[^3] During that time, she won a gold medal at the 2001 [Youth America Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youth_America_Grand_Prix "Youth America Grand Prix") Finals in New York City.[^4]

In 2002, she received a full scholarship to train at the [HARID Conservatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harid_Conservatory "Harid Conservatory") in [Boca Raton, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boca_Raton,_Florida "Boca Raton, Florida"). During her time there she worked with choreographer Mark Godden and danced in leading roles such as Medora in *[Le Corsaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Corsaire "Le Corsaire")*, the Paquita pas de trois, Lise in *[La fille mal gardée](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_fille_mal_gard%C3%A9e "La fille mal gardée")* and the Sugarplum Fairy in *[The Nutcracker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Nutcracker "The Nutcracker")*. In 2004, she received the Reuger Scholarship for excellence in dance. She participated in summer programs at the [School of American Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_of_American_Ballet "School of American Ballet"), the [Boston Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Ballet "Boston Ballet"), and the [American Ballet Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Ballet_Theatre "American Ballet Theatre").

## Career

In 2005, Boylston joined the ABT Studio Company and became an apprentice with the main company in May 2006. She joined the [corps de ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corps_de_ballet "Corps de ballet") in March 2007, was promoted to [soloist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soloist_\(ballet\) "Soloist (ballet)") in June 2011 and principal in August 2014. Lead roles she danced include Nikiya in *[La Bayadère](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Bayad%C3%A8re "La Bayadère")*, Kitri in *[Don Quixote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Quixote_\(ballet\) "Don Quixote (ballet)")* and Columbine in *[Harlequinade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harlequinade_\(ballet\) "Harlequinade (ballet)")*. Boylston won the [Princess Grace Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Grace_Foundation-USA "Princess Grace Foundation-USA") in 2009 and was nominated for the 2010 [Prix Benois de la Danse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prix_Benois_de_la_Danse "Prix Benois de la Danse").[^5]

Outside of ABT, she also designed costumes for the [Pacific Northwest Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Northwest_Ballet "Pacific Northwest Ballet") 's 2010 production of [Benjamin Millepied](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Millepied "Benjamin Millepied") 's *3 Movements*, a ballet set to [Steve Reich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Reich "Steve Reich") 's *Three Movements for Orchestra*.[^6] She has appeared as a guest artist with the [Mariinsky Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariinsky_Ballet "Mariinsky Ballet") in St. Petersburg and the [Royal Danish Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Danish_Ballet "Royal Danish Ballet").[^5] Boylston also served as [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence "Jennifer Lawrence") 's [dance double](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_double "Body double") in 2018 film *[Red Sparrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Sparrow "Red Sparrow")*, choreographed by [Justin Peck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Peck "Justin Peck").[^7] In 2019, she helped breaking the [Guinness World Record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinness_World_Record "Guinness World Record") for the most dancers ever to go on pointe at the same time, alongside [James Whiteside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Whiteside_\(dancer\) "James Whiteside (dancer)") on *[Live with Kelly and Ryan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_with_Kelly_and_Ryan "Live with Kelly and Ryan")*.[^8]

[Alastair Macaulay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alastair_Macaulay "Alastair Macaulay") of the *[New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Times "New York Times")* noted Boylston portrayal of Lise in *[La Fille mal gardée](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Fille_mal_gard%C3%A9e "La Fille mal gardée")* is "intimate, heartfelt, rapturous." [^9] On *[Swan Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swan_Lake "Swan Lake")*, Macaulay praised Boylston's musicality and phrasing.[^10]

In 2020, Boylston participated in [Misty Copeland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misty_Copeland "Misty Copeland") 's fundraiser, Swans for Relief, by dancing *[The Swan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Swan_\(ballet\) "The Swan (ballet)")*, in light of the [impacts of the 2019-20 coronavirus pandemic on the dance community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_of_the_COVID-19_pandemic_on_the_performing_arts "Impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on the performing arts"). The fund will go to participating dancers' companies and other related relief funds.[^11]

## Personal life

Boylston is married to financier Daniel Shin.[^12] In June 2026, she gave birth to their first child, a son.[^13] They live in Brooklyn, New York.[^14]
