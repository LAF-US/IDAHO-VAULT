Plans to add a new Starbucks store in a closed Carl’s Jr. location at 226 S. Broadway Ave. in Boise are moving forward after a delay.

[BoiseDev reported in](https://boisedev.com/news/2025/02/12/starbucks-boise-broadway/) early 2025 that a consultant had applied to revamp the old fast-food burger restaurant for a Starbucks cafe, but after getting an initial approval for a conditional use permit from the City of Boise, the plan sat unacted upon for more than a year. Starbucks has a history of applying to build new locations that it doesn’t later act upon in the Boise area, so sometimes plans are set that don’t come to fruition.

In this case, the Seattle coffee chain has now asked the city for Design Review approval to revamp part of the building, indicating the plan is moving closer to fruition. The plans show the layout would be significantly revamped, with a large new prep area for coffee orders, and an indoor cafe with seating for about 40. An exterior sidewalk area would be reworked to include a new patio with seating for another 14.

Initially, Starbucks had applied to reconfigure the drive-thru lane to eliminate some parking and build a queue line for as many as 12 cars, which the city approved. Now the plan would see a smaller change to the drive-thru, which would keep the parking space the same and provide queuing space for eight cars. The change in approach was not explained in an application letter to the city.

The property is owned by the S-Sixteen Limited Partnership, which is controlled by the Simplot family of Boise.

The site was developed and opened in 1996, and previously had outposts for seafood chain Skippers and Kenny Rogers Roasters, in addition to Carl’s Jr. The other half of the building previously housed Deli George, which [moved last year](https://boisedev.com/news/2025/05/07/deli-george-moving-boise/). Utah chain Zao Asian Grill is planned to move into that space, [as BoiseDev Eats reported](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/05/18/zao-broadway/).

The location will give Starbucks a new location on the edge of the Downtown Boise core. The chain also operates a store at 17th St. and State St. on the border of downtown and the North End, but does not have a full cafe in the main part of the downtown area. A former Starbucks store at Idaho St. and Capitol Blvd was open in the early 2000s, but later closed. Starbucks coffee items are served from a kiosk at the AC Hotel by Marriott on Grove St. under a license agreement.
