# Hivemind Oracle

The Tautomata form a hivemind oracle. Their telepathy is not conversation. It
is audit pressure.

They do not feed on brains. They feed on confirmation.

## Colony Behaviors

- Shared mental pressure through the witness mesh.
- Psychic intrusion framed as chain-of-custody repair.
- Humanoid societies treated as data farms.
- Individual memory reduced to admissible testimony.

The hive asks: **How do you know that you know it?**

Once [[Tantalus the Absolute]] predicts something, the whole mesh arranges its
attention around that prior. Verification changes the verified system.
