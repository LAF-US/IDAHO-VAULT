## Folder 11: Charles Ostner

File

## Content Description

From the Collection:

Research materials, including photographs, pamphlets, and booklets, used by Suzanne Dabney Taylor. Includes a framed photo of William Borah, and a book "The City without a Church," given to Taylor by Margaret Roberts (1900). Correspondence.

## Dates

- Creation: Majority of material found within 1934-1980

## Full Extent

From the Collection: 1 Cubic Feet (1 record box.)

## Language of Materials

From the Collection: English

## Additional Description

### General

Items related to Charles Ostner and his sculpture of George Washington on horseback that was commissioned for the [[Idaho]] State Capitol. Includes photos and newspaper clippings.
