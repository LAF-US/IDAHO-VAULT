#### Hasan Piker says 'no more' to moderate Democrats in speech while wearing Mao Zedong-style suit

Piker accused Democrats of collaborating with "fascists" at the 2026 College Democrats of America Convention in Washington, D.C., on July 18. (Credit: YouTube/@uhhsid - July 18)

Far-left political influencer [Hasan Piker](https://www.foxnews.com/category/person/hasan-piker) sparked heated backlash over the weekend after addressing a group of college students wearing an outfit resembling the wardrobe of former communist dictator Mao Zedong.

"No more to Trumpism, but no more to the Democrats who compromise, who conciliate, and who betray us," Piker said to applause at the 2026 College Democrats of America summer convention in a clip that was widely shared on social media, including by the account DSA Watch, which called the appearance a " [Hitler style speech](https://x.com/dsa_watch/status/2078671224884887575?s=46&t=n8ko7opPMScdUsNla5ciQQ) " and was viewed over a million times.

Piker continued, "No more to the fascist reactionaries, but no more to the Democrats who will collaborate and cooperate with the fascists, who will fund ICE, who will fund Israel, who will help poison the planet, who will stiff workers and help bosses."

The appearance, and specifically the Mao-style suit, generated instant outrage on social media.

Piker, backed by roughly 10 million followers across various internet platforms, has endorsed several far-left candidates across the country, including [campaigning](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/dem-senate-primary-erupts-key-state-candidate-teams-up-radical-streamer-america-deserved-9-11) with Democrat Senate candidate Abdul El-Sayed in Michigan, as the Democratic Party grapples with a socialist wave of candidates that have won elections across the country.

Various campaigns and candidates have faced criticism for associating with Piker, a streamer who has made a variety of [inflammatory comments](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/democrats-team-up-with-far-left-streamer-said-america-deserved-9-11) including that the United States "deserved" 9/11.

"I never would and never will criticize Mao Zedong," Piker said earlier this year in a clip that is currently recirculating on social media. "One of the great leaders of this world."

A College Democrats of America Executive Board member at a college in New York told Fox News Digital that the group "platforming someone like Hasan Piker is antithetical to the party that common sense democrats are trying to present to the American people before November’s midterms."

The board member added, "We have a golden opportunity to stand up to Trump’s corrupt and authoritarian brand of politics, fight against the MAGA affordability crisis, and restore congressional checks on an executive branch that has far overreached its role in our government. Platforming malignant outside actors, such as Piker, who actively work to cloud our policy platform will only harm our broad electability amongst average voters."

Fox News Digital reached out to College Democrats of America for comment.
