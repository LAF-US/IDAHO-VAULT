A Boise jewelry studio invites visitors to come and experience the art of jewelry creation.

SLVR 72 opened its store on Hill Road in 2023, giving guests a place to experience hands-on silversmithing workshops, permanent jewelry, charm bar, ear piercings, laser engraving, and more.

“We want guests to feel seen, heard, and cared for the second they set foot in our studio,” co-owner Heather Mangone said. “We are a space where friends gather, couples celebrate, and friendships blossom — a space to slow down and create something beautiful, unique, and lasting with your own hands.”

![SERVPRO Boise - Jan25](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/5b2a85ae-933e-4c98-82a5-e207d65304e9.png)

SERVPRO Boise - Jan25

SLVR 72 provides solid gold, sterling silver, and 14k gold-filled for visitors to use while making jewelry.

“We only offer materials that stand the test of time,” Mangone said. “Our charm bar (where people can make charm necklaces and bracelets), we do not carry the cheap gold-plated items that tarnish or turn your skin green. Our charm bar is water proof & life proof honestly.”

In addition to guests making their own jewelry, SLVR 72 sells a variety of handmade rings, bracelets, necklaces, earrings, and bolo ties.

![Freeform - Invested in Boise - 2:1](https://static-v2.boisedev.com/assets/8d57412c-01ee-47ef-b704-9f71ec57d3ba.png)

Freeform - Invested in Boise - 2:1

“My husband and I were both born and raised here in Boise, and the most important thing to us is that we care so much about the quality and experience people can expect from us,” Mangone said. “We want you to have the best, most memorable time in our studio, and we want you to know and trust the high quality of our materials.”

SLVR 72 [offers group events,](https://slvr72.com/) including in-home permanent jewelry parties and studio-based group bookings.

To book a workshop, event, or studio visit, visit SLVR 72’s website.
